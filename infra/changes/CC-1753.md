# CC-1753 · Hold proposed supplier-risk ownership

## Summary

Add a test-held planning overlay for every Semester subprocessor: the already
disclosed risk tier, a proposed primary seat, a distinct proposed backup seat,
a target for the first human review and a link to the existing exit-plan set.
The overlay remains proposed and cannot be read as a vendor assessment or an
accepted assignment.

## Environments and tenants affected

None. This change does not alter a provider, credential, tenant, database,
deployment or production setting. It does not contact or review a vendor and
does not assign a person to an operating seat.

## Risk

A planning row could be mistaken for completed procurement diligence. The
register keeps the authoritative Owner and Last review cells blank, labels the
overlay proposed, and states that vendor evidence, terms, regions and exit
paths have not been reviewed. Tests require exact subprocessor coverage,
distinct candidate seats, current targets, disclosed tiers and existing exit
plans.

## Rollback

Revert PR #1753. This removes the proposed overlay and returns SUP-035 to its
prior stale gap text; no environment or tenant rollback is required.
