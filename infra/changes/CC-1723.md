# CC-1723 · Maintain Starter dependencies

## Summary

Dependabot receives a dedicated weekly npm-ecosystem updater for the
standalone `starter/` pnpm manifest and lockfile. Minor and patch updates are
grouped, major updates remain isolated, and repository tests fail if the
updater loses its directory, ecosystem, cadence, pull-request bound or grouped
update types.

## Environments and tenants affected

None. This change alters candidate update configuration, tests and controlled
documents only. It does not deploy Starter, apply its migrations or policies,
activate a tenant, change credentials, connect an institution or use
institutional data.

## Risk

The updater may open future pull requests against a standalone pnpm tree with
independent CI. This candidate can validate the updater contract and its
failure controls, but it cannot prove a future Dependabot run, migration,
policy application, worker or deployed service. `video/`, `pipeline/` and the
broader `zite/` snapshot remain outside this updater. TR-15 also remains open
for the seven inline Edge Function handlers that lack executable behavior
tests.

## Rollback

Revert PR #1723 to remove the Starter updater and its repository guard. No
infrastructure state, credential or tenant data needs restoration.
