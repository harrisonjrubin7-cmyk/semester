# CC-1755 · Hold advisory service and tenant impact evidence

## Summary

Add a fail-closed advisory-impact assessment to the existing governed
lockfile inventory. Each lockfile names candidate services, while exact
package-to-service evidence and authoritative tenant reach are required before
a shipping-code assessment can be complete. Customer impact and notification
remain separate decisions.

## Environments and tenants affected

None. This repository-only contract does not ingest a finding, prove a package
ran in a deployed service, identify a tenant, change credentials or provider
state, accept a vulnerability, contact a customer, or deploy code.

## Risk

A candidate service map could be mistaken for a deployment or tenant
inventory. The model labels candidate scope separately, defaults tenant reach
to unknown for shipping code, and keeps SUP-031 at building until scanner
ingestion, exact tracing, authoritative inventory, durable storage, assignment
and operated evidence exist.

## Rollback

Revert PR #1755. This removes the assessment contract and restores SUP-031's
prior explicit gap; no environment or tenant rollback is required.
