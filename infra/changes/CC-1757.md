# CC-1757 · Bind assessed advisories to finding evidence

## Summary

Add a repository-only intake adapter that binds one assessed supply-chain
advisory to one immutable scanner run, artifact digest, commit and detection
timestamp. It opens the existing remediation lifecycle, preserves unresolved
service and tenant impact blockers, and evaluates the existing fail-closed
notification route without composing or sending a message.

## Environments and tenants affected

None. This change does not ingest a scanner result, persist or assign a real
finding, identify a tenant, decide customer impact or legal duty, access an
institution contact, compose or send a notice, record delivery, close an
incident, change provider or tenant state, or deploy code.

## Risk

A repository finding could be mistaken for operated remediation or incident
closure. The adapter requires exact scan evidence, carries every assessment
blocker, returns `customerNoticeSent: false`, and fixes
`incidentClosureEligible` at `false`. Durable storage, authenticated ingestion,
queues, senders, receipts and operated history remain explicit gaps.

## Rollback

Revert PR #1757. This removes the intake adapter and restores SUP-036's prior
unconnected lifecycle evidence; no environment or tenant rollback is required.
