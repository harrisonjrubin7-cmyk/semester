# CC-1719 · Run the target-size sweep nightly and fail closed

## Summary

The scheduled real-browser accessibility workflow now scans every registered
destination at phone and desktop widths in Comfortable, Snug and Tight density
modes. The target-size instrument fails on any non-exempt target below the
WCAG 2.2 AA 24-pixel floor, a control that answers nowhere, an unreachable
destination or a browser page error. Two measured AA defects are corrected in
the journey external link and Campus Directory category filter.

## Environments and tenants affected

None. The change affects candidate source, a scheduled repository workflow,
generated accessibility evidence and repository tests only. It does not
deploy, activate a service, connect an institution, use real institutional
data, change credentials or modify infrastructure.

## Risk

The sweep exercises default synthetic states in headless Chromium. It does not
establish accessibility conformance and does not cover signed-in, error, modal,
tablet, real-device or manual assistive-technology states. The 44-pixel AAA
measurement remains an informational design aim; only the 24-pixel AA floor
and incomplete-instrument conditions fail the run.

## Rollback

Revert PR #1719 to remove the nightly target-size step and restore the prior
journey-link and directory-filter dimensions. No infrastructure state,
credentials or tenant data needs restoration; the rollback is a source change
only.

## Evidence

- Pull request: https://github.com/harrisonjrubin7-cmyk/semester/pull/1719
- `app/scripts/targets-sweep.mjs` walks the shared destination inventory at six
  viewport/density combinations and fails closed on findings or incomplete

- `app/src/lib/targetnightly.test.ts` guards the scheduled workflow and
  fail-closed browser-sweep contract.
- The local real-browser acceptance run opened 63 of 63 destinations in every

  missed destinations and page errors.
- Hosted CI, CodeQL and HawkScan conclusions remain attached to the exact
  candidate head.
