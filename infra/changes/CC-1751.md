# CC-1751 · Design the supplier-compromise response runbook

## Summary

Add a repository-governed supplier-compromise response runbook and advance the
matching strategic expansion row from `not-started` to `designed`. The runbook
defines containment, evidence preservation, blast-radius assessment,
communications, recovery, and tabletop requirements without claiming that any
exercise or live response has occurred.

## Environments and tenants affected

None. This change affects repository documentation, its generated strategic
register, and the tests that hold their truth boundary. It does not change a
runtime, environment, credential, tenant, provider, deployment, or institution.

## Risk

Reviewers could mistake a designed runbook for operated evidence. The document,
register gap, and test all state that no tabletop was executed and no evidence
artifact exists. The document-map and expansion-register tests expose missing or
inconsistent governance references during CI.

## Rollback

Revert PR #1751. That removes the runbook and its test, restores SUP-033 to
`not-started`, and regenerates the strategic expansion register; no environment
or tenant rollback is required.
