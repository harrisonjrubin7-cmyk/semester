# CC-1756 · Hold supply-chain notification triggers

## Summary

Add a fail-closed routing contract for supply-chain customer-notification
bases. Confirmed impact requires complete service and tenant evidence;
contractual duty, regulatory duty with an exact deadline, or an authorized
legal/privacy uncertainty decision may route a notice while preserving every
unresolved assessment blocker.

## Environments and tenants affected

None. This repository-only contract does not decide a legal duty, ingest a
real incident, identify a tenant or supplier, access contacts, compose or send
a message, record delivery, change provider or tenant state, or deploy code.

## Risk

A `notice-required` route could be mistaken for a sent notice or a legal
decision made by code. The model requires an evidenced authorized basis,
returns `customerNoticeSent: false`, preserves unknown impact, and leaves the
authoritative duty store, contacts, sender, delivery and operated evidence as
explicit gaps.

## Rollback

Revert PR #1756. This removes the routing contract and restores SUP-034's prior
missing-trigger gap; no environment or tenant rollback is required.
