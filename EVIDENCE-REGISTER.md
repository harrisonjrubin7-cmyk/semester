## Evidence classes

| Class | Can establish | Cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| repository source/test | behavior exercised in the named tree and command scope | deployment, operation, independent assurance, legal sufficiency, customer acceptance or outcomes |
| generated artifact/build | contents and budgets of the generated candidate | deployed revision, external delivery, continuous availability or target configuration |
| deployment/configuration observation | state observed in one named environment at one time | historical uptime, staffed operation, another environment or customer approval |
| operational exercise | one scoped process performed by named participants | universal capability, contractual service level or independent assurance |
| professional/independent review | reviewer conclusion for the exact reviewed scope and date | certification or compliance beyond the report; future unchanged operation |
| executed company/customer record | accepted authority, obligation or decision for named parties | general availability, another customer or product outcome |
| measured outcome record | result under an approved method, population and denominator | causality, generalization, endorsement or reference rights without separate support |
