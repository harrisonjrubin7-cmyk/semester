M0—M10 sequence
M0 · evidence required

Company foundation

Incorporation, IP, banking, domain, policy library, source control, and key-person continuity.

Proof and next gate

Proof: The company can sign a pilot and operate accounts safely.

Next: Store executed company and continuity evidence; repository documents alone do not close this gate.

M1 · partial

Safe core app

Authentication, tenant model, persistence, RLS, Action Center, source labels, accessibility baseline, and monitoring.

Proof and next gate

Proof: A student safely uses persistent private data in a real two-account test.

Next: Close production auth, tenant-isolation, persistence, accessibility, support, and monitoring evidence.

M2 · partial

Registration Readiness

Path, term plan, conflict detection, backups, advisor agenda, and official handoff.

Proof and next gate

Proof: 10-20 students complete the workflow in observed usability testing.

Next: Finish the bounded workflow, then record observed usability evidence without enabling registration writes.

M3 · evidence required

Controlled pilot

A 25-75 student cohort, support, feedback, metrics, and an outcome report.

Proof and next gate

Proof: The pilot produces its predefined evidence-backed result.

Next: Secure design partners, owners, consent, support coverage, measures, and a signed pilot boundary.

M4 · blocked

Institution confidence

SSO, minimal read-only data, tenant configuration, procurement pack, DPA, and incident/restore evidence.

Proof and next gate

Proof: One institution approves and launches a scoped deployment.

Next: Institution and provider approvals plus hosted restore, incident, security, and procurement evidence.

M5 · not started

Repeatable adoption

Templates, implementation center, support playbooks, pricing, and first paid conversion.

Proof and next gate

Proof: A second deployment requires little custom work.

Next: Complete one controlled institutional launch before standardizing a second.

M6 · blocked

Learning and advising expansion

Course context, cited study support, advisor workflows, and controlled Hermes.

Proof and next gate

Proof: Students and staff use connected workflows safely.

Next: Prove the core pilot and approve connected data, AI provider, advisor permissions, and evaluation.

M7 · not started

Platform expansion

Career, campus resources, opportunities, and student-controlled evidence.

Proof and next gate

Proof: Additional modules reuse the same control plane.

Next: Admit modules only after shared identity, policy, evidence, and lifecycle controls are proven.

M8 · blocked

Deep integrations

LTI, OneRoster, approved SIS gateway, reconciliation, and parallel run.

Proof and next gate

Proof: Integration health and data quality are proven across terms.

Next: Funded partner scope, approved credentials, reconciliation, failure handling, and term-length evidence.

M9 · blocked

Bounded system-of-record modules

One specific institutional workflow becomes authoritative.

Proof and next gate

Proof: Parallel run, reconciliation, approvals, rollback, and audit evidence pass.

Next: Name one bounded authoritative workflow and obtain institution, security, legal, and operational approval.

M10 · not started

University operating platform

Multi-campus operation, modular replacements, and mature assurance and operations.

Proof and next gate

Proof: Multiple institutions operate modules predictably at scale.

Next: Earn this status through repeated operating evidence; architecture and code alone cannot close it.
