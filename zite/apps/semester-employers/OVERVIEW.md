# Semester Employers

A portal for employers and partner organizations. Employers sign in, post internships, jobs, research roles and mentorships that students see in Semester → Career, and review who applied. They only see the portfolio items each student chose to make public.

Hiring rules are enforced in Semester's own Postgres database: only the person who posted an opportunity can edit it, remove it or decide on its applicants. Students can't apply after an opportunity closes or is removed. Every change is logged in a record nobody can edit.
