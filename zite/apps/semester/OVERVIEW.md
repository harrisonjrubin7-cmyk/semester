# Semester

Semester is a student action platform. It gives students one governed place for their academic, campus and career life. It also gives faculty, advisors, staff and administrators role-based workspaces on the same connected records.

- **Who it's for:** students first. Faculty, advisors and staff work in their own role workspaces, and institution admins use a separate Institution Console.
- **How it's organized:** seven areas (Today, Plan, Learn, Help, Campus, Progress, You), each answering a plain question like "what now?" or "who can help?".
- **Trust:** every record shows where it came from (Official, Connected, Made here, Yours, Sample, AI-assisted). Privacy uses one question everywhere: "Who can see this?". Consequential actions are reviewed, approved by a second person and audited.
- **Foundation phase:** all demo records are labeled Sample. SIS, LMS, SSO and calendar connectors stay off until an institution authorizes them. Ask Semester writes cited, AI-assisted answers (via OpenAI) from only the records the member can see. Institutions are invite-only: admins issue a code, joins stay pending until approved, and admins can remove sample data before going live.
