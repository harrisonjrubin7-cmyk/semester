# Semester Company OS

The internal operating system for the Semester company team. It runs customers (pipeline, contracts, invoices, implementation, support), operations (requests, risks, incidents, release evidence, site publications, Trust Center, marketing, app review) and board reporting. No student content is ever loaded here.

- **Who it's for:** Semester employees only. Sensitive actions need a company role and a second person's approval.
- **Decision register:** the open inputs from the implementation blueprint (launch cohort, AI providers, retention, plans and so on). Someone proposes an answer with its source, and a different person confirms it before it counts.
