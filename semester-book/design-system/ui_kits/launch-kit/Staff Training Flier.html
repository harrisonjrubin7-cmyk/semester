Get ready for the Registration Readiness Pilot

Thirty minutes to learn what students will see, what you'll see, and where to send questions.

What students get

  • One next step for registration, with the source of each fact
  • Holds explained, with the office that owns them
  • A choice to share their plan with you

What you get

  • Plans students chose to share, nothing else
  • Hand-offs routed to your office
  • Every view logged in the audit trail

What doesn't change

The SIS stays official. Semester reads it and never edits records. Grades, holds and aid are never sent to AI.

When something looks wrong

Tell the student the registrar's record is the source, then use “Flag for registrar”. Support replies within one business day.
