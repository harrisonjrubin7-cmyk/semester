How to run Semester

Everything the company does — business, sales, marketing, product, engineering, security, success, support and people — with owners, cadence and the evidence that shows it is done. A planning document, not legal, tax or financial advice.

1. What Semester is, in one paragraph

Semester is the student action platform: one daily workspace that turns a student's semester into the next clear step, with the source of every fact shown beside it. It works alongside a school's official systems, sells to universities through paid one-term pilots, and keeps student records out of AI providers. Today it is a solo-founder LLC, bootstrapping, with one paid university pilot signed.
