SEMESTERVanderbilt University · Fall 2026 pilot
Every student arrives at registration ready.
The Registration Readiness Pilot gives Vanderbilt students one clear next step — holds, requirements, deadlines — with the source of every fact beside it. Your official systems stay official.

What students get

  • Today: the next action, ranked by what blocks registration
  • Holds and requirements, labelled by source
  • Degree progress and an advising agenda
  • Sharing they control, with an access history

What stays official

  • Your SIS, LMS and registrar records
  • Grades, transcripts and billing
  • Decisions made by your offices
  • Semester reads; it does not overwrite

Success, measured together

Students with no unresolved hold at their registration timeBaseline → target, agreed at kickoff
Advising appointments arriving with an agendaBaseline → target
Weekly active students completing a next actionReported weekly

Data, security, access

Governed by Vanderbilt’s DPA and FERPA addendum. Hosted on Vercel + Supabase; subprocessors listed. Student records never reach an AI provider. SAML single sign-on. HECVAT Full and an accessibility conformance report in preparation.

How the term runs

Weeks 1–2 SSO, data feed, baseline. Weeks 3–12 students live, weekly KPI review with your champion. Weeks 13–14 results review and renewal decision on a named date.
