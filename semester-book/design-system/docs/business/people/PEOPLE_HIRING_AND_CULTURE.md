> Every customer promise connects to a financial owner, delivery owner, support process, security/privacy control, product capability and evidence trail.

## Org design

Founder/CEO → product and design; engineering and platform; learning and academic operations; student success and implementation; sales and GTM; customer success and support; security, privacy, compliance and legal ops; finance and business operations; partnerships and developer ecosystem; people and culture.

## Hiring sequence

| Stage | First priority hires |
| --- | --- |
| Prototype to pilot | Full-stack/platform engineer, product designer, implementation/CS lead |
| First paid pilots | Institutional sales lead, security/compliance adviser, support/implementation capacity |
| Pilot conversion | Product manager, data/analytics, registrar/academic operations expert |
| Expansion | Platform/SRE, partnerships/integrations, marketing/content, finance/operations |
| Platform scale | Developer relations, marketplace/partner team, regional operations, research/outcomes |

Every hire still waits for its revenue trigger (docs/run/Hiring Plan.html).

## Every role has

Mission, outcomes, competencies, technical/domain requirements, decision rights, metrics, interview process, references, compensation and equity range, onboarding plan, 30/60/90 deliverables, access needed, security/privacy training, review expectations.

## Cultural standards

- Student agency over surveillance
- Evidence over hype
- Accessibility by default
- Trust before shortcuts
- Interoperability over lock-in
- Human judgment in high-stakes decisions
- Reliability before vanity features
- Documentation is part of the product
- Every critical workflow has a rollback
- Every public claim has evidence
- Every customer promise has an owner
- Every incident creates a learning loop
