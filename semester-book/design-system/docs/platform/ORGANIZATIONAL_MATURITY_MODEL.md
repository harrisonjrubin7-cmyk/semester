# Culture, cadence and organizational design

_Semester platform maturity program · 2026-10-05 · Source: uploads/what else is needed to become the apple google and.pdf. Status is design-stage: nothing is "done" until repo code, tests and release evidence exist._

**Principle:** Semester must become the system people prefer to use every day, the system institutions trust to operate, and the system partners prefer to build on.

## Standard

- Principles: student agency over surveillance; evidence over hype; trust before shortcuts; interoperability over lock-in; accessibility by design; human judgment for high-stakes decisions; systems over heroics; reliability before vanity features; documentation is part of the product; every claim needs evidence; every action needs an owner; every critical workflow needs a rollback.
- Functions: product and design; engineering and platform; learning science and academic operations; student success and implementation; security, privacy and compliance; GTM and customer success; finance, legal and operations; developer ecosystem and partnerships; research and evidence.
