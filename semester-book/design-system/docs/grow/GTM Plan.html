Go-to-market plan and price book

Owner: founder. Status: draft, October 2026. Prices are planning assumptions, not published prices, until the pilot results and counsel review are in. External numbers come from Market Research, where each is sourced.

Strategy in one line: universities first, through one paid one-term pilot per institution, with named success measures and a renewal decision date. Vanderbilt is pilot #1; its results become the case study that sells pilot #2–#5.
