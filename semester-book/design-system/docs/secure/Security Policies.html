Security policy set

Version 0.1 draft · October 2026 · Owner: founder (acting security lead). Reviewed every year and after any incident. These policies map to the HECVAT and SOC 2 control areas; we hold neither attestation today.

Scope: Semester production systems (Vercel hosting, Supabase Postgres and auth, the AI gateway), company laptops and accounts, and every person with access. Student data belongs to the institution and is handled only as its DPA allows.

1. Access control

HECVAT: Authentication, Authorization · SOC 2: CC6

  • Least privilege by role. Company roles (sales, finance, marketing, investors) get no access to student data.
  • Production database access is limited to named engineers, granted through just-in-time requests that are logged.
  • MFA is required on every company account. Customer staff sign in through institution SAML SSO.
  • Access is reviewed every quarter, and removed within 24 hours when someone leaves.
  • Row-level security on every table holding student data, tested in CI.

2. Encryption and keys

SOC 2: CC6.1, CC6.7

  • TLS 1.2 or higher in transit, and AES-256 at rest (provider managed).
  • Secrets live in the platform secret store, never in the repo. They rotate every year, and immediately if exposed.
  • Backups are encrypted with the same controls.

3. AI and student data

  • The AI gateway strips and blocks student-record fields before any model call. The block list is tested in CI and red-teamed before each release.
  • Model providers are under no-training, zero- or limited-retention terms, and are listed as subprocessors.
  • Students are told when content is AI-assisted. A person confirms any consequential action.

4. Vendor management

  • Every subprocessor gets a security review before use: their SOC 2 or equivalent, a DPA, and the data location.
  • The subprocessor list is published, and institutions get 30 days' notice before a new one is added.
  • Vendors are reviewed every year.

5. Incident response

  • Severities: S1 student data possibly exposed; S2 pilot down or wrong data shown; S3 degraded; S4 minor.
  • For S1 and S2: an incident lead is named within 15 minutes, and evidence is preserved.
  • The institution is notified within the DPA window (target: within 24 hours of confirming an S1). Customers hear from us before the public does.
  • A blameless review within 5 business days, with every action item tracked to closure.

6. Business continuity and disaster recovery

  • Targets: RPO 24 hours (daily backups plus point-in-time recovery where available), RTO 8 hours.
  • A restore drill every quarter, timed and recorded.
  • The founder is a single point of failure. Credentials and runbooks are in a sealed emergency kit with a named backup contact.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • The institution sets retention. By default, data is deleted within 30 days of contract end, with written confirmation.
  • Logs are kept for 1 year. Audit logs are kept as long as the contract requires.
  • Student data requests (view, correct, delete) are routed through the institution.

8. Secure development

  • Every change goes through a pull request with review. CI runs tests, RLS tests, dependency checks and secret scanning.
  • Staging mirrors production configuration with synthetic data. Real student data is never used in development.
  • Critical vulnerabilities are patched within 7 days, and high ones within 30.
  • A penetration test before the pilot goes broad, then every year.

9. Acceptable use and devices

  • Company devices: full-disk encryption, automatic updates, screen lock, and the ability to wipe them remotely.
  • No student data on personal devices, in email attachments or in chat tools.
  • Phishing and security training when someone joins, then every year.

10. Logging and monitoring

  • Auth events, admin actions, data access and AI gateway blocks are logged with the actor's role.
  • Alerts for sign-in failure spikes, error rate, sync lag and unusual data export.
