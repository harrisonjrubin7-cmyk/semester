Product brief · October 2026

One place for a student's whole semester, built with the institution.

Semester connects to an institution's own systems: the SIS, the LMS and identity. It turns their official records into one clear plan for each student, while staff get a console to support, approve and audit.

For students

  • Courses, deadlines and registration readiness in one plan
  • A study planner that adapts to their pace
  • Ask Semester, an AI study helper that never sees their student records
  • Accessible by default: captions, contrast, screen readers

For staff

  • Advisors see who needs help, and why
  • Registrars get SIS reconciliation and approvals
  • Faculty get course pages and announcements
  • Support and moderation queues

For the institution

  • Runs on the institution's data, under its rules
  • Every action is logged with the role that took it
  • SAML single sign-on
  • Configurable branding and policies

How data is handled

  • The institution is the system of record. Semester reads from it and never replaces it.
  • The AI only ever sees course content and what the student types, never student records.
  • Company staff (sales, finance, investors) never see student records.
  • Data agreements follow FERPA, with a DPA for each institution.

Where we are

  • Pre-seed, founder-led, bootstrapped.
  • First paid pilot signed with Vanderbilt University. Dates not yet set.
  • In progress for the pilot: HECVAT Full, VPAT/ACR, SSO, cyber and E&O insurance.
  • We describe status, not certifications we don't hold.

What a pilot looks like

One term, a defined group of students and staff, and connections to the SIS and SSO. Success measures are agreed up front with the institution, and a results report comes at the end of the term.
