What Semester does for you

One section per role. October 2026, pilot stage. Features depend on which ones each institution turns on.

Students

Today · Plan · Learn
  • One plan with your courses, deadlines and registration readiness
  • A study planner that adapts to your pace and reminders you control
  • Ask Semester, an AI study helper that never sees your records
  • Campus community: groups, events, Q&A, mentorship and a verified marketplace

You choose what's shared, with whom, and can reset it.

Advisors

Caseload
  • See who needs attention, with the reason and its source
  • Meeting notes and next steps the student can see
  • Registration holds and readiness at a glance

Read access is limited to your assigned students and is logged.

Faculty & TAs

Courses
  • Course pages, announcements and study groups
  • Course AI policy: set what Ask Semester may help with
  • Endorse answers in course Q&A

Grades stay in the LMS and SIS. Semester shows them; it doesn't replace them.

Registrars

Records
  • SIS reconciliation: see and fix mismatches
  • Approvals with a full decision trail
  • Consequence copy before every irreversible change

The SIS remains the system of record.

Student affairs

Community
  • Choose which community and marketplace features are on
  • Moderation queue and marketplace safety rules
  • Club registration, budgets and peer-leader programs

IT & security

Console
  • SAML SSO and role mapping
  • Integration health for the SIS, LMS and calendar
  • Audit log of every staff and admin action

Security status is shared honestly. HECVAT Full is in progress; no certifications are claimed.

Institution leaders

Outcomes
  • Pilot success measures agreed up front
  • A results report at the end of the term
  • Aggregate adoption and engagement, with no individual records

Families

Shared by the student
  • See only what the student chooses to share
  • Access history is visible to the student
