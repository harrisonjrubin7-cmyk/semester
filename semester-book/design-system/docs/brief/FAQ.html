Frequently asked questions

For institution leaders, IT, registrars, faculty and students. October 2026.

The product

What is Semester?

A student and staff platform that sits on top of an institution's existing systems. It turns the institution's official records into one plan for each student and gives staff a console to act on it.

Does it replace our SIS or LMS?

No. The SIS and LMS remain the systems of record. Semester reads from them, shows each record's source and sync time, and sends approved changes back through the institution's own processes.

Who uses it?

Students, advisors, registrars, faculty, support staff, moderators and institution administrators. Each role sees only what it needs.

Data, privacy and AI

Does the AI see student records?

No. Ask Semester works only from course content and what the student types. Grades, enrollment, financial and other student records are never sent to an AI model.

Who at Semester can see student data?

Only named support and engineering staff, under the institution's DPA, and every access is logged. Sales, finance, marketing and investors never see student records.

How does FERPA apply?

Semester acts as a school official under the institution's direction, under a signed DPA or FERPA addendum. The institution decides retention and deletion.

Where is data hosted?

On US cloud infrastructure: application hosting plus a managed Postgres database. The DPA lists every subprocessor, and we give notice before adding a new one.

Security and accessibility

Do you have SOC 2?

Not yet. We share our current security status honestly. HECVAT Full is in progress for our first pilot, and SOC 2 is planned. We don't claim certifications we don't hold.

SSO?

Yes: SAML single sign-on with your identity provider. Role mapping is configured with your IT team.

Is it accessible?

We design to WCAG 2.2 AA, and a VPAT/ACR is in progress. Every status is shown with a word and an icon, never colour alone.

Pilots and pricing

What does a pilot involve?

One term with a defined group of students and staff, plus SIS and SSO connections. Success measures are agreed at kickoff and reported at the end of the term.

Who else uses Semester?

Semester is early. Our first paid pilot is signed with Vanderbilt University.

How is it priced?
