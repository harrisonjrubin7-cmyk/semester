User guide

For students, staff and institution admins. October 2026, pilot edition. What you see depends on your role and on which features your institution has turned on.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with your school account (single sign-on).
  2. Check Today. It shows what needs you now, and why.
  3. Connect anything extra you want, such as a personal calendar, under You → Connect.

Every item shows where it came from: ◆ Official (from your school), ⇄ Connected, ○ Yours, or ✦ AI-assisted, along with when it last updated.

For students

Plan

Calendar, deadlines and registration readiness. A green check means your school's records show you're clear to register.

Learn

Courses, the study planner and Ask Semester. Ask Semester works only from course material and what you type. It can't see your grades or records, and your instructor's course policy decides what it can help with.

Community

Groups, events, Q&A, clubs and mentorship. Only verified members of your campus can see posts. Use Report on anything unsafe.

Marketplace

Buy and sell with verified campus members. Always pay in the app, and meet at a safe spot. You can't sell graded work, exams or answer keys.

Privacy

Under You → Privacy & data you decide who sees your profile details, view what's shared, and reset personalisation.

For advisors

Caseload lists your students who need attention, with the reason and its source. Notes you mark as shared are visible to the student.

For faculty and TAs

Post announcements, set your course AI policy, and endorse answers in course Q&A. Grades stay in your LMS.

For registrars

The Reconciliation queue shows mismatches between the SIS and Semester. Approvals show what will change before you confirm.

For student affairs

Turn community and marketplace features on or off, review reports, and manage club registration and peer-leader programs.

For institution admins and IT

In the console: SSO and role mapping, integration health, the audit log, and feature flags.

Getting help

Students: use your school's help channel first. Staff: use Help → Contact support in the app. If something looks wrong in your records, your school's office is the authority. Semester shows its data, but the school owns it.
