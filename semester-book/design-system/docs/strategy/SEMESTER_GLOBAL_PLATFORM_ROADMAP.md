# Global platform roadmap

_Semester strategy · 2026-10-05 · Source: uploads/anything else to finish semester and make it the m.pdf. Design-stage evidence only; scores rise with repo, test and release evidence._

> Semester wins when students prefer it daily, institutions trust it operationally, and partners prefer building on it.

- Internationalization, localization, RTL
- Time zones, academic calendars, term structures
- Currency/tax abstraction, regional payments
- Regional data residency, hosting, subprocessors
- Regional privacy and accessibility law
- Local identity systems
- Different credential and grading models
- Government reporting requirements
- Local support and implementation

Do not hardcode US higher-ed assumptions into the education graph. See docs/platform/GLOBAL_EXPANSION_ARCHITECTURE.md.
