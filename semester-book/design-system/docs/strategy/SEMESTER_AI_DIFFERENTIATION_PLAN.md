# AI differentiation plan

_Semester strategy · 2026-10-05 · Source: uploads/anything else to finish semester and make it the m.pdf. Design-stage evidence only; scores rise with repo, test and release evidence._

> Semester wins when students prefer it daily, institutions trust it operationally, and partners prefer building on it.

Safer and more useful than general chat, not just more conversational.

## Every answer can show

- Context used
- Sources
- Policy applied
- Model/provider selected
- Uncertainty
- Official versus guidance
- Action the user can take
- When a human should be involved

## AI trust UI

Answer · source citations · institution/course policy · context used · confidence and limits · suggested next actions · ask an advisor/instructor/office · give feedback · report a problem.

## Deterministic policy engine

Prerequisites, co-requisites, credit limits, registration windows, holds, degree requirements, residency, transfer equivalencies, academic standing, grade rules, regrade policy, graduation, payment-plan eligibility, feature/role/consent eligibility, retention, AI policy. **The AI can explain a rule. The policy engine decides it.**
