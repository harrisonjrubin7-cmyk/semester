1. The short version

Semester uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to sign you in, keep you secure and remember your settings. We don't use advertising cookies, and we don't let third parties track you across sites.

3. Public website

The marketing site uses no advertising or social-media tracking. If we add analytics, they will be aggregate and cookieless where possible, and we will update this notice first.

4. Your choices

  • Strictly necessary items can't be switched off; Semester won't work without them.
  • Functional and analytics items can be cleared in your browser or turned off in You → Privacy & data.
  • Where law requires consent (for example in the EU or UK), analytics stay off until you agree.
