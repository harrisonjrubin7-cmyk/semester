Data Protection Addendum (FERPA)

Addendum to the Master Services Agreement · Template · Institution paper takes precedence where required

Draft for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Brackets [ ] mark details to fill in. Institutions often require their own paper; use this as Semester's starting position and fallback.

1. Roles

Institution is the owner and controller of Education Records. Semester is designated a school official with a legitimate educational interest under FERPA (34 CFR §99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)), performs an institutional service, is under Institution's direct control for its use and maintenance of Education Records, and uses them only for the authorized purposes below.

2. Purpose

Provide the Services in the Order Form. No other use. No sale, no advertising, no profiling for non-educational purposes, no AI model training.

4. Redisclosure

Semester won't redisclose Education Records except to subprocessors listed in Annex B under written terms at least as protective as this DPA, to the student with Institution's authorization, or as required by law with prompt notice to Institution unless legally prohibited.

5. Security

  • Tenant isolation enforced in the database (row-level security on every table, tenant ID on every row).
  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
  • Least-privilege access for Semester personnel, MFA, access logged and reviewed [quarterly].
  • Audit log of record access by staff users, available to Institution.
  • AI gateway blocks Education Records from model providers; tested before launch and on each change.
  • Backups with tested restore; documented incident response plan.

6. Incident notification

Semester notifies Institution's security contact without undue delay and within [72] hours of confirming unauthorized access to Education Records, with the facts known, the data involved, steps taken and a contact. Semester cooperates with Institution's investigation and any required notices; Institution controls notices to students.

7. Student rights

Semester supports Institution in responding to requests to inspect, correct or delete records within [10] business days of Institution's request. Corrections to official records are made in the Official System by Institution.

10. Audits

On reasonable notice, once a year, Semester answers Institution's security questionnaire (HECVAT) and provides evidence summaries. On-site audits by agreement.

11. Children

Where any user is under 18, Semester follows Institution's instructions and applicable law; marketplace features stay off for users under 18.
