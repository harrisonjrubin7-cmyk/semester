1. Our commitment

Semester should work for every student and staff member, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control, magnification or captions. We design and test to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

3. How we test

  • Automated checks on every change before it ships.
  • Manual keyboard and screen-reader testing (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack) on pilot paths each release.
  • Zoom to 200%, reflow at 320 px, reduced motion, high contrast and forced colors.
  • Plain language, visible focus and target sizes of at least 44 px in the product.

5. Report a barrier

Email access@[domain] or use "Report a barrier" in Help. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology if you're comfortable sharing it. We reply within two business days with a fix or a workaround. Students can also contact their school's disability services office, which can work with us directly.
