Community & marketplace guidelines

Plain-language rules. The Terms of Service are the binding version.

Do

  • Help each other: answer questions, share study tips, show up for your groups.
  • Keep it on campus: meet at the suggested safe spots and pay in the app.
  • Report what's wrong. Reports are confidential.

Don't

  • Harass, threaten, demean or share someone's private information.
  • Sell or trade graded work, exams, answer keys, or offers to do someone's work.
  • List prohibited items: weapons, drugs, alcohol, counterfeits, anything illegal.
  • Ask for payment outside the app, or impersonate anyone, including school offices.

What happens

SeverityExampleTypical action
LowSpam, off-topicContent removed, reminder
MediumIntegrity violation, scam attemptRemoval, feature limit, school notified per policy
HighThreats, self-harm risk, illegal itemsImmediate removal; escalated to the school's designated contacts

You can appeal through Privacy & safety, and a different moderator will review it.
