1. Structure

This Agreement sets the terms for every Order Form signed under it. Each Order Form lists the services, term, fees and named measures. The Data Protection Addendum (DPA) governs student and personal data. If documents conflict, the order of precedence is: DPA, then Order Form, then this Agreement, then documentation.

2. Definitions

  • Services: the Semester platform and any implementation or support listed in an Order Form.
  • Institution Data: data Institution or its users provide or connect, including Education Records as defined by FERPA.
  • Authorized Users: students, staff and others Institution permits to use the Services.
  • Official System: Institution's SIS, LMS or other system of record.

3. Services

  • Semester provides the Services described in the Order Form and the documentation.
  • Official Systems remain authoritative. Semester reads from them as Institution approves and doesn't change official records unless an Order Form expressly allows a write integration, approved in writing.
  • Semester may update the Services if it doesn't materially reduce agreed functionality, security or accessibility.

4. Institution responsibilities

  • Name an executive sponsor, an operational champion and IT, registrar and security contacts.
  • Provide SSO configuration and the agreed data access in time for the implementation plan.
  • Make sure its use and its disclosures to Semester comply with FERPA and its own policies.

5. Data protection and security

  • Semester acts as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest under FERPA, under Institution's direct control for its use of Education Records, as set out in the DPA.
  • Semester keeps an information security program described in its security policies and HECVAT response, including tenant isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, access logging and incident response.
  • Semester doesn't send Education Records to AI model providers and doesn't use Institution Data to train AI models.
  • Semester doesn't sell Institution Data or use it for advertising.

6. Accessibility

Semester designs to WCAG 2.2 AA, provides an accessibility conformance report (ACR) on request, and fixes reported barriers on a timeline agreed in the Order Form.

7. Fees and payment

  • Fees are in the Order Form. Invoices are due [net 30] from receipt.
  • Fees exclude taxes; Institution's tax-exempt status is honored with a valid certificate.
  • Late amounts may be suspended after written notice and a [30]-day cure period. Suspension never deletes data.

8. Term and termination

  • This Agreement runs until all Order Forms end.
  • Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within [30] days of notice.
  • Institution may terminate a pilot Order Form at the end of the pilot term without cause.
  • On termination, Semester makes Institution Data available for export for [30] days, then deletes it as the DPA requires and certifies deletion on request.

9. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care and uses it only for this Agreement. Public-records laws that apply to Institution take precedence.

10. Intellectual property

  • Semester owns the Services and improvements to them. Institution owns Institution Data.
  • Institution grants Semester the right to process Institution Data only to provide the Services.
  • Feedback may be used without obligation, with no Institution Data attached.

11. Warranties

  • Semester warrants the Services will perform materially as documented and that it won't knowingly introduce malicious code.
  • Each party warrants it has authority to enter this Agreement.
  • [Other warranties disclaimed to the extent the law allows; counsel to draft.]

12. Indemnities

[Semester: third-party IP infringement claims against the Services. Institution: to the extent its governing law allows. Many public institutions can't indemnify; counsel to adjust.]

13. Limitation of liability

[Cap: fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. Carve-outs: data breach caused by Semester's breach of the DPA (super-cap [x] or insurance limits), confidentiality, indemnity. No indirect damages. Counsel to set.]

14. Insurance

Semester will keep cyber liability and technology errors & omissions insurance at the limits in the Order Form or Institution's requirements, and provide certificates on request. Status: not yet bound. This is a launch gate.

15. Publicity

Neither party uses the other's name or marks without written approval, except that Semester may list Institution as a customer once Institution approves the wording.

16. General

Governing law: [Institution's state where required, otherwise Semester's state]. Notices in writing to the contacts in the Order Form. No assignment without consent, except to a successor of Semester's business that assumes this Agreement and the DPA. Entire agreement; amendments in writing.
