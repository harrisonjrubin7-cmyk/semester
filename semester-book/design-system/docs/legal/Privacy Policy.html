How we use it

To provide the service, keep it secure, support you, and improve it using aggregate data. Personalisation uses only the signals you allow, and you can reset it.

Who we share it with

Your school; subprocessors that host and run the service (listed at [subprocessors URL]); payment processors for marketplace purchases; and authorities when the law requires it. Nothing else.

Your choices

See, correct or delete your data through Privacy & data in the app, or through your school. Control who sees your profile details, turn off device permissions, and reset personalisation.

Security and retention

Encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access and every access logged. Retention follows your school's agreement. By default, data is deleted within 30 days after it ends.

Children

Semester is for postsecondary students. Where a school enrols users under 18, the school's consent and the applicable laws apply, and some features stay off.
