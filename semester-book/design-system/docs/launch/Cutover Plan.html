Production cutover plan · Vanderbilt pilot

Owner: founder. Status: draft, October 2026. Companion to the launch runbook. Times are relative to launch day (L); clock times are US Central.

Scope: moving the pilot from staging to production — environments, identity, SIS data, configuration and DNS. The runbook’s go/no-go gates must all be green before step C1 starts. Every step names its check and its undo.

1. Environments

EnvironmentHostingDataWho has access
LocalSupabase CLI + Next.js devSynthetic seed onlyEngineers
StagingVercel preview + Supabase staging projectSynthetic, plus Vanderbilt test IdP accountsEngineers, Vanderbilt IT testers
ProductionVercel production + Supabase production project (separate keys)Real student records under the DPANamed operators only, MFA required, access logged

Production never receives a copy of staging data, and staging never receives production data. Secrets are set per environment and are not shared.

4. Point of no return

There isn’t one. Until C11, students see nothing. After C11, the cohort flag turns everything off in under a minute without deleting data. Data is deleted only on Vanderbilt’s written instruction under the DPA.

6. Sign-off

Cutover is complete when C1–C11 and every section 5 check are logged with evidence, and the sponsor confirms by email. The log is attached to the week-1 review.
