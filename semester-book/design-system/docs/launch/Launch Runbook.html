4. Rollback

Trigger any one of these: a suspected student-data exposure; SSO down for more than 30 minutes; SIS data shown wrongly to a student; error rate above 2% for 15 minutes.

Steps: 1) turn off the pilot cohort flag; 2) show the holding message; 3) tell the Vanderbilt contact within 1 hour; 4) if student data might be involved, follow the incident response plan and the DPA notice terms; 5) fix, retest in staging, and re-run the go/no-go before re-enabling.

5. Support in week 1

Staff questions go to the support desk (target first response under 1 hour during pilot hours). Student questions go through Vanderbilt's help channel first and are escalated to Semester only when needed. Every issue is logged with its cause and fix, and the log feeds the week-1 review.
