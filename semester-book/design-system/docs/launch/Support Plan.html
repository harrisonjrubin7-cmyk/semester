Day-1 and week-1 support plan · Vanderbilt pilot

Owner: founder. Status: draft, October 2026. Companion to the launch runbook and cutover plan.

Reality check: Semester is one founder. This plan is sized for that: narrow hours, a clear first line at Vanderbilt, and a short list of things that get an immediate response.

5. Ready-to-send replies

SituationReply outline
Can’t sign inUse your Vanderbilt login at the pilot link. If it fails, contact the campus help channel with the time and the error shown.
My information looks wrongSemester shows what the registrar holds. We’ve flagged it and the registrar will confirm within one business day. Nothing here changes your official record.
Is AI reading my records?No. The assistant never receives student records. It sees only what you type, and you can turn it off in settings.
I’m not in the pilotThe pilot is for a set group this term. Your school will share any wider rollout.

6. Logging

Every ticket records severity, role, area, cause, fix and time to resolve. No student record content goes in a ticket; reference records by internal ID only.

7. Week-1 review agenda

ItemOutput
Sign-in and adoption by roleNumbers from the ops console
Tickets by severity and causeTop 5 causes and their fixes
Data accuracyReconciliation and spot-check results
IncidentsNone, or the incident report
Changes for week 2Agreed list; anything that needs a go/no-go gate re-run

8. Exit to steady state

Week-1 cover ends when there are no open S1/S2 issues, two consecutive days pass without a new S2, reconciliation is clean, and the sponsor agrees. Support then moves to pilot hours (08:00–18:00 weekdays, S1 always on) and sponsor updates go weekly.
