Hiring plan and job descriptions

Owner: founder. Status: draft, October 2026. Semester is bootstrapped: every hire is triggered by revenue or by a contract obligation, never by a calendar date.

Rule: no role is opened until its trigger is true and its cost is covered by signed revenue. Anyone touching student data signs confidentiality terms, completes FERPA and security training, and gets least-privilege access, reviewed quarterly.

2. How we hire

  1. Write the job description from this document; post with a salary range.
  2. 30-minute screen: motivation and the role's must-haves.
  3. Paid work sample (2–4 hours, real but not production work, no student data).
  4. Two references, one a former manager.
  5. Offer with IP assignment, confidentiality and a background check where the institution contract requires it.

Job descriptions

Founding full-stack engineer

Contract-to-hire · remote (US) · reports to founder

Build Semester with the founder: the student app, staff workspaces and the data layer that keeps every institution's records isolated.

You'll own

  • Features end to end in React + TypeScript on Supabase (Postgres, RLS, Auth) and Vercel
  • Row-level security policies and the tests that prove them
  • SIS and LMS read integrations (SAML SSO, LTI 1.3, OneRoster)
  • CI, releases and on-call rotation with the founder

First 90 days

  • Ship the SAML SSO and SIS read sync for the pilot
  • Land RLS test coverage on every student table
  • Take one on-call week with the support plan

You have

  • 3+ years shipping production web apps in TypeScript
  • Postgres you can reason about: schemas, indexes, policies
  • Care for accessibility and plain interfaces
  • Comfort working directly with a founder and with customers

Good to have

  • Higher-ed systems (Banner, Workday Student, Canvas)
  • Supabase or row-level security in production
  • Security reviews (HECVAT, SOC 2)

This role is not a role where you only take tickets. You'll make product calls.

Implementation & customer success lead

Part-time to full-time · remote (US) with campus travel · reports to founder

Get each institution live and make the pilot succeed against the measures it agreed.

You'll own

  • Implementation plans from signature to launch
  • Weekly sponsor updates and the week-1 review
  • Staff training for advisors, registrar and student affairs
  • Results reports and the annual conversion conversation

First 90 days

  • Run setup for pilot #2 using the launch runbook
  • Own day-1 / week-1 support for one launch
  • Draft the case study from the Vanderbilt pilot

You have

  • Experience implementing software at colleges or universities
  • Clear writing to executives and to front-line staff
  • Comfort with data mapping and spreadsheets

Good to have

  • Advising, registrar or student success background
  • FERPA training

Security & compliance lead (fractional)

Fractional · remote · reports to founder

Turn Semester's security policies into evidence that institutions and auditors accept.

You'll own

  • HECVAT responses and the evidence register
  • SOC 2 readiness plan and vendor management
  • Access reviews, incident response exercises and the restore drill
  • Pen-test scoping and remediation tracking

First 90 days

  • Review the HECVAT Full answers against real controls
  • Run one tabletop incident exercise
  • Produce a SOC 2 gap list with costs

You have

  • Security program experience at a SaaS company
  • Familiarity with FERPA and higher-ed security reviews
  • Practical, evidence-first approach

Good to have

  • CISSP, CISM or similar

This role is not a full-time hire yet.

Account executive, higher education

Full-time · remote (US) · base + commission · reports to founder

Find and close the next universities, using the pilot motion that worked.

You'll own

  • Pipeline from target list to signed pilot
  • Discovery, demos and mutual action plans
  • Security and legal review coordination with the founder
  • Forecast in the weekly metrics review

First 90 days

  • Learn the GTM plan, price book and claim rules
  • Run 15 discovery calls
  • Bring two pilots to security / legal review

You have

  • Track record selling software to colleges or universities
  • Comfort with long, multi-stakeholder sales
  • Honest selling: status, not promises

Good to have

  • Student success or registrar buyers

Product designer

Full-time · remote (US) · reports to founder

Own the Semester design system and the product experience for students and staff.

You'll own

  • The design system: tokens, components, patterns, templates
  • Flows for new workflows from the catalog
  • Accessibility review on every release
  • Research sessions with students and staff

First 90 days

  • Audit the design system against the shipped app
  • Redesign one high-traffic flow with research
  • Set up the accessibility review in the release checklist

You have

  • Portfolio of shipped product work, including complex data or forms
  • Working knowledge of WCAG 2.2
  • Can prototype in code or work closely with engineers who do

Good to have

  • Higher-ed or civic product experience

Support specialist

Part-time · remote (US), pilot hours · reports to implementation lead

Answer staff and escalated student questions quickly and route the rest to the right place.

You'll own

  • The support queue during pilot hours
  • Known-issues notes and reply macros
  • Ticket tagging that feeds the product backlog

First 90 days

  • Learn the support plan and severities
  • Handle a launch week alongside the implementation lead

You have

  • Support experience, ideally software or a campus help desk
  • Calm, plain writing

This role is not access to raw student records: tickets reference internal IDs only.
