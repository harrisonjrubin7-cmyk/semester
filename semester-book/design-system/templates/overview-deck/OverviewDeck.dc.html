One place for a student's whole semester.

Product overview · October 2026

The problem

Students juggle a dozen systems. Staff see a different picture than the student does.

Deadlines, holds and requirements live in separate tools.

Students find out what went wrong too late to fix it.

Advisors and registrars piece the story together by hand.

What Semester is

A layer on top of the institution's own systems, not a replacement.

SIS · LMS · SSO→Semester→One plan per student · a console for staff

Every item shows its source: official, connected, personal or AI-assisted.

For students

Know what's next, and why.

  • Courses, deadlines, registration readiness
  • An adaptive study planner
  • Ask Semester, an AI study helper
  • Accessible by default

Groups, events, mentorship and a verified campus marketplace.
