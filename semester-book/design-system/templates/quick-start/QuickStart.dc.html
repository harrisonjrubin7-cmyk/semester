Get set up on Semester in five minutes

Semester puts your classes, deadlines and campus services in one place. You sign in with your VUnetID; nothing new to remember.

Three steps

  1. 1
    Sign in with Vanderbilt. Go to semester.app and choose Vanderbilt University. You’ll land on the usual Vanderbilt sign-in page with your VUnetID and two-step login.
  2. 2
    Connect Brightspace. Choose Connect, then approve read access. Semester copies your courses, assignments and due dates. It never submits work or changes grades for you.
  3. 3
    Open Today. Today shows what’s due, what’s next and anything that changed since you last looked. Check it once each morning.

Read the labels

Every date, grade and answer in Semester says where it came from.

LabelWhat it means
Institution verifiedStraight from Vanderbilt systems such as Brightspace or YES. This is the official record.
YoursSomething you added: a personal reminder, a note, a study card.
ExternalFrom outside Vanderbilt. Useful, but check it against your syllabus.
EstimatedWorked out by Semester, such as how long a reading may take. Never official.

Your privacy

  • AI features can’t read your student records unless Vanderbilt has switched that on, one kind of data at a time. Settings › Privacy shows what’s on.
  • Instructors see your approved accommodations, never the reason for them.
  • Other students can only find you if you turn on Findable in Connections.
  • Apps in the Marketplace never receive your student records.
  • Your data is stored in the United States only.
If you need help now

Open Safety from the menu for one-tap calls to VUPD and the University Counseling Center. Anonymous reports go straight to Vanderbilt; Semester doesn’t keep a record of them. In an emergency, call 911.

Something wrong?

If a date or grade looks off, trust Brightspace and tell us: Help › Report a problem. Sign-in trouble goes to the Vanderbilt IT help desk. Semester questions: support@semester.app.
