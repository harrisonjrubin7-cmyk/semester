## Purpose

Prepare accurate facts and decisions for a broker and counsel. This checklist does not recommend limits, establish coverage, or permit an insurance claim.

## Exposure packet

- [ ] Entity, locations, ownership, workforce/contractors, revenue/funding and jurisdiction facts confirmed.
- [ ] Product/services, user ages/geographies, customer types, data categories, AI/integration/payment scope and hosting/vendors described accurately.
- [ ] Security/privacy/accessibility controls and open findings disclosed without certification claims.
- [ ] Contract requirements, indemnity/liability positions, service commitments and customer certificates inventoried.
- [ ] Prior incidents, claims, complaints, outages, disputes and known circumstances reviewed with counsel/broker.
- [ ] Business continuity, backups/recovery, vendor concentration and key-person dependencies documented.

## Coverage review with broker/counsel

Assess applicability and exclusions for general liability, cyber/privacy/network security, technology E&O/professional liability, D&O, workers' compensation/employers liability, crime/social engineering, employment practices, media/IP, property/equipment, business interruption, and other jurisdiction/customer-specific coverage.

## Policy controls

- [ ] Named insureds, covered services/territories, retroactive dates, limits/retentions, sublimits, exclusions and endorsements reviewed.
- [ ] Vendor/AI/payment/privacy/accessibility/student-data activities fit the application and policy wording.
- [ ] Incident/claim notice contacts, deadlines, consent-to-incur-cost and panel-provider rules recorded in restricted operations materials.
- [ ] Certificates and contractual statements match bound coverage exactly.
- [ ] Premium/payment, renewal/non-renewal, change-notice and record owners assigned.
- [ ] No website, proposal, questionnaire or contract states coverage until evidence is current.

## Decision record

Record broker/counsel, quotes/options, chosen coverage or reason declined, residual risks, contract impact, approver, bind/effective/expiry dates, evidence location, and renewal lead time.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Exposure facts, applications, quotes, exclusions, limits, premiums, coverage, claims, insurer/broker advice, and binding require founder records and licensed professional confirmation.
