## Purpose and scope

Control policies, procedures, standards, runbooks, templates, external claims and evidence so people use an approved current version and prior decisions remain auditable. Source-controlled product documentation is included; privileged, customer-confidential, personnel and sensitive security records stay in approved restricted systems.

## Required metadata

Every controlled document identifies title, unique ID or path, owner, approver, version, status, effective date, review/expiry, classification, superseded document, dependencies and record location. Drafts state their review boundary prominently.

## Lifecycle

1. **Draft:** author identifies authority, sources, affected systems/claims and reviewers.
2. **Review:** domain owners verify implementation, legal/contractual and operational accuracy.
3. **Approve:** authorized person records decision, date, scope and conditions.
4. **Publish:** approved audience receives the immutable/current version; restricted evidence remains restricted.
5. **Operate:** owner trains affected people and collects evidence.
6. **Review/change:** material changes repeat review; emergency changes receive retrospective review.
7. **Supersede/archive:** current copy links to replacement; prior approved copy is retained under schedule/hold.

## Change classes

Editorial changes cannot alter meaning. Material changes include responsibilities, controls, commitments, data use, security/privacy/accessibility posture, customer rights, pricing/service terms or public claims and require reapproval. Never edit an executed agreement or prior approval record in place.

## Repository rules

- Link to canonical sources; do not maintain contradictory duplicate policy text.
- Repository implementation evidence does not prove real-world operation.
- No credentials, privileged advice, restricted reports, personal data or customer-confidential evidence.
- Pull-request approval is technical review unless the named business/professional authority explicitly accepts it.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

Use the [policy exception process](POLICY-EXCEPTION-PROCESS.md). Missing, expired, conflicting or unauthorized documents are treated as nonconforming and may block release, publication, sale or activation.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Named owners/approvers, classification system, restricted repository, retention periods, training, legal holds and operating evidence require company and professional decisions.
