## Purpose

Create a concise, auditable record of material company decisions, the evidence considered, the authority used, conflicts, limits, and follow-up. This log is not a substitute for formal board/member/manager consents or legal records when those are required.

## Decisions that require this log

Company formation/authority; equity/debt/fundraising; hiring/termination or major vendor commitments; pricing/payment/refunds; insurance; material contracts/deviations; new data/AI/integration use; launch/rollback; risk acceptance; security/privacy/accessibility exceptions; incident disclosures; customer activation; public compliance/outcome claims; and material roadmap/scope changes.

## Cannot be completed from source code

The founder must supply the actual decision, authority, conflicts, financial/customer/professional evidence, approvals, and controlled record locations.
