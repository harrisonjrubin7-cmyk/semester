## Purpose and objective

Maintain safe minimum operations, protect people and data, communicate accurately, and recover or wind down responsibly during staff loss, cyber/privacy incident, vendor failure, regional disruption, financial distress, facility/device loss or other material interruption.

## Activation

The continuity owner or incident commander may activate when a critical owner, service, vendor, access path, funding source or operating condition is unavailable beyond the approved tolerance, or when normal operation would increase harm. Record time, authority, scope and review cadence.

## Minimum viable operations

Priority order:

1. safety, incident containment and lawful/customer notifications;
2. identity, access, evidence preservation and secure communications;
3. support, data rights, customer contact and offboarding;
4. product read-only/degraded operation or safe shutdown;
5. financial, payroll, vendor and legal deadlines;
6. restoration of normal delivery only after verification.

No growth, release or customer commitment outranks safety and recoverability.

## Scenario playbooks

| Scenario | Immediate action | Continuity path | Evidence before recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| key person unavailable | activate named backup; restrict sole-owner changes | delegated runbook and access review | backup acceptance and access audit |
| cloud/vendor outage | fail closed/degraded mode; contact vendor | offline/read-only/manual path or approved replacement | integrity and reconciliation checks |
| cyber/privacy incident | invoke incident response; preserve evidence | isolate affected scope and approved communications | containment, access and recovery validation |
| regional/device loss | account/device revocation and alternate location | controlled replacement devices/services | secure configuration verification |
| severe cash constraint | invoke approved runway trigger | freeze nonessential spend and narrow scope | authorized forecast and obligations review |
| company wind-down | counsel-directed preservation and notices | export, deletion, contract/vendor/account closure | stakeholder-approved closure register |

## Contact and access package

Maintain in restricted storage: primary/backups, emergency contacts, bank/provider/customer/professional contacts, break-glass instructions, critical contracts, insurance notice requirements, system inventory and recovery credentials. The repository contains no secrets.

## Exercises

Exercise key-person loss, critical-vendor outage, incident communications, restore/rollback, support continuity, data export/deletion and secure shutdown. Record scenario, participants, environment, results, timing, gaps, owners and retest. A tabletop does not prove technical recovery.

## Recovery and stand-down

Restore in dependency order, validate security/data integrity/accessibility, reconcile queued/manual work, communicate status, monitor recurrence, capture evidence and obtain authorized stand-down. Review claims, contracts, insurance and customer obligations after the event.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Named responders/backups, tolerances, contacts, funding, contracts, insurance, provider capability, exercises, customer communications and observed recovery require controlled operational evidence.
