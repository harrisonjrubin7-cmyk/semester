## Purpose

Provide a completion and evidence checklist for company formation, authority, ownership, records, conflicts, workforce, IP, finance, tax, insurance, vendors, and continuity. Checking a box requires a dated record; repository text alone is not evidence.

## Formation and authority

- [ ] Legal entity name, type, jurisdiction, formation date, and good-standing status confirmed.
- [ ] Registered agent, registered address, principal business address, and required registrations confirmed.
- [ ] Governing documents, amendments, organizer actions, and initial consents collected.
- [ ] Directors/managers/officers and their appointment/authority documented.
- [ ] Contract, bank, payment, hiring, spending, debt, equity, and data-risk approval thresholds adopted.
- [ ] Beneficial-ownership and other required filings reviewed with counsel.

## Ownership, financing, and records

- [ ] Capitalization/ownership ledger reconciled to executed grants, issuances, transfers, and vesting terms.
- [ ] Securities-law and tax treatment of issuances reviewed by qualified advisers.
- [ ] Founder contributions, loans, reimbursements, and related-party transactions documented.
- [ ] Fundraising claims, investor materials, and data-room access governed and substantiated.
- [ ] Minutes/consents and a decision log cover material company actions.
- [ ] Record locations, access controls, retention, legal holds, backup, and destruction owners adopted.

## Intellectual property and brand

- [ ] Founder, employee, and contractor invention/IP assignments reviewed and executed where required.
- [ ] Third-party content, datasets, fonts, media, code, model/provider terms, and open-source obligations inventoried.
- [ ] Trademark/name clearance and filing strategy reviewed; no registration claim made without evidence.
- [ ] Domains, repositories, cloud accounts, social accounts, certificates, and recovery methods registered to controlled company accounts where appropriate.
- [ ] Departing-person access and asset transfer process tested.

## Workforce and advisers

- [ ] Employee/contractor classification reviewed for each location and relationship.
- [ ] Confidentiality, invention assignment, acceptable use, security/privacy, and code-of-conduct terms approved and executed as applicable.
- [ ] Payroll, benefits, workers' compensation, leave, workplace, immigration, and local obligations reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] Background/access decisions are role-appropriate, lawful, documented, and non-discriminatory.
- [ ] Advisor roles, compensation/equity, conflicts, confidentiality, and authority limits documented.

## Finance, tax, and insurance

- [ ] Bank/payment accounts, signers, segregation of duties, reconciliations, expense controls, and records approved.
- [ ] Federal/state/local registrations, income/franchise/sales/payroll tax, nexus, and information-return obligations reviewed.
- [ ] Accounting method, chart of accounts, subscription/revenue recognition, deferred revenue, refunds, chargebacks, and closing cadence approved.
- [ ] General liability, cyber, technology E&O/professional liability, D&O, workers' compensation, and other applicable coverage assessed.
- [ ] Contractual insurance statements match bound coverage; certificates/renewals are controlled.

## Vendors and continuity

- [ ] Vendor inventory identifies owner, purpose, data/access, contract, renewal, assurance, alternatives, and offboarding.
- [ ] Security/privacy/legal/financial review thresholds and exception authority adopted.
- [ ] Critical account ownership, MFA, break-glass recovery, backups, and succession documented.
- [ ] Business continuity, crisis communications, key-person risk, and financial runway triggers approved and exercised.

## Evidence register

For each completed item record: evidence title/location, responsible owner, reviewer/approver, date, version, confidentiality, next review/renewal, related risks, and any limitations. Never store credentials, sensitive personal records, privileged advice, or restricted customer documents in a public repository.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Every checklist item above ultimately requires company records, professional review, account statements, executed agreements, filings, insurance records, or named-person acceptance. Current status is **UNVERIFIED** until those records are supplied and reviewed.
