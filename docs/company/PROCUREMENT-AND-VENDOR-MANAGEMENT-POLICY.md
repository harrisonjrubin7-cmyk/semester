## Purpose

Ensure third parties are selected, approved, monitored, renewed and offboarded according to verified business need, authority, cost, data/access risk, security, privacy, accessibility, continuity and exit feasibility.

## Scope

Applies before acquiring, renewing, materially changing or terminating software, cloud, AI/model, professional, data, payment, marketing, implementation or other third-party services. Free trials and employee-created accounts are included when company, student or customer data/access is involved.

## Intake

Record business purpose, owner, alternatives, total committed cost, term/renewal, data and system access, users, hosting/region, AI/model use, subcontractors, accessibility impact, customer obligations, continuity need and exit plan.

## Risk tiers

| Tier | Example criteria | Minimum review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| critical/high | student/customer content, privileged production access, payment, identity, sole critical service | security, privacy, legal, finance, accessibility where user-facing, continuity and executive approval |
| medium | metadata, internal confidential data, operational dependency, institution-directed integration | proportionate security/privacy/legal/finance review |
| low | no sensitive data/access and readily replaceable | owner and finance review; confirm classification |
| student-directed | user's own account and decision, not Semester's contracted processor | truthful notice and product privacy review; do not imply vendor approval |

Classification follows actual data/access and contract, not vendor reputation.

## Due diligence

For applicable tiers obtain and review current assurance, DPA/privacy terms, security controls, incident notice, retention/deletion, subprocessors, residency/transfers, availability/recovery, accessibility, AI training/use, intellectual property, insurance, financial viability, pricing/renewal, support and exit/export. Public marketing claims are not evidence of acceptance.

## Contract and approval

- Contracting entity and signer must have authority.
- Required customer obligations must flow down to the vendor.
- Non-standard risk, auto-renewal, unilateral change, liability, data use, publicity, audit, assignment and termination clauses are escalated.
- No vendor receives production credentials or data before approvals and least-privilege configuration.
- Every approved processor appears in the applicable vendor/subprocessor and data-flow records.

## Ongoing monitoring

Review critical/high vendors at least annually and on breach, ownership, terms, subprocessor, region, product, data-flow or assurance change. Track owner, contract/renewal, cost/use, findings, exceptions, evidence expiry, incidents and exit readiness.

## Offboarding

Revoke users, tokens, keys and integrations; export required records; verify deletion under the approved retention/legal-hold plan; recover assets; stop billing/renewal; update inventories, data flows, CSP/allowlists and customer disclosures; test replacement or continuity where critical.

## Emergency procurement

Emergency use requires documented incident need, minimum scope/access, available authorized approval and a time-bounded exception. Complete full review or remove the vendor within `[TBC]` days.

Thresholds, authorized buyers/signers, actual contracts, assurance reports, DPAs, regions, insurance, vendor financial condition, customer flow-downs, exceptions and operating reviews require controlled external records and named-person approval.
