## Purpose

Define how Semester makes decisions, operates the product, protects student trust, serves individual users, evaluates institutional pilots, and refuses commitments that exceed current evidence or capacity.

## Operating principles

1. **Evidence before claim.** Repository implementation, target-environment operation, independent review, legal approval, and customer acceptance are separate evidence layers.
2. **One accountable owner.** Every material outcome has one accountable seat, a named primary, a backup, a decision deadline, and an escalation path.
3. **Narrow before broad.** Operate a bounded, reversible, measurable pilot before expanding integrations, cohorts, service commitments, or geographies.
4. **Student agency.** Use clear consent, minimal data, understandable controls, export/deletion paths, human oversight, and no hidden profiling or high-impact autonomous decisions.
5. **Fail closed.** An unapproved tenant, capability, provider, claim, contract deviation, or high-risk feature remains unavailable.
6. **No paper compliance.** A policy counts only when implementation, ownership, training, evidence, exceptions, and review operate.

## Decision classes

- **Founder/CEO:** strategy, financing, hiring, pricing authority, contract authority, risk appetite within lawful bounds, and final company resource allocation.
- **Product:** scope, quality bar, first win/proof, research, prioritization, and user-safety acceptance.
- **Engineering/operations:** architecture, release implementation, reliability, monitoring, recovery, and technical capacity.
- **Security/privacy/accessibility:** independent stop authority for unresolved P0/P1 risks in their domains.
- **Revenue/success/support:** qualification, truthful proposals, implementation capacity, adoption, service, renewal, and offboarding.
- **Customer authority:** customer data, users, systems, configuration, institutional risk acceptance, and customer launch approval.

No single person may approve both a launch-critical control and the evidence that the control operated when a second qualified reviewer is reasonably available.

## Individual motion

The initial individual motion is invitation-based validation of manual/local-first value. Broad acquisition or payment requires approved consumer terms/privacy, age posture, support, monitoring, billing/refund/cancellation, data rights, accessibility, and security evidence. Growth metrics never override safety or consent.

## Institutional motion

The initial institutional motion is discovery and preparation for one bounded cohort/use case. Activation requires executed scope, named customer authority, data/integration boundaries, target-environment acceptance, trained operators, support/recovery/offboarding evidence, and a joint GO decision. Semester does not replace the SIS/LMS/registrar or claim official-record authority without explicit implemented and accepted scope.

## Escalation and stop-work triggers

Immediately escalate and withhold the affected motion for suspected secret exposure, unauthorized/cross-tenant access, material data loss, unsupported legal/compliance claim, uncertain customer/data authority, critical accessibility barrier without an equivalent path, unavailable monitoring/support, failed recovery/offboarding, or an unapproved high-impact AI use.

## Records and evidence

Material decisions use the founder decision log. Releases, incidents, access reviews, vendor reviews, legal approvals, drills, customer approvals, and exceptions are versioned, dated, owned, retained under an approved schedule, and linked to affected claims. Sensitive evidence stays in an access-controlled system; the repository holds only safe summaries where appropriate.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Entity facts, ownership/IP chain, signing authority, workforce status, tax/accounting posture, bank/payment authority, insurance, budget/runway, named personnel and backups, provider contracts, counsel approvals, customer approvals, and observed production performance require founder or qualified external confirmation.
