## Purpose

Organize facts, records, controls, and professional decisions needed for reliable accounting and timely tax compliance. No tax, nexus, filing, or revenue-recognition conclusion is made here.

## Company and account setup

- [ ] Entity type/jurisdiction, tax IDs, elections, ownership/capital, fiscal year and accounting basis confirmed.
- [ ] Bank/payment/credit accounts and signers reconciled; personal/company activity separated.
- [ ] Chart of accounts, bookkeeping system, document retention, close calendar and preparer/reviewer assigned.
- [ ] Founder contributions/loans, equity, reimbursements and related-party transactions documented.

## Transactions and revenue

- [ ] Product/pricing, contracts, invoices, processor payouts, fees, refunds, chargebacks, credits and taxes reconcile.
- [ ] Subscription/pilot/professional-services revenue, deferred revenue, customer deposits and performance obligations reviewed by a qualified accountant.
- [ ] Receivables, payables, accruals, prepaid/deferred costs, fixed assets and vendor commitments tracked.
- [ ] Foreign currency, grants, fundraising, debt, equity or digital assets addressed if applicable.

## Tax and workforce review

- [ ] Federal/state/local income/franchise/gross-receipts/sales-use tax and registration/nexus reviewed for actual activities and customers.
- [ ] Payroll, worker classification, withholding, unemployment, workers' compensation and information returns reviewed by location.
- [ ] Contractor/vendor tax forms and cross-border/payment-reporting obligations collected as applicable.
- [ ] Estimated payments, filings, extensions, notices, audits and correspondence calendar controlled.

## Monthly/annual evidence

Bank/processor reconciliations; general ledger/trial balance; close checklist; financial statements; budget variance/runway; payroll/vendor reports; tax returns/payments; equity/debt schedules; fixed-asset/intangible records; management review; corrections and control exceptions.

## Professional decisions to document

Entity/elections, accounting method, fiscal year, capitalization/expense treatment, software development costs, revenue recognition, sales-tax nexus/taxability, worker classification, equity compensation, fundraising/debt, cross-border activity, R&D/credits if claimed, and record-retention requirements.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Entity/tax facts, accounts, transactions, revenue, expenses, payroll, nexus, filings, elections, financial statements, and professional conclusions require controlled records and qualified advisers.
