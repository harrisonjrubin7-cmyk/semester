## Purpose

Provide an approved, evidence-based view of budget, cash, burn, runway, commitments and decision triggers without converting pipeline or repository activity into financial facts.

## Rules

- Use executed agreements and approved records, not pipeline hopes, for committed receipts.
- Separate actual, committed, forecast and scenario values.
- State timing, currency, tax and collection assumptions.
- Never infer company cash or runway from repository activity.
- Keep bank, payroll, tax and personal data in a controlled financial system.

## Runway calculation

Document the approved method. At minimum show:

- trailing average net burn and normalized forward net burn;
- one-time costs and committed obligations;
- base, downside and no-new-revenue scenarios;
- minimum cash threshold and action lead time;
- sensitivity to collection delay, vendor/AI cost and support load.

`Runway months = usable cash / approved normalized monthly net burn` is only a rough indicator. If burn is zero, volatile, seasonal, or changing materially, use the detailed cash forecast instead.
