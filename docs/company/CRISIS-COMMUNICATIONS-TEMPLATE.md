| --- | --- |
| Version | 0.1 |

| Owner | Incident communications lead |

## Purpose

Provide an approval-controlled structure for accurate, timely, audience-specific crisis communications without speculation, unsupported legal conclusions, false recovery promises, or disclosure beyond verified authority.

## Holding statement

> We are investigating `[VERIFIED DESCRIPTION]` identified at `[TIME/ZONE]`. The confirmed effect is `[KNOWN IMPACT]`. We have taken `[VERIFIED ACTIONS]`. `[WHAT USERS/CUSTOMERS SHOULD DO, OR “NO ACTION REQUESTED NOW”]`. We will provide the next update by `[TIME/CONDITION]` through `[CHANNEL]`.

State unknowns plainly. Do not speculate about cause, scope, attribution, legal status, recovery time or data impact.

## Update structure

1. What changed since the prior update.
2. Confirmed scope and affected service/data/audience.
3. Actions completed and in progress.
4. User/customer action, if any.
5. Service availability or safe workaround.
6. Support/contact route.
7. Next update time.

## Audience checklist

- [ ] Employees/operators and backups.
- [ ] Affected customer authority and contractual contacts.
- [ ] Affected users using accessible/plain language.
- [ ] Vendors/providers needed for response.
- [ ] Counsel, insurer/broker and qualified incident professionals.
- [ ] Regulators/law enforcement only through authorized legal process.
- [ ] Public/media only when approved and necessary.

## Approval guardrails

Do not use “secure,” “contained,” “no data affected,” “fully restored,” root-cause statements, compliance conclusions or deadlines without supporting evidence and approval. Preserve drafts, approvals, recipients, delivery evidence and corrections under the approved schedule/legal hold.

## Closure notice

Summarize verified impact, resolution, remaining limitations, user/customer action, support/data-rights contact and follow-up. Post-incident learning is separate from fault or legal conclusion.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Incident facts, affected parties, notification duties, insurer/customer contacts, timing, approvals and publication require the live incident record and qualified review.
