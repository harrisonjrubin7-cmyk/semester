## Purpose and method

Rate likelihood and impact from 1–5 using approved definitions. Inherent score precedes controls; residual score reflects evidenced controls only. Label evidence **DESIGNED**, **IMPLEMENTED**, **OPERATED**, **INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED**, or **ACCEPTED**. Missing evidence cannot reduce residual risk.

## Treatment rules

- **Avoid:** do not perform/activate the activity.
- **Mitigate:** named actions reduce likelihood/impact and produce evidence.
- **Transfer:** contract/insurance allocation is confirmed, not assumed.
- **Accept:** authorized, time-bounded decision records rationale, residual exposure, monitoring and expiry.

Critical/P0/P1, unlawful or unauthorized risks cannot be silently accepted. Customer-owned risk requires customer authority; founder approval does not substitute.

## Escalation

Escalate new critical/high risks, overdue treatments, expired evidence, failed controls, incidents, changed assumptions and exceptions immediately. Link every closure to dated evidence and approver.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Risk appetite, ratings, financial/customer exposure, named owners, insurance/contracts, treatment acceptance and observed control operation require company and external evidence.
