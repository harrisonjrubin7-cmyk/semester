## Purpose

Authorize, document and review necessary company expenses while preventing self-approval, undisclosed conflicts, threshold avoidance, unsupported reimbursement and inaccurate tax treatment.

## Principles

Expenses must serve a documented business purpose, fit an approved budget or exception, use reasonable cost, avoid personal benefit/conflict, and have sufficient evidence for accounting and tax review. No one approves their own reimbursement.

## Required record

- date, vendor, amount, currency and payment method;
- business purpose, project/customer and budget category;
- itemized receipt or documented exception;
- requester, approver and approval date;
- attendees/business relationship for meals or events where required;
- contract, security/privacy review and asset owner where applicable;
- tax treatment determined by the qualified reviewer, not the requester.

## Prohibited or restricted expenses

Personal, unlawful, deceptive, discriminatory, bribery/kickback, unsupported cash-equivalent, unapproved political, unsafe, sanction-restricted, or conflict-hidden spending is prohibited. Alcohol, gifts, entertainment, travel upgrades, home-office equipment, subscriptions and charitable/community spending require the approved category-specific rule or written exception.

## Reimbursement and corporate cards

Submit within `[TBC]` days. Finance reviews completeness and duplicate risk before payment. Card access is least privilege, revocable, reconciled monthly and never shared. Lost cards or suspected misuse are reported immediately.

## Exceptions

Document reason, amount, approver, risk, duration and corrective action. An exception cannot override law, contract, sanctions, anti-bribery duties, tax requirements or a required independent review.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Thresholds, tax treatment, eligible categories, payment timing, card program, approvers and record system require founder, accountant and counsel confirmation.
