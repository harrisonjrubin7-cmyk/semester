> **PROPOSED — NOT ADOPTED.** Builds on [`ANNUAL-OPERATING-PLAN.md`](../ANNUAL-OPERATING-PLAN.md), [`QUARTERLY-OPERATING-REVIEW.md`](../QUARTERLY-OPERATING-REVIEW.md) and [`COMPANY-FIRST-YEAR-MEASURES.md`](../../COMPANY-FIRST-YEAR-MEASURES.md). **No target is set here.** Targets are the founder's decision (F6), recorded as a decision, and the first-year-measures test refuses a target with no decision behind it.

## OKR system

### Shape

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Levels** | Company and function only. No individual OKRs below about 15 people |
| **Count** | At most 3 objectives per quarter; 2–4 key results each |
| **Objective** | A qualitative outcome a student, institution or the company can feel. Not a project, not "launch X" |
| **Key result** | A measured change with a baseline, a source, an owner and a date |
| **Commit vs stretch** | Mark each key result **commit** (expected 1.0, a miss is a finding) or **stretch** (0.7 is a good result). Most should be commit while the company is small |
| **Scoring** | 0–1 at quarter end, plus a one-line "what we learned". Confidence (red/amber/green) updated weekly in 5 minutes |
| **Guardrail** | Every growth or speed key result is paired with a trust guardrail that must not move the wrong way: P0/P1 incidents, accessibility task success, support contact rate, data-rights clock breaches |
| **Owner** | One accountable person per key result |

### Key-result eligibility

A key result is accepted only if it passes all of these. This keeps OKRs from turning into a to-do list.

1. **Eligible metric.** It uses a first-year measure in state *measured* or *instrumented*. A *defined*-only measure may be a key result only if the key result is itself "instrument it", with the date the first reading is due. (States are defined in [`COMPANY-FIRST-YEAR-MEASURES.md`](../../COMPANY-FIRST-YEAR-MEASURES.md#where-it-stands).) Reading counts toward analytics only through the existing rule that a new analytics mark lands with its question and its migration.
2. **Outcome, not activity.** Publishing a document is not a result, with one exception: **evidence key results**, which target a filed artifact (for example, a proof from the [proof calendar](../../PROOF-CALENDAR.md)) and are labelled as evidence, not outcome.
3. **Falsifiable.** Someone other than the owner could read the number and agree it was hit.
4. **Not an aspiration for others.** A key result cannot depend on a customer or counsel acting, unless the key result is the request and its response time.
5. **Not tradable for trust.** No key result may be reached by weakening a gate, a claim rule or a data protection.

### Cadence

| When | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Confidence update on each key result, inside the operating review | 5 minutes |
| Monthly | Scorecard review; key results at risk get an owner action | Inside the monthly review |
| Quarterly | Score, learn, reset in the [QBR](#quarterly-business-review) | Part of the 90–180 minutes |
| Mid-quarter | A key result may be changed once, with a decision record. Otherwise it is scored as written | — |

## Annual planning process

One plan year, built in twelve weeks, reforecast quarterly rather than rewritten. The structure is [`ANNUAL-OPERATING-PLAN.md`](../ANNUAL-OPERATING-PLAN.md); this adds the process and the discipline.

| Weeks before lock | Step | Output | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T−12 | **Retrospective on evidence.** What each objective actually delivered, what each decision log entry achieved, what we stopped | One page: keep / change / stop | Founder, with an advisor challenge |
| T−10 | **Strategy memo** | ≤ 6 pages: thesis, segments and motions, the 3 bets, what we will not do, risks | Founder |
| T−8 | **Capacity and constraints.** Founder hours by month with academic and life blackouts; hiring and contractor capacity; independent-evidence lead times (assessors, counsel) | Capacity table | Founder, ops |
| T−6 | **Budget and hiring plan** within runway and the hiring rule (F12); reconcile to [`BUDGET-AND-CASH-RUNWAY-TEMPLATE.md`](../BUDGET-AND-CASH-RUNWAY-TEMPLATE.md) | Budget; hiring plan with triggers ([04](04-organization-and-hiring.md)) | Founder, CPA |
| T−4 | **Draft plan and company OKRs** for the first quarter | Plan draft | Founder |
| T−3 | **Advisor / board review** with a written pre-read | Written questions and answers | Advisors |
| T−2 | **Pre-mortem** (see [culture](05-culture.md#healthy-disagreement)) | Top ten ways this plan fails and a tripwire for each | Founder + one outside voice |
| T−1 | **Revise** | Final plan | Founder |
| T−0 | **Lock.** Decision record; plan, budget, OKRs and hiring triggers published to the team | Decision record (Class A) | Founder; board approves from Seed |

**Discipline:**

- **Three bets.** A plan year has at most three company-level bets. A fourth displaces one.
- **Plan to a stop list.** The plan names at least three things Semester will *not* do this year. The audit's breadth is preserved as a requirement; sequencing is the plan.
- **Reforecast, don't rewrite.** Quarterly the plan is reforecast within its bets. Changing a bet is a Class A decision.
- **Dates are conditions.** Where a plan item depends on evidence expiry, customer authority or external review lead time, it is dated as a condition, not a promise ([`ANNUAL-OPERATING-PLAN.md`](../ANNUAL-OPERATING-PLAN.md#quarterly-sequencing)).

## Quarterly business review

The QBR is a decision meeting. The pre-read does the explaining.

| | Pre-seed | Seed and later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Length** | 90 minutes | 3 hours |
| **Attendees** | Founder; operator advisor; others by invitation | Founder, function leads, advisors/board members as set in [01](01-board.md) |
| **Pre-read due** | T−3 business days | T−3 business days |

**Pre-read** (each item links, none is pasted): scorecard, OKR scoring with "what we learned", cash and 13-week view, pipeline source, product quality and incident summary, risk and evidence-expiry registers, trust ledger, claims expiring, decisions last quarter and their outcomes, capacity and key-person view.

**Agenda:**

| Min (3 h) | Item | Output |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 15 | Close last quarter's decisions and overdue actions | Done / late / dropped |
| 30 | Score OKRs; the one-line learning for each | Scores |
| 20 | Scorecard exceptions only: red, unknown more than one quarter, or moved more than expected | Owners for each |
| 25 | Each market motion separately: **GO / GO WITH CONDITIONS / PAUSE / NO-GO**, with scope, conditions, expiry, owner, reversal trigger ([`QUARTERLY-OPERATING-REVIEW.md`](../QUARTERLY-OPERATING-REVIEW.md#required-decisions)) | Motion decisions |
| 20 | Trust ledger and risk: acceptances expiring, exceptions, incidents' structural fixes | Renew, close or escalate |
| 15 | People, capacity, key-person ([08](08-continuity.md)) | Capacity decisions; hiring changes |
| 25 | Next quarter: OKRs, resources, the **stop list** | Locked OKRs |
| 10 | Decision records written now, not later | Records |
| 5 | What did this meeting change? If nothing, shorten it next time | — |

**Rules:**

- **Every QBR stops something.** At least one initiative, report, meeting or register is deleted or paused, with the reason. A review that only adds is not a review.
- **Unknown is red after a quarter.** A scorecard cell that stays `UNKNOWN` for more than one quarter is scored red, because not measuring is a decision.
- **No waiver by review.** A QBR cannot waive a customer, counsel, security, privacy, accessibility, insurance, accounting or provider authority ([`QUARTERLY-OPERATING-REVIEW.md`](../QUARTERLY-OPERATING-REVIEW.md#required-decisions)).
- **Outputs are records:** the signed motion decisions, the revised budget and forecast with assumptions, risk acceptances with expiry, the evidence and legal queues with owners and dates, claim changes, items withheld from sale or activation.

## Operating metrics

### Status rules

| Status | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **GREEN** | Meets its target **and** an artifact or query result is attached |
| **YELLOW** | Within a stated tolerance of target, or at risk with an owner action |
| **RED** | Misses target, or `UNKNOWN` for more than one quarter |
| **UNKNOWN** | No reading exists. Legitimate, visible, and never rendered green |

Every number states: source, definition, cohort, period, owner, and whether it is **observed, estimated, proposed or unavailable**.

### Leading indicators of governance health

These tell the founder whether the system itself is working. Reviewed monthly.

| Indicator | Healthy | Warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decisions inside their clock | Most Class A/B closed on time | Several aging past the clock |
| Seats without a backup | Falling quarter over quarter | Flat for two quarters |
| `UNKNOWN` cells in the scorecard | Falling | Same cell unknown two quarters |
| Evidence artifacts filed vs scheduled | On or ahead | Behind by more than a month |
| Governance hours | Within budget | Over budget for a month |
| Dissent recorded in the decision log | Occasional, with outcomes reviewed | Never (disagreement is hidden) or constant (decisions are not being made) |
| Stop list items actually stopped | Every QBR | A QBR that stopped nothing |

## What to delete

If a scorecard row has no owner action attached in two consecutive months, remove it from the scorecard and keep it in its source. If an OKR is scored the same way for three quarters running, it is a routine, not an objective.
