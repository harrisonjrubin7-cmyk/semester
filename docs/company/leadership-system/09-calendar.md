# 10 · Twelve-month executive operating calendar

> **PROPOSED — NOT ADOPTED.** Months are relative (**M1–M12**) because adoption is the founder's decision. If adopted now, **M1 is October 2026** and M12 is September 2027. The [proof calendar](../../PROOF-CALENDAR.md) likewise says "the clock starts with day 1 of the 90-day program; no date is invented", and this page follows that. **Academic-calendar anchors are generic placeholders; confirm every one against the registrar's published calendar before dating anything.** Nothing here is a target or a promise; items marked *condition* depend on evidence, counsel, or a customer.

## How to read it

- **Q0** is the foundation quarter (M1–M3). **Q1–Q3** are M4–M12. The first annual plan locks at the end of Q0 and covers M4–M15; the next planning cycle begins in M12.
- Every row names an owner seat and the artifact it produces. A row with no artifact is deleted.
- **Blackouts** are capacity dips (exams, breaks, travel). In a blackout: no launches, no new commitments, no contract signatures, weekly meetings move to writing, the backup or interim operator holds on-call ([08](08-continuity.md#capacity-planning-for-a-student-founder)).
- The proof artifacts come from [`PROOF-CALENDAR.md`](../../PROOF-CALENDAR.md) (months 1–3, then quarterly). This page does not redefine them; it places them next to the governance work so the founder sees one calendar.

## Standing rhythm (every month)

| When | Event | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Weekly, Monday** | Weekly operating review (45–60 min); Trust Review (30 min) | Top three issues; trust-ledger lines |
| **Weekly, Friday** | Written team update | One page |
| **Business day 5** | Monthly advisor note | 1–2 pages |
| **Mid-month** | Monthly company review; scorecard; 13-week cash view | Decisions; the company note |
| **Monthly** | Key-person register and seat-without-backup count | Number moves down |
| **Quarterly, final two weeks** | QBR; board or advisor quarterly meeting (packet sent 5 business days before); trust and access review; decision review | Locked next-quarter OKRs and stop list |
| **Semiannual** | Unplug drill; promotion and compensation cycle (once there are people) | Filed drill report |
| **Annual** | Governance review (what to delete); advisor renewal; policy and insurance renewal; independent accessibility and security review | Deletions; renewed or replaced advisors |

## Reconciling with the 90-day program

The [90-day launch program](../../90-DAY-LAUNCH-PROGRAM.md) and the proof calendar are *product and evidence* schedules. This calendar adds *governance, people and continuity*. When they disagree on timing, the evidence-and-gates schedule wins and this calendar moves; a governance item is never allowed to pull forward a gate.

## What to delete

At Month 12, remove any row that produced no decision or artifact in the year, and any month theme that the team cannot state in one sentence from memory.
