## Purpose

Make an evidence-based decision to continue, constrain, invest, pause, or stop each market motion. The review distinguishes repository completion from actual company operation, customer acceptance, professional approval, and observed outcomes.

## Pre-read

- annual plan and prior-quarter commitments;
- approved financial statements, cash and 13-week forecast;
- current pipeline source and delivery-capacity view;
- product quality, incident, support, privacy and accessibility measures;
- risk, evidence-expiry, legal-review and policy-exception registers;
- pilot scorecards and customer-approved interpretation, if any.

## Review agenda

1. Close prior decisions and overdue actions.
2. Reconcile objectives, actual evidence and variance.
3. Review cash, burn, runway, committed spend and downside triggers.
4. Review individual and institutional motions separately.
5. Review security, privacy, accessibility, legal and operational gates.
6. Review team capacity, concentration risk and vendor dependency.
7. Approve next-quarter outcomes, resources and stop conditions.

## Required decisions

For each market motion record **GO**, **GO WITH CONDITIONS**, **PAUSE**, or **NO-GO**, its scope, evidence, conditions, expiry, owner, and reversal trigger. A quarterly decision cannot waive customer, counsel, security, privacy, accessibility, insurance, accounting, or provider authority.

## Risk and scenario review

Review base, downside and severe-but-plausible cases. At minimum cover loss of a critical operator, delayed sales, failed external review, security/privacy incident, vendor outage or insolvency, unexpected support load, cost growth, and customer offboarding.

## Outputs

- signed decision record and approved next-quarter outcomes;
- revised budget/forecast with assumptions;
- risk acceptances and exceptions with expiry;
- evidence and legal queues with owners/dates;
- public-claim changes, if approved;
- explicit items withheld from sale or activation.

## Cannot be completed from source code

Actual finances, pipeline, staffing, customer results, professional approvals, governing authority and risk acceptance require controlled company records and named-person approval.
