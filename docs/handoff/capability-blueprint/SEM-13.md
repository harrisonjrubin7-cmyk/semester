# SEM-13 — Pilot-to-annual with signed measures

Status: Proposed build specification, 2026-10-08. Not a verified implementation, test result, deployment, certification or approved commitment.

## Accountable roles and dependencies
Proposed owner: Customer success + sponsor + research/finance. Actual owner is unassigned.
Dependencies: Metric definitions, baseline, cohort, agreed scope and support.

## Requirements
Before pilot, sign measurement appendix specifying eligible cohort, baseline, sources, formulas, thresholds, privacy, observation window, limitations, commercial decision and owners. Allow go/extend/remediate/stop; no automatic annual conversion.

## Workflow
Agree measures → baseline → controlled activation → recurring review → close evidence window → jointly validate → annual decision and new scope authorization.

## Acceptance tests
Denominator excludes neither inconvenient users nor outages silently; changed success criteria require signed version; result shortfall triggers decision not retroactive threshold edits.

## Metrics to define and measure
signed-measure completeness; sponsor-validated outcomes; pilot support cost; conversion/extension reasons. Define eligible population, numerator/denominator, observation window, source, exclusions, uncertainty and owner before reporting. No achieved values or universal targets are asserted.

## Activation boundary
Thresholds/prices are unset until approved; conversion is commercial/institutional authorization, not proof of causal educational impact.

All relevant tenant isolation, privacy, source-authority, accessibility, monitoring/support, audit, recovery and institutional authorization gates remain mandatory. Evidence attaches to exact artifact/configuration and tenant/domain scope. Unresolved critical isolation, prohibited egress, unauthorized-action or official-integrity defects block activation.
