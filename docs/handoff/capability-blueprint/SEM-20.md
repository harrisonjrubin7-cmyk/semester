# SEM-20 — Renewal evidence and long relationships

Status: Proposed build specification, 2026-10-08. Not a verified implementation, test result, deployment, certification or approved commitment.

## Accountable roles and dependencies
Proposed owner: Customer success + institution sponsor. Actual owner is unassigned.
Dependencies: Signed measures, controls, outcomes, cost/support, export rights.

## Requirements
Maintain per-account evidence over terms: goals, role adoption, outcomes, accessibility, incidents, integration health, delivery costs, support and change requests. Renewal/expansion reviews include unresolved risks, portability and planned work with separate authority gates.

## Workflow
Baseline goals → recurring sponsor review → track value/harms → prepare renewal evidence → jointly validate → renew/adjust/exit → approve new domain separately.

## Acceptance tests
Renewal pack separates actuals/estimates and includes outages/attrition; no student-private content in commercial reporting; customer can export and exit without coercion.

## Metrics to define and measure
verified goal progress; unresolved issues; renewal/expansion reasons; implementation cost; exit quality. Define eligible population, numerator/denominator, observation window, source, exclusions, uncertainty and owner before reporting. No achieved values or universal targets are asserted.

## Activation boundary
Long relationships are earned through measured value, trust and portability, not forced lock-in or optimistic case-study claims.

All relevant tenant isolation, privacy, source-authority, accessibility, monitoring/support, audit, recovery and institutional authorization gates remain mandatory. Evidence attaches to exact artifact/configuration and tenant/domain scope. Unresolved critical isolation, prohibited egress, unauthorized-action or official-integrity defects block activation.
