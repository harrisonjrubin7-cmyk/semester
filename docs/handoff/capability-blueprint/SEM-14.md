# SEM-14 — Real-cohort outcome evidence

Status: Proposed build specification, 2026-10-08. Not a verified implementation, test result, deployment, certification or approved commitment.

## Accountable roles and dependencies
Proposed owner: Research/outcomes lead + institution reviewer. Actual owner is unassigned.
Dependencies: Authorized real data, approved protocol, privacy, metric lineage.

## Requirements
Prespecify cohort, baseline, outcomes, attrition/missing-data handling and comparison design. Separate usage, workflow completion, reported clarity, staff efficiency and educational outcomes. Review research governance and applicable ethics requirements with institution. No fabricated cohort results.

## Workflow
Approve protocol → enroll/authorize → baseline → collect minimal events/surveys → validate completeness → analyze uncertainty/confounding → institution review → scoped publish.

## Acceptance tests
Replayed events do not inflate success; missing users remain accounted for; observational findings are not causal claims; small-group disclosure is protected.

## Metrics to define and measure
completion; clarity; support friction; staff time; attrition; uncertainty; adverse effects. Define eligible population, numerator/denominator, observation window, source, exclusions, uncertainty and owner before reporting. No achieved values or universal targets are asserted.

## Activation boundary
No real cohort dataset or effect estimate supplied here. Evidence requires authorized data and protocol, not synthetic dashboard numbers.

All relevant tenant isolation, privacy, source-authority, accessibility, monitoring/support, audit, recovery and institutional authorization gates remain mandatory. Evidence attaches to exact artifact/configuration and tenant/domain scope. Unresolved critical isolation, prohibited egress, unauthorized-action or official-integrity defects block activation.
