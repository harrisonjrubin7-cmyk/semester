# SEM-06 — Migration factory safer than staying

Status: Proposed build specification, 2026-10-08. Not a verified implementation, test result, deployment, certification or approved commitment.

## Accountable roles and dependencies
Proposed owner: Integration/migration lead + institution sponsor. Actual owner is unassigned.
Dependencies: Inventory, mappings, reconciliation, support, cutover authority.

## Requirements
Compare staying and migration risks using documented integrity, operational, security, accessibility, cost and continuity evidence. Factory templates discovery, extraction, transformation, rehearsals, exceptions, training, coexistence and exit. Migrate by domain rather than blanket authority.

## Workflow
Baseline incumbent risk → profile/extract → map → rehearse → reconcile → review comparative risk → approve or defer bounded cutover.

## Acceptance tests
Equal counts with a changed grade fail; missing historical term blocks relevant transfer; denied access or recovery gap triggers no-go; staying risk is not asserted without institution evidence.

## Metrics to define and measure
rehearsal defects; critical differences; measured recovery; migration toil; documented residual risk. Define eligible population, numerator/denominator, observation window, source, exclusions, uncertainty and owner before reporting. No achieved values or universal targets are asserted.

## Activation boundary
Replacement safer than staying is a decision supported by comparative evidence, not a slogan. Institution approves residual risk and transfer scope.

All relevant tenant isolation, privacy, source-authority, accessibility, monitoring/support, audit, recovery and institutional authorization gates remain mandatory. Evidence attaches to exact artifact/configuration and tenant/domain scope. Unresolved critical isolation, prohibited egress, unauthorized-action or official-integrity defects block activation.
