# SEM-10 — Evidence-backed Trust Center

Status: Proposed build specification, 2026-10-08. Not a verified implementation, test result, deployment, certification or approved commitment.

## Accountable roles and dependencies
Proposed owner: Trust owner + legal + security. Actual owner is unassigned.
Dependencies: Control register, evidence storage, approved claims, supplier inventory.

## Requirements
Public layer shows scoped policies, registries, accessibility/status/incident routes and accurate capability states. Controlled procurement room provides dated authorized evidence. Every claim links control, owner, scope, reviewer and expiry; no raw customer evidence in public pages.

## Workflow
Collect control evidence → review claim → publish appropriate disclosure → track expiry/change → refresh or withdraw unsupported claim.

## Acceptance tests
A planned control cannot show certified; supplier reports do not certify Semester; expired restore evidence prompts re-review; buyer answers essential data-flow questions unaided.

## Metrics to define and measure
valid claim/evidence links; expired claims; questionnaire turnaround; open evidence gaps. Define eligible population, numerator/denominator, observation window, source, exclusions, uncertainty and owner before reporting. No achieved values or universal targets are asserted.

## Activation boundary
Certification and compliance wording need specific external/legal basis. HECVAT completion is not certification.

All relevant tenant isolation, privacy, source-authority, accessibility, monitoring/support, audit, recovery and institutional authorization gates remain mandatory. Evidence attaches to exact artifact/configuration and tenant/domain scope. Unresolved critical isolation, prohibited egress, unauthorized-action or official-integrity defects block activation.
