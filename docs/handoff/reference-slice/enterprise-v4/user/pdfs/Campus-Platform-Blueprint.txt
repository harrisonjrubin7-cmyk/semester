Semester Campus Life should unite four connected products: a marketplace, clubs,
global/career opportunities, and a student-controlled social layer. Club-management systems
commonly combine rosters, events, RSVPs, check-ins, communications, and approval
workflows; Semester should provide these functions without exposing protected academic data
in social spaces.

Students should filter by:
Category: job, internship, study abroad, club, event, campus service, peer opportunity.
Verification: institution verified, partner verified, student-posted, external.
Timing: application deadline, term, start date, recurring event schedule.
Location: campus, remote, city/region, country, time zone.
Academic fit: major, discipline, course-credit option, class year.
Career fit: industry, skills, job type, paid/unpaid, work authorization.
Study abroad: language, program model, estimated budget, housing, accessibility,
internship component.
Club fit: interest area, meeting time, dues, beginner-friendly, open membership,
application required.
Accessibility: wheelchair access, captions, virtual attendance, accommodation contact.
Commitment: estimated hours, event duration, schedule compatibility.
Students need both a filter drawer for detailed search and fast filter chips such as “Paid,”
“Deadline this week,” “Remote,” or “No application required.”
Ranking should prioritize:
1. Exact search terms.
2. Explicitly saved interests and filters.
3. Verified listings.
4. Near deadlines and relevant time windows.
5. Recently updated opportunities.
6. Clearly explained recommendations.
Show explanations such as:
Required filtering
Search ranking and transparency
Recommended because you saved “product design”
Shown because it matches your Spring study-abroad filter
Deadline in 5 days
Do not rank or target opportunities using grades, disciplinary records, disability
accommodations, counseling/wellness activity, or private messages. Student privacy rules
require careful treatment of education-record data in institution-provided services.

Roster functions:
Join, invite, approve, deny, remove, or suspend membership.
Assign time-limited officer roles.
Record dues status only where permitted.
Maintain attendance and participation records.
Create leadership-transition checklists.
Limit exports by role, purpose, and audit trail.
Keep alumni and former members separate from active rosters.
Preserve member privacy controls.
Attendance, RSVP, and QR check-in are established club/event patterns and should remain
optional—not a hidden surveillance mechanism.
Study-abroad applications commonly include an initial application, institutional eligibility
review, nomination, provider decision, commitment, and pre-departure requirements. The
exact sequence varies by institution and program.

Key application states:
Draft → Submitted → Eligibility review → Nominated
→ Provider review → Accepted / Waitlisted / Declined
→ Student committed → Pre-departure → Completed / Withdrawn
Do not represent an automated preliminary eligibility check as a final academic or admission
decision.
Saved → Preparing → Applied → Interviewing
→ Offer → Accepted / Declined → Active internship → Completed
Students should see deadlines, application materials, interview notes, recruiter
communication preferences, work/credit requirements, and reminders. Employers should not
gain access to grades, course schedules, or protected records through this tracker.
