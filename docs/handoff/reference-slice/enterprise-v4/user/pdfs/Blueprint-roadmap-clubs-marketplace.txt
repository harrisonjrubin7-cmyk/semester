Semester Campus should be designed as a connected campus life platform with four major
systems working together: marketplace, clubs and events, study-abroad operations, and a
social/feed layer. Existing student-engagement systems commonly combine organization
management, rosters, RSVPs, event tracking, and communications, while privacy guidance
requires careful separation between community activity and protected academic records.
The architecture should separate domain modules while sharing identity, permissions, search,
notifications, moderation, and analytics.

A core product rule should be that academic records and social/community records are not
treated as one dataset. FERPA guidance emphasizes that institutions must protect student-
record information and limit inappropriate disclosure.
Study-abroad portals should function as guided workflow systems rather than simple forms.
Institutional and provider processes commonly include submission, document collection,
review, nomination or approval, and final commitment steps.

Rule Requirement
Safety first Emergency and critical institutional notices override ranking
Explicit interest first Followed orgs, saved searches, and opted-in topics rank above inferred content
Explainability Every recommendation should display a reason
Relevance limits Repetitive or low-quality posts should be throttled
Sensitive-data exclusion No ranking from grades, accommodations, or private application outcomes
