Semester should be framed as a unified Education OS with three connected layers: a student-
facing app, an institution operations system, and a business operations console that carries a
person from prospect to applicant to enrolled student to active campus member. Higher-ed
platforms increasingly combine student engagement, targeted communications, applicant
portals, registration, and SIS-connected workflows in one connected experience rather than
separate tools.
The cleanest structure is:
Semester Inter App: student/community experience for announcements, messaging,
profiles, campus activity, and self-service.
Institution OS: staff-facing workflows for admissions, registration, scheduling, student
services, and compliance.
Business OS: executive/operations layer for communications, lifecycle automation,
analytics, and cross-department administration.
A modern student platform typically acts as a centralized hub for events, communications,
roles, and services, while the applicant-to-enrolled journey is increasingly managed as one
lifecycle record that flows into the SIS without re-keying.
Semester Inter App should combine announcements, direct messaging, email orchestration,
broadcast posts, and structured profiles in one communication layer. Student engagement
platforms commonly provide targeted announcements, push/email/in-app messaging,
personalized roles, and campus-wide content publishing.

Business OS, Institution OS, Education OS
Education OS is the umbrella platform: shared identity, permissions, workflows, data model,
integrations, analytics, notifications, and policy controls. A unified student data core is
valuable because schools need a single source of truth and cross-department communication
between recruitment, registration, billing, and student support.
Institution OS is the operating layer for staff and offices:
Admissions.
Registrar and scheduling.
Advising and student success.
Financial aid and billing coordination.
Student affairs and engagement.
Communications and case management.
Reporting and compliance.
Business OS is the executive and operational control plane:
Campaign management.
Workflow automation.
Revenue/enrollment funnel reporting.
Staffing queues and SLAs.
Service operations dashboards.
Institutional analytics and retention metrics.
Cross-functional administration.
A practical product map for Semester is:
Person identity across prospect, applicant, student, staff, alumni.
Organization graph for departments, clubs, programs, offices.
Permissions and lifecycle states.
Unified notification and communication preferences.

A single person can move through Semester like this:
1. They become a prospect aer requesting information.
2. They receive personalized outreach, portal access, and event invitations.
3. They submit an application and track missing items in the applicant portal.
4. Admissions issues a decision and activates enrollment next steps.
5. Once confirmed, the person is provisioned into registration, advising, and Inter App
spaces.
6. As an enrolled student, they receive announcements, message advisors, join communities,
build a profile, and register for classes.
7. Their portfolio and campus identity continue through internships, clubs, and alumni
engagement.
