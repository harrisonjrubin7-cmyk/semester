## Metric framework

Revenue: contracted recurring, recognized, cash receipts and one-time services kept separate. Adoption: eligible/provisioned/activated/active denominators. Delivery: contracted/authorized/enabled. Economics: gross margin and cost allocation. Liquidity: reconciled cash and scenario threshold. Reliability: scoped incidents and service metrics. Risk: residual severity, owner and due action. All values remain placeholders.
