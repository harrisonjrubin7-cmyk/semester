# Registration Readiness pilot charter

## Purpose
Help the approved student cohort see what may need attention before registration, through a source-aware planning experience.

## What Semester does
- Shows approved, institution-connected information with visible sources.
- Explains derived readiness states and routes blockers to the right office.
- Provides receipts, support handoffs and reconciliation records.

## What stays official
Vanderbilt's systems and offices remain authoritative for enrollment, academic records, holds, prerequisites, overrides, degree audit and policy interpretation.
