Subject: Check you're ready to register for Spring 2027

You can now check, in Semester, whether you're ready to register for Spring 2027.

**A readiness result is not enrollment.** You still register in YES when your time ticket opens. Vanderbilt's system is always the official record.

Each item shows where it came from:
- **Institution verified** — confirmed by Vanderbilt
- **Connected** — imported from a connected system
- **Student entered** — added by you
- **Estimated** — a planning calculation, not an official record
- **Needs review** — an office may need to check something

If a source is out of date, Semester will tell you and won't give a final result. If something looks wrong, use "Report a problem" on the item, or contact [support contact].
