Registration-readiness pilot — brief and scope record

Signed paid pilot with Vanderbilt — launch Spring 2027, pending institutional activation. This document is for Wyatt Rose (Registrar’s Office) to review, correct and sign before any configuration begins.

What the pilot does

Students in an approved cohort see a source-labelled readiness check for Spring 2027 registration: what Vanderbilt’s systems say, how current it is, and the next safe step. Blockers route to the right office. Every request returns a receipt.

What it never does

  • Enroll a student, confirm a seat, remove a hold or override a prerequisite.
  • Change an official record or interpret Vanderbilt policy.
  • Show an out-of-date or unknown result as final — those become “needs review”.
  • Send student records to an AI provider.
