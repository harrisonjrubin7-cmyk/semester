## Eight rollback classes

### T01 Tenant leak or restricted disclosure

Trigger: Confirmed or credible unauthorized retrieval, including titles/search/AI context.

Containment: Hold affected scope; preserve safe evidence; security/privacy incident escalation.

Resume evidence: Negative suite, impact review, remediation and fresh acceptance.

### T02 Unauthorized official or financial write

Trigger: Mutation outside actor, source-system or institution authority.

Containment: Kill new writeback; checkpoint commands; reconcile committed/uncertain outcomes.

Resume evidence: Source reconciliation and domain-authorized correction; fresh write authority.

### T03 Corruption or reconciliation failure

Trigger: Integrity failure or unexplained divergence from authoritative source.

Containment: Hold writes; isolate repair/restore; preserve audit and queues.

Resume evidence: DB/object checksums, external-state reconciliation, post-backup change review.

### T04 Withdrawn or expired authority

Trigger: Applicable approval withdrawn, expired or scope-mismatched.

Containment: Hold only affected operation/cohort; prevent fallback expansion.

Resume evidence: New current exact-scope approval; gate-negative retest.

### T05 AI policy or gateway bypass

Trigger: Direct/proxy/worker route evades policy, budget, source access or kill.

Containment: Hold relevant AI dispatch; preserve permitted audit; settle reservations.

Resume evidence: All-entrypoint enforcement, injection/kill/budget retest.

### T06 Identity disclosure

Trigger: Pseudonym/private identity exposed outside accepted disclosure scope.

Containment: Hold affected surface; privacy-safe transition; no name-reveal fallback.

Resume evidence: Privacy impact acceptance, corrected visibility and regression evidence.

### T07 Missing support, alerts or backup

Trigger: Required coverage unavailable or alert delivery/acknowledgement fails.

Containment: Hold activation/expansion; preserve urgent authorized handoff.

Resume evidence: Named coverage and successful operated alert/support drill.

### T08 Accepted service threshold breached

Trigger: Populated, approved service/error/freshness/capacity threshold exceeded.

Containment: Hold affected feature; diagnose against exact agreed metric/window.

Resume evidence: Recovered accepted operating window and fresh release review.
