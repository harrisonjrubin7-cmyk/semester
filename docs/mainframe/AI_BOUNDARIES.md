# AI boundaries

AI sits beside the domain engines. It uses the same commands a person may use. It does not get a second permission path.

## Allowed

Explain, draft, summarize, plan, and retrieve sources the caller may already read. Permission is checked on retrieval, on tool execution, on cached answers, and on the output.

## Not allowed on its own

Official grades, academic-record changes, enroll or drop, financial approval, or an override of institutional policy.
