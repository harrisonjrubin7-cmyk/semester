## Plain-language summary

No consumer subscription price, renewal structure or refund rule is approved by this draft. Before charging, Semester must clearly show total price/tax, billing frequency, trial, renewal, cancellation, refund and price-change terms, obtain any required affirmative consent, provide accessible cancellation at least as easy as purchase, and issue confirmations.

## Consumer decision fields

`[PLAN/PRICE/CURRENCY/TAX]`, `[PROCESSOR]`, `[TRIAL]`, `[BILLING/RENEWAL]`, `[RENEWAL REMINDER]`, `[PRICE-CHANGE NOTICE]`, `[CANCELLATION EFFECT]`, `[REFUND/PRORATION]`, `[FAILED PAYMENT/GRACE]`, `[CHARGEBACK]`, `[AGE/GEOGRAPHY]`, `[SUPPORT/RECORDS]`.

No preselected paid upgrade, hidden renewal, false urgency, obstructive cancellation, forfeiture of statutory rights, or retention of payment access after cancellation is permitted. Cancellation must distinguish stopping renewal, loss of paid features, account/data export/deletion, institution-sponsored access and legally required records.

## Institutional distinction

Institutional fees, term, non-renewal, termination, credits/refunds, implementation, acceptance and offboarding belong in the executed agreement/order form; consumer policy must not silently amend them. Students must not be charged for an institution-sponsored service without a separately clear lawful transaction.
