## Plain-language summary

Marketing is optional and separate from service, security, legal and support communications. Refusal or withdrawal does not remove core access. Semester must not preselect consent, bundle it with unrelated terms, use education records or sensitive data for targeting, or keep sending after a valid opt-out.

## Consent template

“Yes, I want Semester to send me `[EMAIL/SMS/OTHER CHANNEL]` about `[SPECIFIC CONTENT]`. Messages may be sent by `[ENTITY/VENDOR]` at `[FREQUENCY OR VARIABLE DESCRIPTION]`. Consent is optional. I can withdraw through `[ONE-STEP METHOD]`. See the Privacy Notice.”

Record exact text/version, channel/address, source/context, timestamp/time zone, affirmative action, age/jurisdiction logic, vendor, proof, withdrawals/suppression and permitted purpose. Transactional/service messages use a separately approved basis and cannot carry disguised marketing.
