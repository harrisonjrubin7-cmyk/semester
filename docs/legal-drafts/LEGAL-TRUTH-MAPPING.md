## Evidence classes and truth rule

| Evidence class | Can establish | Cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| repository source/test | behavior at a named revision | deployment, staffing, legal sufficiency, independent assurance or customer acceptance |
| deployment/configuration | artifact/configuration present in a named environment | continuous operation, institutional approval or outcome |
| operational exercise | one scoped process practiced at a date | universal or contractual performance |
| external professional/independent evidence | exact reviewed scope, issuer, date and conclusion | broader compliance, certification or future state |
| executed customer/company record | accepted obligation/decision for named parties and version | general availability or another customer's approval |

Legal and public language may not exceed the weakest required evidence class. A missing owner, date, environment, scope, expiry, jurisdiction, age posture, limitation or approval makes the affected claim unusable.
