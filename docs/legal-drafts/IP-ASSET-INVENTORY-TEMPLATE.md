## Plain-language summary

This inventory records what Semester may own, license, receive, publish, protect, or need permission to use. A repository file, domain account, design, contribution, generated asset, or dependency is not proof of ownership. Counsel and authorized business owners must verify chain of title, licenses, restrictions and required notices.

## Minimum inventory groups

- source code, database schemas, tests, infrastructure, build/release materials and internal tools;
- Semester names, logos, design system, domains, social/app-store accounts and marketing assets;
- product content, documentation, curricula/templates, datasets, evaluation sets and generated media;
- inventions, methods, know-how, confidential information and trade secrets;
- inbound/outbound licenses, open-source packages, fonts, icons, media, APIs, provider models and customer/institution materials;
- founder, employee, contractor, collaborator and prior-project contributions and executed assignment evidence.

## Product-behavior and evidence mapping

| Area | Current evidence | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| repository provenance | Git history and file tree | authorship record is not full chain-of-title evidence |
| third-party software | [`docs/SUPPLY-CHAIN.md`](../SUPPLY-CHAIN.md) and lockfiles | dependency licenses do not cover all content, services or legal conclusions |
| course/user materials | provenance/access controls in product | provenance is not copyright ownership or reuse permission |
| domains/digital assets | existing registers and account records | beneficial ownership, recovery and transfer authority require confirmation |

## Approval and use blockers

Verified legal entity; contributor identity/status; executed assignments/releases; prior-employer/school obligations; third-party license and notice review; AI/provider terms; brand clearance; domain/account control; customer/institution rights; geography; age/privacy/publicity rights; confidential treatment; disputes; renewal calendar; and counsel disposition. No asset may be labeled “owned,” “registered,” “exclusive,” “patented,” or “licensed for” a use without corresponding evidence.
