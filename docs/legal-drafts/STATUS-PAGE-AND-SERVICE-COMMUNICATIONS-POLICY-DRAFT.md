## Plain-language summary

Semester will communicate verified material service conditions through approved channels when staffing and systems are operating. A status page is not an uptime guarantee, incident-notice commitment, or substitute for direct contractual/legal communication. Update frequency and response targets remain unapproved.

## Communication classes

Planned maintenance; degradation/outage; security/privacy incident; data-rights or account issue; vendor/integration disruption; customer-specific issue; resolved/post-incident review. Each record identifies affected service/scope, first confirmed time, current verified impact, user action, mitigation, next update condition/time, owner, channel, resolution and retrospective link where appropriate.

Do not speculate about cause, attribution, data access, recovery time, legal status or scope. Correct prior statements visibly. Protect personal/customer/security-sensitive information while meeting legal/contract duties. Direct customer/regulator/insurer/law-enforcement notices follow separately approved authority.
