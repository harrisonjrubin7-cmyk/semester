## Facts and materials

Describe the exact proposal, current behavior, desired outcome, users, data, systems, providers, integrations, payments, claims, communications, support, accessibility, AI, safety, and operational owners. Attach the current draft, redline, source evidence, target configuration, data flow, risk/issue list, decision history, and known gaps. Separate verified facts, assumptions, requests, roadmap items, and unresolved external facts.

## Questions for review

1. What legal entities, roles, authority, jurisdictions, ages, and laws or contract terms may apply?
2. What notices, consent, agreement, filing, policy, accessibility, security, privacy, AI, employment, tax, insurance, IP, or records requirements need resolution?
3. Which language, workflow, evidence, approval, or operational control must change before publication, signature, payment, data use, or activation?
4. What decision, limitation, alternative, owner, expiry, and re-review trigger should be recorded?

## Triage

| Impact | Examples | Default action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| critical | unlawful/unauthorized processing, safety risk, signature/payment without authority, material misrepresentation | stop affected action; escalate immediately |
| high | institutional data, minors, incident, high-impact AI, nonstandard liability/IP/security/privacy/accessibility term | qualified review before commitment |
| standard | approved-template use within unchanged facts and scope | confirm version/authority and retain approval evidence |

## Product-behavior and evidence mapping

Repository links can establish documented design or implementation at a named revision. The intake must separately identify deployment, operation, customer/institution, professional, contractual, company, jurisdiction, age, and ownership evidence and mark anything absent as unresolved.

## Required output and blockers

Record reviewer, scope, advice/decision, approved language, assumptions, required actions, prohibited actions, owner, due/expiry, evidence, and re-review triggers in the approved system—not only chat or email. [Legal review required](../launch-readiness/LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md) remains blocking until qualified reviewers approve the applicable material. Intake completion is not legal approval.
