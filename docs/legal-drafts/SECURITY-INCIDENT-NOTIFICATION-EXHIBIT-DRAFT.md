## Plain-language summary

This exhibit would define how suspected and confirmed security or privacy incidents are assessed, contained, documented, and communicated. No 24/7 response, fixed notification deadline, recovery time, cause, scope, or legal conclusion is promised until counsel and operating owners approve an evidence-backed commitment.

## Required terms and workflow

Define incident/security-event terminology; severity; discovery and confirmation; affected service/data/users; incident commander and backups; containment; evidence preservation; forensic/vendor support; customer cooperation; authorized contacts/channels; update cadence; notice content and approval; regulator/user/law-enforcement authority; remediation; recovery validation; post-incident review; retention; and conflict/precedence with law and contract.

Communications must distinguish suspected from confirmed facts, avoid speculation, protect personal and security-sensitive information, and visibly correct material errors. A public status update does not replace direct contractual or legally required notice.
