This addendum would limit institution-provided and institution-authorized student data to the exact educational pilot purpose, users, term, systems and instructions. It does not itself establish FERPA, COPPA or state-law compliance or authorize K–12/minor processing.

Subject to counsel-approved terminology, Semester must use data only for documented customer instructions; limit access to approved roles/purpose; prohibit sale, ads, hidden profiling and generalized model training; prevent unapproved re-disclosure; support correction/export/deletion/offboarding as actually implemented; preserve customer control; and disclose limitations.

Students retain applicable rights and transparent controls. Institution authority does not permit unrelated marketing or broad individual surveillance. No data is used for admissions, aid, discipline, grading, disability, health, immigration or other high-impact decisions through autonomous AI.
