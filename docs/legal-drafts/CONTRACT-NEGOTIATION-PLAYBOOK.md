# Semester contract negotiation playbook — draft

- **Owner:** Legal coordinator with Deal Desk
- **Applies to:** institutional NDAs, evaluations, pilots, subscriptions, services, DPAs, security/accessibility/AI exhibits, and amendments
- **Approval authority:** qualified counsel and authorized business, finance, security, privacy, accessibility, product, engineering, insurance, and executive owners as applicable

## Plain-language summary

This playbook keeps proposed language aligned with actual product, operational, financial, legal, and risk capacity. It does not authorize anyone to give legal advice, bind the company, accept customer paper, or trade one uncontrolled risk for another.

## Negotiation workflow

1. **Intake:** identify parties, authority, document set, opportunity, scope, data, users, jurisdictions, timeline, procurement rules, and customer redlines.
2. **Baseline:** select counsel-approved forms and current evidence; record every material blank, assumption, dependency, conflict, and expiry.
3. **Triage:** assign each issue to legal, privacy, security, accessibility, AI, product, engineering, operations/support, finance/tax, insurance, IP, or executive review.
4. **Respond:** explain the truthful current position, offer only approved alternatives, and mark roadmap or professional-services work separately.
5. **Deviation control:** record requested language, baseline, risk, operational/technical work, financial impact, approvers, expiry, and precedent treatment.
6. **Pre-signature reconciliation:** ensure MSA, Order Form, SOW, DPA, exhibits, proposal, product configuration, support, pricing, and claims agree.
7. **Activation handoff:** translate executed obligations into named owners, controls, tests, evidence, dates, monitoring, renewals, and offboarding tasks. Signature alone is not activation approval.

## Non-negotiable without explicit specialist approval

- Unsupported compliance, certification, accessibility-conformance, security, uptime, recovery, outcome, integration, customer, or AI claims.
- Use of student or customer data beyond the approved purpose/map, including advertising, unrelated profiling, undisclosed training, or high-impact autonomous decisions.
- Official-system writes, unaccepted integrations, unlimited custom work, unstaffed 24/7/SLA commitments, or untested deletion/recovery obligations.
- Unapproved prices, discounts, credits, taxes, insurance, indemnities, liability terms, IP transfers, audit rights, data regions, provider terms, or governing law.
- Publicity, logo, testimonial, partnership, reference, or outcome use without separate authority.

## Product-behavior and commercial mapping

Repository evidence supports only the exact implementation and tests cited. Operational, target-environment, independent, customer, professional, and contractual evidence remain separate. The proposed defaults in [Commercial governance](../operating-model/COMMERCIAL-GOVERNANCE.md) are not an approved price book or negotiation authority.

| Negotiated promise | Evidence required before approval |
| --- | --- |
| feature, configuration or integration | source evidence plus target-environment acceptance and customer dependencies |
| privacy, security, AI or accessibility position | approved schedule, current scoped evidence, limitations, age/jurisdiction analysis and owner acceptance |
| SLA, support or recovery | staffing, monitoring history, exercised escalation/recovery and approved remedies |
| implementation, custom work or outcome | accepted SOW, capacity, objective criteria and explicit non-guarantee of customer outcomes |

## Execution blockers

Confirmed entities/signatories; counsel-approved final redline; resolved deviations; approved economics/tax/insurance; exact scope/data/integrations/providers; accepted security/privacy/accessibility/AI position; feasible staffing/support/recovery/offboarding; obligation register and owners; no blank material term; and signed internal GO with no open P0/P1.
