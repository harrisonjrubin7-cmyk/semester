## Governance and responsibilities

Institution names sponsor, champion, IT, security, privacy, accessibility, procurement/counsel and support contacts; provides lawful authority, minimum data, communications, users, source validation, UAT and weekly participation. Semester names equivalent owners; configures only approved scope; provides training, documented support, evidence, rollback, export/deletion and offboarding. Joint governance cannot replace either party's independent legal/security authority.

## Success and guardrails

Attach baseline, event/data sources, definitions, privacy threshold, cadence, owner, caveats and decision rule. Logins alone are not success. No causal GPA/retention/graduation or efficiency claim without appropriate design/evidence. Safety, accessibility, privacy, support burden and data quality are guardrails; harm or invalid authority can pause the pilot.

## Launch, change, pause and closeout

Activation requires executed paper, target configuration, role/isolation/access acceptance, security/privacy/accessibility review, UAT, support/monitoring, incident/recovery/export/deletion/offboarding rehearsal and signed GO. Scope changes require written impact/price/risk/acceptance. Define pause/rollback, data export, access revocation, deletion/retention/legal hold, final report, reference permission and convert/extend/offboard decision.
