## Plain-language summary

Each party may disclose limited non-public information for the stated purpose. The receiving party must limit access, protect the information, use it only for that purpose, and return or destroy it subject to approved retention and legal obligations. This draft is not signed and does not itself authorize access to student data, systems, source code, security evidence, or production environments.

## Draft terms for counsel

1. **Confidential information.** Define protected written, oral, visual and electronic information, including product, security, technical, commercial, customer and institutional information, and the method for confirming oral disclosures.
2. **Exclusions.** Address information independently developed, lawfully received without duty, already known without restriction, or public without breach, with the receiving party carrying any agreed burden of proof.
3. **Use and access.** Use only for `[PURPOSE]`; disclose only to representatives with a need to know and enforceable duties; no reverse engineering, benchmarking, publicity, rights grant, or competitive use except as counsel approves.
4. **Protection.** Apply at least `[AGREED STANDARD OF CARE]`, least privilege, secure transfer/storage, and prompt internal escalation. Security artifacts may be limited to named reviewers through an access-controlled room.
5. **Compelled disclosure.** Provide lawful notice where permitted, disclose only what is required, and cooperate on protective treatment at the disclosing party's expense as negotiated.
6. **Return, deletion and survival.** Specify request/termination actions, backup and legal-hold exceptions, certification method, confidentiality survival by information class, and treatment of trade secrets.
7. **No warranty or commitment.** Evaluation information is provided under negotiated limitations; neither party must transact, disclose any particular item, or grant system/data access.
8. **Remedies and general terms.** `[INJUNCTIVE RELIEF, LIABILITY, ASSIGNMENT, EXPORT, ENTIRE AGREEMENT, AMENDMENT, COUNTERPARTS AND SIGNATURE TERMS FOR COUNSEL.]`
