## Plain-language summary

The proposed statement describes Semester's accessibility goal, automated checks, user preferences, known limitations and barrier-reporting route. It deliberately does not claim WCAG 2.2 AA conformance.

## Evidence mapping

Bind every statement to the current release: automated axe/label/style/contrast/target evidence, keyboard/reflow/zoom/reduced-motion checks, supported browser/AT matrix, document/media boundaries, open issues, manual evaluation and barrier-response operations.

## Required decisions and blockers

Name an owner and backup; complete qualified manual keyboard/screen-reader/zoom evaluation; remediate/retest material findings; decide ACR/VPAT scope; approve response/escalation targets that can be staffed; verify company-site and app reporting paths; counsel reviews jurisdiction/procurement language.

## Prohibited claims

Confirm intended user ages, minor/school-authority posture and launch jurisdictions before publication; accessibility and accommodation duties may vary by audience and context.

Do not state “accessible,” “WCAG compliant,” “ADA compliant,” “Section 508 compliant,” certified, fully tested or formally conformant beyond the exact dated evidence.
