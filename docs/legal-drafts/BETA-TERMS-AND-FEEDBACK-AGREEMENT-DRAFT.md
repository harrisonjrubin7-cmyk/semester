## Plain-language summary

Beta use is limited evaluation of identified features with known gaps, change risk and no unsupported availability/outcome promise. Beta labeling does not excuse unsafe security/privacy/accessibility, misleading claims, unlawful processing, missing data authority, or inability to export/offboard.

## Individual and institutional boundary

Individual beta terms do not authorize institution education records or system access. An institution beta requires an executed pilot/evaluation agreement, data roles, security/privacy/accessibility review, responsibility matrix and signed activation decision. `[COUNSEL: review consumer warranty/liability, minors, research/IRB, compensation, employment and jurisdiction issues.]`

## Publication/activation blockers

Counsel-approved scope; eligible participants and age posture; release/security/accessibility/privacy gates; known-limitations disclosure; staffed support; consent/measurement; data rights/offboarding; payment terms if any; target-environment acceptance; and versioned affirmative acceptance. “Beta” never converts a P0/P1 risk into acceptable launch risk.
