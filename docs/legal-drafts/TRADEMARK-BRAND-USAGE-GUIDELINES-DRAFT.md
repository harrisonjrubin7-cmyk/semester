## Plain-language summary

These guidelines describe how approved Semester names and visual assets may be used without implying trademark registration, endorsement, partnership, customer status, institutional approval, or permission from another rights holder. No mark is represented as owned, cleared, filed, or registered until supporting records and counsel review exist.

## Usage rules

1. Use the exact approved spelling, capitalization, artwork, colors, clear space, minimum size and accessible alternative text in `[CONTROLLED BRAND ASSET LOCATION]`.
2. Do not redraw, distort, recolor, animate, combine, translate, pluralize, possess, abbreviate, turn into a verb, or embed a mark in another name/domain/handle without written approval.
3. Use `™`, `SM`, or `®` only when counsel confirms the correct symbol, mark, territory, goods/services and registration status. Never use `®` for an unregistered mark or outside an approved basis.
4. Do not use an institution, customer, partner, provider, student, employee or third-party name/logo as a reference, endorsement, compatibility claim, testimonial or case study without separate written authority and approved wording.
5. Descriptive compatibility statements must be truthful, necessary, non-prominent and reviewed; do not mimic another party's trade dress or suggest official affiliation.
6. Licensees and vendors must use only supplied assets for the written purpose, channel, territory and term; no sublicensing, domain/handle registration, alteration or post-termination use.
7. Report suspected infringement, confusing names, impersonation, misuse and takedown demands to `[LEGAL/BRAND CONTACT]`; staff must not threaten enforcement or concede rights without counsel.
