## Plain-language summary

This procedure would govern access, export, correction, restriction/objection, deletion, and related privacy requests. The product contains request and self-service capabilities, but a database due date or written runbook does not establish a universally applicable legal deadline, staffed operation, completed response, or institutional authority.

## Procedure

1. **Intake:** record request type, authenticated requester, relevant entity/tenant, received channel/time, scope, and only necessary detail; prevent secrets and another person's records from entering the queue.
2. **Identity and authority:** verify the requester, guardian/agent/institution authority, applicable customer role, and conflict without collecting excessive identifiers.
3. **Applicability and deadline:** determine data roles, jurisdiction, contract, exceptions, extension rules, and the authoritative response clock with counsel/customer direction.
4. **Search and preserve:** identify systems, device-only limits, subprocessors, shared records, backups, audit/financial records, legal holds, and active incidents.
5. **Execute:** use approved export, correction, restriction, deletion, or refusal process with least privilege, dual review where required, failure handling, and provider propagation.
6. **Respond:** provide accessible plain-language status, result, limitations, refusal/exception basis, and appeal or regulator/customer route as approved.
7. **Close and evidence:** retain the request, verification fact, actions, manifest/receipt, approvals, exceptions, communications, incident/escalation, and final delivery evidence without duplicating responsive content.

## Product-behavior mapping

The [Data-rights request runbook](../DATA-RIGHTS-REQUEST-RUNBOOK.md) describes authenticated intake, trusted status transitions, isolation, and rehearsal expectations. Repository structures and tests do not prove daily queue monitoring, named responders, legal determinations, provider completion, whole-tenant export/deletion, or target-environment operation.

## Activation blockers

Confirm requester/data-subject age, guardian or school authority and applicable jurisdiction before applying identity, consent, deadline, response or deletion rules.

Counsel-approved applicability and clocks; named trained primary/backup; monitored accessible channels; trusted operator surface; customer routing; identity/authority process; legal-hold and incident integration; current data inventory; provider procedures; production-equivalent exercises; appeal/escalation; evidence retention; and notices that match actual behavior. Do not promise a response deadline until those controls and the applicable authority support it.
