## Plain-language summary

This procedure routes subpoenas, warrants, court orders, preservation demands, regulatory inquiries, emergency requests, national-security process, and informal government contacts to qualified counsel. Staff must not disclose data, confirm a user, promise secrecy, or alter records based on an unverified request or this draft alone.

## Intake and control record

1. Preserve the original request, envelope/header, delivery method and receipt time without clicking untrusted links or contacting the requester through details supplied only in the request.
2. Assign `[REQUEST ID]`; restrict access; notify counsel and the security/privacy owners; pause ordinary handling only as counsel directs.
3. Verify issuer, identity, authority, jurisdiction, service, signature, scope, deadline and authenticity through an independently sourced channel.
4. Counsel determines validity, objections, notice/gag restrictions, preservation, minimization, conflicts, emergency standard, cross-border issues, cost and response authority.
5. Identify responsive systems and data by exact account/tenant, category, time period, custodian, source, retention and subprocessor. Do not create data that does not exist.
6. Collect with least privilege, chain-of-custody records, hashes where appropriate, dual review and a disclosure log. Produce only counsel-approved material through an approved channel.
7. Notify the affected customer/user unless prohibited or counsel determines notice is not permitted or appropriate; record the reason and review date for delayed notice.
8. Close with the request, decisions, production index, approvals, communications, costs, preservation/hold status and post-matter deletion or retention instruction.
