# Support and Escalation Policy — Draft

This draft is not approved, published, or in force and does not create a response-time, availability, service-level, dedicated-support, or 24/7-coverage commitment.

## Controlled sources

The public-language source is the [Support Policy draft](../legal/SUPPORT-POLICY-DRAFT.md). The operating source is [Support Operations](../market-readiness/SUPPORT-OPERATIONS.md). Contractual service commitments, if approved, belong in the applicable agreement and service-level schedule. Conflicts must be resolved before publication.

## Intended support model

Support should maintain separate, clearly published routes for product questions, institutional/operator requests, accessibility barriers, privacy and data-rights matters, security reports, safety concerns, and billing issues. Each accepted request should receive a record, classification, accountable owner, allowed diagnostic scope, communication history, resolution state, and follow-up without copying unnecessary student content.

## Severity and escalation

Severity should consider safety, security or privacy impact, loss of access, data integrity, cohort size, workaround availability, contractual scope, and time sensitivity. Suspected incidents must enter the applicable incident process. Accessibility, privacy, security, billing, and legal matters must route to the accountable specialist or external adviser rather than being closed as ordinary product support.

Support access to user or institutional data must be least-privileged, purpose-limited, authorized, time-bound where applicable, and auditable. Support must not change official institutional records, impersonate another user, or promise an outcome outside its authority.

## Current readiness

**RED for activation.** Current repository material does not establish an accepted production queue, staffed hours, named primary and backup owners, monitored channels, approved targets, escalation drills, or observed response performance. Any response targets in runbooks are planning targets until staffing, contracts, and operating evidence approve them.

## Plain-language summary

Semester intends to give users a clear way to ask for help and to route urgent or sensitive issues to the right owner. The effective policy must state the channels and support level that are actually staffed. It must not imply 24/7 coverage, a contractual SLA, or a dedicated success manager without approved evidence.

## Publication and activation blockers

- [ ] Support channels, hours, queue, primary owner, backup, and monitoring are operationally accepted.
- [ ] Severity, escalation, privacy, accessibility, security, safety, billing, and incident routes are tested.
- [ ] Counsel approves the public language and its relationship to contracts and consumer requirements.
- [ ] Any response or resolution target is staffed, measured, approved, and accurately scoped.
- [ ] Diagnostic access and record retention match the privacy notice and institutional agreements.

Until these blockers close, support availability and response performance must not be marketed as production-ready.
