## Age and institutional boundaries

No minor testimonial, student record, institution mark or employee statement is used without counsel-confirmed authority and all required individual/guardian/institution approvals. A student's consent does not necessarily authorize an institution logo; an employee's statement does not necessarily bind the institution.
