## Plain-language summary

This index controls how existing RFP answers may be reused. The canonical technical library contains useful repository-linked answers, but it is a point-in-time source and may contain statuses or statements that have changed. It must never be copied wholesale or treated as approved customer-facing language without a current evidence and claims review.

## Allowed answer states

| State | Permitted meaning | Minimum response treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `VERIFIED — REPOSITORY` | exact implementation/test evidence exists | state environment, date, limits, and missing production proof |
| `VERIFIED — EXTERNAL` | current independent/customer/operational evidence exists | identify scope, issuer, date, expiry, and access limits |
| `CONDITIONAL` | available only after named configuration/approval | list every dependency and do not imply current activation |
| `PLANNED / NOT AVAILABLE` | not sellable or operational today | state the gap without a delivery promise |
| `NOT SUPPORTED` | intentionally excluded | do not imply workaround or roadmap unless approved |
| `OWNER TO SUPPLY` | repository cannot prove the fact | require accountable human evidence before submission |

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not infer company facts from code; convert a mock/test integration into an availability claim; describe repository controls as production operation; claim certification, compliance, conformance, penetration testing, customer results, references, uptime, response times, recovery objectives, insurance, financial standing, or provider terms without current evidence; or promise a roadmap date through an RFP response.

Where the source library conflicts with the current finalization baseline, evidence register, legal draft, deployment state, or approved customer scope, use the more conservative truthful answer and queue the source for correction.

## Product-behavior and evidence mapping

| Evidence layer | What it supports | What it does not establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| code, configuration and automated test | exact implemented behavior at a named revision | deployment, staffing, legal sufficiency or customer acceptance |
| CI or deployment record | checks completed or artifact deployed at a date | named-institution activation, continuous operation or outcome |
| operational exercise | one scoped process was practiced | universal or contractual performance |
| independent assessment | the assessor's exact scope, period and conclusion | broader certification, conformance or future state |
| signed customer record | accepted scope or decision for that customer | general availability or another institution's approval |

## Submission blockers

Every response must state or explicitly mark not applicable the intended ages/minor posture and jurisdictions when they affect product, data, accessibility, AI, consent or contractual answers.

Current question set; named response owner; immutable evidence snapshot; cross-functional review; company facts supplied by accountable owners; confidential-material authorization; all deviations approved; commercial terms approved; and final consistency check against the proposal, contract, DPA, security/accessibility evidence, and target-environment status.
