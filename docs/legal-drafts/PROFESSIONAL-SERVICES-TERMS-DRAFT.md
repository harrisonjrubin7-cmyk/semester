## Plain-language summary

These terms provide a framework for separately scoped implementation, configuration, training, integration, migration, advisory, or other professional services. No service, deliverable, staffing level, deadline, fee, integration, or outcome is committed until an authorized statement of work identifies it and the parties sign the required documents.

## Draft framework

1. **Statements of work.** Each SOW states deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, dependencies, customer inputs, staffing, schedule, fees, expenses, acceptance, evidence, change control and precedence.
2. **Customer cooperation.** Customer provides timely authorized decisions, personnel, access, source materials, test accounts, approvals and feedback. Delay remedies and re-planning require written agreement.
3. **Personnel and subcontractors.** Define permitted personnel, screening/training requirements, location, replacement, supervision, subprocessors and customer policies. No named resource is promised without written confirmation.
4. **Access and data.** Least privilege, approved environments, data minimization, credentials, logging, support access, secure transfer, retention, deletion and incident routing follow the controlling security/privacy terms.
5. **Acceptance.** Objective criteria, review period, rejection detail, cure/retest, partial acceptance and deemed-acceptance rules are `[TO BE NEGOTIATED]`. Silence is not acceptance unless counsel expressly approves that rule.
6. **Fees and changes.** Rates, milestones, taxes, expenses, cancellation, rescheduling and overages are `[TBD]`. Scope changes require an authorized written change order.
7. **Work product and IP.** Distinguish pre-existing materials, platform/configuration, customer materials, bespoke deliverables, feedback, open source and licenses. `[COUNSEL TO SET OWNERSHIP AND LICENSE TERMS.]`
8. **Risk allocation.** Warranties, remedies, indemnities, liability, insurance, compliance, termination and transition must align with the controlling agreement and applicable SOW.
