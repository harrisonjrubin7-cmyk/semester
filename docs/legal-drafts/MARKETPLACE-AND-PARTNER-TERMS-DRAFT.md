# Semester marketplace terms and partner terms — outline draft

**Status:** outline of required terms, not contract text. Every item **requires qualified human counsel review.** The marketplace is not live and no motion in `GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md` authorises it. This outline exists so the term set is designed before the product is, and so no marketplace or partner claim is made first.
**Owner:** Harrison Rubin (coordinator). Counsel unassigned.

## Part 1 — Marketplace terms (student ↔ Semester ↔ provider)

Three agreements, kept distinct so each party sees what binds it.

| Agreement | Parties | Must settle (counsel) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketplace Terms of Use | Student buyer and Semester | Semester's role; listing accuracy disclaimer; order, cancellation, refund and dispute path; prohibited purchases; student-age purchasers; limits on liability; governing law; link to Terms and Privacy Notice |
| Provider Agreement | Provider and Semester | Eligibility and onboarding; identity/sanctions checks; licences and insurance; listing content rules; fees and commission; payout schedule, reserves and holds; tax forms and reporting; data use limits (no use of student data beyond fulfilling the order); IP in listings; indemnity; suspension and termination; audit and complaint handling; independent-contractor status |
| Provider data and student-contact addendum | Provider and Semester | What student data the provider receives; no marketing without consent; retention; security minimums; breach notice to Semester; deletion on request |

Required policies referenced by the above: Acceptable Use, Prohibited Items list, Refund and Dispute policy, Provider Code of Conduct, Review and Rating policy, Safety policy for in-person services.

Product behaviour the terms may not outrun: provider approval workflow, order state, entitlement, dispute record, payout state, moderation case (audit domain table "Marketplace"). Terms are drafted from the built behaviour, not the reverse.

Open questions for counsel: marketplace facilitator, payment-facilitator or agent characterisation; who is merchant of record; sales-tax collection; 1099-style reporting; consumer refund rights that cannot be waived; sanctions screening vendor; whether an institution-approved marketplace differs from an open one; essay-mill and academic-integrity prohibited-listing wording.

## Part 2 — Partner terms

Partners are not providers: they integrate with, distribute, or co-market Semester.

| Partner type | Agreement | Must settle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology partners (SIS/LMS/identity/library/dining/housing vendors) | Integration and data-exchange agreement | Roles and data flows; API terms; credential custody; security minimums; uptime/degraded-mode expectations; change notice; trademark use; who answers the customer; liability split; exit and data return |
| Reseller / channel / consortium | Reseller or referral agreement | Authority limits (no binding Semester to claims); approved-claims list (`PUBLIC-CLAIMS-APPROVAL-REGISTER.md`); data handling; fees; territory; term/termination; compliance with marketing and anti-corruption laws; public-sector procurement rules |
| Employer / career partners | Employer participation terms | Student consent for sharing; no discriminatory targeting; verified-credential rules; permitted use of student data; posting rules and moderation; complaint route |
| Content / publisher partners | Content licence | Licence scope; AI-use rights; attribution; takedown route |
| Research / pilot partners | Evaluation or research agreement | Consent, review-board position (COUNSEL-BRIEF F1), data de-identification, publication rights |
| Alumni / association | Community terms | Directory consent; fundraising-communication rules; institution policy |

## Part 3 — Shared clauses to settle once

Order of precedence; definitions (customer data, student data, derived data, aggregated data); personal-data roles and DPA link; sub-processor notice; security exhibit; incident notice (via the decision workflow); audit rights; insurance; confidentiality; IP and feedback; publicity (no logos/quotes without claim-specific permission); warranties and disclaimers; indemnity; limitation of liability; term, suspension, termination, transition assistance; governing law and disputes; assignment and change of control; force majeure; amendments; notices.

## Release gate

No marketplace or partner term goes to a counterparty until: queue rows Q-11/Q-12 close; payment and tax advice is written; provider governance and dispute operations exist; counsel records permitted use (internal / negotiable / executable).
