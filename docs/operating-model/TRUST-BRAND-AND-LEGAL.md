# Trust, brand protection and legal horizon scanning

Semester holds trust-sensitive student data and shows up at high-stress moments: registration day, a missed deadline,
a hold on an account. Its reputation is part of the product.

**The no-surveillance statement:** Semester does not compute risk scores about students for anyone
else to read. Warnings such as "you can't afford to miss this class" go to the student alone. It does not report a
student's activity to faculty or administrators without that student's knowledge. It does not sell or share
student-level data with sponsors or employers.

## Horizon scanning

One named owner (the privacy owner until a general counsel is hired) monitors the areas below and brings a written
summary to the **quarterly policy review**. That review happens every quarter, whether or not there is a crisis.

| Area | Watch for | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal and state privacy law | New state privacy statutes, student-data laws | `policy.retention`, DPA template |
| FERPA guidance | Department of Education guidance and enforcement letters | Classification matrix, school-official language |
| COPPA and minors | Dual-enrollment and under-13 users | Age gate, consent |
| ADA Title II / web accessibility | Compliance dates and technical standard | Accessibility roadmap |
| State AI laws and education-sector AI policy | Disclosure, impact-assessment, procurement rules | AI governance board agenda |
| Subscription/auto-renewal rules | Click-to-cancel, renewal notices | Student plan billing |
| Email/SMS marketing law | CAN-SPAM, TCPA, consent rules | Notification cadence |
| Employment/opportunity discrimination | Rules on screening for jobs and internships | Career module |
| Financial promotion and lending restrictions | Rules on student-loan and credit marketing | Sponsor principles |
| International privacy and data residency | GDPR, cross-border transfers for international students | Hosting region, DPA |
| Copyright, model training, AI output policy | Licensing of course material; training-data rules | Source locker, provider terms |

Each quarterly summary lists what changed, what Semester has to do about it and by when, and who owns each item. It
goes into `DECISIONS.md`.
