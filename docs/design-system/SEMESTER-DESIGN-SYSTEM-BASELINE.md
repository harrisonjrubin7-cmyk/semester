## 3 · Strengths to preserve

The value/semantic split with no colours in `tokens.css`; the generated export and its snapshot test; the shrinking hex LEDGER in `hex.test.ts` (10 files, each with a reason); per-ground contrast measured on every surface; motion roles zeroed by `prefers-reduced-motion` and `data-calm`; one trust vocabulary (`lib/status.ts`, `lib/source.ts`); the exhaustive screen table; CLAUDE.md's "revert the fix and watch the guard go red" standard.
