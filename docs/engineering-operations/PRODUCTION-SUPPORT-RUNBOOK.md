## Intake and triage

1. Confirm the supported environment, cohort, hours and contact route; never imply support outside the approved scope.
2. Verify identity and tenant before discussing account data. Collect time, route, device/browser, correlation/error code and blocked job—not unnecessary course content, prompts, sensitive attributes, tokens or screenshots of records.
3. Classify severity by harm and blocked outcome. Route security/privacy, accessibility, data-integrity, academic-deadline and official-system ambiguity immediately to the named domain owner.
4. Give a safe workaround, owner and next-update expectation only when staffed and approved. Do not give authoritative academic, legal, medical, financial or institutional advice.
5. Use time-limited support access only when essential, consented/authorized, least-privilege and audited; revoke and review it at closure.
6. Escalate to incident command for P0/P1, repeated cohort impact, cross-tenant risk, data loss, monitoring blind spot or unsafe workaround.
7. Close with resolution, affected scope, communications, evidence, access revocation, root cause/problem link, follow-up owner/date and user confirmation where appropriate.
