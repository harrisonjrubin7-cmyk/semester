## Required operating model

Before activation, publish approved coverage hours and private contact routes; name trained primary and backup for incident command, engineering, security/privacy, accessibility, support/customer communication and customer technical/sponsor roles. Maintain a coverage calendar, handoff, acknowledgement/escalation ladder, access/grant review, communication authority, substitute process and exercise cadence.

P0: active exposure, cross-tenant access, destructive integrity loss or immediate safety/rights harm—contain immediately and activate authorized legal/customer routes. P1: core workflow unavailable, major accessibility barrier or serious integrity/reliability risk—urgent mitigation/rollback and scheduled updates. P2/P3 follow staffed support triage and documented priority. These are internal severity definitions, not promised clocks.
