# Campus announcement templates

Short texts a school can send in its own name to announce a Semester pilot. The school sends them, through its own channels. Semester does not send campus announcements and has no mailing list of your students.

Fill in every `{{placeholder}}` before sending. `announcements.test.ts` holds this page to a fixed list of placeholders, and to one rule: every student-facing template says that taking part is optional and has no effect on grades or standing.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| `{{school_name}}` | The school's name as students know it |
| `{{pilot_name}}` | What the school calls the pilot |
| `{{start_date}}` | The day invitations go out |
| `{{end_date}}` | The day the pilot ends |
| `{{contact_name}}` | A named person students can write to |
| `{{contact_email}}` | That person's address |
| `{{privacy_page}}` | The link to the school's own privacy notice for the pilot |

## Template: invitation to students

Subject: An invitation to try Semester, from {{school_name}}

{{school_name}} is running {{pilot_name}}, a small pilot of Semester, a study planner that keeps your courses, deadlines and study in one place.

You are invited to try it from {{start_date}} to {{end_date}}.

Taking part is optional. Saying no, or stopping part way through, has no effect on your grades, your standing or anything else at {{school_name}}.

Semester runs on your device. It does not submit anything to {{school_name}} for you and does not change your records. You can take your data with you or delete it at any time.

How {{school_name}} handles the pilot is at {{privacy_page}}.

Questions: {{contact_name}}, {{contact_email}}.

## Template: reminder, one week in

Subject: Semester: one week in

A reminder that {{pilot_name}} is running until {{end_date}}. If you have not tried Semester yet, the quick-start guide takes about ten minutes.

Taking part is optional, and not taking part has no effect on your grades or standing.

If something is wrong or confusing, use Say something in the app, or write to {{contact_name}} at {{contact_email}}.

## Template: note to faculty and staff

Subject: {{pilot_name}}: what students may mention

Some students are trying Semester, a study planner, as part of {{pilot_name}} from {{start_date}} to {{end_date}}.

You do not need to do anything. Semester does not contact faculty, submit work or change records. A student may show you a deadline or a reading list in it; the official version is still yours.

If a student says Semester shows something about your course that is wrong, the fix is the syllabus or the official listing, not the app. Questions: {{contact_name}}, {{contact_email}}.

## Template: the pilot is paused

Subject: {{pilot_name}} is paused

{{pilot_name}} is paused while we fix a problem. Your work in Semester is still on your device.

Nothing about your grades or standing is affected. We will write again when it resumes, or by {{end_date}} at the latest.

Questions: {{contact_name}}, {{contact_email}}.
