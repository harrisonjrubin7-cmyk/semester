# Semester: quick start for students

Semester keeps your courses, deadlines and study in one place. It runs on your device. Signing in is optional, and it only adds one thing: the same semester on your phone and your laptop.

This page takes about ten minutes to follow.

## 1. Add your first course

Open **Add a course** and upload a syllabus. Semester reads it and shows you the course, its dates and its readings.

Check every date before you keep it. A syllabus can be wrong or out of date, and Semester can misread one. Untick anything that is wrong.

## 2. Check Today

**Today** shows what is due next. If the next deadline is not the one you expected, open it and fix it.

## 3. Look at your week

**Calendar** shows classes and deadlines by day, by month or for the whole semester. Fix any class time that is wrong.

## 4. Study once

**Study** lets you work through a course with cards, reading, video or slides. Try one short session on the course you added.

## 5. Know what leaves your device

**Privacy and your rights** lists what stays on your device and what leaves it. In short:

- Signed out, your semester stays on your device. Two things can still leave it: an AI question, when you press the button, and map tiles, while the map is open.
- Signed in, what you typed also syncs to your account, so your other device has it.
- Asking the AI sends that question to the AI provider set in Settings: Anthropic unless you or your school chose another, such as OpenAI. It goes only when you press the button.
- There is no third-party analytics in this app.

## 6. Know how to leave

**Take it with you** downloads everything, or sends it to Drive or OneDrive. **Delete my account**, in Settings, empties every table Semester can reach for you.

## 7. When something is wrong

Go to **Settings › About** and use **Say something**. It sends the screen you were on and what you expected. It does not send your courses or notes.

For anything about your courses, advising, health or safety, the **Support** screen shows which campus office to go to. Semester does not replace any of them.

## What Semester will not do

- It does not submit anything to your university for you.
- It does not change your registration, grades or records.
- It does not score you, rank you or predict how you will do.
