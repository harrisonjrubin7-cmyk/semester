# Advisor quick-start guide

Use this guide before advising students in a controlled Semester pilot. It takes about fifteen minutes. Semester is a planning aid; the registrar, degree audit and advisor remain authoritative.

## Before the first appointment

1. Read the [known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md) and the advisor section of the [first-day checklists](FIRST-DAY-CHECKLISTS.md).
2. Open the student-facing degree and planning views in a test account.
3. Identify the official degree-audit, registration and advising links students must use for final decisions.
4. Confirm the named campus contacts for registration, financial aid, accessibility and urgent support.
5. Agree with the pilot lead which student-shared view, if any, advisors may review.

## In an appointment

- Ask the student to open the plan they chose to share. Do not ask for their device, password or unrelated notes.
- Check requirements and dates against the official system before advising a consequential change.
- Explain any mismatch in plain language and record which source must be corrected.
- Leave the final action with the student unless the institution’s normal process assigns it to the advisor.

## Boundaries

Semester does not decide eligibility, register a student, change an academic record or predict whether a student is at risk. Product activity is not a proxy for effort, wellbeing or academic standing.

## Escalation

Use the campus route for policy or record questions and the Semester feedback route for product defects. A barrier involving assistive technology follows the accessibility route in the [FAQ](FAQ.md). An outage or stale integration should be checked against the public status page and the official source.
