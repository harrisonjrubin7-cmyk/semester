# Known limitations

What Semester does not do yet, or does only partly, stated plainly so nobody finds out the hard way. This page is part of the launch package. It is reviewed before each cohort starts.

## Your information

- **Dates come from syllabi.** Semester reads what it is given. A syllabus can be wrong, out of date or misread, so check each date before you keep it.
- **The degree view is a planning aid, not your official degree audit.** Your school's audit, and your advisor, decide what counts.
- **Files attached to notes stay on the device they were added on.** They do not sync, so a lecture deck attached on a laptop is not on your phone.

## Accounts and sign-in

- **Signing in with your university account (SSO) is not switched on** unless your school has told you it is. Pilots start with invitations.
- **Syncing needs an account.** Without one, your semester lives on one device.

## The AI features

- **There is a monthly limit on AI requests per account.** When you reach it, the AI features stop until the next month; everything else keeps working.
- **AI answers can be wrong.** Check them against your course materials.

## Connections to school systems

- **Semester is not connected to your school's student information system or learning management system** unless your school has turned a connection on. Those connections are off by default.
- **Semester never submits anything to your school.** No registration, form or assignment goes anywhere from the app.

## Getting help

- **Asking Semester support from inside the app is not switched on yet.** Use Say something in Settings › About, which reaches the same people.
- **The status page does not send notifications.** Open `/status.html` to see whether Semester is up; it checks from your browser each time. The pilot contact your school named will also tell you about an outage.
