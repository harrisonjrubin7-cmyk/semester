# Administrator operations guide

This guide is the day-to-day companion to the activation runbook for a controlled institutional pilot. It does not activate a tenant or replace the institution’s change, privacy, security or records procedures.

## Daily review

1. Check the public status page and any open incident notice.
2. Review source freshness and correct expired or ownerless content before students rely on it.
3. Route support, privacy and accessibility reports to the named people agreed for the pilot.
4. Confirm no feature, integration or audience has expanded beyond the approved scope.

## Before publishing content

- Use the [content readiness register](CONTENT-READINESS-REGISTER.md).
- Name an individual owner, an official source, a review date and a correction route.
- Publish estimates only where the register permits them and label them as estimates.
- Never publish unverified emergency contacts, policy text or accommodation routes.

## Change and incident handling

- Freeze planned maintenance during the institution’s protected registration and finals windows.
- For a customer-impacting incident, name what happened, who is affected, what to do, the next update and where to get help.
- Keep the institution’s named contact in the communication path.
- Use the approved pause or rollback path when the acceptance criteria fail; do not improvise changes in production.

## Access and privacy

Grant only the role required for the task. Do not use support access without the student’s explicit, time-limited approval. Never export private student content for training, engagement scoring or a pilot presentation.

## Weekly pilot review

Review support volume, accessibility reports, source corrections, open incidents and the measures agreed in the [pilot measures and baseline worksheet](PILOT-MEASURES-BASELINE-WORKSHEET.md). Record decisions and owners; do not turn product activity into a hidden student score.

## End of pilot

Choose expand, extend or stop against the written criteria. Export or delete pilot data according to the agreement, revoke temporary access and integrations, and preserve only the evidence the approved retention schedule requires.
