# Frequently asked questions

Answers for students and staff in a Semester pilot. Each answer says what is true today. The [known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md) page lists what is not finished.

## For students

### Do I have to use Semester?

No. Taking part in a pilot is optional, and not taking part has no effect on your grades or standing.

### Do I need an account?

No. Semester runs on your device. An account adds one thing: the same semester on your phone and your laptop.

### What leaves my device?

Signed out, your semester stays on your device. Two things can still leave it: an AI question, when you press the button, and map tiles, while the map is open. Signed in, what you typed also syncs to your account. An AI question goes to the AI provider set in Settings: Anthropic unless you or your school chose another, such as OpenAI. The Privacy screen lists every case.

### Does my school see my work in Semester?

Not by default. Your rows are protected by row-level security keyed to your account. The Privacy screen names each exception, such as a help request you choose to send to a campus office.

### Is my work used to train AI?

No. The Privacy screen says so, and nothing in Semester is used to train anything.

### Can Semester get my deadlines wrong?

Yes. It reads your syllabus, and a syllabus can be wrong or misread. Check each date before you keep it. Your instructor's version is the official one.

### How do I leave?

**Take it with you** downloads everything. **Delete my account**, in Settings, empties every table Semester can reach for you.

### Something is wrong. Who do I tell?

Use **Say something** in Settings › About. For anything about your courses, advising, health or safety, the Support screen shows which campus office to go to.

## For faculty and staff

### Does Semester contact me or submit anything?

No. It does not submit work, contact faculty or change records.

### Can I see what a student does in Semester?

No, unless the student chooses to share something with you, and the Privacy screen tells them exactly what that shares.

### A student shows me a wrong date. What do I do?

Fix it at the source: the syllabus or the official listing. Semester shows what it was given.
