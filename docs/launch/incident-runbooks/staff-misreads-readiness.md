# Runbook: staff treat readiness as official enrollment (RSK-003)

Trigger: a staff member, advisor or student is recorded or heard saying a readiness result means the student is enrolled or registered, or a staff action records an enrollment-like outcome from readiness.

1. **Correct the record.** Tell the affected person in writing that a readiness result is not enrollment and that the institution's registration system is authoritative.
2. **Correct any communication** that used the wrong wording (`receipt_language` control).
3. **Retrain.** Re-run the registrar onboarding module (`communications/staff-onboarding-registrar.md`) for the person.
4. **Review screen copy** if the wording caused the confusion; route to registrar_product_owner.

Do not change institutional records to match a misreading. Do not add an enrollment field to make it "consistent".
