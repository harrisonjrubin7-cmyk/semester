# What is Semester? A 60-second script

This is the script for a short video. It is not a video yet: a recording counts only when it has captions and a transcript, and this page is that transcript.

About 150 words, read at a normal pace.

## Script

Semester is one place for your semester.

Upload a syllabus, and it finds the deadlines and readings. You check them before you keep anything, because a syllabus can be wrong.

Today shows what is due next. The calendar shows your week. Study gives you cards, reading and slides for each course.

It runs on your device. You do not need an account. If you sign in, your phone and your laptop show the same semester.

When you ask the AI a question, it goes to the AI provider in your settings, Anthropic unless you chose another, and only when you press the button. There is no third-party analytics.

Semester does not submit anything to your school, change your records or score you.

You can take everything with you, or delete it, at any time.

That is Semester.

## Before it is recorded

- Captions, checked by a person rather than only generated.
- A transcript beside the video: this page.
- No music under speech, so the words stay clear.
- Every claim above checked against the Privacy screen on the day of recording.
