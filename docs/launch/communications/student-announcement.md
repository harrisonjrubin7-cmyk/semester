**Subject:** Registration readiness pilot

You can now check whether Semester has the information needed to help you plan for registration.

A readiness result is not enrollment. Your institution's registration system remains the official record of your classes and registration status.

Each item shows where it came from:

- **Institution verified:** confirmed by your institution
- **Connected:** imported from a connected system
- **Student entered:** added by you
- **Estimated:** a planning calculation, not an official record
- **Needs review:** an office may need to check something

If a source is out of date, Semester will say so and will not present a final result. You can still see what it did show, and when it was last updated.

If something looks wrong, tell us. Report a discrepancy from the readiness page with the item name and what you expected. Do not send your student ID or grades in the message.
