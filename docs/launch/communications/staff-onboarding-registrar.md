# Registrar onboarding: registration readiness pilot

Audience: registrar staff. Duration: 15 minutes. Status: draft, **not approved**.

## Job to be done

Review students' readiness cases, route blockers to the right office, and request overrides that someone else decides.

## What you can and cannot see

- You see the cases in your queue and the source evidence attached to each.
- You do not see student records outside your assigned scope.
- You cannot edit a SIS value or mark a student enrolled.

## Objectives

1. Read source and freshness labels.
2. Distinguish a readiness state from enrollment.
3. Route a blocker without editing an institutional record.
4. Request an override without self-approving it.
5. Escalate stale-source or reconciliation issues.

## Practice scenario

A student has a current time ticket but stale prerequisite data.

Expected actions:

- Acknowledge the stale source.
- Request a refresh or route the case for review.
- Avoid any "ready" or "enrolled" claim.
- Leave an auditable note.

## Prohibited actions

- Do not mark a student enrolled.
- Do not replace a SIS value manually.
- Do not expose unrelated student records.

Escalation: `docs/launch/support-handoff.md`. Misreading runbook: `docs/launch/incident-runbooks/staff-misreads-readiness.md`.
