# First-day checklists

One list per role, for the first day someone uses Semester. Each list is at most seven steps and twenty minutes. A list nobody finishes teaches people that checklists are optional.

These lists are `FIRST_DAY` in `app/src/lib/launch/checklists.ts`, where every step names the screen it happens on. `checklists.test.ts` fails when this page and the code disagree.

## Students

About 19 minutes.

1. Add one course from its syllabus, and check the dates it found before keeping them. (5 min)
2. Open Today and check the next deadline is the one you expected. (1 min)
3. Look at the week in the Calendar, and fix any class time that is wrong. (3 min)
4. Try one study session on the course you added. (5 min)
5. Read what leaves your device and what stays on it. (3 min)
6. Know where Take it with you is, so leaving is always one step. (1 min)
7. Know where Settings › About is, to say something is wrong from inside the app. (1 min)

## Faculty

About 12 minutes.

1. Read the University screen: every campus service and what the app can honestly do for each. (3 min)
2. Check how one of your syllabi imports, so you know what students see. (5 min)
3. Read what Semester can and cannot show you about a student. (3 min)
4. Know where Settings › About is, to report a problem from inside the app. (1 min)

## Advisors

About 14 minutes.

1. Skim How this works, so you can answer “where is that?” from students. (3 min)
2. See the degree view a student sees, and how it differs from the official audit. (5 min)
3. Know the Support map a student is shown, so a referral matches it. (3 min)
4. Read what a student has to share before you can see it. (3 min)

## University administrators

About 18 minutes.

1. Read the University screen as a student sees it, and note any service your school describes differently. (3 min)
2. Name an owner for each item in the content readiness register; the sources can follow. (10 min)
3. Read the Privacy screen as a student would, and note anything your school says differently. (3 min)
4. Confirm a student can take their data with them before you invite any. (2 min)
