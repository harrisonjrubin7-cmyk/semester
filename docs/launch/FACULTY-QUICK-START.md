# Faculty quick-start guide

Use this guide before students in a controlled pilot begin using Semester. It takes about fifteen minutes. Semester does not replace the syllabus, learning management system, gradebook, accommodation process or instructor communication.

## Before the pilot

1. Read the [known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md) and the faculty section of the [first-day checklists](FIRST-DAY-CHECKLISTS.md).
2. Give the pilot lead the current syllabus and the official course link. Do not send grades, accommodation details or private student records.
3. Import the syllabus into a test account and compare every date, weighting and policy with the source document.
4. Record the named course contact who can correct the official source when a student reports a mismatch.
5. Confirm how students should reach the campus help desk and accessibility office.

## What students may ask

- “Which date is official?” The syllabus or learning system is authoritative. A student should correct Semester only after checking that source.
- “Can you see my plan or study history?” Not unless the student explicitly shares a supported view. The pilot does not create hidden faculty access.
- “Can Semester submit this?” No. Students submit work in the institution’s official system.
- “Can this affect my grade?” Participation in the pilot is optional and does not affect grades or standing.

## When something is wrong

Ask the student to preserve their work, note the page and time, and use the in-product feedback route. For a barrier involving keyboard, zoom, screen reader or switch access, use the accessibility route in the [FAQ](FAQ.md). For an outage, check the public status page before asking the student to repeat work.

## End-of-pilot review

Bring examples of source mismatches, workflow friction and accessibility barriers to the pilot review. Do not export student notes, infer engagement or rank students from product activity.
