## 1. The strategy in one page

**Thesis.** Every part of a student's educational life works in Semester from day one: natively when necessary, connected when available, governed everywhere, supported operationally. Commercially: **Semester is the governed layer between a student and the institution's fragmented systems.** The student adopts it because it removes their own friction; the institution pays for governance, rollout and cohort-level readiness.

**Where we stand (facts).** A product blueprint of unusual breadth (152 entries under `app/src/screens`, 1,219 test files, 171 migrations) and almost none of the evidence an institution needs to say yes: no customer, no revenue, no confirmed legal entity, no independent security or accessibility review, no operated recovery, no staffed support. Five of twelve council seats are vacant and the founder holds or acts in six of the seven held. Paid and enterprise motions are RED. Design-partner discovery is GREEN. Invitation-only validation is YELLOW.

**So the first problem is trust and proof, not growth.** The three years are sequenced around closing it.

| Year | Name | Outcome (measurable) | Capital state (assumptions) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** to 2027-09-30 | Earn the right to be trusted | Gates G0 to G2 closed; one design-partner charter signed by 2027-06-30; first cohort of 50 to 200 live by 2027-09-15 | About $0.25M to $0.55M spent; pre-seed or lean path by 2027-05-31 |
| **2** to 2028-09-30 | Prove it repeats | First cohort closes out against its baseline by 2028-03-31; first annual agreement effective 2028-07-01; 3 cohorts across 2 units | Net need about $0.8M to $1.6M; seed by 2028-06-30 on evidence |
| **3** to 2029-09-30 | Make it a business | 6 agreements (floor 4, stretch 12) across 2 or more institutions; first renewals at 85% or better; repeatability pack; broad-sale decision by 2029-06-30 | Net need about $0.5M to $3.0M; cumulative $1.55M to $5.15M; break-even is **not** reached in the horizon |

**How we win.** Neutral across incumbents' systems; governed and accessible by default; implemented by method rather than custom code; honest about what is live. **The moat is evidence that has been operated and independently reviewed** ([`MOAT-PLAN.md`](MOAT-PLAN.md)); feature count, model access and data volume are not moats.

**Standing decisions this honours.** Depth at Vanderbilt before breadth (`DECISIONS.md` §1). No legal conclusion without counsel. No claim outside the claims register. No university-administered money before the written IP determination (proposed as a standing rule, FD-2026-003, because [`IP.md`](../../IP.md) shows it is the one exposure that can still be made worse).

**What we will not do:** claim replacement of a system of record ahead of the ladder; score individual students; monetise student data or attention; build a second institution's adapters before the reopening conditions fire; call anything live, secure, accessible or compliant without its evidence.

**The seven decisions that matter in the next 90 days** ([`FOUNDER-DECISION-LOG.md`](FOUNDER-DECISION-LOG.md)): adopt the strategy and claim ceiling (2026-10-18); adopt the funding rule (2026-10-11); form the advisory board (2026-11-15); confirm the entity path (2026-11-15); approve the north star and Year 1 targets (2026-11-15); reaffirm Vanderbilt-first with tripwires (2026-11-30); commission the validation spend (2026-12-15).

**The three biggest risks** ([`RISK-REGISTER.md`](RISK-REGISTER.md)): founder capacity and key-person dependence (SR-006, 25); the entity and IP chain (SR-016, 20); dependence on one institution saying yes (SR-032, 20).

## 3. How the labels work

Every statement carries one: **FACT** (cites a path that exists), **DECISION** (already made and written down), **HYPOTHESIS** (believed, untested, with the test named), **ASSUMPTION `A-nn`** (numbered below), **COUNSEL** (a matter for qualified human counsel; this package draws no legal, tax, accounting, financing or insurance conclusion).

## 7. Verification

`app/src/lib/ops/strategy.test.ts` holds the package to the tree. It fails when:

- a file of the package is missing, or a relative link in it does not resolve;
- an assumption (`A-nn`), risk (`SR-nnn`), decision (`FD-2026-nnn`), gate (`G0` to `G4`), claim level (`C0` to `C4`) or replacement rung (`R0` to `R5`) is cited but not defined, or a launch or company risk (`FR-nnn`, `CR-nnn`) is cited but absent from its register;

- the scorecard names a first-year measure that does not exist, omits one of the 23, marks a first-year measure as new, or leaves a new metric unmarked;
- a deadline in a milestone table, a risk "due" cell or a decision "decide by" cell is not a real ISO date on or after the evidence date;
- any decision-log entry is anything other than PROPOSED, or has fewer than two options, or an index date that disagrees with its entry;
- the files disagree about the evidence date or commit; or
- the operating-system register still lists company strategy as missing, or points somewhere other than this package.

Each guard was run against a deliberately broken copy of the package to confirm it goes red (results in the pull request description).

## 8. Implications

**Accessibility.** The package is plain Markdown: a single heading hierarchy per file, tables with header rows, no colour-only meaning (scorecard states are words, not colours), no images. It changes no screen. Strategy-level: scorecard S7 and P5 hold the product to unaided assistive-technology success and zero open blockers at each gate; the accessibility advisor and a compensated tester panel are Year 1 deliverables; accessibility cost is in the Year 1 budget.

**Security and privacy.** No code, secret, student data or customer data is added or touched. The package constrains future work: cohort-only metrics with a minimum cell of 10 and never per student; the north star's mute-rate guardrail; new analytics marks only through D-005 with their migration; benchmark governance written before any collection; advisors work from synthetic data under confidentiality terms; the funding rule protects the ownership chain; legal, privacy and tax conclusions are routed to counsel.

**Operational and runbook.** This package gives the quarterly "one-page strategy memo" in `docs/operating-model/OPERATING-RHYTHM.md` a home and a refresh rule. New standing routines it implies, each with an owner and a start date: weekly P9 burn-down review (from 2026-10-18); monthly scorecard review and claims audit (from 2026-11-30); quarterly strategy refresh (first 2027-01-15); decision-log review weekly. Runbooks to write next (not written here, because each needs the owner who will run it): advisory-board charter (2026-11-15), discovery-log template (2026-11-15), claims-audit procedure (2026-11-30), and a gate-review checklist per gate (before each gate date). No existing runbook, migration or system behaviour changes.
