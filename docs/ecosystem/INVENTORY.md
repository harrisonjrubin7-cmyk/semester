## How to read a status

`REPO-CROSSWALK.md`'s ladder, with the coverage programme's word beside it:

| Ladder | Programme word | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| specified | specified | a document or the prototype describes it; nothing in the repository does it |
| schema-present | in progress | its tables exist, with no screen or module |
| code-present | in progress | a screen or library module exists in source |
| connected | implemented | the screen reaches real persistence or a server command, end to end |
| tested | verified | connected, plus an automated test of the behaviour |
| deployed | deployed | its schema is in the applied listing **and** the app build was seen running |
| operational | deployed | monitoring, ownership, support and recovery exist for it |

Nothing reaches `deployed` in this pass: the schema listing is verified (below) but the production app was not probed from this session, and `deployed.json` says so. A green build is not a deployment.

## 1. Semester operating systems (confirmed)

`handoff/PHASES.md`, "One super OS, three layers", plus the company layer. These are product operating systems, not device operating systems.

| OS | Purpose | Prototype workspaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student / Education OS | one identity and one lifecycle record (prospect → applicant → student → alumni); the student's daily loop, study, registration readiness, money, community, career | public, applicant, student, family, alumni, success, clubs, social, studio |
| Institution OS | faculty and staff: teaching, advising, registrar, finance, support, admissions, career hub, campus services, trust console | faculty, ta, advisor, institution, admissions, career, campus, finops, support, trust |
| Business OS | campus finance and leadership: aggregates only, Banner Finance stays the ledger of record | insights, executive |
| Semester company and partners | runs Semester itself: company, platform operations, marketplace moderation, employers; the programme's own evidence register | company, operations, marketplace, employer, os |

Institutional systems of record (SIS, LMS, campus card, housing, Banner Finance) stay authoritative behind each connection. Semester centralises the experience and the workflows, not the systems of record.
