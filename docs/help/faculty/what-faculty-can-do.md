# What faculty can do

> **Type:** explanation · **Audience:** faculty · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

This page explains what an instructor can do in Semester today without an institution, and what needs the school; stop reading if your school has already configured the faculty tools for you.

**Status:** PARTIAL. The faculty course tools exist in the code and are off by default. As an individual you get a personal workspace. You cannot publish to students, see a roster or enter grades.

## What you can do on your own

1. Open **Settings**, then **Courses**. Under **You are here as**, pick **Teaching**.
2. The app describes the role this way: build a course from your own syllabus, keep the term's dates, and write the handout, the slides and the practice paper.
3. Use the same tools a student has: **Add a course**, **Write a document**, **Make a deck**, **Practice paper** and **Sheet or table**.

Everything you make stays on your device. It is yours until you export it as a file.

## What changes when you pick Teaching

- The words the app uses change.
- Screens about being a student are hidden: **The degree**, **Exam runway**, **Catching up**, **Group work**, **Meal plan**, **Housing**, **Registration**, **Classmates**, **Activities**, **Money**, **Applications** and **Launchpad**. The app lists them and says nothing is deleted.
- You are shown "Build course materials, assignments, lessons and feedback drafts now."

## What needs your school

The app states the limit in plain words. Publishing, rosters and official grading "require an institution-assigned course role and an approved learning-system connection." Choosing the Teaching role does not grant that access.

A faculty course studio is built in the code. It lets an instructor publish rules and guidance for a course, and it shows a course's published AI rule to students before the assistant answers. It appears only for an account the school has made faculty on a course, and only when the school has turned it on. It is off by default.

## What students see today

Without that studio on, a student records the course's AI rule from the syllabus on their own copy of the course. It is recorded, not enforced. Nothing in Semester sends it to an instructor.

## What an institution must do

A school turns on faculty tools, assigns course roles and connects its learning system. The institution guides cover those steps: [institution guides](../../guides/institution/). For design and rollout notes, see [Faculty enablement](../../FACULTY-ENABLEMENT.md), [Faculty course studio design](../../FACULTY-COURSE-STUDIO-DESIGN.md) and the [faculty quick start](../../launch/FACULTY-QUICK-START.md).

## Where the data goes

Your personal workspace is saved on your device and syncs only if you sign in. Nothing you make there is sent to students or to your school. See [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](../students/back-up-and-sync.md).
