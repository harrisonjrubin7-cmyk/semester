# What families can see

> **Type:** explanation · **Audience:** families, students · **Owner:** `privacy` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

This page explains what a parent, guardian or other supporter can do in Semester today; stop reading if you are a student looking for how to share, which is on [Share with family](../students/share-with-family.md).

**Status:** PARTIAL. There is no family account, no guardian login and no family dashboard. A supporter sees only what one student chooses to share, by code.

## What a family member can do today

- Use the app for yourself. Anyone can open Semester and use it as a student does. It needs no account.
- Receive what a student shares. The student makes an eight-character code on their **Family** screen and hands it to you. You sign in, go to **Family**, find **Were you given a code?**, enter the characters and choose **Accept**. Then **Open what is shared with me** shows the items the student chose, read only.
- Each time you open it, the student can see that you did. Every share ends at the date the student set, and the student can stop it sooner.

## What you cannot see

- Grades, messages, study activity, attendance, locations, health records or advisor notes. The screen says none of these are ever pulled from the student's account.
- Anything the student did not choose to put in the plan. Each plan is separate, and one person's items never appear in another person's view.
- Your student's university records. Those need an authorization from the university, scoped to the record and the action.
- Payments. The screen says they are not offered.

## The "Authorized family" setting

Under **Settings**, **Courses**, the list **You are here as** includes **Authorized family** and **Parent or payer**. Picking one changes the words the app uses and hides screens that are about being a student, such as **The degree** and **Meal plan**. The app states that choosing a role does not grant access to any student's records. Nothing is deleted when you switch back.

## What a school must do for more

A school decides whether families get access to its own records, for example billing details. The app says this needs "a current authorized-payer relationship granted by the institution." Semester gives no such access to an individual. The steps for an institution are in the institution guides: [institution guides](../../guides/institution/).

## Where the data goes

A student's shared plan is copied to the account service for the person it is shared with, and the student can read a log of when it was opened. For the design and the limits, read [Supporter and family privacy model](../../SUPPORTER-FAMILY-PRIVACY-MODEL.md). Students should read [Share with family](../students/share-with-family.md).
