# Control what the AI sees

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `privacy` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to see exactly what the assistant receives, and the controls you have over it; stop reading if you never press an AI button.

## What you need first

- Nothing. These controls are screens you can read without a key.
- Know the rule: AI text is sent when you press the button and not before.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Semester Intelligence**.
2. Read **What it can see**. It lists what travels with a question.
3. Read **What it can do**. It lists what the assistant may offer to change, and what it cannot touch.
4. Under **What it knows about you**, write a line the assistant should always know, such as a formatting preference, and choose **Add**. Delete a line to remove it.
5. When you make a study guide, choose the text yourself. In **Create study guide**, under **1. Choose source material**, tick only the sources you want and use **Preview** to read each one.
6. To clear the notes the Guide remembers, open **Alerts**. Under **Assistant memory**, read the remembered notes and choose **Clear assistant memory**.
7. To read what the whole app sends and where, open **Privacy and your rights** and read **What the AI features send, and to whom**.

## What you will see

- The assistant always gets your course codes and today's date, and what the screen you are on says it is showing.
- For a question about your own records, it also gets the deadlines in the window you asked about, and the grades, attendance and unit names for the courses you named. Cards travel only when you name one course and ask about its material.
- It never gets, whatever is asked: your notes, your drafts, your files, anyone in People or Letters, or any key or token.
- **Find sources** on the **Sources** screen is the one place anything reaches past Anthropic: your question becomes a web search query, with the course name.
- The assistant offers actions such as ticking off a deadline or adding an action. Nothing happens until you tap it, and each change has an Undo. It cannot delete anything, and it cannot touch a grade, a dropped score, the grading scale or a date that came from a syllabus.
- Voice questions are recognised by your browser, not by Semester. In Chrome that means the audio goes to Google.

## If it does not work

- You do not want the assistant to know a line: delete it under **What it knows about you**.
- You want no AI at all: do not save a key or press an AI button. Courses added by hand, Today, the calendar, cards and your own actions work without one.
- A course says its syllabus forbids AI: record that on **Edit the course** under **What the syllabus says about AI**. It is recorded, not enforced, and the drafting tool will not write for a course unless the setting says it may.
- An AI answer is wrong. Check it against your course materials.

## Where your data goes

Text you send goes to Anthropic, whose own terms govern it there. The privacy screen states that the app has no third-party analytics. Read **Privacy and your rights**, and [Action explainability and student control](../../ACTION-EXPLAINABILITY-AND-STUDENT-CONTROL.md). See also [Export or delete your data](export-or-delete-your-data.md).
