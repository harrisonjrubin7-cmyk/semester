# What works without a connection

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to know what you can still do on a plane, in a basement library or when the signal drops; stop reading if you are always online.

## What you need first

- The app opened at least once while you were online, so the browser has stored it ([Install the app](install-the-app.md)).

## Steps

1. Open the app while you are online and visit the screens you will need, such as **Today**, **Calendar**, **Courses** and **Study**.
2. When you lose the connection, keep working. The app shows a line: **Everything here is saved on this device. Anything that needs the internet waits for the connection.**
3. Reload the page if you need to. The app opens from the copy your browser stored.
4. For audio, play an episode while online first. The app keeps what you have played, not everything.
5. When the connection returns, signed-in changes sync by themselves.

## What you will see

| Works offline | Needs a connection |
| --- | --- |
| Opening the app and its screens | Signing in or creating an account |
| Today, the calendar, your courses and deadlines | Syncing to another device |
| Studying with cards, quiz, read and cram | Reading a syllabus, asking the assistant, making a guide |
| Adding actions, notes and events | Fetching a calendar link |
| Editing a course | Map tiles on **Getting there** |
| Lessons and decks you have already played | Deleting a signed-in account on the server |

- If you are signed in and offline, the status reads **Offline** or **Offline · changes waiting**. Your changes are saved on the device and go up when the connection is back.
- Signed out, nothing waits. Everything stays on the device.

## If it does not work

- The app does not open offline: it was not stored. Reconnect, open it, and try again.
- A screen asks for the network: it is one from the right-hand column of the table. It waits for the connection.
- **Two versions need your review** appears after you changed the same thing on two devices. Open the review and choose which version to keep. Both are saved until you do.
- Audio will not play offline: you did not play it while online first.

## Where your data goes

Offline, everything you change stays on the device. Online and signed in, it syncs ([Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md)).
