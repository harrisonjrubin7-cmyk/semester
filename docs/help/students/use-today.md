# Use Today

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to read the Today screen and act on its next step; stop reading if you want the whole term, which is on the calendar.

## What you need first

- At least one course with deadlines, or an action you added yourself ([Add your own actions, notes and files](add-your-own-actions.md)).

## Steps

1. Open **Today**. The shortcut is `T`.
2. Use the four tabs at the top: **Today**, **Hours**, **This week** and **Done**.
3. Under **What would help most today?**, pick one of **Build my semester**, **Understand my degree path**, **Prepare for registration** or **Organize my week**. You can change the pick any time.
4. Read **Next best step**. It names one action and where it came from.
5. Act on it:
   - **Start** opens the action.
   - **Done** ticks it off.
   - **Snooze until tomorrow** hides it for a day. **More snooze times** offers other lengths.
   - **Not relevant** hides it because it does not apply to you.
   - **Something is wrong** or **Report incorrect information** tells the app the item is mistaken.
   - **Why this?** says why the item is on top.
6. Under **Coming up**, choose **See full plan** to open the calendar.

## What you will see

- Each item carries a source badge. **Student entered** means you typed it, and Semester has not checked it with your institution. **Estimated** means Semester worked it out and it is not an official figure.
- Finished items move to **Done**, so the day leaves a trace.
- With the sample still on screen and no courses of your own, the next step reads **Start your semester** and asks you to add a course.
- With no courses at all, Today reads "Nothing on Today yet" and offers **Add your first course**.

## If it does not work

- Today is empty or shows old items: the sample courses do not drive Today until you choose **These are mine**. Add your own course ([Add your syllabus and get your deadlines](add-your-syllabus.md)).
- A date on Today is wrong: fix it at the source ([Check a date and fix it](check-and-fix-a-date.md)).
- An item you snoozed or dismissed shows on another device: those choices stay on the device where you made them.
- See [When something is missing or wrong](when-something-is-missing.md).

## Where your data goes

What you tick off is saved on your device. If you sign in, it syncs with the rest of your semester. Snooze and dismiss choices stay on the device ([Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md)).
