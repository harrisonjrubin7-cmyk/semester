# Add your own actions, notes and files

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to keep things that are not in any syllabus, such as an appointment, a note or a file; stop reading if you want to add a course.

## What you need first

- Nothing. This works with no account and no AI key.

## Steps

To add an action:

1. Open **Personal**. The shortcut is `M`.
2. Choose the **Actions** tab, then **+ New action**.
3. Fill in **Action**, **Date** and **Time**.
4. Pick **Personal** or one of your courses.
5. Under **How often**, pick **Once**, **Every day**, **Every weekday**, **Every week**, **Every other week** or **Every month**.
6. Choose **Add action**.

To add something in one line:

1. Press `Q`, or choose **Add something in one line** in the header.
2. Type a line such as "dentist tuesday 9am". Any part can be left out.
3. Check what the app understood. It shows this before it writes anything.
4. Or keep it as a different kind of item: **Action, no date**, **Course note**, **Source**, **Study session**, **Question for advisor** or **Idea**.

To keep notes and files, use the **Notes** and **Files** tabs on **Personal**. The **Events** tab holds appointments.

## What you will see

- Your actions appear on Today beside coursework, without pretending to be coursework.
- With nothing yet, the screen says "Nothing of your own yet."

## If it does not work

- The line was misread: cancel at the check step and type it again with a clearer date.
- A note or file is missing after you signed in on another device: attached files stay on the device that has them.
- See [When something is missing or wrong](when-something-is-missing.md).

## Where your data goes

Actions, events and notes are saved on your device and sync if you sign in. Files you attach stay on the device and are not synced. Your notes are never sent with a question to the assistant ([Control what the AI sees](control-what-the-ai-sees.md)).
