# Study from your guide

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to study with cards, a quiz or a cram sheet, and to make a guide from chosen text; stop reading if you want to add readings, which has its own page.

## What you need first

- A course with material. The sample courses come with cards. For your own course, add readings first ([Add a reading to a course](add-a-reading.md)).
- For **Create study guide**, an AI key ([Set up the assistant](set-up-the-assistant.md)).

## Steps

To study a course:

1. Open **Study**. The shortcut is `S`.
2. Find your course under **Your courses**. Each course lists how many units and cards it has.
3. Choose a way in: **Cards**, **Field guide** or **Watch**. The link that ends "ways", such as "All 11 ways", opens the rest: **Slides**, **Doc**, **Quiz**, **Figures**, **Cases**, **Cram** and **Listen**.
4. On the cards page, choose **Drill all** to go through every card, or pick a unit. The page names your **Weakest unit**.
5. Tap a card to flip it. Try each answer before you flip.

To make your own guide:

1. On **Study**, choose **Create study guide**.
2. Under **1. Choose source material**, tick the text you want used. Choose **Preview** to read a source first.
3. Under **2. Choose your formats**, pick one or more, such as **Flashcards** or **Practice Quiz**.
4. Choose **Create study guide**.

## What you will see

- A new course starts with every card marked **Unseen**. The page says so: nothing here knows what you know until you drill some.
- The **Exam radar** counts the days to your next exam.
- **Only checked text is sent. Personal notes are excluded until you select them.** The guide maker states this above the source list.

## If it does not work

- The guide maker says it needs Claude: set up the assistant. Cards, quiz and the other study modes on existing material work without a key.
- Cards are thin: add the readings. A thin reading returns few cards by design, because everything must come from the text.
- Wrong card content: AI-assisted material can be wrong. Check it against your course materials.
- See [When something is missing or wrong](when-something-is-missing.md).

## Where your data goes

Your answers and card history are saved on your device and sync if you sign in. Text you select for a generated guide is sent to Anthropic when you press the button ([Control what the AI sees](control-what-the-ai-sees.md)).
