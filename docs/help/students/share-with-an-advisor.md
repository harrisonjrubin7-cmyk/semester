# Share with an advisor

> **Type:** explanation · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

This page explains what you can do today to prepare for an advising appointment and why you cannot send anything to an advisor from the app; stop reading if you only need to prepare questions.

**Status:** PARTIAL. The advisor meeting mode exists in the code and is off by default. This page describes the app with it off. The deployed setting is a build variable that this page cannot see.

## What you can do today

1. On **Today**, choose **Prepare for advising**. It opens your degree page, **The degree**, which lists what is left of a major or a minor. It is a planning aid, not your official degree audit.
2. Press `Q` for **Add something in one line**, and keep the line as **Question for advisor**. The question is saved with your semester.
3. Write the questions down in **Personal** under **Notes**, or in a document ([Add your own actions, notes and files](add-your-own-actions.md)).
4. Take what you wrote to the appointment yourself. You can print it or send it from your own email.

## What is not available

With the mode off, these are not available. If your build has it on, **The degree** shows an **Advisor meeting** tab, and this page does not cover it.

- Sending a plan, a note or a question to an advisor from the app.
- An advisor reading your record. Semester is not connected to your school's systems unless your school has turned a connection on, and those connections are off by default.
- Booking or recording the appointment with your school.

The design for the meeting mode is in [Advisor meeting mode](../../ADVISOR-MEETING-MODE.md). Your school, not Semester, decides what counts toward your degree.

## Where your data goes

Notes and questions stay on your device and sync if you sign in. Nothing reaches an advisor. See [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md).
