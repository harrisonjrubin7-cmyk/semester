# Use the calendar

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to see classes and deadlines by day, month or term and to add something of your own; stop reading if you only need the next step, which is on Today.

## What you need first

- At least one course ([Add your syllabus and get your deadlines](add-your-syllabus.md)). The sample courses fill the calendar until you remove them.

## Steps

1. Open **Calendar**. The shortcut is `K`.
2. Pick a view: **Day**, **3 days**, **Month** or **Semester**.
3. Narrow what you see with **All**, **Classes**, **Due** or **Campus**.
4. In **Month**, use **Previous month** and **Next month** to move. Select a day to read its list under the grid.
5. To add something on a day with nothing on it, follow the hint under the grid: **Double-tap it to put something there.**
6. To add something from any screen, press `Q` for **Add something in one line**. Type a line such as "core quiz tomorrow", then check what the app understood before you keep it.
7. To see another calendar's dates here, add it under [Bring in a calendar](bring-in-a-calendar.md).

## What you will see

- Each day in the month grid has a count of what is on it. Your classes repeat weekly, and deadlines show with their course.
- **Semester** gives one list of the whole term, with a bar for how loaded each week is. Exams are marked.
- Calendars you bring in are kept apart from your classes and labelled.

## If it does not work

- A class time is wrong: fix it on **Edit the course** ([Check a date and fix it](check-and-fix-a-date.md)).
- A feed you added is out of date: add the link again. Adding the same calendar refreshes it instead of duplicating it.
- The calendar shows only what your files and feeds contain. It does not read your university's records, so a date your professor never posted is not there.
- See [When something is missing or wrong](when-something-is-missing.md).

## Where your data goes

Events you add are saved on your device and sync if you sign in. A calendar link you add is fetched to read its dates. See [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md).
