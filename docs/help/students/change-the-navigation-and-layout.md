# Change the navigation and layout

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to change how you move around the app and how each screen is arranged, or to find a screen you cannot see; stop reading if the bar already suits you.

## What you need first

- Nothing. The choice is for the device you are on, so a phone and a laptop can differ.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Layout and navigation**. The screen is titled **Layout**.
2. Under **Navigation**, pick one of: **Tab bar** (marked default), **One feed**, **Home screen**, **Shelves**, **Workspace** or **Study guides**. Only one is ever drawn.
3. Under **The bar**, choose which screens sit in the tab bar. Use the arrows to move them and the cross to remove one. **Me** stays and stays last.
4. Under **Layout**, pick **Drawn**, **Grouped** or **Soft**. The layout changes how a screen is arranged and nothing else.
5. Under **The bar itself**, choose **Show labels** or **Icons only**, and choose a badge setting: **Only what is due**, **Everything** or **None**.
6. Under **What Today shows**, pick **Cards**, **Compact rows** or **Timeline**.
7. Under **The directory**, pick **A list** or **Tiles**.

## What you will see

- Every screen stays reachable in all six navigations, and nothing is hidden by your choice.
- The search in the header reaches every screen. **Everything** lists them all.

## If it does not work

- You cannot find a screen: use search in the header, press `/`, or open **Everything** or **All apps**. The shortcuts are on [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).
- A screen is gone from your tab bar: add it back under **The bar**.
- You chose a navigation you dislike: open **Settings**, then **Layout and navigation**, and pick **Tab bar**.
- Some screens are hidden because of the role you chose under **Settings**, **Courses**. Nothing is deleted. Switch the role back and they return.

## Where your data goes

The choice is saved on your device. See [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md).
