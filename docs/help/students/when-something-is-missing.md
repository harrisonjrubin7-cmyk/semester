# When something is missing or wrong

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `success` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page when a course, a deadline or a piece of your work is gone, a date is wrong, or the app misbehaves; stop reading if the problem is with your university's own systems.

## What you need first

- Nothing. These screens work with no account.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Semester Intelligence**, then **Recovery plans**. The screen is titled **Recovery**.
2. Under **Recovery mode**, pick what happened. The choices include **A deadline changed**, **A schedule conflict appeared**, **A course or section changed**, **Something did not happen as planned**, **I have too many urgent actions** and **I need help from a person**. The screen shows the impact and no more than three options. Nothing changes because you looked.
3. Read **Is my work safe?** and **Is anything waiting?** They say whether your work is only on this device and whether anything is waiting to sync.
4. If something is gone, read **Something is gone**:
   - A course you removed comes back when you import its syllabus again. The deadlines and the guide are rebuilt, and answers you recorded are not.
   - A deadline ticked by mistake is under **Done** on Today.
   - The app also takes copies of your whole workspace by itself, on this device, up to 7 days back.
5. To go back to an earlier copy, choose **Export, or go back to an earlier copy**. It shows what going back would change before anything happens.
6. To keep a copy of the libraries on this device, choose **Download a recovery copy**.
7. For a real person, open **How this works** and use **What is going on** under **Describe the problem**, or **Who can help with what**.

## What you will see

- The Recovery screen says Semester will not notify faculty, advisors or staff because you use it. Your plan stays private unless you choose what to share.
- Links on the screen lead on: **Sort out a bad week**, **Reconnect a calendar or account**, **Import a syllabus again**, **Your account and sync** and **Ask a person**.

## If it does not work

- A course or reading is missing: see [Add your syllabus and get your deadlines](add-your-syllabus.md).
- Two devices disagree: open **Account** for the last sync and what it decided ([Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md)).
- Storage is full: open **Your data and how it is running**. It lists every collection, largest first. Nothing is deleted for you.
- To see whether the service is down for everyone, open **How this works**. It links the status page, which checks the service from your own browser.
- **How this works** also lists **Known limitations**, which says what does not work yet.
- Report a fault with **Saying something is wrong** under **About**, and attach **Export diagnostics** from **Privacy and your rights**.
- The app is not an emergency service. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

## Where your data goes

The copies the app takes by itself are stored in this browser. They are not uploaded, not synced, and not in your export. **Download a recovery copy** gives you a file, and you decide where it goes. See [Export or delete your data](export-or-delete-your-data.md).
