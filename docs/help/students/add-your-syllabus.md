# Add your syllabus and get your deadlines

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to turn a syllabus file or pasted text into a course with deadlines; stop reading if you only want to type a course in by hand.

## What you need first

- The syllabus as a PDF, a Word file, slides, a text file or a zip. You can also paste its text.
- An AI key for the reading step. If the screen says "Needs Claude", follow [Set up the assistant](set-up-the-assistant.md) first. Adding a course by hand needs no key.

## Steps

1. Open **Add a course**. The shortcut is `N` ([Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md)). On Today, the button **Add a course** goes to the same place.
2. Choose **Choose files — PDF, Word, slides, text, or a zip**. Or choose **…or paste the syllabus in as text**, paste into **Paste the syllabus**, and choose **Take this text**.
3. Add the readings too if you have them. The syllabus gives dates and weights. The readings give the study guide.
4. Optional: write a note in **Anything it should know**, such as "skip chapter 4".
5. Choose **Build the course from** and the word count shown on the button.
6. Read **Review your course**. Under **The dates it found**, each date shows the sentence it came from. Untick any date that is wrong, or open **Edit title or date**.
7. Choose the button that starts with **Add** and your course code. The count of dates is on the button.

## What you will see

- The new course appears in **Courses**. Its deadlines appear in Today and the calendar ([Use the calendar](use-the-calendar.md)).
- Nothing is saved until step 7. The screen shows what it found and what it threw away first.
- If you add a course that already exists, the screen shows what moved, what was reworded and what was removed, and the button starts with **Replace** instead of **Add**. Your ticks and drill history stay.

## If it does not work

- "Needs Claude" means no AI key is set. Use [Set up the assistant](set-up-the-assistant.md), or use **Add it by hand**: type the course code into **Add a course by hand** and choose **Add**. You fill in the rest on **Edit the course** ([Check a date and fix it](check-and-fix-a-date.md)).
- **The course was not built** appears when reading fails. Choose **Try building it again**.
- A date is wrong: the reader can misread a syllabus. Fix it on **Edit the course**. The quote under each date tells you whether the syllabus or the reader was wrong.
- The file picker takes documents only, not photographs. Paste the text instead.
- For anything else, see [When something is missing or wrong](when-something-is-missing.md).

## Where your data goes

The files are read in your browser. When you press the build button, the text is sent to Anthropic to be read, and not before. The course is then saved on your device, and synced only if you sign in. See [Control what the AI sees](control-what-the-ai-sees.md) and [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md).
