# Accessibility settings

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `accessibility` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to find the settings for text size, contrast, motion and reading load; stop reading if you want a conformance statement, because this page makes none.

## What you need first

- Nothing. These settings work with no account and are saved on your device.

## Steps

To change the most common settings quickly:

1. Choose **Accessibility** in the header. A panel opens: **Make Semester easier to use**.
2. Pick **Large** for **Text size**, or **More space** for **Reading spacing**.
3. Switch on **Reduced motion**, **Increase contrast** or **Plain language** as you need.
4. Choose **Focus View** to show less at once.
5. Choose **Read aloud** to have the screen read to you, and **Stop audio** to stop it.
6. Choose **All accessibility settings** for the full page.

To use the full page, open **Settings**, then **Colour and type**:

1. Under **Access and focus**, pick a preset: **Focus**, **Easier reading** or **Lower load**. A preset sets the options below it, and you can change any one afterwards.
2. Switch on any of: **Plain language**, **One step at a time**, **Predictable layout**, **Sensory-friendly first** and **Increase contrast**.
3. Under **Type**, set **Text size** (Compact, Normal, Large, Largest), **Line spacing**, **Reading width** and the **Body text** face. **Hyperlegible** is one of the faces.
4. Under **Motion**, pick **Follow my device**, **Less motion** or **Low stimulation**.
5. Under **The ground**, pick a background. **Match my device** follows your system's light or dark setting.
6. Under **How it talks to you**, pick **Direct**, **Supportive** or **Minimal**. The figures stay the same and only the words around them change.

## What you will see

- Text size and line spacing move everything on every screen. Buttons and the tab bar keep their size so that tap targets stay reachable.
- Your device's own reduced-motion setting is always honoured.
- The first link on every screen is **Skip to content**. The app can also be used from the keyboard ([Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md)).
- Under **Settings**, **Layout and navigation** lets you pick **Show labels** or **Icons only** for the tab bar.

## If it does not work

- A change seems not to apply: check that you changed it on the device you are using. The layout choice is for each device. The access and focus options follow you to your other devices when you are signed in.
- Text is still too small in a part of the app: tell the team with **Saying something is wrong** under **About**.
- Every screen has an **About this screen** button that explains it.
- This page makes no claim of conformance with any accessibility standard. The project records that no human review with assistive technology has been done. See [the feature truth table](../../FEATURE-TRUTH-TABLE.md).

## Where your data goes

Settings are saved on your device. If you sign in, how the app is set up is listed as part of what syncs ([Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md)).
