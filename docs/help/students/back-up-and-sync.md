# Back up, sync and what stays on your device

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `privacy` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to keep a copy of your semester, use two devices and see what never leaves this one; stop reading if you use one device and accept the risk.

## What you need first

- For sync: an email address and a password, or a Google, Microsoft or Apple account. You must be at least 13.
- For a backup file: nothing. It works signed out.

## Steps

To keep a backup file:

1. Open **Take it with you**.
2. Under **What to take**, check the formats. **Everything, as data** is the one file to keep if you keep one.
3. Under **Where it goes**, choose **Download as one zip**, or **Download as separate files**.
4. To restore, go to **Bring one back** and choose **Restore from a backup file**.

To sync between devices:

1. Open **Account**.
2. Enter your email address and password and your date of birth, then choose **Create the account**. Or pick **Google**, **Microsoft** or **Apple**. If you have an account, choose **I already have one**.
3. Sign in on the second device in the same way.
4. To stop syncing, choose **Sign out (keeps data on this device)**.

To undo a bad change on this device:

1. In **Take it with you**, open **Go back a day**.
2. Pick a copy and read what going back would change.
3. Choose **Take a copy now** to add your own copy before you try something risky.

## What you will see

- The first sync sends what is on the device up. If the account already holds a semester, the two are merged and you keep both sides' courses, notes and ticked boxes.
- The header shows your status: **Not signed in**, **Syncing**, **Synced**, **Offline**, **Sync conflict** or **Sync trouble**.
- If two devices changed the same thing, **Two versions need your review** appears and both versions are kept until you choose.

## What stays on this device

- Files you attach to notes.
- Your AI key, if you set one.
- The daily copies the app takes. They are kept for a week, are not synced, and are not in your export.
- Text you were part way through writing, kept for a fortnight.
- Some libraries keep their own file. The screen lists them under **The other workspaces** and gives each its own **Save the workspace file** button.

## If it does not work

- **Sync trouble**: your work is still saved on this device. The line says what failed. Open **Account** for details.
- **Sync conflict**: another device keeps changing the same semester. Nothing was overwritten.
- The browser may clear storage to make room. On **Account**, choose **Ask the browser to keep it**.
- A restore looks wrong: the **Go back a day** list shows what each copy would change before anything happens.
- See [When something is missing or wrong](when-something-is-missing.md).

## Where your data goes

Signed out, nothing leaves the device. Signed in, everything you typed syncs, except what is listed above. The full list is on **Privacy and your rights**. See also [Export or delete your data](export-or-delete-your-data.md) and the [data retention, export and deletion](../../DATA-RETENTION-EXPORT-DELETION.md) page.
