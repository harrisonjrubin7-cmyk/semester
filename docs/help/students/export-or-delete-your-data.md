# Export or delete your data

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `privacy` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to take your data out of Semester, delete your account, or wipe this device; stop reading if you only want a backup, which is on the sync page.

## What you need first

- To export: nothing. It works signed out.
- To delete your account: you must be signed in.
- To wipe this device: nothing.

## Steps

To export your semester:

1. Open **Take it with you**.
2. Under **What to take**, pick what you want: courses as Markdown, deadlines as CSV, the calendar as ICS, notes, your own actions, attachments, or **Everything, as data** as one JSON file.
3. Choose **Download as one zip**, or **Download as separate files**.

To download every row held in your account (signed in):

1. Open **Privacy and your rights**.
2. Choose **Download my account data**. You get a file.

To delete your account (signed in):

1. Download your data first, as above.
2. On **Privacy and your rights**, scroll to **Delete my account** and choose it.
3. Confirm in the box that follows. The deletion runs on the server as one step: it completes or it does not happen.

To wipe this device:

1. On **Privacy and your rights**, find **Erase from this device**.
2. Choose it, type the word the box asks for, and choose **Erase it**.

## What you will see

- **Delete my account** removes every row that names your account, then the account and its sign-in. If it fails, you stay signed in and are told what did and did not happen.
- Deleting your account does not touch this device. Your semester stays here until you erase it.
- Two things survive account deletion: records that are not only yours, with your name cleared from them, and billing records. The page lists them under **What deleting leaves behind**.
- **Erase from this device** has no undo. If you are signed in, the account is not touched and the data can sync back.

## If it does not work

- **Delete my account** says it failed: you are still signed in, and the screen says what did and did not happen. Try again, or use **Export diagnostics** and send it with your report ([When something is missing or wrong](when-something-is-missing.md)).
- You are not signed in: there is no account to delete. Nothing about this semester has ever left the device.
- Your documents and files made in the app are on this device only. Export them from their own screens as files before you erase the device.
- Something these screens cannot do: open Settings, then **About**, and use **Saying something is wrong**. The report goes to the person who builds the app.

## Where your data goes

Exporting and erasing happen on your device. Deleting an account happens on the server. For retention periods and what the app counts about you, read **Privacy and your rights** and [data retention, export and deletion](../../DATA-RETENTION-EXPORT-DELETION.md).
