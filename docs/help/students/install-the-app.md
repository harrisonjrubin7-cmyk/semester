# Install the app

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to put Semester on your home screen or desktop so it opens in its own window; stop reading if the web page in a tab is enough for you.

## What you need first

- The app's web address, opened once while you are online. The public page is `https://harrisonjrubin7-cmyk.github.io/semester/`.
- A browser that can install web apps. The app has no install button of its own. The install option is your browser's.

## Steps

1. Open the app in your browser and wait for Today to load.
2. On an iPhone or iPad, use Safari: press Share (the square with the arrow out of the top), then "Add to Home Screen", and open Semester from the new icon.
3. On Android or a computer, look for your browser's own install option in its menu or address bar. The name differs by browser.
4. Open Semester from its icon. It opens in its own window with no browser bars.
5. While you are online, open the screens you will want later. Then see [What works without a connection](what-works-offline.md).

## What you will see

- The same app, in a standalone window or full screen. It is "this same page with its own icon; nothing is downloaded and no account is involved."
- Your data is the same as in the browser tab. It is saved on the device by your browser for that address.

## If it does not work

- No install option appears: your browser may not offer it for this address. The app works in a tab either way.
- An iPhone reminder does not arrive: reminders only reach an iPhone once the app is on your home screen ([Turn on reminders](turn-on-reminders.md)).
- You installed from a different address than the one you used before: the installed app starts with its own empty copy. Copy your data across with a backup file ([Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md)).
- Storage may be cleared by the browser: on **Account**, choose **Ask the browser to keep it**. Installing the app is what most often changes the browser's answer.

## Where your data goes

An installed app keeps its data on your device, the same as the tab. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in. See [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md).
