# Check a date and fix it

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page when a deadline, meeting time or weight is wrong, or when a professor moved a date by email; stop reading if the course is not in the app yet.

## What you need first

- A course that is yours. The four sample courses cannot be edited until you choose **These are mine** ([Start with the sample semester or your own](start-with-the-sample-or-your-own.md)).
- For the email route, an AI key ([Set up the assistant](set-up-the-assistant.md)).

## Steps

To correct a date by hand:

1. Open **Courses** and choose the course.
2. Choose **Edit this course**. This opens **Edit the course**.
3. Find the part that is wrong: **The course**, **Which term**, **How the grade is built**, **When it meets** or **Deadlines**.
4. Change the value. To add one, use **+ Add a deadline**, **+ Add a meeting** or **+ Add a grading row**.
5. Check the line that says what changed. It starts with **Nothing changed** until you change something.

To fold in an email or post that moved a date:

1. On the course page, choose **Fold in an announcement**. This opens **A change to a date**.
2. Choose **An email or post** or **A calendar**.
3. Under **Which course**, pick the course. Paste the message under **The announcement**.
4. Read each proposed change. Every row quotes the sentence it came from. Take the ones you agree with.

## What you will see

- Corrected dates appear in Today and the calendar.
- A moved deadline keeps its identity, so a box you already ticked stays ticked.
- Nothing moves until you take the change. Edits to a course are kept, and a later import does not overwrite them.

## If it does not work

- The course page has no **Edit this course** button: it is a sample course. Choose **These are mine** first.
- **A change to a date** says it needs Claude: set up the assistant, or edit the date by hand on **Edit the course**.
- A date looks wrong and the quote under it is right: the syllabus said it. Check the syllabus, or ask the professor.
- The app shows dates from your files, not from your university's records. It cannot confirm an official date for you.

## Where your data goes

Edits stay on your device and sync only if you sign in. The announcement text goes to Anthropic when you ask for the change to be read. See [Control what the AI sees](control-what-the-ai-sees.md).
