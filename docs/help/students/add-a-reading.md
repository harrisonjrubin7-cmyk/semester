# Add a reading to a course

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to put a chapter, handout or lecture notes into a course so that every study mode includes it; stop reading if you are adding a syllabus, which is a different page.

## What you need first

- A course in the app. The sample courses accept readings too.
- The reading as pasted text, or as a PDF, Word or text file.
- An AI key for cards from your material ([Set up the assistant](set-up-the-assistant.md)). Without one you can still attach the files.

## Steps

1. Open the course and choose **Add a reading to this course**. Or open **Add a reading** from the directory.
2. Under **Which course**, check the course.
3. Under **Where it belongs**, pick a unit, or **A unit of its own, placed by session**.
4. Under **How much to make**, choose **Brief**, **Usual** or **Thorough**, and **Plainer** or **Harder**. The number you give is a ceiling, never a quota.
5. Under **The material**, paste the text, or choose **Choose course files** under **Attach files**.
6. Choose **Review and add to** your course.
7. Read what the screen says it would change. Accept it to add the material.

## What you will see

- Before anything is written, the screen shows what it would change. Nothing you had is replaced. The new material is layered over the syllabus.
- Once accepted, **Cards**, **Quiz**, **Read**, **Cram** and the slides for that course all include it.
- Everything you add is listed at the bottom of the screen, where you can take it out again.

## If it does not work

- "Needs Claude" shows: set up the assistant, or attach the file only.
- A thin reading gives a few cards. That is deliberate: nothing is invented to fill a quota.
- Images become figures for the unit. A PDF, Word file or text file is read into the box as well as attached.
- To remove something you added, use the list at the bottom of the screen.

## Where your data goes

Attached files stay on this device and are not synced. Pasted text goes to Anthropic only when you ask for cards to be made ([Control what the AI sees](control-what-the-ai-sees.md)).
