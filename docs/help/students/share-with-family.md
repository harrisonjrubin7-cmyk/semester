# Share with family

> **Type:** explanation · **Audience:** students, families · **Owner:** `privacy` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

This page explains what the Family screen does today and what it does not; stop reading if you only want to know whether a parent can log in to your account, which they cannot.

**Status:** PARTIAL. You can prepare and preview a plan on your device. Sharing it with a real person by code is built into the app, and it was not confirmed end to end against a live account service for this page.

## What exists today

- The **Family** screen lets you decide what one person would see, item by item, and look at exactly that. Everything starts at **No access**.
- A plan is private preparation until you share it. The screen says nothing sends an invitation or a message. Payments are not offered.
- There is no parent login. A parent cannot see your account, grades, messages, study activity, attendance, locations, health records or advisor notes. The screen says none of these are ever pulled from your account.
- Each plan has an end date. Every share ends at most one term from today.

## How a plan is built

The screen has four tabs: **People**, **Selected**, **Preview** and **Backup**.

1. On **People**, choose **Add someone**. Give a name, how they relate to you, and a **Planned through** date. The email is optional and is never contacted.
2. For each category, such as **Bills & payments** or **Housing & move-in**, choose **No access** or **Selected items only**.
3. On **Selected**, write the individual items for that person.
4. On **Preview**, read exactly what they would see. Remove the plan and the preview empties.
5. Choose **Save plan**.

## Sharing by code

With a signed-in account, the **Preview** tab offers **Share it with** the person. It makes a code of eight characters. You hand it over yourself, because Semester sends nothing. The code works once, for seven days, and you can call it off before it is used. After they accept, you see when they open it, and you can stop sharing.

The other person signs in, goes to **Family**, finds **Were you given a code?**, enters the eight characters and chooses **Accept**. Then **Open what is shared with me** shows the items you chose, read only. Each time they open it, you can see that they did.

## What is not available

- A family dashboard, notifications to a parent, or a view of your grades.
- Any link to your university's records. Student or university records need an authorization from the institution.
- Payments. The screen says they are not offered.

If a school wants to give families access to its own records, that is a school decision. See [What families can see](../families/what-families-can-see.md).

## Where your data goes

A plan is saved on your device. Items you share by code are copied to the account service for the person you chose. Read **Privacy and your rights** and [Export or delete your data](export-or-delete-your-data.md). The design is in [Supporter and family privacy model](../../SUPPORTER-FAMILY-PRIVACY-MODEL.md) and [Consent sharing design](../../CONSENT-SHARING-DESIGN.md).
