# Start with the sample semester or your own

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page on your first visit to decide what to do with the four courses the app comes with; stop reading once your own courses are in.

## What you need first

- A browser. Nothing is installed and no account is needed.
- Your own syllabus, if you want to replace the sample ([Add your syllabus and get your deadlines](add-your-syllabus.md)).

## Steps

1. Open the app. A first visit shows **Your syllabi. One brain.** Choose **Show me** for a short tour, or **Skip**.
2. In the tour, choose **Good** to move to the next step. The tour is short and changes nothing.
3. Look under the header for the strip **The semester this app ships with**. It means the courses on screen came with the app.
4. If those courses are not yours, choose **Not mine**. The four sample courses are removed and your own courses are what is left.
5. If they really are your courses, choose **These are mine**. They become ordinary courses that you can edit.
6. Add your own course with **Add your first course**, or with **Add a course** if the sample is still on screen.

## What you will see

- After **Not mine**, the app says "Removed. Your own courses are what is left." Today, Courses, Calendar and Study then read "Nothing on Today yet" or a similar line, with **Add your first course** and **Add a course by hand**.
- After **These are mine**, the same four courses stay, now editable.
- The sample courses cannot be edited until you choose **These are mine**.

## If it does not work

- To see the tour again, open **How this works** and choose **Show me around again**. Settings → **About** also has **Replay onboarding**.
- You removed the sample and want it back: on the empty Today screen, choose **Look at the sample semester**. You can switch it off again in Settings.
- The strip does not ask a second time. Either answer ends the question.

## Where your data goes

Nothing leaves your device unless you sign in or press an AI button. See [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md).
