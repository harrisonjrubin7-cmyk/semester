# Turn on reminders

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students · **Owner:** `product` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/help.test.ts`

Use this page to choose which reminders the app shows and when it stays quiet; stop reading if you need an alarm that rings with the app closed, which a browser cannot promise.

## What you need first

- A course with deadlines, so there is something to remind you about.
- A browser that allows notifications. On an iPhone or iPad, the app must be on your home screen first ([Install the app](install-the-app.md)).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Alerts**. The page says nothing is on until you turn it on.
2. Under **Quiet hours**, set a start and an end. One window covers every reminder.
3. Under **Tell me when**, switch on the reminders you want. The defaults include **Class starting in 15 minutes**, **Anything due today, at 8am** and **Exam in one week**.
4. Choose **Allow notifications**. The browser asks once.
5. To add your own rule, choose **Add a reminder rule**. A rule adds to the defaults and never removes one.
6. To silence one course, switch it off under **Silence a course**. Its deadlines stay on screen.
7. To get reminders with the app closed, switch on **Send reminders to this device**, if the page offers it.

## What you will see

- While the app is open, switched-on reminders appear when they come due.
- The screen states its own limit: waking a phone with the browser closed needs a push server. If the page says this build has no push key, reminders show only while the app is open.
- **Alerts** also lists the notices the app would have sent you.

## If it does not work

- You refused the browser's question. The app can only ask once. Undo it in the browser's own settings for this site.
- Nothing arrives on an iPhone: add the app to the home screen first, then open it from there.
- Reminders arrive late or not at all with the app closed: treat them as a nudge, not an alarm. Set an alarm on your device for anything that matters.
- A reminder names the wrong date: fix the date ([Check a date and fix it](check-and-fix-a-date.md)).

## Where your data goes

Reminders are worked out on your device. If you switch on **Send reminders to this device**, the text of each queued reminder (a deadline title and its course) is held on a server until it is sent, then deleted. Switching it off deletes the queue. See [Back up, sync and what stays on your device](back-up-and-sync.md).
