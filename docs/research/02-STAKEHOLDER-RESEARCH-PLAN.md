# 02 · Stakeholder research plan

Twelve groups, one plan. Terms (`C0`–`C4`, source classes, `route`) are defined
in [`01-EVIDENCE-MODEL.md`](01-EVIDENCE-MODEL.md). Method detail is in
[`03-INTERVIEW-GUIDES.md`](03-INTERVIEW-GUIDES.md) and
[`04-TESTING-AND-OBSERVATION.md`](04-TESTING-AND-OBSERVATION.md).

The existing cadence in [`RESEARCH-AND-SERVICE-DESIGN.md`](../operating-model/RESEARCH-AND-SERVICE-DESIGN.md)
(weekly triage, monthly interviews, termly benchmark) is kept as the *rhythm*.
This page decides *who, what and in what order*.

## Starting position

Measured by [`VALIDATED.md`](../../VALIDATED.md) and
[`PILOT.md`](../../PILOT.md): no real user has used the product. So:

- **Students are the only group with a product to put in front of them.** They
  go first and most deeply.
- **Every other group starts as problem research**, not product testing. For
  families, registrars, finance, employers, alumni and partners there is nothing
  yet to test, only workflows and needs to understand.
- **Nothing here depends on an institutional customer existing.** Institutional
  groups are reached as individuals and through public or consented artifacts
  first. [`PUBLIC-CLAIMS-APPROVAL-REGISTER.md`](../../PUBLIC-CLAIMS-APPROVAL-REGISTER.md)
  (CLM-013) prohibits naming any institution, quote or endorsement today; the
  plan assumes that stays true through research.

## Research versus selling

The founder is also the seller. A conversation that ends in "send me
information" is a sales call; a conversation where the participant describes
how they did it last time is research. Keep them apart:

- Research sessions never pitch. The product is shown only in tasks and concept
  stimuli, and is labelled as an early concept.
- Interest expressed in a sales or [discovery call](../market-readiness/DISCOVERY-CALL-PLAYBOOK.md)
  is class `opinion`. It is useful for qualifying a buyer and worthless as
  evidence of need.
- A person who has been sold to is a poor participant for the same question for
  at least one term.

`Gate` = the decision the research feeds. `First` = the first study, in order.
Sample sizes are first-round defaults for a qualitative pattern (C2 needs five
independent people per segment); they are floors, not targets.

## Sequence and why

1. **Weeks 0–2 · foundation.** Consent and data-handling approved
   ([`08`](08-ETHICS-CONSENT-DATA-HANDLING.md)); repository stood up
   ([`06`](06-RESEARCH-REPOSITORY.md)); pilot measures pre-registered
   ([`07`](07-PILOT-AND-LONGITUDINAL.md)). Nothing is recruited before consent
   text has counsel's review.
2. **Weeks 2–8 · students and accessibility.** Pilot starts; first-win
   usability; AT sessions; diary. These are the only studies that touch the
   product, and the accessibility group is run in parallel, not last, because
   a barrier found late costs the most.
3. **Weeks 4–10 · the people students depend on.** Advisors, faculty, student
   affairs. These test whether student-side findings are plausible from the
   other side of the desk. A student need that no advisor recognises, or an
   advisor workflow no student has, goes to both sides for explanation, not to
   the roadmap.
4. **Weeks 6–12 · institutional workflows.** Registrar, finance, IT.
   Observation and artifacts before opinion.
5. **Deferred until student findings reach C2.** Families, employers, alumni,
   partners. The audit's career, alumni and marketplace surfaces are the least
   evidenced; cheap desk and interview work is fine now, but concept testing of
   them waits until the core is understood, so the research budget goes where a
   decision is closest.

## Cross-cutting rules

- **Accessibility is a stratum, not a group at the end.** Recruit disabled
  participants into every student and staff study, *and* run the dedicated
  panel. Ask about assistive technology and what helps, never about diagnosis
  or documentation.
- **Proxies are not substitutes.** An advisor's belief about what students want
  is `opinion` about students. A parent's belief about their student's
  preferences is `opinion`. Ask each group about its own behaviour.
- **Segment coverage is tracked, not assumed.** The recruitment matrix in
  `RESEARCH-AND-SERVICE-DESIGN.md` (first-year, transfer, adult, graduate,
  international, online, commuter, disabled, first-generation, working;
  small/large, public/private, community college, system) is a standing
  checklist, and a gap is reported alongside any finding that claims breadth.
- **Power asymmetries are recorded.** Participants who are the founder's
  classmates, staff whose employer is a prospective customer, and students in
  a professor's course get extra protections ([`08`](08-ETHICS-CONSENT-DATA-HANDLING.md)).
- **Stop rule.** A study stops when the next participant has stopped changing
  the answer to its decision question (and the sample floor is met), or when
  it exceeds its time box; it does not run to a number for its own sake.

## What this plan will not produce

It will not produce institutional demand evidence from students, student
demand evidence from administrators, or outcome evidence from interviews.
Where the audit's rebuild thesis needs one of those, the gap is named in
[`05-AUDIT-VALIDATION.md`](05-AUDIT-VALIDATION.md) with the cheapest study that
would close it.
