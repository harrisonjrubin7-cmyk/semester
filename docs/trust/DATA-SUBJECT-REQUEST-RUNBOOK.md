## Claim ceiling and activation blockers

Permitted: “Semester has repository controls and a detailed runbook for scoped privacy requests.” Prohibited: operated daily queue, universal deadline, complete search/export/deletion, regulator compliance, or customer-approved workflow. Blocks: named trained owner/back-up, qualified applicability/clock decisions, monitored accessible channels, trusted tooling, current inventory, provider process, target exercises, incident/hold/appeal integration, customer routing, metrics, and evidence review.
