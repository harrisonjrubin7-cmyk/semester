## Required experience

AI must be identifiable before use and at the output where confusion is plausible. Plain-language notice must be timely, accessible and consistent with the actual route. It must not transfer responsibility to a student or hide a material limitation in terms alone.

### Before use

Disclose the feature's purpose; that AI is involved; whether the route is Semester-controlled, institution-directed or student-directed; what categories of data and sources are sent; provider identity or class when known; material retention/training facts; who can access retained information; course/institution rules; meaningful non-AI alternative; and a link to fuller information. Obtain affirmative choice or institutional authorization where the scoped analysis requires it.

### At output or action

Label the output AI-assisted; show available sources/provenance and unresolved uncertainty; state that output may be wrong; distinguish suggestion from official record or human decision; show applicable policy/limits; require preview and fresh human confirmation before any approved consequential action; and provide reporting, correction and human-escalation routes.

### High-impact boundary

Semester AI is not the decision-maker for admissions, aid, grading, discipline, accommodations, health, immigration, employment or other consequential eligibility. If AI supports an allowed adjacent task, an authorized human must independently review the relevant source, make the decision, document the basis and provide applicable correction/appeal paths. Initial automated adverse-action uses are prohibited.

## Minimum notice template

> **AI-assisted feature.** This tool uses AI to help with `[PURPOSE]` and may be wrong or incomplete. It sends `[DATA CATEGORIES]` to `[PROVIDER/CONTROL MODEL]` for `[PURPOSE]`; applicable retention and training terms are `[VERIFIED TERMS/LINK]`. Check important information against the cited source and official institutional systems. This output is not an official record or decision. You can use `[NON-AI OPTION]`, report a problem at `[APPROVED ROUTE]`, or contact `[HUMAN OWNER]`.

Bracketed values are blockers. The notice may not be published with unknown or misleading values.

## Evidence state

**Code/config evidence.** Source/disclosure and unavailable-state tests exist for selected intelligence experiences; prompt rules characterize tool calls as proposals; the institutional gateway uses structured source-aware output. Feature controls and provider routes can differ, so one component does not prove universal coverage.

**Operational evidence.** Draft AI disclosures and legal exhibits exist. No complete route-by-route notice inventory, accessibility/usability acceptance, customer/course approval, staffed report-response trail or proof that deployed notices match every provider and retention configuration is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Inventory every AI entry and output state; verify screen-reader, keyboard, language and comprehension behavior; reconcile provider/data/retention text against target configuration; test non-AI and human routes; submit an unsafe/incorrect-output report through closure; obtain qualified and customer approval.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may say it has defined pre-use, at-output, source, limitation, human-review, non-AI and reporting notice requirements, with partial repository implementation on selected paths.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim universal notice coverage, informed consent, explainability, human review, instructor/institution approval, accuracy, safety, unbiased output, zero retention, non-training or legal compliance without current route-specific proof. Never present AI output as a human communication, official record, professional advice or final institutional decision.
