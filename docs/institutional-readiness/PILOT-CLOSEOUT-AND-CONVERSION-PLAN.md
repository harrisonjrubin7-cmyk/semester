## Four controlled paths

| Path | Entry evidence | Required next actions | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| convert conditionally | signed outcome decision supports the piloted scope; no blocking trust issue | authorized proposal/order; legal/procurement/security/privacy/accessibility review; production readiness/UAT; pricing/billing approval; separate activation | executed documents, accepted readiness and activation record; billing/cash/revenue remain separate facts |
| extend | remaining question, approved reason and bounded end date | versioned charter/scope/scorecard/data authority; revised responsibilities and offboarding date | signed extension and preserved original results; no silent scope drift |
| pause | remediable named issue and customer intent | contain access/data/integrations; owner/date/retest; participant/customer communication | signed resume/stop decision and retest evidence |
| stop | targets/guardrails not met, priority changed or authority ends | communicate; offer approved export; revoke invitations/access/roles/credentials/integrations; apply retention/deletion/hold; close support/financial obligations | reconciled closeout signed by data/system owners, with exceptions and expiry |

## Universal closeout checklist

- [ ] Freeze final cohort, scope, configuration, measurement versions and decision evidence.
- [ ] Reconcile invitations, activations, users, roles, support grants, service identities, credentials, integrations and open changes.
- [ ] Deliver approved user/customer export and verify receipt where required; never include another tenant or unapproved data.
- [ ] Revoke/deactivate access, credentials, providers, integrations, write paths and temporary environments; verify effective denial.
- [ ] Execute approved retention, deletion, backup-tail and legal-hold handling; record exceptions and future deletion dates.
- [ ] Close/reassign incidents, vulnerabilities, accessibility barriers, support cases, data-rights requests and contractual obligations.
- [ ] Reconcile usage/cost, invoices/cash if any and assets; do not convert billing records into recognized-revenue claims.
- [ ] Communicate completion, ongoing rights/support route and known residual obligations.
- [ ] Preserve a minimized evidence package and schedule exception follow-up.

## Conversion safeguards

Conversion is a new transaction and operating decision. It requires authorized scope/price/terms, capacity, data/provider/integration authority, exact-target security/accessibility/operations evidence, customer acceptance and separate activation. Expansion repeats readiness for every added cohort/workflow/system. Reference, logo, quote and case-study permission are separate, specific, revocable approvals.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Conversion/offboarding decision logic, export/deletion/access/revocation and commercial governance controls support this plan at differing levels.

**Operational evidence.** No named pilot, signed decision, annual proposal/order, renewal, expansion, offboarding execution, customer reference, billing, cash or revenue is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Populate the record; complete outcome review; choose/sign one path; exercise and reconcile closeout controls; execute any new commercial/legal/readiness approvals; retain customer acceptance.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may describe a controlled convert/extend/pause/stop process. A favorable pilot decision remains conditional until a new agreement and readiness/activation gates close.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim conversion, renewal, expansion, annual contract, recurring revenue, recognized revenue, customer retention, reference permission, completed deletion/offboarding or continued production authority without the corresponding executed and reconciled evidence.
