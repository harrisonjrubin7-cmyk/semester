## Trust, delivery and economics review

Report incidents and near misses; privacy/rights/accessibility/safety cases; security findings; support and staffing load; changes/deviations; integration/reconciliation quality; release/rollback/recovery; user withdrawals and complaints; unresolved known limitations; AI/cloud/provider cost; implementation/support hours; and proposed remediation. Use aggregate, privacy-reviewed evidence and preserve dissent.

Do not calculate or claim retention, GPA, graduation, wellbeing, institutional cost savings, causal impact, ROI or payback unless the charter defined an appropriate approved method, baseline, comparison, source and qualified interpretation before launch.

## Decision rules

- **Convert conditionally:** accepted results justify only the piloted scope; a new authorized agreement, readiness check and activation decision are still required.
- **Extend:** a bounded question remains answerable; approve the revised scope/date/data/scorecard and do not erase the original result.
- **Pause:** a named issue has an owner/remediation/retest/date and data/access remain governed during the pause.
- **Stop/offboard:** export, access revocation, retention/deletion/hold, credential/integration shutdown, communication and completion evidence proceed without a renewal presumption.
- **Expand:** never inferred from conversion; every new cohort, workflow, data class, provider, integration or write re-enters applicable gates.
