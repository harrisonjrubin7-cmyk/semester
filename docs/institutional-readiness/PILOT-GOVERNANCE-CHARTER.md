## Governance bodies and authority

| Body / seat | Minimum authority | Decisions | Cannot delegate implicitly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| executive sponsors | business authority for each party | scope, material risk, go/no-go, convert/extend/stop | signing, legal or system authority they do not hold |
| pilot lead/champion | day-to-day coordination | agenda, adoption support, actions/escalation | trust, official-record or commercial approval |
| product/implementation | workflow/configuration knowledge | bounded configuration and corrective change proposals | expansion or activation outside charter |
| security/privacy/accessibility | relevant qualified authority | approve/decline risk, data/provider/accessibility changes | customer/legal acceptance where separately required |
| technical/system owners | control target systems and data | identity/integration/release/reconciliation decisions | outcome or contract claims |
| measurement owner/reviewer | source/query/privacy competence | data quality, metric interpretation and suppression | rewriting frozen definitions after results |
| support/incident owners | staffed response authority | triage, containment, escalation and communication | unsupported promises or specialist advice |

## Decision cadence

- **Pre-launch:** confirm authority, scope, responsibilities, baseline, evidence, guardrails, stop/rollback/offboarding and signed go/no-go.
- **Weekly:** review safety/trust, scope/change, data quality, denominator, scorecard, reliability/support, issues/decisions and owner actions.
- **Midpoint:** continue, correct, pause or stop. Expansion is a new approved change, not a reward for superficial adoption.
- **Final:** report observed results and caveats; decide convert conditionally, extend under new authority, stop or offboard.
- **Incident/threshold event:** safety and rights controls may pause or stop the pilot regardless of adoption or commercial pressure.

## Change and evidence rules

New population, data class, provider/model, integration, write, metric, workflow, environment, promise or material configuration re-enters applicable legal/trust/technical review and requires recorded customer authority. Minutes distinguish facts, proposals, decisions, owners and due dates. No green status or outcome statement exists without a current evidence link; dissent, missing data and unresolved risk remain visible.
