## Submission gates

Map every row to the actual workbook; validate cited evidence exists and is permitted to share; reconcile answer dependencies and contradictions; recheck company/provider/configuration facts; separate provider assurance from Semester assurance; complete qualified legal/privacy/accessibility review; redact sensitive material; freeze and approve the response; record transmission and receipt. ## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Multiple HECVAT drafts/registers and control/evidence sources support questionnaire preparation.

**Operational evidence.** This is not an official HECVAT workbook or signed attestation and establishes no current customer request, external assurance, complete controls, legal compliance, submission or acceptance.

**Missing test/proof.** Obtain the exact workbook; map and substantiate each question; reverify target/provider/company facts; perform qualified review; approve and submit the frozen response; retain receipt and remediation commitments.
