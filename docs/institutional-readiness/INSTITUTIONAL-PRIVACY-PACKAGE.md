> This package organizes technical and operational privacy evidence. It is not legal advice, a final notice/DPA, a lawful-basis conclusion or a compliance certification.

## Pilot privacy boundary

Use only data necessary for the approved bounded workflow: participant/account identity where required, student-entered planning content, authorized source/sync metadata, preferences/consent, support records, minimal audit/security events and approved AI/provider metadata. Prefer local/manual or read-only setup. Exclude high-risk records/decisions and individual risk profiling. No official write, family/guardian inference, model training, behavioral advertising or unrelated marketing use without separately approved authority and controls.

## Customer-specific data map

For every field/flow record subject/population, source/authority, purpose/permitted use, classification, necessity, system/environment, sender/recipient/subprocessor/provider/region, identity/pseudonymization, read/write/AI scope, access roles, security, retention/deletion/backup/hold, export/correction/revocation, audit, source/freshness/precedence, incident, offboarding and owner/approval. Unknown fields stay out. A provider listed in code/register is not approved for the customer.

## Rights and governance

- Approve customer/Semester roles and instructions, participant notices/consent where applicable, age/minor posture, prohibited uses and official-system responsibilities with qualified counsel/customer authority.
- Enforce least privilege, tenant/resource scope, support-access consent/expiry/revocation and audited privileged actions; conduct target access reviews.
- Provide accessible intake and verified handling for export/access/correction/revocation/deletion/appeal, reconciled across device, database, provider, backup, institutional records, legal holds and contract duties.
- Apply retention and deletion by approved category; a draft schedule or purge test does not establish final periods or target execution.
- Approve AI purpose/provider/model/fields/training/retention/human-review/kill switch; code/policy controls permissions, not prompts alone.
- Report only approved aggregates and suppress small cells (current proposal: 10); never expose individual sensitive signals or enable surveillance.
- At offboarding, stop collection/integrations, revoke access/credentials, return/export authorized data, execute deletion/retention/hold decisions and obtain confirmation.
