## Questionnaire workflow

1. Log customer, requestor/authority, scope, due date, confidentiality, intended use, architecture/environment and secure delivery channel.
2. Answer each item as **implemented**, **pilot-scoped**, **planned**, **not applicable**, **customer responsibility** or **gap**; cite exact evidence/version/date and limitation. Never guess or copy an expired answer.
3. Reconcile contradictions against current code/config, controlled documentation, external records and target readback; repository evidence does not prove target operation.
4. Route security, privacy/AI, accessibility, legal/contract, finance/insurance, operations and integration answers to accountable reviewers; counsel reviews legal representations.
5. Redact secrets, exploit detail, personal/student/customer information and unrelated confidential material. Use a controlled trust room/grant with expiry and access logging where appropriate.
6. Approve/version/deliver; record recipient, files/versions, classification, date, commitments, exceptions, owner/due/expiry and follow-ups.
7. Feed every accepted commitment into contract, implementation, release, support and renewal gates; correct withdrawn/expired claims across recipients/channels.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Extensive product, trust, engineering, legal-draft, commercial and rollout materials support evidence-mapped questionnaire responses.

**Operational evidence.** No complete customer questionnaire, approved trust-room disclosure, signed procurement decision, external assurance set, insurance/accounting/legal closure or named activation record is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Confirm company facts/authority; appoint reviewers/backups; complete professional/external evidence; run package redaction/access/expiry exercise; answer a real scoped questionnaire; reconcile commitments into signed paper and named-tenant gates.
