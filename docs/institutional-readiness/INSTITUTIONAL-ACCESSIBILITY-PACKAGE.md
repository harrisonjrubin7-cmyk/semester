## Current evidence

Semester has repository automation for labels/names, focus-related structures, landmarks, dialogs, reduced motion, drag alternatives, titles, type/contrast and selected browser accessibility/reflow checks. Controlled design standards cover keyboard, focus, zoom/reflow, motion, touch targets, responsive states and accessible communications. This is valuable regression/self-assessment evidence—not a WCAG conformance determination, independent audit, ACR/VPAT or proof that every route/assistive technology/user need works.

## Named-pilot acceptance

Freeze exact workflows, roles, devices/browsers/assistive technologies, content/language and customer criteria. Test onboarding/first win, planning, help/support, privacy/export/delete, error/offline/recovery and approved institutional/operator flows with keyboard, screen reader, zoom/reflow, contrast/motion and accessible alternatives. Recruit representative participants where appropriate; record issue/severity/workaround/owner/date, remediate/retest and publish limitations/accommodation route. Critical blockers stop launch regardless of schedule or commercial status.

Procurement answers must distinguish target/standard, automated evidence, self-assessment, human/qualified result and third-party conformance statement. Never reuse an accessibility result across materially different versions/scope without review.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Strong automated regression and documented design/AT protocols cover important foundations and selected journeys.

**Operational evidence.** No complete qualified journey audit, representative assistive-technology/user testing, approved accommodation operation, current ACR/VPAT or customer acceptance is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Define pilot scope; run qualified manual and representative tests; remediate/retest; validate all delivered content/channels; staff accommodation escalation; complete ACR/VPAT decision and customer acceptance.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may say it targets WCAG 2.2 AA and maintains named automated/self-assessment controls, while clearly disclosing missing formal evaluation.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim WCAG/Section 508 conformance, universally accessible, accessibility-certified, VPAT/ACR complete, barrier-free, independently audited or institution-approved without the exact qualified evidence.
