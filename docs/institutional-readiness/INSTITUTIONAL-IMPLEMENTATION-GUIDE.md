## 1. Qualify and freeze scope

Name legal customer, sponsor/champion, authorized signers and Semester/customer owners/backups for implementation, product/data, identity/integrations, security/privacy/AI, accessibility, support/incident, measurement, billing and offboarding. Approve one bounded cohort/workflow/milestone, success/guardrails, exclusions/prohibited uses, authoritative systems, budget/procurement path, dates and no-fit/stop rules.

## 2. Execute commercial, legal and data authority

Complete authorized order/evaluation terms and applicable data/security/support schedules, privacy notices/consent, subprocessor/provider terms, insurance/tax/payment posture, retention/rights/incident/offboarding and customer instructions. Do not load live student/customer data, invite users or create production credentials before authority closes.

## 3. Design and configure an isolated target

Create named tenant and immutable configuration/version record. Configure approved domains/identity, roles/capabilities, cohort, flags/kill/read-only, data sources/integrations, AI/providers, retention and support/accessibility contacts. High-risk and writeback capabilities default off. Export and independently read back effective configuration; a screen selection is not authoritative proof.

## 4. Validate integrations and authority

Use synthetic/test identities and records first. For every identity/data/provider route prove tenant/resource scope, least privilege, denied paths, claim/field mapping, source/freshness/precedence, rate/error/retry/idempotency/reconciliation, revocation/deprovisioning, degraded/manual fallback, audit and authoritative provider readback. If no approved adapter exists, run without it; never simulate it as live.

## 5. Acceptance and operational rehearsal

Run exact-candidate clean release evidence; student first-win and approved staff/operator journeys; two-role/cross-tenant tests; accessibility/device/state matrix; security/privacy/rights; data quality; monitoring/alert; support/escalation; incident/communication; feature kill/read-only; application rollback/forward database repair; provider-backed recovery plan; export/delete/offboarding and capacity/cost. Record defects, workarounds, residual risk and accountable customer/Semester acceptance.

## 6. Go-live decision and bounded operation

Launch only on signed GO with no open P0/P1, current evidence, named trained primary/backups/customer contacts, staffed channels/hours, approved communications/cohort/baseline and immediate stop/rollback path. Begin with the smallest approved cohort and capabilities. Review metrics/guardrails/data quality/support/incidents/access/providers/cost weekly; changes require impact and approval before execution.

## 7. Convert, expand or offboard

At midpoint/final, report results with denominator/missingness/caveats and decide continue/correct/pause/stop. Conversion/expansion needs new authorized scope/order/capacity and gates for new capabilities/cohorts. Otherwise stop collection/integrations, export/return data, revoke identities/credentials/access, apply deletion/retention/holds, settle support/billing, transfer artifacts and obtain closure confirmation.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Commercial, trust, engineering, product-design and institutional controls support this evidence-gated sequence.

**Operational evidence.** No named customer scope, executed authority, configured target, accepted live provider, representative UAT, staffed operation, launch council decision or completed offboarding exists.

**Missing test/proof.** Qualify a named partner; execute paper/data authority; assign roles/backups; configure/reconcile target; complete all UAT/rehearsals/professional reviews; obtain launch and final/offboarding acceptance.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may use this guide for conditional joint implementation planning and isolated evidence work.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim implementation, migration, integration, deployment, go-live, support, customer acceptance, outcome or institutional readiness from repository materials, demos, sandbox tests or unsigned plans alone.
