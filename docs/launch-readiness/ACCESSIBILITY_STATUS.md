# Accessibility status

**Target:** WCAG 2.2 AA
**Current claim:** automated accessibility controls exist; conformance has not been independently established

## Evidence observed

- Automated axe checks cover representative desktop and phone journeys.
- Keyboard/focus, labels, contrast, touch targets, terminology and responsive behavior have dedicated tests or sweep scripts.
- The product includes a barrier-reporting path and accessibility-support content.
- During this assessment, the Guide listen-mode cold-load defect was fixed and its desktop/phone axe regression passed.

## Limits

Automated tools do not establish conformance. No completed qualified audit, ACR/VPAT, full keyboard matrix, multi-browser assistive-technology matrix, screen-reader report, zoom/reflow assessment, cognitive-accessibility review or institution acceptance is recorded for the candidate.

## GA completion criteria

1. audit critical journeys with qualified human reviewers;
2. test keyboard-only use, focus order/visibility, 200% and 400% zoom/reflow, reduced motion and high contrast;
3. test supported browser/screen-reader pairs, including at least VoiceOver/Safari and NVDA/Firefox or Chrome;
4. remediate P0/P1 issues and document bounded exceptions;
5. publish an accurate accessibility statement and support route; and
6. issue the appropriate ACR/VPAT only after review and approval.

## Safe procurement statement

“Semester targets WCAG 2.2 AA and runs automated accessibility checks. Independent conformance review and an ACR/VPAT are not complete; therefore Semester does not currently claim full WCAG or Section 508 conformance.”
