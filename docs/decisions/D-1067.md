## D-1067 · Sell one institutional package and replace the LMS gradebook through phased migration

**Decided 2026-10-01.** Semester Institutional is the university offer: one agreement for the student operating system, native LMS and gradebook, institutional controls, implementation, integrations, migration, training and hypercare. Institutions choose the first pilot cohort and sequence, not a disconnected menu of products.

Semester is intended to become the institution's LMS and gradebook of record. The change happens through agreement, bounded pilot, integration conformance, a parallel run, rehearsed migration, authorized cutover and evidence-based expansion. Until cutover approval, the incumbent system remains authoritative. A package or feature flag never grants data access, write authority, or permission to change the system of record.

This supersedes the “no duplicate LMS/gradebook” boundary in the 27 September Faculty Course Studio decision and the D-108 hold. The original privacy boundary remains: faculty see only students and records within verified course and term grants; student plans, study behavior and private AI activity are not exposed to instructors. AI does not assign final grades or decide misconduct.

The existing gradebook provides weighted schemes, append-only grade versions, moderation, release, regrades, registrar export and authorized passback. Course Studio remains narrower than a complete native LMS authoring system, and no institution has completed integration, parallel-run, migration or cutover evidence. This decision sets the product and commercial direction; it does not claim a live institutional deployment.
