## D-1541 · The pilot's contracts describe the existing readiness store, checked in seven YAML files

**Decided 2026-10-13.**

The registration-readiness pilot's capabilities, records, events, audit keys, routes, commands and release decision are kept in seven YAML files under `docs/contracts/`. `app/src/contracts/contracts.test.ts` parses them in the normal suite, so CI enforces every cross-reference and rule.

The contracts describe the store already on `main` (`private.registration_readiness_*`, its save RPC and the eight-state workflow). They do not introduce a second store. An earlier draft written from the brief alone was replaced because it described tables that do not exist.

Why: the brief warned that the schema, route checklist, policy engine and runbook would otherwise drift into separate documents. A checked registry makes drift a failing test, and grounding it in the store stops the contracts from describing a design the code does not implement.

Consequences:

- `yaml@2.9.1` is an exact-version devDependency of `app/`. No YAML parser existed in the lockfile.
- The five confirmed pilot outcomes are a display vocabulary mapped onto the store's eight states. The mapping is proposed and needs confirming before any screen ships.
- Advisor caseload and institution-admin audit capabilities are added, as confirmed.
- Routing and referral commands from the brief are removed, because the store does not implement them.

Not done: no screen, route, or worker calls the store yet. The audit key for transitions is provisional until the save RPC's audit call is read. Nothing is deployed, and the release decision is `pending`.
