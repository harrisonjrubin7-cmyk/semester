## D-1541 · The pilot's contracts live in seven checked YAML files, not in separate documents

**Decided 2026-10-13.**

The registration-readiness pilot's capabilities, record passports, events, audit keys, routes, commands and release decision are kept in seven YAML files under `docs/contracts/`. `app/src/contracts/contracts.test.ts` parses them in the normal suite, so every cross-reference, the outbox and audit rules, role scopes, and the release-approval rule are enforced by CI rather than by review.

Why: the brief's own warning is that the schema, route checklist, policy engine and runbook would otherwise drift into separate documents. A checked registry makes drift a failing test.

Consequences:

- `yaml@2.9.1` is an exact-version devDependency of `app/`. No YAML parser existed in the lockfile.
- The brief's inconsistencies were normalised rather than copied, and each change is listed in `docs/contracts/README.md`. The student capability is narrowed to its own route.
- Three commands and two event payloads are marked provisional until they are specified.

Not done: the four capabilities in the release decision are the only ones specified. The other sixteen of the brief's twenty, the SQL migrations, the route package and the runbook are open. Nothing is deployed, and the release decision is `pending`.
