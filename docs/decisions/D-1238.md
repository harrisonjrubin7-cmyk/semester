## D-1238 · One migration pack: the one beside the Migration Center is canonical, and my engine is its executor

**Decided 4 October 2026.**

Two migration packs reached `main` within thirty seconds of each other: the pack
in `app/src/lib/migration/` (policy, gate, exception queue, ledger, Center bridge
and workbooks, written against the Migration Center's own vocabulary) and the one
recorded in D-1185 (an engine that executes checks against extracts, with its own
gate, queue, ledger, stage machine, bridge and documents). Two gates, two queues
and two ledgers for one migration is the failure this repository's audits keep
finding, so this keeps one of each.

- **The pack beside the Center is canonical.** Its gate, queue, ledger, sign-off,
  rehearsal rules, bridge and workbooks stay. `migration-assurance/` is removed.
- **The engine is kept, as the executor behind that gate.** The pack's checks
  took rows a caller had already assembled, and nothing ran a domain against
  source, target and crosswalk files. `engine.ts` and the domain declarations do,
  and each check is proven on the real data by injecting a defect of its own
  kind. `adapter.ts` is the only seam; it emits the pack's `CheckResult`s.
- **The scope gate is kept.** The pack had no notion of data classes, so nothing
  refused T4 data or a grade without an approval.
- **Ported into the pack:** unproven and attested-empty checks in the gate;
  an `origin` on exceptions so a defect the migration introduced is never waived
  or descoped; `reopen` and `applyRun`, so a fix is proven by the next run;
  `cutoverPlanProblems` (triggers may be tightened, never loosened) and
  `archiveReadiness`.
- **The stage machine is not kept.** The Center owns the stages and its database
  refuses a stage move without evidence; a second one in the ledger would be a
  second record of the same fact. What was unique in its gates is in the pack's
  rehearsal, sign-off and archive rules.

A defect was found in the pack's bridge on the way and is fixed here: a
validation with an open critical failure was recorded as one the Center passes,
because the Center gates a validation on `rows_failed` alone.

This supersedes the layout in D-1185, not its reasoning.

**Not done.** Ledger and queue persistence in Postgres (it means extending the
Center's tables and needs the owner's approval). Identity, courses, career and
documents still need one evidence class from outside the extracts. Legal
conclusions stay with the institution and counsel. Nothing here connects to a
source system or loads production.
