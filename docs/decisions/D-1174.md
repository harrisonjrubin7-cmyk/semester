## D-1174 · Operate Semester from checked registers, and state the baseline as it is

**Decided 2026-10-04 (proposal status: the architecture review accepts the class targets and the roadmap separately).** Reliability and operations are held as typed registers in `app/src/lib/sre/` with prose in [`docs/sre/`](../sre/README.md): a service catalog bound to the repository, a multi-window burn-rate alert policy that extends `governance/error-budgets.ts`, an alert register with a state ladder that ends at `delivery_tested`, fifteen runbooks, a Little's-law capacity model, failure experiments, a change gate with freeze windows, cost drivers and a scorecard computed from the rest.

The baseline is recorded as found: no backup person for any role, no alert delivery-tested, no measured SLI, every RTO and RPO unmeasured, every platform limit unverified. Promoting any of those is an edit that has to cite evidence a test can find. Class recovery targets are proposals to measure against, not claims.

Not decided here and not done: the second operator, the coverage window, the freeze calendar, the provider spend cap and budgets, the region posture (P-12), the emergency notification path, and every legal conclusion, which stays with qualified counsel. No production system, secret or setting was changed.
