## D-1658 · A student's request to their instructor is a versioned record on the gradebook, decided by faculty and read by teaching assistants

**Decided 2026-10-20**, by the assistant implementing Slice 3 (student → instructor requests: `faculty/requests` in `docs/ecosystem/COVERAGE-MATRIX.csv`). The founder can overrule any of it. Nothing here is applied to a hosted database.

**1. A request is its own record, not a message.** `20261021120000_course_requests.sql` adds `course_requests` (one row per request, with its current state and its one decision) and `course_request_actions`, an append-only row for every act: filed, withdrawn, asked about, answered, approved, denied. Each act records who did it, the version it produced, the key, and the note the other party reads. Only the four `course_request_*` functions write either table. `lib/mail.ts`'s extension email stays what it was: a draft on the student's device.

**2. Three capabilities at the gradebook's per-term scope `<school>/<CODE>/<TERM>`.** `course_requests:create` goes to `student`, `undergraduate_student`, `graduate_student` and `transfer_student`, the roles that already carry `grades:receive`. `course_requests:decide` goes to `faculty`. `course_requests:view` goes to `faculty` and `teaching_assistant`. A grant on another course, another term, the untermed course or at another school authorises nothing. Nobody decides a request they filed.

**3. Owner decisions, as defaults.** Only faculty decide, and TAs read without deciding: an extension or an incomplete changes what the course of record expects of a student. Reasons and notes are at most 2,000 characters, the same as regrade requests. An incomplete needs one approver, not two. Each student may have one request in progress per course-term, kind and item. A student may withdraw while the request is open or awaiting their answer. A denial or a question needs a note; an approval's note is optional.

**4. States and the expected version.** A request moves open → info_requested (the instructor asks) → open (the student answers) → approved | denied, and the student can withdraw it while it is open or info_requested. Every change after filing names the version the caller read. A request that has moved since is refused with SQLSTATE 40001 and nothing is written, so two instructors cannot both decide one request. The app says "this request changed since you read it" and offers a reload, never a retry. Approved, denied and withdrawn are closed, and a closed request refuses every change.

**5. One idempotency ledger.** Keys spend through `private.gradebook_replay` / `_spend` under four new kinds (`request_file`, `request_withdraw`, `request_respond`, `request_decide`). Replay is checked before the version, so a decision whose first answer was lost answers as it did instead of reading as stale. The kind check also allows Slice 1's `submit`.

**6. Two new event types.** `course_request.filed` and `course_request.decided` (v1, `education_record`, `student_record`, in the LMS group of `packages/institution/src/events.ts`) are emitted through `private.emit_domain_event` in the same transaction. No existing type named a request to an instructor. The payload is the request, course, term, kind, item, outcome word and version. It never names the student and never carries the reason or the note.

**7. Where it lives.** It is two sections of the existing Gradebook screen, behind its gate, on the same course and term picker. `AskInstructor` sits under a student's released grades and `RequestQueue` under the instructor gradebook. There is no new screen or route. `lib/gradebook/requests.ts` is not called by `loadBook`, so a school without the migration keeps its grades and reads "not set up at your school yet".

**8. A request is something a person did.** `lti_account_untouched` counts `course_requests`, and the requests go with the student's account (`OWNED_TABLES`, `RETENTION.md`).

**Not done.** The gradebook does not honour a granted date. Gradebook items have no due date at all, so an approved extension is recorded only as `granted_due` on the request, and an approved excused absence or incomplete does not mark a grade (`grade_entries.mark` could carry `excused` or `incomplete` once the owner decides it should). No notification goes out, because nothing drains the outbox. There is no registrar view, and no report of requests across courses. The app has not been driven in a browser. The migration has not been applied anywhere hosted; merging to main applies it to the canonical project. The database suites ran on PostgreSQL 16; production runs 17. If #1653 merges first, this file's `lti_account_untouched` must gain its `learning_submissions` row before this merges, and the reverse holds too.
