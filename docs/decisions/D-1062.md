## D-1062 · Admissions and financial-aid records are the school's own record of what a person decided, kept append-only, off until the school switches them to Core, and never touch the student-accounts ledger

**Decided 1 Oct 2026.** D-151 lists `admissions` and `financial_aid` as modules a school may take into Semester. Student accounts already exist (D-146) and are not rebuilt. This is the records slice of the two modules that did not.

- **Records, not decisions.** Semester admits, denies, awards, scores, ranks and recommends nothing. A person records every status with a reason. No column, function or screen word makes an order of applicants a ranking; the list is in the order of the school's own reference, and a test reads the migration, library and screens for the words.
- **Statuses go forward.** Each application and award takes its status from the last row of an append-only history. The only way back is a correction entry that names the entry it corrects and needs `admissions:decide` (admissions) or `aid:approve_high` (aid). An admit, deny or waitlist needs `admissions:decide`.
- **A student only by the registrar's link.** `enrolled` is refused until the registrar links an admitted applicant to a student reference. The link creates no student, account or record subject. An applicant has no account here and so no access; a linked student reads no applicant file.
- **A second person for a large award.** At or above `student_account_settings.high_value_cents` (D-146's number and default, not a second one), an award waits for somebody holding `aid:approve_high` who is never its recorder. Until then it cannot be accepted or disbursed and its student does not see it. The rule is held at the table, not only in the function.
- **The ledger is read, never written.** A disbursement may name a student-accounts `aid_credit` by id; the database checks school, student, amount, not reversed, not used twice. Nothing here writes to the ledger and no money moves.
- **Refused at the field.** A run of exactly nine digits (with or without dashes or spaces) and 13 to 19 digit card runs, in any typed text. No federal, tax or identity column exists.
- **Post-secondary only.** Every mutation refuses a school whose `schools.edition` is not `higher_ed` (D-139, D-140). Nothing checks an applicant's age at a post-secondary school.
- **Off until the school says so.** Refused unless the school has switched the module to Core, and while frozen or `kill.core_modules` is engaged; every doubt reads Connect.

**Not decided.** Retention of any of it. What a school's status rules, cycles and funds are: the forward table is the author's reading, not any registrar's or aid office's. Whether one threshold should serve aid and the ledger.

**Not done.** Intake forms, documents, test scores, essays, a rubric, yield modelling, aid packaging, FAFSA or ISIR import, disbursement to a bank, 1098-T, satisfactory academic progress, any payment. No door to the screens beyond `#/admissions` and `#/aid`. Applied to no database; applying `20260930270000_admissions_aid.sql` needs the owner's decision.
