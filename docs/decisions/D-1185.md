## D-1185 · Migrations are verified for meaning beside the Migration Center, and evidence is a replayed, hash-chained ledger

**Decided 4 October 2026.** *Superseded in part by D-1238: the code now lives in `app/src/lib/migration/` beside the Center's pack, and the stage machine and duplicate gate, queue, ledger and bridge described below were retired.*

The Migration Center (`app/src/lib/migration/`) is kept as the project record:
twelve stages, approvals, and a database gate per stage. Its evidence is counts
about a sample file, claimed by the migration lead, and its validation gate asks
that every row mapped. That cannot see a grade moved to another student, a
balance rebuilt through a float, a revoked consent loaded as active, or a
shuffled outline, all of which leave counts equal.

So the verification that stands behind those claims lives in
`app/src/lib/migration-assurance/`, and does not replace or write to the
Center's tables:

- row-count equality is a precondition and never evidence;
- every check is proven on the real data by injecting a defect of its own kind,
  and a check that examined nothing holds the verdict rather than passing it;
- a defect the source already had is inherited and goes to the exception queue;
  one the migration introduced is fixed in the mapping and cannot be waived;
- the run's state is a replay of a hash-chained ledger, so a stage is entered
  only when its gate passes against signed, current evidence;
- the bridge to the Center refuses to turn an inconclusive verdict into counts
  the Center's pass rule would accept.

**Not done.** The ledger and exception queue are files. Persisting them means
extending the Center's tables, with RLS, a check suite and `RETENTION.md`
entries, and applying that to production needs the owner's approval. No
retention period is defaulted: the institution sets them and counsel reviews
them. Education-record status and consent rules are for the institution and
counsel. Nothing here connects to a source system or loads production.
