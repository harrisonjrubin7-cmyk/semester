## D-1179 · Adopt an evidence model in which opinion caps at C1, enforced by a register that fails the build

**Decided 2026-10-04 (proposal status: the product seat accepts it separately).** Product decisions are linked to research through five confidence levels (C0–C4) and seven source classes. A stakeholder opinion, feature request, third-party audit or model synthesis is class `opinion` or `desk` and cannot exceed C1. A hard-to-reverse decision needs C3, a user-reversible one C2, and an outcome claim C4. A decision below its minimum is allowed only as a recorded bet with a gap, kill criterion, owner and unexpired review date. The pack is [`docs/research/`](../research/README.md); `app/src/lib/ops/researchregister.ts` holds the rules and its test fails a register that overclaims or holds personal data.

The register is seeded with 50 assumptions, all C0, and 8 open decisions, none decided. It holds no insights because no research has been run. Building a capability is not counted as evidence of need.

Not decided here and not done: no participant is recruited and no consent text is approved; human-subjects status, minors, staff-participant protections and the research retention clocks wait for counsel; no `RETENTION.md` row; no product, telemetry or consent-mechanism change; the stale payment and count statements in `VALIDATED.md` are recorded in `docs/research/05-AUDIT-VALIDATION.md` and not edited.
