## D-1242 · Journeys the brief names are proposed, and only adopted journeys reach the alert register

**Decided 4 October 2026.**

Seven journeys the platform brief names had no objective in `SLOS-AND-ERROR-BUDGETS.md`: calendar load, course access, grade retrieval, registration submission, billing statement and payment, communication delivery, connected-source sync. They are added to `error-budgets.ts` as `proposed`, each with its own bad events, listed apart from the adopted eight in the doc.

1. **The figures are hypotheses, not decisions.** They follow one rule (money, records and anything that can disclose another person's data at 99.95%; a daily read at 99.9%; a journey depending on a system Semester does not run at 99.5%). An owner adopts or changes each; none is quotable to a customer.
2. **A proposed journey never reaches the pager.** `lib/sre` builds a page-severity burn alert, component and runbook per journey, so it now reads `ADOPTED_JOURNEYS`. Removing the `proposed` flag is the adoption, and then fails with a message naming the route, component and runbook still to add.
3. **A registration refusal with a stated reason is a good event.** The objective is that the system answers exactly once and says why, not that every request succeeds.

**Not done.** No journey is measured; nothing reads these against real counts. "Learning submission" is not covered (only draft save is). Emergency communications may deserve a stricter objective than 99.9% and are not separated out.
