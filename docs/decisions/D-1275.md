## D-1275 · The GTM financial model is a console tab on sample data, the business documents index the controlled ones, and five price and scope conflicts are left to the founder

**Decided 5 Oct 2026.** A founder brief asked for a GTM playbook, an interactive financial model, a pilot outreach system, a security and compliance plan and a combined PDF. Most of the surrounding material was already controlled in `docs/commercial/`, `docs/finance/`, `docs/trust/` and `docs/legal-drafts/`. The work added only what was missing and recorded the rest in [`../business/INVENTORY.md`](../business/INVENTORY.md).

- **What was decided.** The model is `app/src/finance/`, opened as the operations console's **Finance model** tab, so the `console:operate` grant read by the database is its authorization. It runs on local sample data, reads no ledger, writes no browser storage, and tags every output `forecast`; a test refuses any row tagged `actual` until a real actuals feed exists. The paid-pilot gate is an input (`firstPaidPilotMonth`), and a critical banner shows whenever institutional revenue is modelled, because the paid institutional pilot is NO-GO (`GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md`). Marketplace revenue is a separate line that never enters core revenue, gross profit, break-even or unit economics.
- **Documents.** `docs/business/` links the controlled artifacts and adds only the delta. Each file names the existing artifacts it builds on and why a new file was needed. Nothing existing was edited except the developer repository map and the admin guide, which the repository's own guards require to name a new directory and a new Console tab. No `[APPROVED]` item exists, because no named approver is recorded for any customer-facing wording.
- **PDFs.** `app/scripts/generate-gtm-pdf.mjs` assembles four reports from named headings in the documents and prints them through headless Chromium. A heading that has been renamed fails the build, and the customer-safe pack fails if it contains `[INTERNAL`, an internal price, or ARR, CAC, LTV or runway language. `marked`, already in the lockfile through `mermaid`, is declared as an `app` dev dependency; no package was added.
- **Held by.** `financialModel.test.ts` (75 tests, formulas worked by hand, each warning with a raising and a quiet case) and `FinancialModel.test.tsx` (22). Six engine mutations were run; five were caught, and the sixth showed forward runway was untested and now has a test. The generator's two guards were each shown to fail on a planted fault.

**Not decided here.** These conflicts are the founder's, and the model carries each as a labelled input rather than a choice: pilot length (26 weeks in `pilot.ts` and D-134, 8 to 12 weeks in the brief; the model defaults to 6 months and warns otherwise); the student price ($7.99 and $59 in D-134, $15 a month in D-1154, $8.99 and $69 in the brief); the institutional price and its unit (enrolled or active students) and whether the $30,000 minimum sits between the deal-desk department and campus minimums; the AI pool (2,400 requests per student per year costs more than the platform fee at the architecture document's higher per-call figure, and "request" is not that document's unit); and the marketplace commission (12% in the brief, 15% in the workbook). No price, target or forecast is approved. The public 72-hour breach-notice wording, the stale HECVAT draft answers and the other overclaims listed in the inventory were found, not changed.
