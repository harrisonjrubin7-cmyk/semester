## D-1344 · The inbox's park, retry, dead-letter and replay policy exists as pure code over a port, and nothing calls it

**Decided 6 Oct 2026**, as the next step of D-1319 (action 8b of `docs/finance/NATIVE_FINANCIAL_PLATFORM.md` §14), taken on the owner's instruction to merge and continue.

- **What was decided.** `supabase/functions/_shared/payments/inbox.ts` holds what happens to a verified event once it is in `provider_event_inbox` (D-1324): `record` writes a minimized projection once, `drain` tries what is due, parks an invoice that arrives before its subscription, retries on a backoff that doubles from a minute to six hours, and dead-letters after eight tries; `replay` puts a dead-lettered event back for a person. It knows no database: the inbox is a port, the apply function and the clock are arguments, and an in-memory store refuses every move the table trigger refuses.
- **Nothing calls it.** `billing-webhook` is unchanged and still answers 500 for an early invoice so the provider retries. That is the safety net, and it stays until a drain job exists and has been watched: answering 200 for a parked event with nobody draining the inbox would lose the first payment.
- **Held by.** `app/src/lib/payments/inbox.test.ts`: the status moves must equal the trigger's SQL; four planted bugs (dead-letter from `received`, no first-miss guard, `>` for `>=` on the retry limit, failed→parked) each turn it red.
- **Not decided here.** The eight tries and the backoff are working numbers, not a policy anyone signed. The drain job, who may replay, a console view, and the retention period for the inbox are separate and need the owner.
- **Stays as it is.** The checkout holds, D-146, D-1236, D-1319 and D-1324 stand.
