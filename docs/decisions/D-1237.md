## D-1237 · An appeal must be filed within thirty days of the decision

**Decided 2026-10-04.** A moderation decision can be appealed for thirty days from the decision, not from the report and not for ever. It is held in both places that reason about it: `APPEAL_RULES` in `app/src/community/moderation.ts`, and `public.appeal_community_decision` and `public.my_community_notices` in `supabase/migrations/20261004130000_community_appeal_window.sql`, which `app/src/community/appealwindow.test.ts` holds to each other by reading the last definition of each function. A late appeal is refused with `the appeal window has closed`; an expired decision is no longer reported `appealable`, so no screen offers an appeal the function would refuse; and the student notice says "within thirty days" in words, because its test forbids digits so that a number in it can only be a score.

Why: with no window, whether a person could still appeal depended on whether the retention sweep had yet deleted the case, which is an accident of scheduling and not a rule anyone could be told. Thirty days is the recommendation in §11.4 (D5) of [`docs/SUPPORT-AND-TRUST-SAFETY-OPERATING-MODEL.md`](../SUPPORT-AND-TRUST-SAFETY-OPERATING-MODEL.md); the owner asked for gap G-13 to be built, and it was taken as accepting that figure. Changing it is one constant and one number in the migration, and the test fails until they agree. Counsel has not reviewed the length (`Q-20` in [`LEGAL-REVIEW-QUEUE.md`](../../LEGAL-REVIEW-QUEUE.md)).

Not decided and not done: a deadline for *deciding* an appeal (the same document proposes ten business days). It needs a reader, an overdue state in the reviewer queue, to be more than a number, and no reviewer queue reads one today.
