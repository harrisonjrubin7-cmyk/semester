## D-1284 · The shared key charges a school's meter for a school's account

**Decided 2026-10-05**, on the owner's instruction to decide the question D-1279 left open: a school's budget was read but not drawn down by shared-key calls.

**Decision.** It is drawn down. A budget a school sets but the shared key does not charge would not bound what the school's students cost, and the school's budget check in D-1279 would pass for ever. For a school's account, `supabase/functions/claude/index.ts` reserves the request's worst case in whole cents on the gateway's own meter (`reserve_ai_budget`) right after the account's own reservation and before the call is counted, releases it wherever the account's is released, and settles it to actual cost rounded up to a cent (`supabase/functions/_shared/tenantai.ts`, `app/src/lib/tenantai.test.ts`). When usage cannot be read, or the connection drops, it settles at the reservation, as the account's own meter leaves its reservation standing. A school with no room is refused 403 (`tenant_budget`); one that cannot be asked, 503.

**Not done.** A stream the client abandons before it ends runs no settle, so the school's reservation expires and is released: the account is charged and the school is not. An answer that cost more than was reserved is charged to the account in full and to the school up to the reservation, because the settle RPC refuses more. The function is behind the provider-activation gate (`D-1031`), is not deployed and was not run on a Deno runtime.

**Seen red.** The meter tests failed before the helper existed (8); the structural test failed against the previous function.
