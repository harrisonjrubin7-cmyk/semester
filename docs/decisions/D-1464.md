## D-1464 · Notifications use one minimized inbox and a durable external-delivery ledger

**Decided 11 October 2026.** This records the Milestone 9 repository
foundation. It does not activate an email, SMS or push provider; deploy the
migration; enable a tenant; or establish the production retention schedule.

- A notification is published idempotently under a school, recipient and
  dedupe key. The in-app record stores a bounded preview, template identifier,
  category and internal destination rather than provider-rendered content.
- Sensitive notices may store only the fixed generic preview. The protected
  detail remains behind the destination's ordinary authorization boundary.
- Preferences belong to the authenticated person's pinned school. Safety,
  account-security and transactional categories cannot be disabled; quiet
  hours must be a complete pair with a known time zone.
- Only the recipient can read or mark inbox items. External delivery rows and
  their transition events are service-only and contain no address, device
  token or rendered sensitive body.
- Delivery workers claim due rows with an exclusive claim id. Settlement is
  claim-bound, increments a bounded attempt count and ends in delivered,
  suppressed, a scheduled retry or dead letter. Transition events cannot be
  updated or directly deleted by the service role.
- The existing pure notification planner remains the authority for tenant
  channels, guardian consent, marketing consent and quiet-hour calculation.
  Wiring every producer and provider to this durable foundation is a separate
  implementation and activation gate.
