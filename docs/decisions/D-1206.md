## D-1206 · The shared key serves models by plan, and an unreadable plan is Free

**Decided 2026-10-04**, on the owner's instruction to restrict the shared AI key's models by plan. The three lists below are this pull request's proposal for that instruction and are one edit to change (`PLAN_MODELS` in `supabase/functions/_shared/clamp.ts`); the instruction is the decision, the lists are what to confirm.

**What.** A call on the shared key may name only the models its account's plan covers: **Free** Haiku 4.5 and Sonnet 5; **Plus** adds Opus 5; **Pro** adds Fable 5.1. Another model is refused with a 400 carrying `error.code = 'model_not_on_plan'` and `error.allowed_models`, before the call is counted, so a refusal costs nobody one of their sixty. The plan is read the way `public.my_entitlements()` reads it: a subscription in a paying status (`trialing`, `active`, `past_due`, `grace`), with its period not ended, **that still holds entitlements**. A plan that is unknown, unreadable or looked up with an error is **Free**, never wider. The app learns the list from that one refusal, asks again once on a covered model and remembers it for a day. A student's own key, a proxy and the OpenAI route are never restricted.

**Why.** The monthly cap counts calls and the models cost very different amounts per call. At the cap on the app's default model (Opus 5) one account costs about as much as Plus's monthly price, and on Fable 5.1 with long output several times it (`docs/commercial/PRICING-UNIT-ECONOMICS-ARCHITECTURE.md`, F1, with its reproducible model). The clamp's own comment says the cap "counted calls; the bill counts tokens". Which model a call names is most of what an account can spend, and it is the one lever that needs no new table.

**The edges.** *Free gets Sonnet 5, not Haiku only:* the syllabus read is the activation job and Haiku would degrade it; the cost of that choice is Free's worst case at the cap, which the dollar meter (P0-c) is meant to bound. *Plus includes Opus 5:* Plus at the 60-call cap on Opus 5 can still exceed the pricing document's 40% worst-case margin rule, so this holds only while dollars are unmetered and Plus is not on sale. *The entitlement requirement:* `run_dunning()` removes a subscription's entitlements at restriction and leaves it `past_due`, so a lapsed payer is told from a paying one by their entitlements, not their status. *A failed lookup serves Free:* the student is offered fewer models, which the refusal's wording explains; the other direction widens the list for anyone who can make a query fail.

**Not done.** `pro` has a plan row and no `plan_entitlements`, so a Pro subscription reads as Free here; Pro cannot be bought (it has no price), and seeding its entitlements is what changes the answer (`sharedplan.test.ts` says so). No per-plan `max_tokens` ceiling: the app's own largest ask is 12,000, so lowering the 16,000 ceiling needs an audit of every `maxTokens` first. The 60-call count is unchanged and nothing meters dollars yet. `docs/finance/02-PRICING-PACKAGING-ENTITLEMENTS.md` proposes allowances of 8 (Free) and 40 (Plus) AI tasks a month; this sets none, and reconciling them with the call count is the owner's.
