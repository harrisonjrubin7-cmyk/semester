## D-1231 · The shared key meters dollars against a plan allowance, reserving the worst case

**Decided 2026-10-04**, on the owner's instruction to proceed with the dollar meter (P0-c of `docs/commercial/PRICING-UNIT-ECONOMICS-ARCHITECTURE.md`). The allowance amounts are this pull request's proposal and one edit to change (`PLAN_ALLOWANCE_MICROS` in `supabase/functions/_shared/aispend.ts`, or `AI_ALLOWANCE_MICROS_FREE|PLUS|PRO` per deployment); the instruction is the decision, the amounts are what to confirm.

**What.** Every shared-key call reserves its worst-case cost (counted input, the whole `max_tokens`, every search the request may run) against the account's monthly dollar allowance — **Free $0.75, Plus $2.00, Pro $4.00** — before it is counted or forwarded, and settles to the usage Anthropic reports when the response ends. A spent allowance is a 429 with `error.code = 'ai_allowance_exhausted'`. The sixty-call count is unchanged and still applies. Money is micro-dollars in `public.usage.cost_micros`; `public.add_spend` does the arithmetic in one statement under the row lock.

**Why.** The call cap counts requests and the bill counts tokens, with models ten times apart in price. Which model a plan may name (D-1206) bounded the worst case; this bounds what it may spend, which is what the owner is exposed to. Sized by the pricing document's rule: worst-case contribution margin of at least 40% at the plan's price (Free is bounded as acquisition cost instead).

**The edges.** *Reserve, then settle:* an abandoned stream costs its reservation, never nothing, for the reason `count_call` runs before the fetch. *A reservation is released* when the call cap, the meter or upstream refuses, and *stands* when the fetch throws or the client disconnects mid-stream, or the stream ends without its closing usage block: each errs towards the owner. *An unknown model is priced as the dearest*, never free. *A meter that cannot answer refuses*, as the call counter does. *Settling can exceed the reservation* only if Anthropic bills above the ceiling; the charge is then unconditional.

**Not done, and to verify.** The `count_tokens` request was not run against the live API (no key in the build environment); if it fails the byte estimate over-reserves. Cache-write factor (1.25) and search cost ($0.01) are marked `verify`. The public copy says "60 a month" (`lib/allowance.ts`) and will be inaccurate wherever the dollar cap binds first, which is an activation precondition. These allowances disagree with `docs/finance/02-PRICING-PACKAGING-ENTITLEMENTS.md` (8 and 40 tasks). The rate card is a constant, not a table; the deadline-grace pool and a protected syllabus import are not built.
