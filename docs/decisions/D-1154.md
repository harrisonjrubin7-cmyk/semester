## D-1154 · The owner's answers to the open revenue-operations decisions: $15 a month, institution prices matched to LMS vendors, immediate plan activation, a named second approver, an accountant of record, and the `gtm_*` tables as the one CRM

**Decided 2026-10-04.** Recorded from the owner's instruction in the session that produced [`REVENUE-OPERATIONS-ARCHITECTURE.md`](../commercial/REVENUE-OPERATIONS-ARCHITECTURE.md) (§12 lists the questions this answers). The owner is Harrison Rubin. Each answer below is the owner's, except the CRM, which the owner delegated and Claude chose.

1. **Individual price: $15 a month, and a yearly bundle at a price not yet stated.** This replaces the $7.99 a month and $59 a year that D-134 set and D-1173 called facts. Not done: the catalog (`commercial_prices`), the pricing page and `plans.test.ts` still print the old figures, and billing stays closed by its code-level hold, so nothing is charged at either price. Still open and first to confirm: which plan carries $15 (Plus was $7.99; Pro was proposed at $14.99 in [`PRICING-UNIT-ECONOMICS-ARCHITECTURE.md`](../commercial/PRICING-UNIT-ECONOMICS-ARCHITECTURE.md)), and the yearly price.
2. **Institution prices: match what Blackboard, Canvas and comparable systems charge.** No number is set. Those vendors' prices are mostly negotiated and not published, so the figure has to come from sourced quotes or public price lists, and the cost floors in the pricing architecture document are unchanged: a matched price below its floor should be seen before it is quoted. The unit (enrolled student, active student, sponsored seat or platform fee) is not stated and stays open.
3. **A signed order form activates the approved plan immediately.** This is what `apply_signed_contract` already does (`tenant_plan.status = 'active'`, from `effective_at`), so no code changes. It is consistent with the rule in [`ORDERING-AND-BILLING-OPERATIONS.md`](../commercial/ORDERING-AND-BILLING-OPERATIONS.md) that entitlement never bypasses security, privacy, accessibility, UAT, support or launch approval only while the module and launch gates stay separate from the plan. Today the entitlement order runs in shadow and enforces nothing ([`ENTITLEMENT-RESOLUTION.md`](../ENTITLEMENT-RESOLUTION.md)), so those gates are not enforced through this path.
4. **Second approver: Bramm Rubin** (spelled as given). This gives the interim deal-desk rule in D-1160 (a vacant seat goes to the next approver above it) a named second seat. The approver must differ from the requester. No approval thresholds are set, and no authority-matrix entry exists yet.
5. **Accountant of record: Harrison Rubin.** This names who keeps the records. It does not make the revenue-recognition, tax, invoice-numbering, credit-balance or close gates in §11 of the architecture document satisfied: those call for a qualified accountant or tax adviser, and the owner is also the seller, so no independent review exists. Those gates stay open until a qualified person is engaged.
6. **CRM: the `gtm_*` tables (Supabase), as the single system. No external CRM now.** They already carry consent, suppression, role-scoped access and the stage history, and a second system would create the shadow records the CRM data model forbids. Revisit when there is a second seller, when email or calendar sync becomes necessary, or when volume outgrows the tables.

**Not decided and not done:** the yearly price; which plan carries $15; any institutional number or unit; the accounting system itself; approval thresholds and discount bands; a qualified accountant, tax adviser or counsel; the catalog, pricing-page and test changes to $15; reconciling `D-1173`'s statement that $7.99 / $59 are facts.
