
## D-1148 · Keep "student action platform" as Semester's public descriptor

**Decided 2026-10-04.** The public descriptor stays "the student action platform", as the company site title and hero already use it. The brand platform in [`docs/BRAND-PLATFORM.md`](../BRAND-PLATFORM.md) proposed no replacement and offered "Student OS" only as an internal and analyst-facing category label.

"Student OS" is not used as a public descriptor because it implies replacing official systems, which `CLM-006` in the public claims register prohibits. The descriptor describes the exercised product and is consistent with `CLM-001`.

This does not approve the descriptor, or any tagline, as a public claim. Each exact use still needs its approval record in the register. A different descriptor would need a new decision and a register review.

Not done: no trademark clearance for "Semester" or any product name; counsel has not been asked.
