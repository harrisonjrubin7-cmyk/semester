## D-1236 · The marketplace stays held, paid ranking is a held boundary, the A–E class model stands, and a licensed processor must hold the funds before any student pays

**Decided 4 Oct 2026** by the owner, on the marketplace and partnership
strategy ([`../commercial/MARKETPLACE-AND-PARTNERSHIP-STRATEGY.md`](../commercial/MARKETPLACE-AND-PARTNERSHIP-STRATEGY.md),
section 12, merged in #1155). The page proposed five decisions; the owner
accepted four as written. They are one decision because each is a condition on
the same thing: that nothing in the marketplace opens before it is safe to.

- **Held.** Marketplace, payments, payouts and provider access stay held until
  the four gates that open the first phase are met by evidence a named person
  verified: named owners and backups (G-OWN), the marketplace tables with
  row-level security, history and a kill switch (G-DATA), counsel-approved
  provider terms (G-TERMS), and a staffed review and trust-and-safety queue
  with a backup (G-QUEUE). Section 12 of the page states where each stands; on
  4 Oct 2026 none is met. The capability register carries the same ceiling:
  `marketplace` is `BLOCKED`.
- **No paid ranking.** Nothing sells placement, ranking or a better standing
  in the marketplace. A provider's tier and quality band come from evidence and
  never from a fee, and ranking is relevance to the student's stated need first.
  This promotes the line "do not create pay-to-win club or opportunity
  placement" in `docs/MARKET-LEADERSHIP.md` from *proposed* to a decision.
  Sponsorship stays a separate, labelled slot under `app/src/lib/gtm/sponsor.ts`
  and is never a ranking input.
- **Class model.** Listings sit in one of five risk classes (information, tools
  and integrations, transactional services, opportunities, held and restricted),
  and the highest-risk attribute decides the class. **Class E stays
  prohibited:** housing leases, health and counselling services, legal,
  immigration, financial products and loans, proctoring or surveillance tools,
  AI providers that would receive student data, and anything for under-18s.
  Opening any of it takes its own decision, outside counsel and a domain
  expert, before a design.
- **Processor holds the funds.** Student-paid checkout (phase M4) needs a
  licensed payment processor that holds the funds and pays providers out.
  Semester never custodies student or provider money. Whether that structure
  keeps Semester outside money-transmission and similar regimes is a question
  for counsel (item 1 of section 11) and is not answered here.

**Not decided here.** The fifth requested decision, naming counsel and the
backup owners for the marketplace, is still open and is the first thing G-OWN
needs. This does not write the test the no-paid-ranking boundary needs (the
page proposes one: ranking is invariant to fee, tier and sponsor status), so
until that test exists the boundary is held by this decision and review, not by
a failing build; the same is true of the held-boundary entry itself, which this
does not add to `ops/strategic-boundaries/`. `docs/MARKET-LEADERSHIP.md`
still lists the paid-ranking line as *proposed* and is not edited here. Also
not decided: take rates, reserves, score weights, response targets and every
other number on the page, which stay hypotheses; and every legal question in
section 11, which stays with qualified counsel.
