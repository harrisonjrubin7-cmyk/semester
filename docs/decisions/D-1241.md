## D-1241 · Opt-in marketing email before exam periods is allowed, with safeguards

**Decided 2026-10-04.** The founder answered decision 9 of [`docs/gtm/GROWTH-OPERATING-PLAN.md`](../gtm/GROWTH-OPERATING-PLAN.md): opt-in marketing email before midterms, registration and finals is allowed. This keeps row 8 of the lifecycle table in [`docs/gtm/BRAND-AND-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`](../gtm/BRAND-AND-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md) §5.7 as written, and loosens the growth plan's earlier rule against messages "timed to exam panic" to exclude only exam pressure used as a lever.

Why it needed deciding: the two documents merged in parallel and disagreed. The strategy allows opt-in marketing email before exam periods, with generic dates. The plan's §6.6 banned messages timed to exam panic and §5 banned any message that referenced an exam, deadline or GPA to prompt a return. Both could not stand.

The safeguards, all unchanged: generic dates only and no school-specific claims; opt-in only, never pre-ticked; the one-a-week cap on all marketing email; and every pattern in §6.6 stays banned, including false urgency, countdowns, fear, "you're falling behind", guilt copy, and any use of grades or GPA. The student's own reminders remain the only school-specific deadline messages. The plan's §5 and §6.6 were reworded to say this.

Not decided and not done: nothing is switched on. Lifecycle email is still off until gate G2 in the plan (a sender, consent capture, suppression, one-click unsubscribe and a retention sweep for contacts). The one-a-week cap and the win-back rule are still proposals awaiting their own decisions. Counsel has not reviewed the consent text or the email-law questions this touches.
