## D-1303 · The student-data sweep reads fifteen owner column names, and an unaudited moderator read of review authorship is recorded, not changed

**Decided 2026-10-05**, by the assistant under the executive authority the founder delegated in-session (D-1287). The founder, or the privacy owner for the open item, can overrule it.

**Context.** D-1298 left the sweep keyed on `user_id` and `student_id` only. Tables that call the same thing `student`, `author`, `requester`, `subject_user_id` and so on were not walked.

**Decisions.**
1. The sweep keys on fifteen owner column names and sets every owner column on a table to the one student, so a policy that reads through any of them is found. Probed tables: 64 → 79. Fifteen more are named, not passed: their owner cannot read the row back (staff- or system-written event tables and person-keyed evidence tables).
2. `approval_request` read by five console roles is listed as intended: `console:operate` reads every request and `requester` is the staff member who asked.
3. `course_review_authors` read by `moderator` is listed as intended but unenforced. `docs/ROLE-LAUNCH-REGISTER.md` promises "author access strictly audited"; the table read writes no audit row. No policy was changed: whether to audit each read or move it behind a definer function that does is the privacy owner's call.

**Consequences.** The floor is 79 and can only rise. Still unswept: tables whose owner column is `created_by`, `subject`, `owner_id` or `account_id`, which mostly name the staff who wrote a row.
