## D-1150 · The institution-side business case counts the buyer's adoption and exit costs, counts only cash savings in the base case, and shows outcomes as a break-even rather than a benefit

**Decided 4 Oct 2026** (procurement operating map,
[`../institutional-readiness/PROCUREMENT-OPERATING-MAP.md`](../institutional-readiness/PROCUREMENT-OPERATING-MAP.md) §9).
D-1042 gave a buyer the savings side: a system counts as removed only when its
contract has ended and the replacement is available. A finance office's next
question is what the buyer spends to get there, and nothing answered it.

- **What is counted.** Five years, as in D-1042. The buyer's own labour for
  identity and integration, for security, privacy, accessibility, legal and
  procurement review, for training, validation and administration; the parallel
  run of the incumbent beside Semester; any metered component and the renewal
  escalator the quote carries; and the exit (export, validation, deletion
  confirmation). The buyer's review labour is counted because it is real and a
  buyer who is not shown it will add it and distrust the rest.
- **What counts as a benefit.** Three classes, not summed. *Cash* is the only
  one in the base case, on D-1042's rule. *Capacity* is reported separately and
  becomes cash only if the budget line is removed or the time is demonstrably
  redeployed. *Outcomes* (persistence, completion) are excluded and shown only
  as a break-even: the number of additional persisting students, at the
  school's own net revenue per student, that would cover the net cost. The
  school owns the attribution.
- **Semester's price is the school's, from a quote.** Nothing here is a
  default. The worked example is in placeholder units, labelled as arithmetic,
  and is **negative on cash alone on purpose**: a model that cannot show a loss
  is a sales tool.
- **Held by.** The example was recomputed independently (cost 770, cash benefit
  480, net −290, break-even 29 at a placeholder 10). It is a worksheet in a
  document; nothing enforces it, and the calculator at `/tools/stack/` still
  covers the savings rows only.

**Not decided here.** Semester's institutional price, the metered components
and caps in a quote, and any claim of savings, ROI or payback, which stay
prohibited until a quote, a measured baseline and approval exist. Also not
decided, and flagged in the pack's risk register for the owner: reconciling the
RFP library's PF-4 and CT-1 with the subprocessor register and contract drafts
(R1), and whether to widen `COMMITTEE_ROLES` (R2).
