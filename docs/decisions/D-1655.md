## D-1655 · A registration hold is resolved by the office that owns it, and readiness that read it goes stale in the same transaction

**Decided 2026-10-20**, by the assistant implementing Slice 2 of the 19 October Claude Design handoff (`SLICE-2-PLAN`: registration blocker → owning office resolves its hold → readiness becomes stale and is re-evaluated fresh → receipt that says it is not enrollment); the founder can overrule any of it. Nothing here is applied to a hosted database.

**1. A hold names its owner as an office scope, and an unowned hold stays the SIS's.** `20261020120000_registration_hold_resolution.sql` adds `registration_holds.owner_office`, a `<school>/<office>` scope checked to sit inside the hold's school — the shape every office grant in `role_grants` already uses. The free-text `office` stays as the label a student reads. The SIS sync sets the new column; a hold without one cannot be resolved in Semester at all.

**2. Who may resolve is a capability over that exact scope.** `registration_hold:resolve`, asked of `private.has_capability('registration_hold:resolve', 'office', owner_office)`, so another office, a school-wide registrar grant and another school all miss. It is given to the seven roles that already publish holds (`institution_action:publish`): an office that can tell a student about a hold can clear its own. `registration:administer` does not reach it, and a registrar override still cannot waive a hold.

**3. One ledger.** `registration_hold_resolve(want_hold, want_key, want_expect)` uses the registration ledger's own key, replay and commit under a new action `hold_resolve`, so a replay and an `idempotency_conflict` behave exactly as an enrollment's do. `want_expect` is what the office saw and must be `held`; a hold released in between is `stale_hold` and nothing is written. It obeys the ledger's kill switches and `writeback.registration_submit`; the flag's role and cohort narrowing is not applied, because it narrows which students may register and an office is not one.

**4. Its own event type, because none fits.** `registration.hold_resolved` (v1, `education_record`, `audit`) joins `packages/institution/src/events.ts`. No type in the registry names a hold, and the nearest, `source.updated`, is classified `internal` — below the education-record floor a student's hold is, so reusing it would mislabel the event. The payload is the hold's id and its office scope: no student id, no reason, no free text. It is emitted through `private.emit_domain_event` in the same transaction as the release and the `hold_resolved` audit row; the check suite injects an outbox failure and shows the release, the audit row and the readiness stamp roll back with it.

**5. Readiness goes stale by a stamp, not by rewriting the aggregate.** The readiness store's state machine allows `stale` only out of `evaluating`, and every version carries a receipt and an event, so a hold resolution may not rewrite a row's state. Instead `registration_readiness_evaluations` gains `stale_since`, set on every evaluation of that student at that school that had already read its inputs (evaluating, ready, blocked, needs_review, unknown), in every term because a hold is not per term. A stamped evaluation reads as `stale` through `registration_readiness_get`, and `registration_readiness_save` checks its edges from that effective state: the only move is to `reconciling`, which clears the stamp, and the next `evaluating` is a new generation reading fresh input. So an evaluation begun before the change can never be saved `ready`. `subject_id` is taken to be the student's account id as text.

**6. Reads.** `registration_holds_for_office(office)` gives an office its own open holds — id, student account, placed time, never a reason. `my_registration_readiness()` gives a student the effective state of their own evaluations, so the screen says "Something changed — check again" because the server says so rather than by guessing.

**7. Screens.** An office sees a **Holds** tab on Registration (`HoldDesk`) when `holdOffices` finds an office-scoped grant at this school; each resolve goes through `ConfirmDialog` with an `ActionPreview` whose "What stays the same" begins "This is not enrollment", and is retried with the same key after a lost reply (`useAttempts`). The readiness view adds **Your school's record**, read only when the registration module is on. Two new component tests call `vi.mock` and join the isolated list in `app/vite.config.ts`.

**Not done.** An optional `hold_id` on `help_requests`: a request reaches whoever answers its destination, which need not be the owning office, so naming the hold there discloses it — an owner's decision. Sending the release on to the SIS (a later sync may re-place the hold until that adapter exists). An evaluator that re-runs a stale evaluation (nothing calls the readiness store yet). The SIS sync populating `owner_office`. Marking `WF-READINESS-001` beyond `proposed`, adding `POL-READINESS-001` and naming the passport's owners — left to the owner. The passport's degradation word is `needs_review`; the store's is `stale` then reconciliation, and this does not rename states. The database suites ran on PostgreSQL 16; production runs 17.
