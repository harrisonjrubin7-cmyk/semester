## D-1318 · The pilot roster is thirteen register roles, and a test login resolves institutional roles only

**Decided 2026-10-05**, by the assistant under the executive authority the founder delegated in-session (D-1287), when the founder said "you decide" about which roles are the pilot's. The founder can change the list; the tests follow `PILOT_ROLES`.

**Context.** Stream 02 gates on "a test login per pilot role in staging". Nothing in the repository defines the pilot roles, and the register has no pilot flag. Two role vocabularies exist and must not be confused: the register's app roles (`public.app_roles`, 69 of them), which are granted by the operations console's `role-grant` duty alone, and the institutional roles (`UniversityRole`, ten) a membership carries after SSO. No SSO claim and no SCIM group assigns an app role, and a test holds that.

**Decisions.**
1. The pilot roster is the thirteen roles in `packages/institution/src/pilot.ts`: student, undergraduate_student, graduate_student, faculty, teaching_assistant, academic_advisor, tutor, learning_center_staff, career_coach, registrar, university_staff, university_admin, integration_admin. Chosen as the roles a pilot university exercises, each at the register's `usable` rung or above, none company-internal.
2. A "test login" proves the institutional half: a person the IdP vouches for, in a mapped group, resolves exactly the institutional role the pilot expects, through the real group mapping and membership resolver. A mock IdP (`mock-idp.ts`) stands in for the university; it does not speak SAML, which Supabase Auth verifies.
3. Whether a person also holds the app role (`registrar`, `university_admin`, ...) stays the console's decision and is out of this path by design. The mapping is not widened to assign app roles from IdP groups.
4. The IdP group names are placeholders. Real names, the university's IdP metadata and test accounts are human inputs; until they arrive no `scim_group_mapping` rows are written from this list.

**Consequences.** The gate "test login per pilot role" is met in mock form and stays open in staging until the university's IdP and accounts exist. Changing the roster is a one-file edit that the tests then check against the register.
