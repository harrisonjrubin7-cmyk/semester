## D-1187 · Privacy operations extend the existing trust and retention documents in one pack, keep templates out of `docs/evidence/`, and decide no legal question

**Decided 4 Oct 2026** ([`../privacy-operations/README.md`](../privacy-operations/README.md)). A privacy-operations role was asked for a data inventory, rights workflow, vendor review, privacy-by-design review, incident handling, user controls and a counsel queue. Most of that already existed in `docs/trust/`, `docs/operating-model/`, `RETENTION.md` and the legal queue, so a second set of standards would have contradicted the first.

- **What was decided.** One folder, `docs/privacy-operations/`, holds only the operational layer: a 24-row register, a no-account rights path, a vendor intake gate, a PIA triage, a privacy incident sequence, a controls map and a privacy counsel queue. Each links to the document that stays authoritative. Where `RETENTION.md` disagrees, it wins.
- **Templates stay out of `docs/evidence/`.** `evidence.test.ts` requires every file there to be registered with an owner seat and validity period, so only dated, real exercises go there.
- **No legal question is decided.** Roles, clocks, notification duties, the age floor and refusal wording are queued as P-01 to P-20, eight of them new.
- **Held by.** Nothing: it is documentation, and no test scans the folder. The suite was not run on this host.

**Not decided here.** Who answers rights requests, any response time, the breach-notice clock (`SECURITY.md` says 72 hours, five documents say none is approved), and whether to fix the two probable defects in 08 §C4 and §C5, which come from reading the SQL and are unproven until the tests in 08 §D fail against the current code.
