## D-1182 · A migration passes on evidence in seven classes, never on counts; the Migration Center stays the system of record and gets a semantic gate beside it

**Decided 4 Oct 2026** ([`../migration/README.md`](../migration/README.md),
`app/src/lib/migration/`). The Migration Center (D-numbered earlier, migration
`20260929200000`) records counts of one sample file and generates `passed` from
them, which is row-count success. An institution's registrar signs for a
transcript, not a row count.

- **Seven evidence classes**: `count`, `key`, `semantic`, `relationship`,
  `history`, `permission`, `outcome`. A domain passes only when every class has
  a check that examined something. A count-only run, and a check that examined
  nothing, are refusals with named reasons (`row_count_only`, `vacuous_check`).
- **Critical failures are never tolerated and never waivable.** Other
  severities have default rate thresholds that a domain may tighten and never
  loosen (`tighten()` throws). Defaults (0.1% / 1% / 5%) are for the first
  institution to argue with.
- **The institution signs for its own data.** Sign-off roles reuse the
  Center's `ApprovalArea`s plus an independent reviewer, security, counsel and
  the executive sponsor; a signature binds to the evidence head and goes stale
  on new evidence; preparers cannot sign; signatures expire at 14 days.
- **Rollback is honest about its limit.** Full until Semester accepts a write;
  after, only as good as a replay exercised in rehearsal; otherwise roll forward,
  said before cutover. A rollback trigger with no observation is `investigate`,
  not health.
- **Evidence holds digests, never student data**, hash-chained, and every entry
  needs a `retainUntil` from the institution's records schedule: the ledger
  refuses to guess a legal number.
- **Seam to the Center, not a fork.** `bridge.ts` writes open semantic failures
  and structural gate reasons into `RunCounts`, per kind of run, so the Center's
  generated `passed` agrees. The Center's tables, trigger and screen are
  unchanged.
- **Held by.** 114 tests in `app/src/lib/migration/`; the ten workbooks are data
  and a test holds `docs/migration/workbooks/` equal to them. Each guard was
  reverted under its test and watched go red (15 of 15; a no-op control stayed
  green).

**Not decided or not done.** Never run on a real institution's data; no
production tenant exists. Legal questions (consent and custody, accommodation
and conduct records, holds, third-party content, and how an institution's
retention duty meets Semester's promise that deleting an account leaves no
archive) are flagged for qualified counsel, not answered. A rehearsal stage in
the Center is a production schema change and needs the owner's approval. The
Center's `date_iso` still reads `a/b/yyyy` as month-first without a setting;
`mapping.ts` refuses it, the Center is untouched.
