## D-1294 · Where a company role may read a student's row is a written list, held by a database suite; the one read wider than documented is recorded, not changed

**Decided 2026-10-05**, by the assistant under the executive authority the founder delegated in-session (D-1287). The founder, or the privacy owner for the open item, can overrule it.

The product rule is that the fourteen company roles (`app_roles.global`) do not read what a student owns. Until now nothing tested it from the company role's side. `supabase/company-roles-student-data.check.sql` does: for each table naming a `user_id` or `student_id` owner it writes a student-owned row, reads it back as the owner (the control), and reads it as each company role. The set of reads it finds must equal a list kept in the suite, with a reason beside each entry, in both directions: a new read fails, and an entry that stops being true fails until it is removed. The probed count has a floor and can only rise.

Four reads exist today. Three are intended and recorded with their reason: `billing_accounts` / `finance_operator` (the company's billing duty), `data_requests` / `data_steward` (working a data request), `community_volunteers` / `trust_safety_senior` (running the volunteer program). **One is recorded as `TO NARROW` and is not changed here:** `community_volunteers` / `trust_safety_reviewer`. The policy admits `community:review`, which a plain reviewer holds, while the program is the senior reviewer's, and it ignores `tenant_id`, so a platform-scope reviewer reads every school's volunteers, including a free-text `revoked_reason`. Whether to narrow it to `community:review_senior` or to document why plain reviewers need the roster is the privacy owner's call; changing a live policy is not a thing to do inside a test pull request.

Beside it, `supabase/ai-audit-content-free.check.sql` pins the column sets of the two AI audit tables, so a column that could hold a prompt or an answer cannot arrive without being decided in the open.

Two things are decided by *not* doing them. `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy` is not added: it can cut the link an LMS's new-window launch may rely on, and there is no LMS to test one against here (revisit with a real LTI launch). The "grep guards" the handoff proposed are not added: the service-role-in-browser guard (`boundaries.test.ts`) and the three-mark `activity` check constraint already do the job.

Not decided here and not done: the 8 tables the sweep cannot yet probe (`messages`, `grade_entries` and six more, named in `docs/execute/01-platform-core-report.md`); owner columns other than `user_id` and `student_id`; what a `security definer` function a company role may call returns; the rest of the stream 01 work list.
