## D-1053 · The executive-assistant layer starts as pure modules; Today, Grades and Registration take the first wiring

**Decided 1 October 2026.**

Three specs for an academic executive assistant were checked against `origin/main` before anything was written. Most of what they describe already exists (recovery, changes, what-if, the Today decision surface, registration day, no-wrong-door routing). What was absent is added as pure modules with tests in `app/src/lib/`:

- `triage.ts` (seven assignment states, private queue order), `deadline-groups.ts` (the five horizons), `outcome-scenarios.ts` (fixed-average scenarios, required-score line)
- `guide-bar.ts` and `calm-controls.ts` (the Semester Guide model, snooze, and the student's calm settings)
- `weekly-reset.ts` (five-step skippable reset; reflection private by default), `plan-recovery.ts` (original plan kept as Scenario A, only `confirm` returns a changed plan), `goal-plan.ts`
- `semester-packets.ts`, `decision-compare.ts` (no winner field, unknown cells say so), `ask-human.ts`, and `calm-language.test.ts` (banned-phrase guard)

Wired: the Guide on Today (`TodayGuide`), Course Outcome Scenarios on Grades (closed until opened; refuses on incomplete weights).

**Registration Mode now shows 72 hours out, not 7 days.** This reverses the window in `docs/REGISTRATION-DAY-MODE.md`, because the spec asks for 72 hours. A window further out is still reachable by turning the mode on.

- A scenario is refused, not warned about, when weights do not total 100, nothing is scored, or nothing is left.
- The queue's weighting is private and returns an order, never a number; Today's action cards still show their score parts (`actions.ts`). Reconciling the two is open.
- `queue()` ranks but the equity gate matches on function names and does not see it. It needs an owner decision on review before a screen uses it.
- Two new device stores are named in the backup coverage test as exempt: `semester.guide-choices.v1` (an expiring display marker) and `semester.calm.v1` (the student's settings, a candidate for the backup).

**Not done:** no screen yet for triage states or deadline groups, the Weekly Reset, Recovery Mode, goal plans, packets, decision comparison or the calm-settings controls; the Guide is on Today only; calm settings have no editor, so the Guide runs on calm defaults. The language guard allowlists three existing uses of "you are behind" (the name of the "When you are behind" screen); renaming it is a product decision.
