## D-1180 · Start the workspace with npm workspaces for `packages/*` only, and defer pnpm and Turborepo

**Decided 2026-10-04.** The root `package.json` declares workspaces for `packages/*` and nothing else. `app/`, its lockfile, every workflow and both Vercel projects are unchanged. `app/` joins the workspace in a separate pull request, once the workflow edits it needs can be proven by CI, and the pnpm and Turborepo choice of P-01 in [`docs/target-architecture/03-TECHNOLOGY-DECISIONS.md`](../target-architecture/03-TECHNOLOGY-DECISIONS.md) is taken after that.

Why: the conversion plan's first pull request said that putting `app/` in a workspace moves nothing. It does. Every CI job runs `npm ci` in `app/` against `app/package-lock.json`, and the two Vercel projects use `app` and `company-site` as root directories, so moving the lockfile edits every workflow and depends on dashboard settings that cannot be checked from the repository. P-01 already names npm workspaces as its fallback, so this is the same direction taken in smaller steps, not a different one.

Measured: on `main` `dac31c9` the suite is 1,265 files, 19,744 passed, 48 skipped, before and after, including under shuffled order.

Not decided and not done: `app/` joining the workspace, a pnpm migration, `turbo.json`, module-boundary checks. Whether the Vercel projects build with a root `package.json` present is unverified.
