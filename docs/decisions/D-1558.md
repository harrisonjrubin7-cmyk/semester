## D-1558 · The developer portal is built as phased vertical slices, starting from a recorded inventory

**Decided 2026-10-13.** The developer portal (the uploaded working specification) is scoped as one
comprehensive v1 design, delivered in phases P0–P9 (see `docs/developer-platform/ECOSYSTEM-INVENTORY.md` §13).
Each phase is a vertical slice with its own exit evidence. No phase reduces the v1 scope.

Why: the specification is design-stage, and the repo's own passport
(`docs/passports/developer-ecosystem.md`) calls it "planning contract only." Building before the
inventory exists would duplicate existing control-plane tables and claim coverage nobody verified.

Not done:

- No code, migration, or test has been written for the portal.
- Production and staging deployment are not authorized (B-7).
- The three source documents the specification cites are not in this repository (B-1).
- Terms placeholders are unresolved (B-2). Institutional authority is unspecified (B-4).
- The reuse-or-add decision for control-plane tables is open (§14, question 3).
