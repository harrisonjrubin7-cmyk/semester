## D-1342 · Per-role onboarding is not coded into the app; it waits for a published journey, and the existing first run is audited meanwhile

**Decided 2026-10-06**, by the assistant under the executive authority the founder delegated in-session (D-1287). The founder, or the product owner, can overrule it.

**Context.** Stream 02 asks for onboarding for each of 13 roles (sign in, connect, privacy choice, AI choice, first home) and an accessibility check per role. Checking main first: D-1330 merged the server side of onboarding as versioned journeys (`onboarding_journeys`, `onboarding_assignments`, `onboarding_step_progress`), written only by functions that read `auth.uid()`, with no journey seeded and nothing in the app calling it. It says what a tour contains is a product decision made by publishing a row. The design source for per-role content, `templates/role-onboarding/`, was never copied into the repository (`docs/handoff/IN-THIS-REPO.md`). The app's first run is one generic five-step flow with no role branching.

**Decisions.**
1. Thirteen role flows are not hard-coded in the app. That would be a second design beside the journeys D-1330 built, with content nobody has supplied. Per-role onboarding is built by publishing journeys and rendering them, once the templates are re-supplied and a product owner decides what each role is told.
2. In the meantime the existing first run is audited properly. Only step 1 of 5 was under axe; steps 2 to 5 are now audited at desktop and phone widths, each reached by pressing the primary button, because the app does not restore a stored step and seeding one audits step 1 five times. The step shown is asserted to be the step audited.
3. The stream's gate "onboarding a11y check per role" is therefore met for the one flow that exists, and stays open per role until role flows exist.

**Consequences.** Nothing a student sees changes. The next stream 02 slice is the sessions list with per-session sign-out and an account switcher, which needs no product content.
