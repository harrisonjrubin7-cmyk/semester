## D-1203 · The Migration Center refuses an ambiguous slash date until the order is chosen, and does not store the choice

**Decided 4 Oct 2026** ([`../migration/README.md`](../migration/README.md),
`app/src/lib/migration/center.ts`). `date_iso` read every `a/b/yyyy` as
month-first. `3/7/2026` is 7 March at one school and 3 July at another, so a
day-first export produced valid, wrong dates that every later check accepted.

- **What changed.** A slash date is read when it cannot mean the other thing
  (a part above 12, or both parts equal) or when the lead has chosen month-first
  or day-first on the evidence screen. Otherwise the row fails as ambiguous.
  A declared order is held to: a date that does not exist that way is not a date.
  Other date forms are unchanged.
- **Not stored.** The order is a choice made while previewing. Storing it per
  project would be a column on `migration_projects`, a production schema change
  that needs the owner's approval, and `TRANSFORMS` is held word for word to the
  database check constraint. The cost is that the lead re-chooses on each file.
- **Held by.** Tests in `center.test.ts` and `MigrationCenter.test.tsx`; three
  reverts (month-first default, order not passed from the screen, `day_first`
  read as month-first) each turned them red.

**Not decided here.** Whether the order becomes a stored project setting.
