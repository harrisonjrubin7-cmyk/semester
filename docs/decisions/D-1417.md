## D-1417 · Source-issued grades, transcripts, aid and guardian projections are online-only

**Decided 10 October 2026.** This records the Milestone 8 repository
foundation. It does not prove adoption by every legacy browser store, a native
encrypted client, deployment, provider approval or institution activation.

- The offline data class is the authority for durable device admission. A
  server response, tenant setting or caller cannot broaden it.
- Source-issued grades, academic records/transcripts, financial aid and
  guardian projections use `cache: never`. Tenant opt-in cannot override that
  decision. Student-authored estimates, grade tracking and financial plans are
  distinct student-owned data and are not reclassified as official records.
- The sync engine enforces the classifier before recovery and each sync, and
  before applying a server-feed change. Old or malformed prohibited entity and
  outbox rows are removed transactionally.
- A content-free purge hook names only class, id, kind and reason so platform
  adapters can erase matching attachment, preview and search-index material
  without receiving the protected payload.
- Attachment reads delete expired or newly prohibited bytes and wrapped keys
  immediately. Foreground/after-sync sweeps remain the bulk cleanup path.
- Unknown classes fail closed. Default-denied classes are purged when tenant
  opt-in is withdrawn. Tests exercise the exact four protected record groups
  at policy, prior-state purge, server-feed and attachment-cache boundaries.
