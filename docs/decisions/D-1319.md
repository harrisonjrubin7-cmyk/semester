## D-1319 · Semester owns its financial layer behind a replaceable payment-rail adapter, keeps its own billing ledger apart from the schools', and builds it in shadow with the checkout hold unchanged

**Decided 5 Oct 2026**, on the owner's instruction to merge the design ([#1312](https://github.com/harrisonjrubin7-cmyk/semester/pull/1312)) and continue. This records the direction that instruction covers and nothing wider.

- **What was decided.** The fourteen documents under `docs/finance/` (start at `NATIVE_FINANCIAL_PLATFORM.md`) are the direction for Semester's own financial layer: Semester owns the catalog, pricing, checkout screens, billing, ledger, refund and dispute workflow, reconciliation, console and customer notices; a regulated rail moves the money, behind one adapter interface (`supabase/functions/_shared/payments/`). Stripe is the first adapter, not the architecture. Semester's own billing gets its own double-entry ledger, kept apart from the school student-account ledger (`ORDERING-AND-BILLING-OPERATIONS.md` already says the second must not be Semester's accounting ledger). Every build step is shadow or sandbox until its `FRG-nn` gate.
- **Built with this.** The first slice (actions 2 to 5 of the plan): the adapter types, a reference mock rail, a Stripe adapter that wires verify and read only, a normalizer proven equal to `billingwebhook.ts` over 28 events, and a contract suite shown red against rails built to break it. No Edge Function imports any of it yet.
- **Held by.** `app/src/lib/payments/` (58 tests): the contract suite with the exact failed set pinned for each broken rail, the parity test with three planted normalizer bugs shown red, and a structural scan that refuses a card or bank-account field in any payments module.
- **Stays as it is.** D-146 (no money moves through Semester, no card stored) and D-1236 (a licensed processor holds funds) stand. Both checkout-hold constants stay false: `billing-checkout/index.ts:27` and `lib/plans.ts:19`. Cancel and billing history are untouched.

**Not decided here and not done.** Any price, discount band, refund window or approval threshold (D-134 against D-1154 is still the owner's, and the proposed bands in `docs/finance/05-BUDGET-GOVERNANCE.md` are not adopted, so the approval design blocks every request until they are). Who counsel, the qualified accountant, the tax adviser and the payments adviser are (D-1154: none is engaged). Any PCI determination or SAQ type; any claim that Semester is or is not a money transmitter. Whether embedded card fields, Semester-scheduled renewals, AI overage billing, a second rail, a wallet, payouts or financing happen at all (`EMBEDDED_FINANCE_ROADMAP.md` stages 3 to 5 each need a new owner decision superseding D-146 and D-1236, and counsel). Opening individual paid acquisition, or a paid institutional pilot. Reading `billing-webhook` through the adapter (action 8) is the next step and is its own pull request.
