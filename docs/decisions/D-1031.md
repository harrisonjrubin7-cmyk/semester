## D-1031 · Semester's shared AI key serves nobody until the owner's provider decisions are recorded with evidence and the deployment switch is set

**Decided 30 Sep 2026.** The `claude` function held Semester's Anthropic key
and served every signed-in account whenever the secret was set. On 29
September it did: the kill-switch drill got 200 from production. Semester had
no legal entity, had accepted no provider terms in its own name, and had
decided nothing about student records, retention, or who may be served
(D-147, `docs/trust/PROVIDER-TERMS.md`).

- **Two halves, and either alone serves nobody.** First, a record of five owner
  decisions in `supabase/functions/_shared/provideractivation.ts`. Each one is
  `recorded` only with an evidence file under `docs/evidence/vendors/` and a
  date. The five are:
  - a legal entity;
  - Anthropic's Commercial Terms, accepted by that entity;
  - a student-records decision (a signed addendum, or counsel's opinion that
    none are sent);
  - a retention setting;
  - an approved audience that covers individual accounts.

  Second, `SHARED_AI_PROVIDER=on` on the deployment, exactly `on`.
- **Asked first.** The function asks before it reads the key, checks the
  caller, counts the call or reaches Anthropic. Until both halves hold it
  answers 501 with `shared_provider_not_activated` and one sentence, which the
  app shows as it is.
- **Nothing is recorded,** because nothing has happened. All five are
  `pending-owner`. `provideractivation.test.ts` refuses a recorded field whose
  evidence is not in the tree, refuses any record at all while
  `docs/evidence/vendors/` is absent, and keeps accepted terms in step with
  `provider-terms.ts`. It also refuses a workflow, config or example that sets
  the switch on.
- **The owner's checklist** is `docs/trust/SHARED-PROVIDER-ACTIVATION.md`,
  rendered from the record.
- **Not changed:**
  - A student's own key and the institution gateway, which spends a
    university's key under that university's policy, are not Semester's key,
    and are not behind this gate.
  - Merging deploys the function (`functions.yml`), which turns production's
    shared key off. That is the intended default. It is the owner's call when
    to merge.
