## D-1293 · The master brief's indigo/slate palette is offered as a ground and an accent, not made the default, and gets no token namespace of its own

**Decided 2026-10-05**, by the assistant, in answer to the owner asking twice for the brief's palette to be implemented. The owner can overrule any of the three below; none is a legal, financial or production step.

**1. Offered, not the default.** `semester` is the fourteenth entry in `GROUNDS` and the twelfth in `ACCENTS` (`app/src/lib/look.ts`), chosen in Settings → Look. The default ground stays Ink. Evidence: the three supplied handoff packages (Parts 1 and 2 and the student screens) share one token system, Ink and Parchment grounds with the sterling and Semester-blue accents, and none contains `#4F46E5` or `#F8FAFC`; the only hit in any of them is a pasted generator script among the uploads. The brief that carries the indigo palette is a briefing note, and says to "use the existing Semester design system where it exists".

**2. No `--semester-*` namespace.** The brief names `--semester-bg-page`, `--semester-primary` and so on. The repo's semantic layer is `--surface-*`, `--text-*`, `--border-*`, `--action-*`, `--status-*`, and a second vocabulary for the same jobs is the second system `DESIGN-SYSTEM-GUIDE.md` exists to prevent. The brief's values reach every one of those variables through the ground and accent instead.

**3. The accent's text shade is `#4338CA`, not `#4F46E5`.** `#4F46E5` is 4.20:1 as tag text on Fog's accent wash and `lib/contrast.test.ts` refused it (4.5:1 needed). `#4338CA` is the brief's own primary-hover value.

Making it the default would change the first impression of every account and retune every screenshot and contrast baseline; the reversible step is to offer it, and flipping the default later is one line (`DEVICE_LIGHT` for light-mode devices, or the `ground: 'ink'` default in `state/shape.ts`).

Not decided here and not done: making `semester` the default; adding the brief's `-subtle` status washes as new tokens (the existing `--app-warn-wash` family covers the job and is measured); any change to the public site's fixed Graphite/Brass palette.
