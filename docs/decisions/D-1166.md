## D-1166 · Hold every AI call site to a child-level inventory, and keep AI governance design in `docs/ai-governance/`

**Decided 2026-10-04.** The family-level AI inventory said each route, prompt and tool set needs a child entry and none existed. `app/src/lib/governance/ai-systems.ts` is those children for what is in the tree (4 doors, 9 systems), and its test fails when any source file that reaches a model, by static import, dynamic import or the institutional client, is not listed. A system's risk tier is the highest of its action tier, whether the model chooses tools, and whether its sources are bound to an institution; a confirmation button enables a tier and never lowers one. The design (gateway, retrieval, tool broker, features, evaluation, shutdown, console, transparency, red-team and launch gates) is [`docs/ai-governance/`](../ai-governance/README.md), the detailed design inside P-09 of D-1144.

Two defects fixed with controls: `confirm` on the institutional gateway now reads `kill.ai_generation` before it claims an action, so a refusal leaves the action unspent; the institutional instruction turn now says its sources are data.

Decided 2026-10-04 and held by `app/src/lib/aistatus.ts`: the client door obeys a fresh signed AI status on every route, fails a school-managed account closed when the status is missing or stale, fails an individual open with a banner, and never fails open from a last-known kill. An answer whose audit record cannot be written is now discarded (`audit-unavailable`).

The 22 consumer tools are registry rows in `app/src/lib/governance/ai-tools.ts` (TB-01), held to the tree and to each tool's own `reach` and `undo`; a tool with no row fails the suite. It is metadata and changes no runtime behaviour.

Recorded as a fact, not fixed: a server kill switch reaches 2 of 5 routes, because the consumer `ask()` door never reads it. A test holds the consumer door to not reading it, so the record is corrected the day that changes.

Not decided and not done: no system has met its tier's gate and every owner seat is unassigned; every evaluation threshold is a proposal with no baseline; admissions and accommodation decisions are not yet intake refusals; the client door, retrieval service, tool broker and console are requirements, not code; cloud and region stay with P-12; every legal conclusion stays with qualified counsel. A human review of the design is still open.
