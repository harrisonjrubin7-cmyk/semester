## D-1167 · Adopt the accessibility and inclusive-design program as the proposal, with evidence rules before claims

**Decided 2026-10-04 (proposal status: the accessibility lead and counsel accept each part separately).** Accessibility becomes a gate at every stage (G0 design, G1 pull request, G2 integrate, G3 ring promotion, G4 institutional launch, G5 public claim) rather than a final check, and every story inherits the universal criteria U-1 to U-30 plus its role's set. The pack is [`docs/accessibility/`](../accessibility/PROGRAM.md).

The evidence rule is the decision that matters most: a guard or a sweep is regression evidence, a recorded manual pass is evaluation evidence, an independent audit is the basis for an ACR, and none of the first two may be cited as conformance. Today that means no sentence claiming WCAG 2.2 AA conformance, tested-with-a-screen-reader or VPAT availability may be published; the statement is drafted and held. The assistive-technology matrix adds TalkBack and voice control as required for the ACR.

Not decided here and not done: no code, test, CI or flag change (the guards in `TESTING-AND-EVIDENCE.md` §2.3 and the nightly sweep job are each their own pull request); nobody holds the accessibility lead seat; the proposed fix times are unaccepted; no mailbox, panel or audit contract exists; every legal conclusion and the wording of the statement and ACR stay with qualified counsel.
