## D-1546 · Delivery work is tracked as capability increments, checked by a script

**Decided 2026-10-13.**

Every piece of delivery work belongs to one capability increment, carries its owner, its hard dependencies, and its release evidence, and moves through the states in `docs/delivery/operating-model.json`. The records live in `registry/increments/` and `docs/delivery/`, and `scripts/check-delivery.mjs` enforces them: approval needs the required fields, enablement needs a safe-off behaviour, and approval for build or release needs each hard dependency at the minimum state.

Why: the delivery specification asks that no work be marked release-ready without evidence, and that hard dependencies not be bypassed. A script can state those rules once and reject a record that breaks them, where a document can only describe them.

Not decided here: who holds each decision right (every approver is `UNASSIGNED` until the institution names them), whether the checker joins CI, and whether the records move from JSON to YAML as the specification's file names suggest. Deployment is not decided and not done.
