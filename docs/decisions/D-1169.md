## D-1169 · Add the platform primitives as an additive package, adopted route by route

**Decided 2026-10-04.** The identity–policy–audit spine lives in `packages/platform` (`@semester/platform`), documented in [`docs/platform/`](../platform/README.md). It reuses the policy decision point, event envelope and workflow machines in `packages/institution` through one door (`seam/`), follows the target-architecture pack's API rules (URL major, `422 idempotency_key_reused`, tenant derived not submitted), and is adopted by strangler phases in `docs/platform/MIGRATION.md`, never with dual-write of authorization decisions. Its eight ADRs are topic-named rather than numbered, to avoid the collisions this log's history records.

Not done: no route, migration or function uses it; the SQL contract in `docs/platform/schema/` has never run against PostgreSQL and is deliberately outside `supabase/migrations/`; no real isolation adapter exists; the FORCE RLS decision, the flag and entitlement convergence, and the package's final location (`packages/platform` vs the pack's `packages/kernel`) are open; every legal conclusion stays with qualified counsel.
