## D-1240 · An account export leaves out a school's guardian restrictions, for the student and the guardian alike

**Decided 4 Oct 2026** (migration `20261004150000_export_withholds_guardian_restrictions.sql`; found in [`../privacy-operations/08-FINDINGS-AND-RECONCILIATION.md`](../privacy-operations/08-FINDINGS-AND-RECONCILIATION.md) C4).
`private.account_export` is `security definer` and follows every cascade below the account's rows, so the court-order flag and staff note a school records on a guardian link (`guardian_link_restrictions`, readable by the school's staff alone) were in both the student's export and the guardian's. A check written from reading the SQL (`export-withholds-guardian-restrictions.check.sql`) failed with "expected 0, got 1" on current code, in CI on PostgreSQL 17 as well as locally.

- **What was decided.** The table is withheld whole, through `private.export_withheld_tables()`, read where the export gathers the account's own rows and where it follows cascades. The file's `withheld` list says so, as it does for the three columns already withheld. Erasure is unchanged.
- **Why the table, not the note.** The record is about two people at once. Handing either one the other's court-order status is the harm the row policy exists to prevent.
- **Held by.** The database check (11 checks, both people's files, with controls), and a test in `erasure.test.ts` that parses the new function and fails if either read of the withheld list is removed (watched going red: "expected 1 to be 2").

**Not decided here.** Whether a data-access request must be answered with this record, or with a statement that one exists, is a question for counsel and is queued as P-21 in [`../privacy-operations/07-COUNSEL-REVIEW-QUEUE.md`](../privacy-operations/07-COUNSEL-REVIEW-QUEUE.md). This withholds it by default, which is the conservative position for safety, and counsel may require otherwise. Also not decided: the second finding, that consent rows survive erasure inside `tenant_policy_audit_event` JSON. Its failing check, `erasure-clears-consent-snapshots.check.sql`, lives in #1198, red on purpose; the owner chose to hold it until counsel decides P-08.
