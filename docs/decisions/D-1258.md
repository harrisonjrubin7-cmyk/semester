## D-1258 · A rights request is answered by a holder of `data_request:handle`, and every move is audited by the table

**Decided 4 Oct 2026** by the agent working the privacy findings, for the owner to confirm (migration `20261004200000_answer_data_subject_requests.sql`; found in [`../privacy-operations/08-FINDINGS-AND-RECONCILIATION.md`](../privacy-operations/08-FINDINGS-AND-RECONCILIATION.md) C1, C2, C3).
`data_subject_request` could be raised and never answered: `authenticated` has only select and insert, no function updated a row, `verified_at` had no setter, and a status change wrote no audit event.

- **What was decided.** Two functions, `answer_data_subject_request(id, status, resolution)` and `verify_data_subject_request(id, rung)`, gated by the existing capability `data_request:handle` (carried by `data_steward`, platform scope) through `private.has_capability`. No role or capability was added, and the migration grants the capability to nobody. The operator may not be the requester. Legal moves: received to verifying or refused; verifying to in_progress or refused; in_progress to completed or refused; a resolved request does not move; in_progress needs a recorded verification; a refusal needs a reason. Two columns record the rung (V0 to V4) and a SHA-256 pseudonym of who verified.
- **Why the audit is a trigger.** An after-update trigger writes `privacy.request_status_changed` (kind, from, to) and `privacy.request_verified` (kind, rung) through `private.record_audit`, so a change made in plain SQL as the service role is recorded too. The answer text stays on the row and never enters an event.
- **What it costs.** The subject can read their own row, so can read the verifier's pseudonym; the pseudonyms are unsalted (08 C8, untouched). Two `public` definer functions join the register.
- **Held by.** `supabase/answer-rights-requests.check.sql` (17 checks, refusals matched by error code, with a control that the operator path works), watched red before the migration ("function public.verify_data_subject_request(uuid, unknown) does not exist") and green after, and mutation-tested (no requester check, no verify gate, no trigger, text in detail: each red); `app/src/lib/rightsanswer.test.ts`.

**Not decided here [COUNSEL REQUIRED, P-03 in [`../privacy-operations/07-COUNSEL-REVIEW-QUEUE.md`](../privacy-operations/07-COUNSEL-REVIEW-QUEUE.md)].** The response clock, who may answer, and which rung suffices for which requester. No response time is chosen or promised, and the 30-day column default is unchanged. There is still no screen, no overdue alert, and no producer for guardian or institution requests.
