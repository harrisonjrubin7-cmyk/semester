## D-1163 · Growth runs under one operating plan: only G0 is open, messages are classed before they are capped, promotion stops in exam periods, and nobody is paid per sign-up

**Decided 4 Oct 2026**, on merge of this pull request
([`../market-readiness/GROWTH-OPERATING-PLAN.md`](../market-readiness/GROWTH-OPERATING-PLAN.md)).
The growth documents held principles, claim ceilings and in-code consent, but
nothing said who is reached, what is sent and how often, what is posted, what is
tested, or who looks at it. The plan is that layer. It adds no capability, no
claim and no collection.

- **Gates.** Every growth element carries the earliest launch gate it can run
  under (G0 to G3, restating `LAUNCH-CAMPAIGN-PLAN`). Only G0 is open:
  preparation, closed rehearsal, evidence-bounded discovery, synthetic demos and
  educational content with no activation implication. No asset names a
  capability the registry does not permit for the channel.
- **Classes before caps.** Every message is critical service, service,
  lifecycle education or marketing. Lifecycle education is treated as marketing
  for consent until counsel says otherwise. In-app caps stay as the code has
  them (5 important, 1 helpful a day). Proposed, not built: one marketing and
  lifecycle email a week per person, two marketing emails a month, SMS only for
  opt-in reminders, no push or SMS marketing, and a stop after the second
  win-back note at 90 days.
- **Exam periods are quiet.** No lifecycle-education or marketing message and
  no upgrade surface in a tenant's published exam period, or the two
  conventional finals weeks where there is no calendar. Service and critical
  messages continue. Deadlines are product data and are never used to time
  promotion.
- **People.** Nobody is paid per sign-up, activation or referral. Families are
  not an acquisition channel. An institution's roster is never a marketing
  list. No under-18 ambassadors or creators, pending counsel.
- **Experiments.** Consent clarity, privacy and security defaults, accessibility
  access, adverse academic decisions, aid, discipline, accommodation,
  crisis routing and notification intensity are never experimented on. Until
  events exist and are validated, experiments are qualitative and reported so.
- **Held by.** `app/src/lib/gtm/growthplan.test.ts` compares the plan to the
  code (caps, quiet hours, small-cell floor, opt-out alert, SMS keywords, sales
  stages, pillar weights, claim IDs, links, the registry's strongest status) and
  was shown to fail against five planted faults. It does **not** enforce the
  proposed caps, the exam-period rule or the sunset; the plan's section 4.8
  says so, and none may be described as a safeguard externally until built and
  shown to fail without it.

**Not decided here.** Any figure for conversion, activation, retention or
referral; the privacy, accessibility and counsel reviewers (all unassigned, so
the plan's Lanes B and C cannot run); the retention windows in the plan's data
inventory; the reading-level target; each item marked requires qualified human
counsel review; and when any gate above G0 opens, which needs its own GO.
