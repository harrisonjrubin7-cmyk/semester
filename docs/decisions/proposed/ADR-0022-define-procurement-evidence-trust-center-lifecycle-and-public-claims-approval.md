## Problem
How does a statement move from draft to public or to a procurement answer, who approves it, what evidence must back it, when does it expire, and what takes it down?

## Decision drivers
1. No statement stronger than the evidence (the repository's rule: evidence in repo, not readiness).
2. Approval recorded by role; counsel for legal representations.
3. Evidence is dated, retrievable by a reviewer, and expires.
4. Withdrawal is fast and rehearsed (`docs/CLAIM-WITHDRAWAL-RUNBOOK.md`).
5. One mechanism covers site, app copy, RFP answers and trust room.

## Alternatives considered
| Option | For | Against | Why not / why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Review pages by hand before each release | No tooling | Misses `site.js` strings; relies on one reader | Not chosen |
| B. Extend `claims.ts` to cover every public string via ids and make site build fail on unknown strings | Uses existing register | Requires site restructuring | Partly: **recommended** for risky strings |
| C. Static lint of risky patterns (prices, "24/7", "FERPA", "compliant", "certified", "forever") with an allowlist tied to claim ids | Cheap, catches regressions | Pattern lists miss new wording | **Recommended** in addition to B |
| D. Remove the trust page until audited | Safest | Loses disclosures that are accurate and hedged | Option if counsel prefers |

## Decision
**Recommended, unratified.** (1) Lifecycle: draft, evidence attached, approver recorded (role, date), counsel review where the row is counsel-required, published, expiry set, withdrawn or renewed. (2) A public string containing a risky pattern must carry a claim id present in `claims.ts` with status `available`, current evidence and an approved wording; pattern list and allowlist live in one test. (3) C-01 to C-08 are resolved by withdrawal, rewording to the scoped statement already drafted, or sourcing; each resolution is its own pull request with `D-<pr>`. (4) Procurement answers (HECVAT, questionnaires, RFP library) draw from approved claim rows only; unanswered rows say "not available" rather than hedging. (5) Trust-center artifacts have an owner, an issue date, a review date and a retrieval path; generated counts (`CONTROL-FACTS.md`) are produced by a gate on the same commit, not afterwards. (6) The university name, "Vanderbilt University" as origin, and a personal signup host are counsel items before any production claim (C-11, C-12). (7) Backup claim-owner is named. Not ratified.

## Consequences
Positive: an unapproved price or "24/7" fails a test. Negative: copy changes need approval and slow marketing; allowlist upkeep. Harder: improvising answers during a sales cycle.

## Impact
- **Data / tenancy:** trust-room tokens and logs are tenant-neutral reviewer data; retention under ADR-0017.
- **Security:** published security statements must match evidence in ADR-0018 drills and ADR-0020 scope.
- **Privacy:** privacy-notice and cookie wording are counsel's (Q-02).
- **Accessibility:** accessibility statement waits for a manual assistive-technology evaluation (Q-06; claims `a11y-human`, `vpat`).
- **Operations (SLO, alert, runbook, support):** support and SLA statements follow ADR-0025.
- **Cost / commercial:** prices follow ADR-0016; sales timelines follow ADR-0015.
