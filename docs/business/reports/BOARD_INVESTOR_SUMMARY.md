## Where the company is `[VERIFIED]`

A built product in invitation-only validation; no customers, no pilot, no revenue, no paid availability. The go/no-go decision of 2026-10-03 holds the paid institutional pilot at NO-GO/RED. Ten blockers stand between today and a paid pilot, among them an independent security assessment, a qualified accessibility review, counsel-approved paper, entity, tax and insurance authority, staffed support and restore exercises. A single founder holds every owner seat.

## The thesis `[ASSUMPTION]`

Win one narrow, measurable institutional workflow through a pilot, convert it to an annual contract, and use measured student outcomes as the proof for renewal and expansion. The company must be viable without advertising or marketplace revenue.

## What the numbers say `[DRAFT]`

1. Volume is the constraint, not price. At about three pilots a year Base does not carry a team.
2. The first design-partner engagement exists to replace the model's rates with measured ones: conversion, cycle length, implementation hours, support load, AI usage.
3. AI economics need a decision: the included pool may cost more than the platform fee at the repository's higher cost estimate.
4. Delivery capacity is tight: one delivery head covers about 0.3 new implementations a month.
