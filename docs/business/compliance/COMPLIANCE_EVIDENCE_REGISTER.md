## Row conventions

- **Status vocabulary:** `Verified-repository` (a test, check or workflow in the tree fails without it; says nothing about production), `Partially evidenced`, `Planned` (designed or scheduled, nothing operating), `Not evidenced`, `Blocked-external` (needs a person outside the repository: counsel, assessor, vendor, customer, insurer).
- **Evidence:** a real path under the repository root, or `NONE - gap`. A path proves the *design or test* exists. Where the trust register says no operating artifact has been produced, the row says `Partially evidenced`, not `Verified-repository`.
- **Owner:** `Harrison Rubin (interim)` means the named primary under [OWNER-AND-ACCOUNTABILITY-MATRIX.md](../../../OWNER-AND-ACCOUNTABILITY-MATRIX.md); every backup is unassigned.
- **Reviewer:** `unassigned` for every row. No security assessor, privacy lead, counsel or accessibility evaluator is named in the repository.
- **Last review:** `2026-10-05 draft read` means a repository read for this document, not a professional review. **Next review** dates are [ASSUMPTION] cadences (monthly 2026-11-05, quarterly 2027-01-05, annual 2027-10-05) or an event ("on gate close", "per release").
- **Label legend:** [VERIFIED] a row's Evidence path proves the stated design/test; [ASSUMPTION] review dates; [DRAFT] the whole table; [REVIEW: x] flags in the Remediation column.
