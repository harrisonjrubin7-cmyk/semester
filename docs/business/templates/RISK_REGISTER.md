## Method **[DRAFT]** (aligned with the strategy register)

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Likelihood (L) | 1 rare, 2 unlikely, 3 possible, 4 likely, 5 almost certain **within 12 months** |
| Severity (S) | 1 minor, 2 moderate (a deal slips a quarter), 3 serious (a gate slips a quarter or a prospect is lost), 4 severe (a gate is lost or a trust event occurs), 5 existential (company cannot continue as planned) |
| Score | L x S. 15 or more: reviewed weekly (next: 2026-10-12). Below 15: monthly (next: 2026-11-05) |
| Ratings | All ratings below are **[ASSUMPTION]**: inherent (before controls); no control is yet operated, so residual equals inherent. Missing evidence cannot lower a rating |
| Status | `OPEN`, `MITIGATING`, `MONITORING`, `ACCEPTED (dated approver)`, `CLOSED (evidence link)` |
| What cannot be accepted | A P0/P1, anything unlawful, anything customer-owned without customer authority. The founder cannot alone accept a risk that needs a professional |
| Treatment | Avoid / mitigate / transfer / accept, per the company register |

## Operating rules **[DRAFT]**

1. Review score 15+ weekly in [`WEEKLY_REVENUE_REVIEW.md`](WEEKLY_REVENUE_REVIEW.md); others monthly.
2. A new risk needs cause, trigger, contingency and an owner on entry.
3. Closure needs a dated evidence link and an approver.
4. When a GR risk matches an FR (launch) risk, the FR priority governs; when it matches an SR/CR risk, the more conservative rating governs.
5. Use the same vocabulary: stage names from [`stages.ts`](../../../app/src/lib/gtm/stages.ts); decisions recorded as `D-<pull request number>`.
