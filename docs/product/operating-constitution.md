# Semester operating constitution

Status: target company/platform charter derived from the attached founding documents and constrained by repository evidence.

Semester is one operating system for the educational journey: student OS, productivity suite, campus super-app, institutional system, governed AI assistant, trusted marketplace, family/guardian platform, and career/alumni/credential continuity. These planes remain in the architecture. Their individual capabilities are exposed only at the maturity current evidence supports.

## Platform laws

1. One identity, tenant, relationship, authorization/consent, education/life/opportunity graph, integration fabric, AI governance layer, registry, inbox, search, design system, support model, trust model, operations console, analytics taxonomy and entitlement model.
2. No placeholder doctrine: a visible path works natively, works through a verified connector, offers an honest manual/import path, is usable when an institution enables it, or is hidden.
3. Native first: integration improves authority and automation; it does not create foundational value.
4. Authority stays explicit. SIS/LMS/institution/provider records never become student- or Semester-authoritative silently.
5. Privacy and tenancy fail closed. Relationship is not consent; route, flag or role label is not authorization.
6. High-impact actions require preview, confirmation, idempotency, receipt, reconciliation, audit and recovery.
7. Offline scope is allowlisted. Sensitive official records, guardian projections, credentials and institutional controls are online-only by default.
8. `live` is an expiring evidence state, not a source-code adjective.
9. Every capability has named product, engineering, design, security/privacy, accessibility, support, rollback, documentation and commercial/legal ownership where applicable.
10. The company does not claim legal compliance, institutional approval, certification, connection or outcomes without authority.

## Governance and lifecycles

Required forums: founder decision log; product, architecture, design, security/privacy, accessibility and AI review; incident review; data governance; vendor/spend approval; customer escalation; marketplace conflict review; student and institutional advisory input.

| Lifecycle | Current state | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | Broad partial product | First-session value, support, privacy, graduation continuity, outcomes |
| Institution | Plans/contracts, no named activation proof | Agreement, provisioning, map, configuration, UAT, training, go-live/offboarding |
| Guardian | Foundation/sandbox | Verified relationship, consent/projection, revocation, support |
| Marketplace | Partial/design | Partner verification, moderation, disclosure receipt, retention, abuse response |
| Integration | Strong contract, no live registry | Credentials/scope, sync, reconciliation, health, recovery, disconnect |
| Support | Partial | Staffed routing, JIT access, updates, root cause, closure, SLA |
| Incident | Documented/partial | Paging, containment, status, recovery, postmortem, corrective action |
| Account/device | Partial | Registration/trust, recovery, revocation, export/deletion, secure wipe |
| Data | Strong policy foundation | Create/use/share/audit/retention/export/correction/erasure evidence |
| Lifelong | Partial/design | Role transition, portable credentials/portfolio and consented continuity |

The complete thesis can be present from inception through safe native workflows and institution-controlled connected surfaces. It is not truthful to call the whole platform GA today.
