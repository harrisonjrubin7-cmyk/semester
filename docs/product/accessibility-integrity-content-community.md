# Accessibility, integrity, content, community and safety

Status: Phase A cross-cutting gap matrix; repository implementation is broad but operational evidence is incomplete.

| Area | Existing foundation | Launch gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accessibility | Semantic/keyboard tests, automated axe coverage, contrast/target sweeps, reading and preference controls | Independent audit, assistive-technology/device matrix, role-specific UAT and remediation ownership |
| Cognitive/inclusive learning | Reduced-distraction, reading and study supports | Validated learner research, localization/RTL decision, accommodation privacy review |
| Academic integrity | Citation/source tools, governed AI patterns and disclosure language | Institution policy mapping, appeal flow, assessment-mode enforcement and no deceptive AI-detection claims |
| Content/files | Local creation, import/export, documents, media and OCR-related components | Canonical storage authority, malware scan, quotas, versioning, durable sharing, retention and institutional-record separation |
| Community | Groups/chat, membership, reporting, blocking and moderation foundations | Staffed moderation, abuse/spam controls, safety SLA, evidence retention and institution escalation |
| Safety | Help and escalation language, support and incident foundations | Verified campus/emergency directory, human coverage, minor-protection review and explicit non-emergency boundary |

## Universal product rules

- All canonical states have programmatic names, keyboard/screen-reader behavior, contrast, reduced-motion handling and a plain recovery action.
- No accommodation, wellness, conduct or support information is exposed through generic analytics, AI context, community or guardian sharing.
- AI assistance is labeled where material, preserves citations/provenance, respects institution assessment policy and requires confirmation for consequential actions.
- Shared content has owner, classification, version, audience, source, retention, export/deletion and report/block paths.
- Safety language routes urgent matters to verified human services and never implies Semester is a monitored emergency service unless contracts, staffing and tests prove it.

## Required evidence

Manual WCAG-oriented review by qualified humans; VoiceOver/NVDA/TalkBack and keyboard scripts; low-bandwidth and zoom/reflow checks; integrity-policy UAT; malware and forbidden-file tests; community abuse tabletop; crisis-routing verification; accessibility and safety issue owners with response targets. Automated passes do not establish conformance by themselves.
