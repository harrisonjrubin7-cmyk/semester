# Brand and creative direction

## Brand idea

**Semester is the operating system for the educational journey.**

The expression should feel calm under pressure, exact about truth, and quietly capable. Breadth is shown through connected journeys, not feature walls.

## Audience promises

- Student: **Everything you need to move forward, in one place.**
- Institution: **One governed engagement and success layer across the student journey.**
- Family: coordination chosen by the student, never surveillance.
- Partner: verified opportunity delivery with explicit data scope and accountable retention.
- Operator: a read-only-first command center with evidence behind every state.

## Creative principles

1. Lead with the next meaningful action.
2. Show provenance as part of the interface, not legal fine print.
3. Use quiet surfaces and strong typography; reserve color for meaning.
4. Prefer one convincing product journey over a grid of claims.
5. Make manual/native mode feel intentional, not second class.
6. Name uncertainty plainly: stale, draft, self-tracked, pending, unavailable.
7. Use real product captures with revision/date and fictional-data labels where applicable.
8. Never imitate another company's trade dress, iconography, copy, or launch style.

## Visual language

- Typography: retain the current Barlow/Barlow Condensed/Cinzel family while defining role-specific use. Cinzel is a ceremonial accent, never body or dense operational text.
- Color: neutral working surfaces; semantic success/warning/risk/info tokens; one Semester accent. No module rainbow.
- Shape: restrained radii and thin borders; elevation communicates layering or urgency, not decoration.
- Motion: short, interruptible, and disabled by reduced-motion preference.
- Imagery: product truth, student-made work, campus context, and system diagrams. Avoid generic smiling-student stock collages.
- Voice: direct, humane, specific, and source-aware. Avoid “revolutionary,” “AI-powered future,” “seamless,” and unsupported outcome language.

## Surface adaptations

- Student product: spacious daily hierarchy and contextual tools.
- Institution/partner: denser evidence-led workspaces with policy and source context.
- Operations: compact, read-only-first, high-contrast state and irreversible-action friction.
- Public site: editorial pacing, strong proof, role-based conversion, and visible limitations.

## Governance

Brand changes require a source screenshot, affected audiences, accessibility review, claims review, and rollback path. Public copy with legal, security, finance, institutional, or outcome implications requires the corresponding approver before publication.
