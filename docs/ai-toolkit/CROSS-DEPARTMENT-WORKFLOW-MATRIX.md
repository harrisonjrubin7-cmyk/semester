# Cross-department workflow matrix

The brief's matrix, with each cell mapped to what exists. **B** = built in the
toolkit, **E** = an existing Semester screen, **—** = not built (listed in the catalog
as *Not built yet* where relevant).

| Workflow | Universal | STEM | Humanities / Arts | Social sciences / Business | Health / Professional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Understand material | E Study guide from selected sources, quotations matched | E Equations & graph; — simulators | E Study guide; — close-reading lab | B Case analysis template; E diagrams | — anatomy / mechanism views |
| Practice skills | E Flashcards, spaced review (FSRS), practice paper | E Work the problem | — language drills | E Spreadsheet | E Medication-math practice (Work the problem), invented cases only |
| Research | B Search plan, screening, evidence matrix, citation audit | B Evidence matrix (methods comparison) | B Evidence matrix; — primary-source analyzer | B Policy memo template + matrix | B Evidence matrix; — guideline comparison |
| Analyze data | B Dictionary, cleaning log, describe, method guide; E Analyse data | E Analyse data | — corpus analysis | B Data Studio; — qualitative coding | B Data Studio |
| Create work | B Assignment workspace, rubric self-check | B Lab report, technical report templates | B Essay template; E Write | B Policy memo, case analysis templates | — care-plan simulation |
| Present learning | E Make a deck; B presentation template | E Deck | E Deck | E Deck | E Deck |
| Reflect and improve | B Reflection, error-log stages, office-hours prep | B Error-log stage (problem set) | B Revision stage (essay) | B Limitations stages | — supervision prep |

The matrix is the reason the catalog is organised by **workflow first, subject
second**: most cells are the same universal tool with a different template, and the
subject-specific tools are the minority.
