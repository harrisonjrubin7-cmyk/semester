# Science and DNA learning safety

## Boundary (shown on every science tool)

> Education only: visualization, simulation, and hypothetical or instructor-provided
> data. No pathogen work, hazardous wet-lab protocols or biological design.

## In this slice

- The **DNA Learning Lab** is `restricted`: listed, labelled *Needs review before
  use*, and not openable — tested, including with an approval list naming it and the
  flag on.
- `pathogen-design` and `wet-lab-protocols` are in `NEVER`: no configuration can
  enable a tool claiming them (tested for every `NEVER` id).
- A topic mentioning pathogens, virulence, gain-of-function, toxin synthesis or
  weaponization shows the science boundary on the dashboard (`boundaryNotice`).
  This is a notice, not a filter: the toolkit generates nothing a filter could
  block. Any phase that adds generation must enforce this on the server.

## What a reviewed DNA lab may do

Transcription and translation exercises, mutation simulation, restriction-digest
simulation and gel-band interpretation — on hypothetical or instructor-provided
sequences only. No real-world sequence design, optimization or synthesis planning.
