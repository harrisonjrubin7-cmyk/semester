## Editorial and community rules

Lead with useful education, source/freshness and official-system fallback. Never use identifiable student/customer records, private conversations, sensitive support context or fabricated examples resembling real people. Label synthetic demos. Do not imply institution partnership, authority or endorsement. Separate sponsored/partner content and never sell behavioral targeting based on educational/private data.

Community participation is voluntary; publish conduct/safety/report/block/appeal/moderation boundaries before operation. Staff primary/backup moderation and urgent escalation, age/institution rules, data retention, accessibility alternatives and shutdown/export/closure. Do not optimize outrage, comparison, streaks, popularity or disclosure. Stop content/community scope on unsafe moderation, unstaffed escalation, misleading claims or rights/accessibility failure.
