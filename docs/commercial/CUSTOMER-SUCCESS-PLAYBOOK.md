## Mandate

Customer success owns scope clarity, decision cadence, responsible adoption, evidence quality and clean exit—not unsupported outcome claims, sales pressure or institutional judgment. It does not replace support, incident command, privacy/security/accessibility ownership, implementation, finance, advising or the customer's authoritative systems.

## Lifecycle

1. **Handoff:** verify legal customer/order, approved scope/exclusions, owners/backups, decision dates, data/authority, pricing/billing status, success plan, support, risks and offboarding. Reject an incomplete handoff rather than reconstructing promises later.
2. **Kickoff:** confirm sponsor/champion/technical/trust/accessibility/support contacts, cohort/workflow, communications, change control and stop conditions. A signed order does not constitute launch GO.
3. **Implementation:** track only evidence-backed readiness; surface blockers and dependencies; prevent scope creep and unapproved integrations/data.
4. **Launch/hypercare:** activate only after signed GO; verify channels/telemetry/rights/recovery; communicate limitations; escalate harm or unauthorized scope immediately.
5. **Weekly review:** examine frozen-denominator activation/first win, meaningful use, reliability, support, trust/guardrails, incidents, decisions and next owners. Missing data is unavailable, not healthy.
6. **Midpoint:** customer and Semester decide continue, correct, pause or stop based on outcome trajectory and guardrails. Adoption never overrides privacy, safety, accessibility, rights or support capacity.
7. **Final:** report observed results with missingness/caveats, incidents/rights/support, unresolved risks and sponsor decision. Obtain separate permission for any logo, quote, case study or reference.
8. **Convert, expand or offboard:** renewal/expansion requires new authorized scope and capacity; otherwise export, revoke, delete/retain, transition and confirm closure.

Maintain a factual decision/action/risk/change/evidence record. Customer interactions must not include unnecessary student data, private support content or individual scoring. Escalate product defects and institutional policy questions to their accountable owners rather than improvising.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Pilot, implementation, scorecard, support, health, renewal and offboarding controls define the intended lifecycle.

**Operational evidence.** No activated customer, staffed success function, completed lifecycle, measured outcome, accepted QBR or reference permission is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Assign/train primary and backup; approve handoff/cadence/templates; operate one bounded customer lifecycle; validate data and escalations; obtain customer decisions, offboarding evidence and any reference permission.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may describe this customer-success operating model and use it for conditional planning.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim customer success operation, adoption, satisfaction, retention, impact, renewal, expansion, referenceability or staffed coverage without observed, permissioned evidence.
