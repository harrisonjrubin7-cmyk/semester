## Operating controls

Maintain decision, risk, dependency, change, action and evidence logs. Every change records scope/cost/date/data/authority/metric/support impact and approvals before work. Stop for unauthorized data, failed isolation/rights/accessibility, unstaffed support, unmeasurable success, unsafe target, overdue P0/P1 or customer withdrawal.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Readiness, engineering, legal-draft, product and pilot artifacts define the required work and acceptance categories.

**Operational evidence.** No customer-specific implementation plan has complete owners, dates, target evidence, UAT, staffing or signed launch/closeout decisions.

**Missing test/proof.** Complete the plan with named parties and dependencies; baseline duration/capacity/cost; run target UAT and operational rehearsals; collect approvals at every exit; preserve convert/offboard evidence.
