## Service boundary

Publish separate student and authorized institutional/operator routes, supported scope and staffed hours only after they are approved and tested. Current procedures do not establish 24/7 coverage, contractual acknowledgement/resolution times, dedicated success management, accessibility accommodation operation or an uptime/SLA promise.

## Operating loop

1. Verify channel, identity, tenant, authority and supported environment before discussing account/customer information.
2. Record blocked task, time, route, device/browser, correlation/error, impact/cohort and consented diagnostics—never passwords/tokens, unnecessary course content, sensitive categories or record screenshots.
3. Classify by harm using the canonical on-call policy: P0 active security/privacy/cross-tenant/data-integrity/safety/rights threat; P1 core workflow unavailable, major accessibility barrier, or serious integrity/reliability risk—even when a temporary workaround exists; P2 other material impairment; P3 question/minor issue. These are internal classifications, not promised clocks.
4. Route security/privacy/data rights, accessibility, safety, billing, legal, academic-deadline/official-system and incident matters to named accountable owners. Support never gives authoritative academic, legal, medical or financial advice.
5. Provide a verified safe workaround, owner and next-update expectation only within staffed/approved scope. Use time-limited, least-privilege, consented/authorized and audited support access; revoke it at closure.
6. Escalate P0/P1, repeated cohort impact, unsafe workaround, data loss, monitoring blind spot, missed update or unowned case into incident/problem management.
7. Close with resolution, affected scope, communication, access revocation, user/customer confirmation where appropriate, known-issue/root-cause link and follow-up owner/date.

Weekly reporting is aggregate, excludes sensitive content and suppresses small cells under the approved threshold (current proposal: 10). Report missing queue/channel data as unavailable. Recurring friction feeds product review; it does not justify exposing individual students or blaming users.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Help/barrier surfaces, support tickets/access controls, severity/runbook language and incident/communication paths exist.

**Operational evidence.** No approved dedicated channels/hours, trained backup rota, tested alert-to-ticket escalation, sustained queue record, customer contacts or measured response performance is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Approve/test channels and hours; assign/train primary/backups/domain owners; configure privacy/accessibility handling; exercise intake/access/escalation/incident/closure; validate reporting and obtain customer acceptance.
