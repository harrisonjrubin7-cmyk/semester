## Eligibility and independence

Invite reference participation only after an authorized customer has completed enough accepted scope to support the exact statement, with no unresolved P0/P1 or dispute that makes the use misleading. Participation is optional and separate from product access, support, pricing, renewal, outcome review and ordinary feedback. A discount or contract term cannot buy a favorable statement; any approved material benefit must be disclosed and reviewed.

## Permission record

For each use, record rights-holder legal entity and authorized signer; Semester entity; exact name/logo/quote/result/artifact; factual source/method/population/window/limitations; media/edits/visual context; audience/channels/territory/language; start/expiry; accessibility; compensation; confidentiality/privacy/education-record/IP/advertising review; participant/institution final approval; withdrawal/correction contact/process; archived published versions and approvers.

Student or staff identity, education record, image/voice, role or quote requires its own valid authority and must not be supplied by institutional permission alone when individual permission is needed. Minors and sensitive contexts remain excluded until qualified review approves authority and safeguards. De-identification must consider re-identification from cohort, dates, role and context.

## Workflow

1. Success/customer owner nominates with evidence and verifies that the account is actually an authorized customer—not a prospect, proposal, design conversation or unlaunched order.
2. Claims owner substantiates exact language and ordinary audience takeaway; privacy/legal/communications/accessibility and rights holder review it.
3. Customer/participant reviews final wording, visuals, placement and limitations before first publication; no blanket future permission.
4. Publish only approved version/channels/term; log use and monitor evidence/relationship changes.
5. On expiry, withdrawal, correction, termination, incident or material change, pause/remove/update every controlled channel and record completion. State practical limits for third-party archives where applicable.

Reference calls require separate customer approval, purpose, attendees, topics, confidentiality, scheduling limits and feedback path. Never share roadmap, security/confidential details or student data to make a sale.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Consent, brand, claim and customer-success drafts define required permission and substantiation controls.

**Operational evidence.** No eligible approved customer, executed specific permission, substantiated permissioned outcome, reference call, published case study or withdrawal exercise exists.

**Missing test/proof.** Complete qualified legal/privacy review; establish permission register and takedown process; identify an eligible completed customer; substantiate exact claim; obtain final rights-holder approval; test expiry/withdrawal across channels.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may describe this proposed reference-governance process; it may not name or imply any current reference.

## Prohibited claims

Do not call a prospect, evaluator, design conversation, signed-but-unlaunched order or unnamed institution a customer/reference; do not use names, logos, quotes, outcomes, calls or endorsement implications without exact current permission.
