## Scope and principles

Manage account-level delivery, relationship and contract risk with human review and respectful choices. Churn means an eligible customer contract/pilot ends without approved continuation under a defined cohort/window; offboarding, disqualification and a deliberate no-fit decision are not necessarily failures. Never score or pressure individual students, infer sensitive traits, hide cancellation, block export/delete or use dark patterns.

## Risk signals and evidence

| Domain | Examples requiring review | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| value/scope | agreed first proof unsupported, scope mismatch, missing denominator, no decision path | approved customer measures and narrative; low raw usage alone is insufficient |
| implementation | overdue customer/Semester dependency, failed UAT, unavailable integration, scope creep | stage exit evidence and change log, not elapsed date alone |
| reliability/support | repeated critical failure, unresolved cases, unstaffed route, unsafe workaround | accepted telemetry/support/incident records; no-data is not healthy |
| trust/rights/accessibility | open P0/P1, privacy/security/accessibility barrier, rights/offboarding failure | immediate override/stop path regardless of commercial value |
| relationship/governance | sponsor/champion departure, no authorized decision maker, missed reviews, unresolved dispute | factual decision/action record; avoid subjective sentiment profiling |
| commercial | unapproved renewal terms, disputed invoice/scope, affordability/procurement blocker | approved contract/billing records; payment status never limits data rights |
| capacity/fit | required scope exceeds product/provider/team capacity or becomes high-impact/official | capacity/readiness evidence and no-fit rules |

## Response

Validate data with the customer; name owner/date; correct product/support/data-quality problems; restore access/rights; reduce or pause scope; offer authorized alternatives; document commercial or implementation changes; and maintain offboarding readiness. P0/P1, isolation, rights, safety or critical accessibility failures trigger incident/stop controls, not a save campaign. A customer may decline without repeated pressure.

At exit, capture reason using an approved limited taxonomy plus optional minimal context, final decision authority, unresolved obligations, export/transition, access revocation, deletion/retention/holds, billing/refund/credits, communication and closure confirmation. Request feedback/reference separately and voluntarily. Report churn only with an approved eligible-contract denominator, window and finance/customer definition.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Health, success, support, incident, renewal, billing and offboarding controls support a future risk process.

**Operational evidence.** No customer cohort, validated risk signals/thresholds, churn/retention rate, intervention result, exit history or operated offboarding record exists.

**Missing test/proof.** Approve definitions/taxonomy/access; validate account-level sources and overrides; run shadow human reviews; exercise pause/offboard; reconcile eligible contract cohorts; measure intervention harms/results and obtain customer/privacy approval.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may use these domains for human-reviewed account-risk discussion and transparent exit planning.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim predictive churn, retention, successful save, benchmark rates or automated risk detection; do not target or score students, obstruct cancellation/offboarding, withhold rights or misrepresent a no-fit exit.
