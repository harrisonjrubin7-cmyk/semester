## Offer

**Working name:** Semester Student Success and Registration Readiness Pilot.

| Term | Controlled proposal |
| --- | --- |
| institution/cohort | one approved institution or program; proposed 50–200 participants (target; the hard bounds are 10–200, see `docs/PAID-PILOT-FRAMEWORK.md`), subject to privacy, support, capacity and customer approval |
| milestone | one bounded registration-readiness or term-start workflow |
| timing | proposed 30–60 day implementation window inside a pilot that always runs 26 weeks (D-134, `PILOT_WEEKS`; the deal desk's 6-month maximum is only the outer limit), plus an agreed measurement window and fixed decision/offboarding date |
| inputs | manual/student-controlled or explicitly approved read-only sources first |
| included | onboarding, Today/This Week, course/task/workload planning, non-authoritative readiness checklist, support discovery, controlled reminders, privacy-thresholded aggregate review, weekly decisions and offboarding |
| excluded | system-of-record replacement; official registration/grade/aid/advising decisions; high-impact individual scoring; admissions, discipline, disability, health, counseling, immigration or conduct decisions; unapproved writeback/integrations |
| price | **[APPROVED PRICE OR “NO-FEE DESIGN PARTNER” — REQUIRED BEFORE PROPOSAL]**; taxes, expenses, payment terms and refund/termination treatment require authorized paper |

If institutional data/integration approval is unavailable, fall back to a student-productivity/workload design-partner scope with manual setup, synthetic administrative demonstrations and student-reported measures.

## Responsibilities and gates

The customer provides a named sponsor and operational champion, authorized cohort/data/source scope, student communications, reviewers for privacy/security/accessibility/procurement/legal, weekly participation and a conversion/offboarding decision. Semester provides the approved configuration, training, published staffed support scope, evidence reporting, incident/rollback path, rights handling and transparent limitations.

Discovery, demo, evidence exchange and conditional scoping are permitted now. Execution, payment/invoicing, live data, invitations and activation require executed approved paper plus signed commercial and launch GO: entity/finance/tax/insurance authority, named owners/backups, target tests/UAT, support/escalation, accessibility/privacy/security review, metrics/guardrails, incident/recovery, offboarding and customer approval. Any P0/P1 or disqualifier keeps activation RED.
