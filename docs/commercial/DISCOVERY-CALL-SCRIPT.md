## Opening

“I’d like to understand the student workflow and the decision you need to make before showing Semester. We will separate what is available now, what would require bounded pilot configuration and acceptance, and what is unavailable.”

Ask: what milestone/workflow fails and how is it observed; current people/systems/friction; isolatable cohort; 7–14 day user change; 3–5 leading indicators plus guardrails; minimum necessary data and excluded sensitive categories; acceptance of manual/read-only/no official writes; identity/integration/security/privacy/accessibility/legal/procurement requirements; sponsor/champion/operators/support/approvers/signatory; budget/decision/implementation dates; disqualifiers; and conversion/offboarding decision.

Probe authority and evidence: who owns the source, may approve the data purpose, can accept target configuration, handles incidents/accessibility/support and can sign. Never collect student records or sensitive case examples during qualification.

## Close

Restate problem, cohort, scope, exclusions, measures, data/authority, stakeholders, open evidence and next decision. Offer qualified demo/evidence exchange/scoping workshop or a documented no-fit. Do not promise capability, price, launch date, compliance or outcome on the call.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** The script aligns with current ICP, personas, pilot boundaries and launch gates.

**Operational evidence.** No completed named-prospect discovery record validates fit or stakeholder authority.

**Missing proof.** Run and file discovery, confirm participant corrections, apply qualification/disqualifiers and obtain authorization before advancing stages.

## Claim ceiling

This script is ready for controlled non-activation discovery.

## Prohibited claims

Do not imply a prospect is a customer/design partner, that activation is available, or that requirements are accepted before recorded confirmation.
