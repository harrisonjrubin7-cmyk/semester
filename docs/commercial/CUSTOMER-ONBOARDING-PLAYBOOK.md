## Entry criteria

Begin only with an authorized customer/order or explicitly non-activation design-partner charter, named sponsor/champion and Semester/customer workstream owners. Freeze approved scope, exclusions, cohort/milestone, decision/offboarding date, data/authority, commercial status and support assumptions. No live data, invitations or target configuration before the applicable agreement and approvals.

## Proposed sequence

| Planning window | Outcomes and exit evidence |
| --- | --- |
| days 0–5 | kickoff; RACI/backups; bounded scope/exclusions; issue/risk/decision/change logs; target dates and no-fit/stop rules |
| days 6–15 | authorized paper/data flow; sandbox/isolated target; role/feature/source plan; baseline and metric design; accessibility/support needs |
| days 16–30 | approved configuration/content; integration only where real/authorized; operator/admin/support training; external reviews and remediation |
| days 31–45 | representative student/admin UAT; role/cross-tenant, accessibility, device, rights, incident, restore/rollback, kill/read-only and offboarding rehearsals |
| days 46–60 | launch-council evidence; frozen baseline; support/monitoring ready; signed GO or documented delay/stop; only after signed GO, release approved communications and invitations |
| weekly after launch | aggregate scorecard, guardrails, reliability/support, risks/changes and named decisions |
| midpoint/final | continue/correct/pause/stop, outcome report, conversion under new approval or clean offboarding |

These windows are planning hypotheses, not service levels or delivery commitments. An unmet gate moves the date; the date never waives a gate. Each exit requires authoritative target readback and accountable acceptance—not only a document, repository test or meeting.

## Communication and control

Publish a shared plan with owners, dependencies, current evidence and limitations. Use approved accessible channels and separate operational communications from marketing consent. Record scope/cost/date/data/support changes before execution. Provide a safe manual/read-only fallback or no-launch decision when an integration or approval is unavailable.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Readiness, implementation, trust, product, support and offboarding artifacts define onboarding work and gates.

**Operational evidence.** No representative duration baseline, completed customer onboarding, accepted target, trained backup team, launch decision or onboarding satisfaction/result is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Populate the plan with a qualified customer; baseline effort/capacity; execute every target/UAT/rehearsal; close external reviews; obtain signed launch and handoff acceptance; preserve deviations and actual duration.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may present this as a proposed onboarding sequence and jointly plan dates subject to gates and dependencies.

## Prohibited claims

Do not promise a 60-day launch, complete onboarding, customer readiness, integration, staffing, satisfaction or repeatable implementation timing without completed evidence.
