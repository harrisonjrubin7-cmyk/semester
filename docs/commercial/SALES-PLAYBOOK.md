## Process

1. Qualify problem, cohort, sponsor/champion, authority, risk, timeline, procurement, budget and offboarding before demo.
2. Run discovery and record disqualifiers; do not force a fit.
3. Demonstrate only approved current scope with **available**, **pilot-dependent** and **planned/unavailable** labels using synthetic/approved data.
4. Exchange technical, security, privacy, accessibility, legal and operational evidence with open gaps visible.
5. Produce a conditional scope/proposal with approved price or placeholder, exclusions, dependencies, success/guardrail measures, implementation, support, decision date and offboarding.
6. Do not execute, invoice, accept live customer data or promise launch until entity/finance/counsel/insurance and named-tenant gates pass.
7. Activate only on signed GO; review weekly; convert, correct, pause, stop or offboard from evidence.

Required CRM record: organization, contacts/roles/authority, problem/milestone, cohort, current workflow, data/integration classes, disqualifiers, evidence requests, risks, decision/budget/procurement dates, next action, owner and stage evidence. Do not store unnecessary student or sensitive personal data.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Qualification, demo, claims, pilot, legal-draft and readiness packages support controlled discovery and evidence exchange.

**Operational evidence.** No approved paying institutional deal, staffed deal desk, executed paper, price authority or activated customer is evidenced.

**Missing proof.** Assign backups/reviewers, qualify a named partner, approve price/paper/insurance, close target gates and record signed commercial and launch decisions.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may conduct non-activation discovery, demos, evidence exchange and conditional scoping.

## Prohibited claims

Do not accept an unconditional launch obligation, invoice/charge, live data, customer reference or enterprise commitment while the applicable gates are red.
