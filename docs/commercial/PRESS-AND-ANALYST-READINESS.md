## Readiness package

Maintain an approved, dated source of truth for company/legal identity, spokesperson/contact, product description and availability, target user/buyer and category language, current screenshots/demo, architecture/data/AI boundaries, accessibility/privacy/security facts and limitations, pricing status, customer/partner/reference permissions, measured results/methodology, leadership bios/photos/right-to-use, brand assets/rights, funding/team/company facts, FAQ/objections, crisis/correction route and claim evidence expiry.

Every statement is classified as observed fact, repository-tested, proposal/roadmap, third-party opinion or unknown. Qualifiers must remain adjacent and understandable. Current baseline authorizes a student-controlled planning/action description and non-activation discovery; it does not authorize customer names, institutional activation, market leadership, GA/enterprise readiness, compliance/certification, independent-security clearance, universal accessibility, live integration, 24/7 support, uptime, outcome/causal impact, traction/revenue/funding or approved price claims.

## Briefing process

1. Confirm outlet/analyst identity, purpose, audience, format, embargo/on/off-record understanding and exact requested facts. Do not assume confidentiality without authorized terms.
2. Prepare approved key messages, proof table, limitations, non-answers, demo environment and named spokesperson/domain experts. Use synthetic/authorized data only.
3. Review exact customer/partner/logo/quote, privacy/security, accessibility, competitive, financial, legal and forward-looking statements with authorized owners.
4. During briefing, distinguish fact from roadmap, correct mistakes immediately and avoid speculation. Record material questions and follow-up owners.
5. Fact-check only verifiable facts when offered; do not demand favorable framing. Log published coverage, material inaccuracies, correction request and claim/evidence updates.

Security/privacy incidents, litigation/regulatory matters, customer-confidential information, vulnerabilities, financial/funding facts and personnel matters route to counsel/authorized owners. Never disclose exploit details or personal/customer data to answer quickly.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Claims/evidence registers, public product/company materials and legal/trust/engineering packages support preparation of a bounded fact sheet.

**Operational evidence.** No approved press kit, spokesperson rehearsal, media list/briefing, analyst assessment, customer/partner permission, permissioned results or published correction history is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Verify company/leadership/financial facts; approve message/fact/FAQ package and assets; reconcile public product state; obtain specific third-party permissions; train/rehearse spokesperson and correction/crisis paths.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may respond to inquiries with approved, evidence-bounded current facts and prominent limitations.

## Prohibited claims

Do not announce or imply launch, customer/partner, endorsement, funding/revenue/traction, market leadership, certification/compliance, universal accessibility, live integration, enterprise readiness or measured impact without authoritative evidence and permission.
