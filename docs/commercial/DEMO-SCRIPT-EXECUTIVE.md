## Before the demo

Confirm approved revision/environment, synthetic or authorized data, visible demo/beta label, working path, limitations, capability legend, accessibility needs, no customer logo/identity and a safe failure fallback. Never enter live student/customer data into an unapproved environment.

## Flow

1. **Problem (1 minute):** restate the prospect’s workflow and decision; confirm this is exploration, not activation.
2. **Student first win (4 minutes):** limited manual context → Today → one source-aware action → correction/defer/help. Show what stays local and what is not official.
3. **Institutional first proof (3 minutes):** explain bounded cohort/workflow, privacy-thresholded aggregate evidence and sponsor continue/correct/stop decision. Label the institutional view as synthetic/pilot-dependent.
4. **Implementation and risk (3 minutes):** manual/read-only first, roles/data scope, accessibility/support, monitoring/recovery, weekly review and offboarding. State the open gates.
5. **Decision (2 minutes):** propose discovery follow-up, evidence exchange, scoped workshop or no-fit; no launch date or unconditional proposal.

Use legend throughout: **available in the demonstrated repository/public scope**, **requires named-pilot configuration and acceptance**, **planned/unavailable**. Show source/freshness and official-system fallback; demonstrate one recovery/limitation rather than hiding it.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Current first-win, pilot-first-proof, claims and readiness materials support this bounded narrative.

**Operational evidence.** No named prospect acceptance, approved live tenant, customer outcome or permissioned reference exists.

**Missing proof.** Rehearse exact environment, approve claims/accessibility, capture prospect questions/corrections and validate next-step authority.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may use this script for a synthetic, evidence-bounded non-activation executive demo.

## Prohibited claims

Do not imply production institutional operation, live integrations, customer approval, measured outcomes, compliance, GA or enterprise readiness.
