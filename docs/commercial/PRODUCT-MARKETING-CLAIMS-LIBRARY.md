Always state environment, population, date, method and material limitations near the claim. Prices, integrations, customer names/logos, statistics, outcomes, certifications, compliance, accessibility, uptime/support, RTO/RPO and institutional approval remain prohibited unless their authoritative evidence and approvers are attached.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Capability registries, tests, public smoke and controlled readiness packages support the narrow formulations above.

**Operational evidence.** No channel/audience copy, counsel approval, broad deployment evidence, customer permission or outcome study authorizes broader claims.

**Missing proof.** Attach exact copy/channel/audience, substantiation, disclosures, accessibility review, required specialist/counsel approvals, expiry monitoring and takedown owner before publication.

## Claim ceiling

These formulations are internal ceilings, not automatic publication approval.

## Prohibited claims

No unsupported “enterprise-ready,” “compliant,” “secure,” “accessible,” “proven,” “unlimited,” “official,” “live integration,” guaranteed result or named-customer claim.
