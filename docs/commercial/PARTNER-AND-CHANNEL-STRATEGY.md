# Partner and Channel Strategy

| Control | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | **CONTROLLED STRATEGY — NO APPROVED PARTNER, RESELLER OR CHANNEL OPERATION** |

| Evidence date | 2026-10-03 at repository revision `a86b3376` |

## Objective and boundary

Use partners only when they improve trusted discovery, implementation or support without obscuring authority, increasing surveillance, creating unsupported commitments or bypassing customer/product gates. A conversation, integration listing, referral, event, logo, named institution or memorandum is not a partnership, endorsement, customer, reseller authority or production connection.

## Channel hypotheses

| Channel | Intended role | Preconditions and guardrails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| advisors/faculty/student-success and campus organizations | trusted discovery/workshops around a bounded student job | institution/individual authority, no official-status implication, voluntary participation, accessible materials, no education-record disclosure |
| student ambassadors | peer demonstration and feedback | training, conduct/safety, no pressure/incentive tied to student data or signups, clear non-official role, supervision and stop path |
| implementation/integration consultants | approved scoped configuration and customer coordination | competency, contract/DPA/security, least privilege, training, audit, customer authority; cannot enable unavailable capability |
| technology/provider ecosystem | documented interoperability/evidence exchange | approved adapter/sandbox/terms/security/privacy/data scopes; listing/compatibility language substantiated; provider status is not institutional activation |
| associations/events/content collaborators | education and qualified introductions | audience/claims/accessibility/sponsorship disclosure, contact consent, no list scraping or implied endorsement |
| referral/reseller | only after direct motion, pricing, support, attribution, refunds, territories and customer experience are repeatable | written authority, compensation disclosure, brand/claim controls, data minimization, conflict/termination and customer ownership rules |

## Evaluation and lifecycle

Record legal entity/owners, purpose, audience, capabilities, financial/conflict terms, data/system access, security/privacy/accessibility, sanctions/ethics/reputation, subcontractors, insurance where required, operational capacity, claims/brand rights, incident/support, audit, termination/transition and evidence. Approve a limited pilot with owner, success/guardrails, expiry and offboarding; review delivery and harm before expansion. Revoke access/assets/claims and notify affected customers when a relationship ends.

Partner-sourced contacts retain their original consent/purpose; a partner cannot consent to unrelated individual marketing. Do not share student/customer data, credentials, confidential artifacts or prospect lists without explicit authority and minimum scope. Discounts/commissions never buy a customer claim, favorable research result or safety/accessibility compromise.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Channel, ambassador, vendor, integration, claims, privacy and commercial controls support due-diligence design.

**Operational evidence.** No approved partner inventory, executed channel agreement, referral/reseller program, trained partner, shared customer, attributed conversion or measured channel quality is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Select a bounded use case; complete legal/security/privacy/accessibility/finance diligence; execute scope/permissions; train/test access and claims; run one monitored partner cycle; reconcile attribution/support/termination and customer feedback.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may discuss these as partner/channel hypotheses and conduct confidential due diligence without implying a relationship.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim partnership, endorsement, certification, marketplace status, reseller authority, integration availability, customer access, channel traction or partner-sourced revenue without executed authority and observed evidence.
