## Position

Semester is a student-controlled academic planning and action layer that helps a person turn courses, deadlines, plans and support options into a clear next step while showing source, freshness and official-system limits. For institutions, the initial design-partner concept is one bounded registration-readiness or term-start workflow—not a replacement for the SIS, LMS, advising judgment or official record.

## Core proof language

Use “designed,” “repository-tested,” “publicly reachable,” “proposed,” “controlled invitation beta,” or “subject to target/customer approval” only when accurate. Say what the evidence was, where it ran and what remains unproven. Lead with student clarity, agency, source visibility, manual/read-only setup, bounded measurement and clean offboarding.

Never promise retention/GPA/graduation causality, universal accessibility, certification/compliance, live SIS/LMS/SSO, official writes, 24/7 support, uptime/RTO/RPO, Vanderbilt approval, production institutional deployment or enterprise scale without current evidence and authority.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Product, trust, legal-draft, engineering and market-readiness packages support accurate non-activation discovery, demos and scoped planning.

**Operational evidence.** No approved broad campaign, paying institutional customer, named activated design partner, permissioned outcome/reference or authorized enterprise claim exists.

**Missing proof.** Approve claims and demo script, reconcile live behavior, qualify a partner, close legal/operational gates, obtain customer permission and file measured results before advancing claims.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may conduct evidence-bounded discovery, demonstrations and scoping using this position and accurate current limitations.

## Prohibited claims
