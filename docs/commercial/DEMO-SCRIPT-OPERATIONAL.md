## Before the demo

Confirm approved revision/environment, synthetic or authorized non-production data, capability legend, accessibility needs, safe fallback and stated purpose. Do not use a customer name, logo, roster, credentials or student record without written authorization. Label every surface **available in demonstrated scope**, **pilot-dependent**, or **planned/unavailable**.

## Flow

1. **Scope and roles (3 minutes):** restate the proposed cohort, milestone, sponsor/champion, student choice, operator permissions and what remains official elsewhere.
2. **Cohort setup (4 minutes):** show the synthetic/manual setup path, minimization, consent/invitation boundary and refused unauthorized path. Do not imply live roster, SSO or tenant activation.
3. **Student operating loop (7 minutes):** minimum setup → Today/This Week → one prioritized action → source/freshness → correction/defer/help → offline/recovery behavior.
4. **Support and safeguards (5 minutes):** demonstrate limitations, accessible alternatives, privacy-safe intake, severity/escalation concept, feature/read-only containment and official-system fallback. State that staffing and response times are unapproved.
5. **Aggregate review (5 minutes):** show only synthetic, privacy-thresholded pilot measures; explain denominator, missing data and why no individual-risk or causal outcome claim follows.
6. **Weekly decision loop (3 minutes):** sponsor/champion reviews adoption, first win, reliability, support, guardrails, scope changes and continue/correct/pause/stop decisions.
7. **Offboarding and next step (3 minutes):** demonstrate export/deletion/offboarding concept, name open gates and choose evidence exchange, scoped workshop or no-fit.

If a path fails, state the failure, use the prepared fallback and record the gap. Never improvise a claim, expose hidden data or silently substitute screenshots for working behavior.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Product journeys, support/recovery controls, aggregate pilot definitions and offboarding materials support a bounded synthetic walkthrough.

**Operational evidence.** No staffed customer operating team, approved target tenant, live roster/integration, measured support loop or customer-accepted report exists.

**Missing test/proof.** Rehearse the exact candidate and roles; complete accessibility review; validate every shown state and refusal; assign support/backup owners; obtain prospect corrections and target/customer acceptance before activation use.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may use this script for synthetic operational discovery and pilot-design discussion.

## Prohibited claims

Do not imply live institutional operation, staffed support, approved response times, accurate production analytics, official workflow authority, customer acceptance or launch readiness.
