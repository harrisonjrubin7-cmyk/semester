> This checklist organizes facts for qualified accounting review. It is not accounting, tax or legal advice and does not authorize an entry, invoice or financial statement claim.

## Contract and customer facts

- [ ] Legal entities, authorized signatures, effective/termination/renewal dates and enforceable terms are identified.
- [ ] Customer approval, committed rights/obligations, scope changes, side letters, concessions and cancellation/refund rights are collected.
- [ ] Fixed/variable consideration, currency, tax, credits, discounts, usage, financing/payment terms and collectability evidence are documented.
- [ ] Pilot, annual license, implementation, integration, support, training and professional-service terms are separated rather than inferred from marketing.

## Delivery facts for accountant determination

- [ ] Each promised product/service, dependency, acceptance criterion and delivery owner is listed; combined/distinct treatment is for the accountant to decide.
- [ ] Price allocation inputs and any standalone-price evidence are provided; absent values remain **[ACCOUNTANT DETERMINATION REQUIRED]**.
- [ ] For each obligation, start/end, customer access/control, acceptance, milestones, usage and evidence source are recorded so the accountant can determine timing/method.
- [ ] Implementation/setup work is described factually, including whether it creates a customer deliverable or only enables future service; do not assume treatment.
- [ ] Variable consideration, service credits, refunds, disputes, termination, renewal, modifications, free periods and pilot-to-annual credits are flagged for review.

## Billing, close and disclosure evidence

- [ ] Executed order, approved price, invoices, collections, credits/refunds, processor/bank reconciliation and accounting-system records agree.
- [ ] Billed, collected, entitled, delivered and recognized amounts are maintained as separate concepts; none automatically proves another.
- [ ] Contract balances, receivables/deferred amounts, costs/commissions, bad debt, tax and disclosures are determined by qualified reviewers under the applicable framework and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Period cut-off, modifications, renewals/cancellations, manual entries and supporting schedules receive preparer/reviewer approval with retained evidence.
- [ ] Product usage/availability evidence used for accounting is complete, immutable enough for purpose, access-controlled and reconciled to the contractual population/window.

## Stop conditions

Do not record revenue from an unsigned/non-enforceable proposal, unresolved customer identity, unapproved price, unperformed/uncertain delivery, missing acceptance where required, disputed scope, unverifiable data or absent qualified accounting determination. Escalate nonstandard terms, bundled services, refunds/credits, variable usage, material financing, termination rights, side arrangements and cross-border/tax questions.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Contract/order/billing concepts and delivery evidence categories exist; this template defines the handoff record.

**Operational evidence.** No executed revenue contract, approved accounting policy/memo, qualified accountant conclusion, reconciled customer billing record, close process or audited financial statement is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Appoint a qualified accountant; select applicable framework/jurisdictions; review executed terms; approve policy and contract memo; configure chart/systems/close controls; reconcile delivery/billing/cash; approve entries and disclosures.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may use this checklist to assemble facts for qualified review and may describe revenue status only as approved by that reviewer.

## Prohibited claims

Do not call a signed order, invoice, cash receipt, product entitlement or repository billing event recognized revenue; do not publish revenue, ARR/MRR, deferred revenue, margin or audit conclusions without approved accounting records.
