## Current launch decision

Evidence-bounded discovery, synthetic demos, evidence exchange and conditional scoping are permitted. Customer activation, broad acquisition, paid availability, institutional production, customer/outcome/reference claims and enterprise launch remain prohibited until their distinct gates pass. “Launch” in working materials must always name the motion, audience, scope and approval state.

## Campaign sequence

| Phase | Permitted work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| internal preparation | reconcile exact product/revision, audience/problem, claim register, landing/CTA route, accessibility, privacy/consent, support/recovery, analytics and correction/withdrawal plan | owners/reviewers approve final assets and exact limitations |
| closed rehearsal | synthetic test traffic/data; test content, CTA, forms, confirmation, suppression/unsubscribe, analytics, accessibility, failure/support and rollback | end-to-end evidence with no unresolved P0/P1 affecting the rehearsal or bounded-discovery scope; production/activation P0/P1 remain explicit blockers for their own motions |
| bounded discovery | direct qualified outreach/content inviting discovery/demo only | monitored consent/response/support, accurate qualification and no activation implication |
| invitation beta | only after individual-beta GO; controlled cohort/capacity and clear beta limits | accepted onboarding/support/monitoring/rights and stop controls |
| named design-partner campaign | only after customer permission and named-tenant launch GO | executed scope, target acceptance, staffed operation and approved wording/logos |
| broader paid launch | separate final GO based on repeatable product, support, billing, outcomes, capacity and legal/commercial authority | not currently authorized |

## Asset and operating controls

Each asset records audience/channel/jurisdiction, objective/CTA, exact claim and evidence/expiry, price status, owner/approvers, consent basis, accessibility (alt text/captions/transcript/contrast/keyboard), identifiers/UTMs, schedule, monitoring and takedown/correction path. No identifiable student/customer data. Distinguish service/transactional communication from marketing. Working opt-out/suppression must operate before live marketing.

Measure qualified next steps and safe activation only when accepted sources exist; traffic/impressions do not prove value. Guardrails include misleading claims, complaints/opt-out/deletion, accessibility barriers, support capacity, P0/P1, privacy/security, unsafe segment effects and cost. Pause the affected channel even when top-line activity increases.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Claims, content, campaign governance, readiness tests, forms, funnel definitions and stop controls support preparation/rehearsal.

**Operational evidence.** No approved broad campaign, live consent/suppression operation, staffed response, accepted conversion instrumentation, launch cohort, customer permission or measured result is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Approve motion/audience/claims/consent; finalize accessible assets; exercise forms/suppression/analytics/support/correction; set capacity/guardrails; obtain the applicable beta/customer/billing/launch GO and preserve results.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may prepare and rehearse campaign materials and run evidence-bounded non-activation discovery within approved scope.

## Prohibited claims

Do not announce GA, paid availability, institutional launch, partnership/customer, integration, certification/compliance, outcome, traction, price or support promise without specific current approval and evidence.
