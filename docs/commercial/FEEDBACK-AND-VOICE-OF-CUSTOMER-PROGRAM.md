## Principles

Feedback is voluntary evidence for improvement, not customer consent, marketing consent, a performance evaluation or proof of impact. Separate product research, support, contractual governance, complaints/rights, testimonials/references and marketing outreach. Participation/refusal must not affect access, grades, support, institutional services, pricing or renewal decisions.

## Program loop

1. **Question:** define decision, population, method, owner, minimum data, risk and what would change; avoid collecting feedback with no decision path.
2. **Authority and recruitment:** obtain customer/research/privacy approval where applicable; use representative accessible recruitment, disclosed incentives and non-coercive alternatives. Do not route around institutional research/age requirements.
3. **Consent:** explain purpose, recording, fields, access, retention, withdrawal and separate optional quote/reference use. Never bundle research with product terms or marketing.
4. **Collection:** combine structured in-product prompts, interviews/usability sessions, support themes, sponsor/operator reviews, accessibility barriers and exit/offboarding feedback. Do not collect unnecessary student content or sensitive categories.
5. **Analysis:** preserve method/sample/nonresponse, distinguish student/staff/buyer/support contexts, code themes with review, suppress small cells and report contradictory/negative evidence. Self-report and anecdotes are not causal outcomes.
6. **Decision:** connect theme to evidence, impact, owner, priority, response and status; close the loop without promising roadmap delivery.
7. **Governance:** honor withdrawal/deletion/retention, restrict raw access, review bias/harm and obtain explicit permission before attribution, logo, quote, case study, reference or public claim.

Track participation, representation, theme coverage, time to acknowledge/decision/close-loop and safety/rights issues—not satisfaction alone. Never create individual sentiment, risk or engagement scores for customer action.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Feedback/help surfaces, pilot qualitative measures, accessibility/reporting routes and research governance concepts exist.

**Operational evidence.** No approved representative research program, participant panel, validated instrument, operated repository, customer permission set, closed-loop history or permissioned quote/reference exists.

**Missing test/proof.** Approve protocol/consent/privacy/retention; recruit representative participants; test accessible methods; train moderators; implement secure analysis/withdrawal/permission records; conduct and close one documented cycle.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may invite clearly voluntary, purpose-limited feedback under an approved protocol and report de-identified themes with method/caveats.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim customer demand, satisfaction, validation, product-market fit, representative sentiment or impact from unsolicited comments, small samples or support cases; do not publish identity, logos, quotes or references without explicit permission.
