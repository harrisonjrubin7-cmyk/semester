The best first institutional design partner is hypothesized to be a private or regional four-year institution, school, program or student-success unit able to isolate one 50–200-person cohort around a registration, orientation, transfer, first-term or advising milestone.

Required: concrete planning/readiness problem; empowered sponsor and operational champion; named IT/security/privacy/accessibility contacts; bounded reversible manual/read-only scope; minimum-necessary low-risk data; 3–5 leading indicators and guardrails; weekly review and decision date; clean offboarding; tolerance for evidence-building rather than unsupported certification or replacement claims.

Disqualify scopes requiring immediate SIS/LMS replacement, official writeback, authoritative degree audit, institution-wide entry launch, unsupported certifications/SLAs, 24/7 support, causal outcome guarantees, individual risk scoring, or health/disability/conduct/counseling/immigration/aid/discipline data. Aggregate reporting needs the approved privacy floor; first scope should not exceed demonstrated delivery capacity.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** The primary pilot offer, qualification scorecard, product boundaries and readiness gates support this narrow hypothesis.

**Missing proof.** Run structured discovery with multiple qualified prospects, record disqualification/fit patterns, select one named partner and obtain sponsor/champion/customer-control acceptance.
