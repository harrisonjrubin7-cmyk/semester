> Draft for commercial, finance and counsel review. It is not an agreement, invoice, launch approval, accounting conclusion or promise of availability.

## 1. Identity and decision

- Institution/program: **[REQUIRED]**
- Sponsor, operational champion and authorized signers: **[REQUIRED]**
- Cohort, milestone and dates: **[REQUIRED]**
- Current workflow/baseline and one bounded objective: **[REQUIRED]**
- Proposal expiry and final convert/extend/stop/offboard date: **[REQUIRED]**

## 2. Scope and responsibilities

List included workflows, features, roles, cohort, environment, data sources and evidence deliverables. List every exclusion, disabled capability, dependency and authoritative system/fallback. Manual/student-controlled or explicitly approved read-only input is the default; official writes, high-impact decisions and system-of-record replacement are excluded unless separately approved and proven.

Attach a RACI naming Semester and customer owners/backups for product, implementation, security/privacy, accessibility, legal/procurement, data/integration, support/incident, measurement, billing and offboarding. A role without a named authorized person is an open gate.

## 3. Delivery and acceptance

Use the controlled implementation plan. Define stages, entry/exit evidence, UAT, training, support hours/channels, incident/rollback, privacy/security/accessibility review, launch GO, weekly/midpoint/final decisions and offboarding. Dates are targets until owners and dependencies accept them; no launch occurs with an open P0/P1.

## 4. Data, rights and safeguards

Attach approved data-flow/minimization, lawful/contractual authority, consent where applicable, retention/holds, export/deletion, subprocessors/providers/AI use, access/audit, incident notice, prohibited data, privacy threshold and customer instructions. State what remains on device and what never enters Semester.

## 5. Measures and guardrails

Attach the success plan and blank scorecard with frozen population, baseline, target/range, source/query/event version, owner, cadence, suppression, guardrails and decision rules. Do not use raw logins or promise causal retention, GPA, graduation or wellbeing outcomes.

## 6. Commercials

- Pilot fee: **[APPROVED PRICE OR NO-FEE DESIGN-PARTNER TERM REQUIRED]**
- Implementation/services fee: **[REQUIRED]**
- Taxes, expenses, payment schedule/terms and PO/invoice requirements: **[REQUIRED]**
- Approved discount/concession and reciprocal value: **[IF ANY]**
- Annual conversion method and pilot credit: **[REQUIRED OR EXPRESSLY NONE]**
- Cancellation, refund, suspension, termination and data treatment: **[REQUIRED IN AUTHORIZED PAPER]**

No figure is final because it appears in code, a mockup, historical decision or this template. Do not invoice, charge or recognize revenue before finance/signing authority approves the order and operations are active.

## 7. Risks, change and exit

List assumptions, known limitations, stop conditions, change control, evidence gaps, rollback, transition assistance, export, access revocation, deletion/retention exceptions and completion confirmation. State that unresolved approval or feasibility may require reduced scope or no-fit.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Controlled pilot, implementation, trust, legal-draft and product artifacts support preparing a conditional proposal.

**Operational evidence.** No completed customer-specific scope, approved price, signed order, named staffed delivery team, accepted target or launch authorization is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Complete every field/attachment; validate feasibility and delivery cost; obtain finance/counsel/security/privacy/accessibility/customer review; execute authorized paper; pass named-tenant UAT and launch GO.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may use this template to prepare a clearly conditional, non-binding proposal for qualified review.

## Prohibited claims

Do not describe an incomplete or unsigned proposal as contracted revenue, a customer, an approved deployment, a committed date, a final price or permission to process live data.
