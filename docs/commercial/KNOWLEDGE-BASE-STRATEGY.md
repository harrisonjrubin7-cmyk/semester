## Purpose and audiences

Provide accessible, source-linked self-service guidance for students, authorized operators, implementers and internal responders without turning documentation into an unsupported product, policy, legal, compliance or service claim. Separate public help, signed-in contextual help, customer/operator runbooks and internal incident/troubleshooting material by access and risk.

## Content model

Every article records stable ID/slug, title, audience/role, task/outcome, owner and backup, source/evidence links, product/environment/version, data/authority boundary, prerequisites, numbered procedure, expected result, failure/recovery/escalation, accessibility/alternative path, privacy warning, related content, created/reviewed/expiry date, approver and status. High-risk identity, data rights, security, billing, incident, institutional configuration and official-system topics require domain approval.

Use plain language, descriptive headings/links, real text rather than image-only steps, keyboard/screen-reader compatible examples and captions/transcripts where media is necessary. Never request secrets or sensitive records in examples. Distinguish current available, customer-configured, proposed and unavailable capability. Link to the authoritative institution/provider for official actions rather than fabricating steps.

## Lifecycle and quality

Intake from support, onboarding, releases, feedback, failed search and known issues; triage by harm/frequency; validate against the exact supported version and role; review accessibility/privacy/security; publish with version/date; measure helpfulness and unresolved handoff without coercion; retire/redirect stale content and update release/support references. Urgent unsafe guidance is unpublished or visibly withdrawn, communicated and incident-reviewed.

Minimum launch set: first-win/onboarding, account/local-vs-sync, sources/freshness/official limits, offline/recovery, accessibility/help, privacy/data controls/export/delete, supported integrations, known limitations, support/escalation and offboarding. Coverage means validated tasks and failure states, not article count.

## Evidence state

**Repository evidence.** Extensive product, trust, implementation, support and operational documentation can seed controlled articles.

**Operational evidence.** No approved publishing platform/taxonomy, complete launch coverage, user-search/helpfulness data, translation/localization process, staffed editorial rota or customer-validated operator collection is evidenced.

**Missing test/proof.** Inventory and classify content; assign owners/reviews; select accessible platform/search/access controls; convert/validate launch set; run representative comprehension/task tests; connect support feedback and stale-content monitoring.

## Claim ceiling

Semester may describe this strategy and use repository evidence to draft versioned help content for review.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim comprehensive self-service, reduced support burden, localized/accessibly validated content, current coverage or customer acceptance from document volume alone.
