Semester’s defensible position is not “another dashboard” or a universal university replacement. It is a student-controlled layer connecting academic material, workload, planning, source visibility and next actions in one coherent experience, with manual/local-first use and explicit official-system boundaries.

Differentiate on: course/deadline context tied to source; one planning/action loop across Today, week and course work; configurable calm/accessibility-oriented experience; student agency and manual/non-AI paths; evidence-bounded institutional proof; and the refusal to turn student-support signals into hidden individual risk scores.

Do not use “only,” “best,” “unlimited,” “more accurate,” “more secure,” “more accessible,” “free forever” or feature-parity claims without a current dated method and equivalent-scope evidence. Compare workflows and boundaries, not cherry-picked feature counts. A competitor’s public change can stale a comparison immediately.
