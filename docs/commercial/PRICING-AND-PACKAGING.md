## Packaging logic

- A bounded pilot may use a fixed cohort/workflow fee plus implementation/services fee, sized by duration, cohort, data/integration complexity, trust/procurement burden, support, reporting and offboarding.
- An annual model may use a minimum platform commitment plus approved sponsored/active-student bands; modules/integrations remain separately scoped to prevent hidden obligations.
- A pilot credit, if approved, defines eligible software fees, excludes pass-through/bespoke costs and expires on the stated decision date.
- Per-student, cohort, module and platform pricing each require discovery; no model is validated by this document.

## Quote and discount controls

Every quote names legal customer, currency, package, cohort/term/workflow/environment, services, data/integrations, support, evidence, exclusions, usage/overage if any, taxes, payment, validity, renewal/cancellation/refund, conversion credit and offboarding. Discounts require authority, recorded reason, floor/margin check, expiry and reciprocal value; never discount away security, privacy, accessibility, rights, support or clean exit work.

Finance must calculate a minimum threshold from observed implementation labor, infrastructure/provider/AI variable cost, support/trust review, reporting, sales/procurement effort, contingency, tax/payment fees and offboarding. Unknown inputs stay placeholders; do not manufacture margin.
