# Company completion model

Status: Phase A company gap matrix. Repository artifacts are not proof that legal, finance, staffing or commercial operations are active.

Semester is operational only when product, trust, operations, company and distribution systems work together.

| System | Repository evidence | Material gap / approving authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | Broad PWA, 60-capability registry, native workflows and governance | Stable quality gate, capability-level UAT and named product/design owners |
| Trust | RLS/audit/consent/data-rights/AI controls and evidence registers | PG17 rerun, independent security/privacy/accessibility review, counsel and institution approval |
| Operations | Console, incident, support, deployment, smoke and recovery foundations | Staffed on-call/support, current alerts, live restore/rollback and measured SLOs |
| Commercial | Pricing, entitlement, billing and contract material | Founder pricing authority, signed providers/contracts, accounting/tax/legal approval and tested refunds/chargebacks |
| Distribution | GTM, pilot, implementation, activation and renewal plans | Validated ICP, real pipeline/cohorts, reference outcomes, customer-success staffing and renewal evidence |

## Company functions and accountable evidence

Founder/product owns scope, claims and go/no-go. Engineering owns delivery and technical recovery. Security/privacy owns risk and incident response. Design/accessibility owns inclusive quality. Institution success owns implementation, training and renewal. Support owns routing and escalation. Finance owns cash, accounting, approvals and revenue treatment. Legal counsel owns entity, IP, privacy, contracts and regulatory advice. Trust/safety owns moderation and marketplace safeguards. Marketing/sales owns truthful acquisition and pipeline hygiene.

Seats may be founder, fractional or external, but cannot remain placeholders at launch.

## Finance and commercial boundaries

The repository and the separate financial package are planning tools. They do not prove revenue, cash, contracts, price acceptance or accounting treatment. Before decision use, validate opening cash, headcount/vendor costs, infrastructure/AI unit cost, signed pricing, pipeline probability, tax nexus, billing/refund controls, monthly close, runway and approval matrix. No code authorizes money movement without an approved provider and tested reconciliation.

## Distribution evidence

Each persona has a defined activation event, time-to-value, retention measure, support burden and outcome measure. Institutional pilots additionally require buyer map, security/procurement package, implementation plan, data map, training, adoption review and renewal decision. Marketplace requires verified supply, demand/liquidity, safety quality and consented application delivery.

## Completion decision

Broad GA remains NO-GO until all P0/P1 gates in `launch-readiness.md` close and the simulations in `full-company-launch-simulations.md` pass on the release candidate. A bounded capability may launch earlier only under the canonical resolver and its own approvals.
