# Company & Business Operations Console

## Boundary

This plane runs Semester as a company. It does not grant product-data access and does not turn forecasts, manually entered figures, or provider observations into authoritative accounting/customer facts.

## Modules

| Module | Native baseline | Connected mode | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive | dated manual snapshots with source/owner | governed rollups from product, CRM, billing, finance, support | invented KPI or unlabelled forecast |
| Revenue Operations | leads/accounts/opportunities/stages/activities | approved CRM adapter and reconciliation | unauthorized outreach or stage inference |
| Customer Success | implementation plan, milestones, training, success plan, renewal risk | product/adoption/support feeds with policy | individual student surveillance |
| Finance | plan, invoice status, budgets, costs, approved actual snapshots | payment/accounting/provider adapters | refunds, purchases, payouts, or accounting approval without human/provider confirmation |
| Marketing & Growth | campaign, asset, content, review, UTM registry | consent-aware analytics and approved channels | spam, dark patterns, fabricated performance |
| Social | account inventory, draft calendar, approval | approved API publish receipt | publish/connect/message without explicit approval |
| Partnerships | pipeline, verification, agreement, onboarding, support, renewal | marketplace performance aggregates | unverified partner exposure or hidden sponsorship |
| People & Governance | roles, responsibilities, requisitions, review calendar, decisions | approved ATS/HRIS | storing sensitive candidate/employee data without policy/system |
| Legal/Procurement | document inventory, questionnaire, contract/policy review, evidence | approved document/e-sign/evidence systems | compliance or legal-sufficiency claim |

## Metric contract

Every metric shows definition/version, source, authority, time window, refresh, inclusion/exclusion, privacy threshold, owner, and reconciliation status. Forecast, actual, target, estimate, and unknown are distinct types and visuals.

## Approval classes

- External communication: marketing owner plus channel/account authorization.
- Contract/legal artifact: business owner plus counsel/authorized approver.
- Money movement: finance/founder threshold approval plus provider confirmation.
- Pricing/plan change: product, finance, and founder approval; customer communication and entitlement migration.
- Sensitive people data: approved system, purpose, access, retention, and responsible owner.

## Current repository gap

Commercial, billing, implementation, evidence, policy, and operating-model scaffolding exists. There is no verified basis to claim a connected CRM/accounting/HR/social operating system. Build manual/native records first, then add adapters that preserve source and reconciliation status.
