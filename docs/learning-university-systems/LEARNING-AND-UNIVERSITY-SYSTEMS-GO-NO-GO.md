# Learning and University Systems Go/No-Go

## Decision

- Individual student use of existing local/source-aware planning, study, writing, recovery, and export tools: **CONDITIONAL GO** with beta limitations and no official-system claims.
- Paid institutional pilot: **NO-GO today** until a named institution supplies approvals, configured identity/integrations, data/source owners, UAT, accessibility/security review, support staffing, and signed activation evidence.
- Full LMS, SIS, registrar, gradebook-of-record, financial-aid, billing, housing-assignment, accommodation-decision, or clinical replacement: **NO-GO**.
- Read-only integration sandbox: **CONDITIONAL GO** only with minimum fields, test tenants, explicit labels, and rollback.

## Why

The repository shows substantial implemented and tested capability, but implementation is not activation. Existing registers explicitly report incomplete faculty workflows, unproven live integrations, unfilled evidence, unmeasured SLOs, missing staffed operations, and held high-authority domains.

## P0 disposition

No new broad feature was approved. Lint compliance, the internal browser accessibility/golden-path gates, and repository-generated register refresh are now restored. Focused AI/source-governance regressions pass: grounded answers expose evidence details and general-knowledge answers say “No source.” Because not every answer has a citable source, consequential-use verification remains open. Other remaining P0 work is to renew stale or missing external evidence, complete HawkScan in an environment with the required CLI, perform independent accessibility/security assurance, verify live provider and account-sync behavior, and execute a real controlled UAT. Until those pass, marketing must say “beta,” “pilot,” “preparation,” or “official handoff” as applicable.
