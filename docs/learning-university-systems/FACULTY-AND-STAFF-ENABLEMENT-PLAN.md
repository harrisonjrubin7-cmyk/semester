# Faculty and Staff Enablement Plan

Before pilot: role-specific quick starts, office hours, course-policy workshop, source-owner training, accessibility checklist, escalation map, and sandbox rehearsal. During pilot: weekly friction review, open issue log, response targets, and release notes. After pilot: outcome review, support burden, content maintenance, policy changes, and expansion decision.

Training completion is evidence only when the named cohort, date, materials, attendance, and follow-up are recorded.
