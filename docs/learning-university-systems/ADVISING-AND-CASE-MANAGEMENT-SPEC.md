# Advising and Case Management Specification

Semester prepares agendas, questions, selected evidence, goals, and follow-ups. Sharing is opt-in, scoped, previewed, revocable where possible, and logged. Advising notes require role-based access, purpose, retention, correction, and student-visibility rules.

Semester does not replace a regulated case-management system until restricted-data roles, segregation, supervision, incident handling, exports, and deletion/legal-hold behavior are validated.
