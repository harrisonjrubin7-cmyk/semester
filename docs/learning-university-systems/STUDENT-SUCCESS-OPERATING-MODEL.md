# Student Success Operating Model

Student success begins with agency: the student sees signals, reasons, data, available help, sharing choices, and consequences. Support uses no-wrong-door routing and minimum-data referrals. Automated risk labels do not trigger adverse action.

Operating roles: student, advisor/coach, referral owner, supervisor, privacy owner, accessibility owner, and support operator. Metrics emphasize successful handoff, time to help, completion, opt-out, false positives, and equity—not surveillance or raw engagement.
