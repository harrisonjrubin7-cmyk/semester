# Student Agency and Transparency Standard

Students can see and correct profile/context data, understand why a recommendation appears, change or reject plans, control reminders, choose what to share, review handoff payloads, export their work, and request deletion subject to legitimate retention/legal holds.

No dark patterns, punitive streaks, hidden ranking, silent AI, or irreversible sharing. Consequential recommendations name source, assumptions, uncertainty, owner, and appeal/human path.
