# University Services Strategy

Sequence services by student value and authority risk. Native: personal planning, preparation, notes, evidence, directories, and consent controls. Integrated: approved read/status data and low-risk updates. Official handoff: registration, aid, billing, housing assignment, clinical/disability decisions, and records. Partner/out of scope: emergency response, clinical care, payment custody, and specialized regulated operations until qualified.

A service is added only when its owner, source, freshness, permissions, recovery, support, accessibility, and metric are known.
