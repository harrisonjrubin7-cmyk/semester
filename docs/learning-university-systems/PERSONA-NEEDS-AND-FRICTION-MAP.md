# Persona Needs and Friction Map

Students need one next action, recovery, mobile access, understandable source labels, and control over sharing. Faculty need low-friction authoring, policy controls, grading safety, migration, and support. Advisors need prepared students without surveillance. Registrars need authoritative records, approvals, reconciliation, and rollback. Accessibility staff need testable accommodation behavior and document accessibility. IT/security need minimum scopes, tenant isolation, auditability, evidence expiry, and incident ownership. Procurement/legal need validated claims and current exhibits.

The common friction is not missing screens; it is unclear authority across native, integrated, and official systems. The product rule is therefore: every consequential view states source, freshness, authority, permitted action, and recovery/handoff.
