# Official Records and Handoff Policy

Official records are read-only unless a domain-specific activation authorizes writes. A handoff carries only the minimum reviewed data and identifies the receiving system. Semester stores the request and receipt, not an invented outcome. Pending, failed, rejected, completed, and stale states are distinct.

No UI text may convert “prepared,” “submitted,” or “accepted for processing” into “completed.” Reconciliation is required before displaying completion.
