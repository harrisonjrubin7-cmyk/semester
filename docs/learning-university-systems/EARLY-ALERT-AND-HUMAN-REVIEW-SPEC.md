# Early Alert and Human Review Specification

Signals are explainable observations, not diagnoses or destiny. Each alert records source, recency, rule, confidence limit, excluded data, intended support purpose, reviewer, action, student notice, correction, and expiry.

A trained human reviews before outreach or consequence. Protected traits and sensitive proxies require explicit governance. Measure false positives, disparities, usefulness, opt-outs, time to help, and harm reports. No automated eligibility or disciplinary decision.
