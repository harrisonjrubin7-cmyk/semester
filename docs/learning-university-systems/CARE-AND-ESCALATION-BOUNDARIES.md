# Care and Escalation Boundaries

Semester may recognize help-seeking language, show immediate resources, and route to institution-approved contacts. It does not diagnose, triage clinical severity, promise monitoring, or imply emergency dispatch.

High-risk flows use plain language, location-aware official contacts when configured, no unnecessary data capture, accessible alternatives, graceful failure, and a visible instruction to contact emergency services when immediate danger exists.
