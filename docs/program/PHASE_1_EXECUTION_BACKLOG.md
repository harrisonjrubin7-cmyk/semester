# Phase 1 execution backlog

**Purpose.** The ordered, smallest-vertical-slice plan for Phase 1 (security, tenancy, recovery, platform spine), derived from the Phase 0 audit. **Date** 2026-10-04. **Status** Phase 0 baseline — a plan, not a commitment and not a readiness claim. Nothing here has started. The ten steps and their order are the program's; this page adds, for each, the evidence it starts from, its first slice, its proof, and what must be true before it begins. Risk IDs are in [`RISK_REGISTER.md`](RISK_REGISTER.md); ADRs are `docs/decisions/proposed/ADR-nnnn-*.md` (all Proposed, none accepted).

**Gate (program definition).** NO-GO until cross-tenant isolation, AI policy enforcement, restore evidence, grant hardening, privileged-function review, production rollback, staging, audit/outbox coverage and platform gateway adoption pass.

## The ten steps

**Sequencing warning.** The program places "staging proof" (6) after grant hardening (2), definer hardening (5) and before RLS-owner hardening (10). Steps 2 and 5 change production-visible privileges. Run their migrations first on a Supabase preview branch or the second project, and only promote after step 6's harness exists; do not apply `database/proposed/anon_grant_reduction.sql` to production directly. This preserves the order while honouring ADR-0012.

## What this plan does not do
It adds no user-facing capability, accepts no ADR, and claims nothing about security, readiness or compliance. Estimates are deliberately absent: the audit did not measure capacity (one person holds every seat, R-018).
