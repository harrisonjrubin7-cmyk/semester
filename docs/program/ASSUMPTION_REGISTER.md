How to read: **Held** is the working assumption the program is currently leaning on. **Evidence for / against** cites repo paths only. **Falsified by** is the observation that would make the assumption false. Owner is a role (one person holds every company seat today: `OWNER-AND-ACCOUNTABILITY-MATRIX.md`, R-018). Validation dates are proposals; the owner has not set them. Review date for the whole register: 2026-11-04, aligned with the risk register.

## Open questions / not verified

1. All validation owners and dates above are proposals; none has been accepted by the owner.
2. A-001, A-002, A-003, A-004 and A-005 each need a read-only look at a system (Vercel, the Supabase catalog and dashboard) that Phase 0 did not use by rule.
3. Whether the nine pricing targets come from an external plan, a discussion, or a newer document (`commercial/READINESS_GAP_MATRIX.md` open questions) is unknown, so A-017's "unrecorded" is a statement about the repository only.
4. A-018 rests on two documents that disagree (`LEGAL-REVIEW-QUEUE.md` Q-00 versus the `privacy` seat in `SEMESTER-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`); I did not resolve which is current.
5. Assumptions held implicitly by the product thesis (for example that students will keep a device-first workflow when their school also provides a server record, ADR 0001 "under review") were not listed as separate rows beyond A-006; interview or usage evidence does not exist in the repository.
6. Legal, compliance and accessibility conformance conclusions are for counsel and qualified assessors; the register records the dependency, not an answer.
