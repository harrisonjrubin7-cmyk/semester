### Exact proof needed to begin selling

Discovery and accurately scoped demos may begin now. A paid proposal should not be represented as launchable until the deal folder contains:

- approved positioning, demo script, known-limitations sheet, and this scorecard;
- legal entity/tax/payment details, insurance decision, and counsel-reviewed pilot agreement/DPA issue disposition;
- current subprocessor list, security/privacy/accessibility summaries, and HECVAT evidence index;
- a price assumption approved by the CEO and a quote showing scope, exclusions, support, and offboarding;
- a prospect-qualified use case, buyer/champion map, target cohort, decision date, and acceptable data scope.

### Exact proof needed to begin launching

- executed pilot agreement and DPA or documented no-personal-data basis;
- named executive sponsor, operational champion, security contact, privacy contact, support owner, and escalation backup;
- tenant/cohort configuration export; approved minimum-necessary data map; feature/kill-switch list;
- cross-tenant/role acceptance results in the target environment;
- qualified accessibility test of the exact student and admin golden paths;
- current vulnerability/penetration review with no open P0/P1 findings;
- production backup/restore and incident-tabletop records with measured RTO/RPO;
- end-to-end export/deletion, support, monitoring, rollback, and degraded-mode acceptance;
- agreed baseline, 3–5 outcome measures, privacy thresholds, weekly review, conversion date, and offboarding plan;
- a signed `GO` decision from every required launch seat. No P0/P1 may be waived.
