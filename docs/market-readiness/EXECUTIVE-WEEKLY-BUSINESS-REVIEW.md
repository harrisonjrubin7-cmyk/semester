# Executive weekly business review

1. Decisions required and last week’s commitments.
2. Launch safety: incidents, findings, privacy/accessibility cases, support capacity, release/rollback status.
3. Institutional funnel: qualified accounts, stage aging, blockers, next decision.
4. Pilot: activation, weekly prepared action, workflow outcome, guardrails, costs and issues.
5. Individual funnel: activation/week-1/week-4, support, deletion/opt-out and channels.
6. Evidence: newly produced, expired, rejected or missing controls.
7. Financial/capacity: cash/runway input, delivery cost, AI/cloud cost, staffing risk.
8. Top three priorities with one owner and due date each.

No green status without linked current evidence. Record “no change” explicitly; do not hide silent weeks.
