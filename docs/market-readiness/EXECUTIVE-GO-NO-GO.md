# Executive go / no-go

**Decision date:** 2026-10-02
**Decision authority:** this repository assessment recommends; it does not replace counsel, customer, security, accessibility, support, finance, or launch-council approval.

| Motion | Decision | What is authorized by this assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| individual student adoption | **CONDITIONAL GO / YELLOW for a clearly labeled, invitation-based beta; NO-GO / RED for broad or paid promotion** | continue product validation only after applicable terms/privacy, support, monitoring, lifecycle, security, accessibility, and UAT gates are accepted |
| design-partner engagement | **GO / GREEN for non-activation discovery, demos, evidence exchange, and scoping; NO-GO / RED for customer activation today** | engage qualified prospects using the bounded offer and accurate limitations; activate only on a named-tenant signed GO |
| paid institutional pilots | **NO-GO / RED** | do not accept a launch obligation or represent the product as launchable until all P0/P1, legal/commercial, staffing, independent-review, and production-rehearsal evidence is complete |
| broad institutional enterprise sales | **NO-GO / RED** | do not market as an enterprise replacement or GA platform |

## What can be sold now

No production use is currently evidenced as safe to sell and activate. Semester is **GREEN for the non-activation process toward a scoped pilot**: discovery, fit validation, evidence exchange, demos, and a conditional proposal that clearly states launch prerequisites. Do not invoice, accept live customer data, or sign an unconditional launch commitment without commercial authority and gate closure.

## What may be piloted with constraints

After full named-tenant acceptance: manual/read-only onboarding, Today/This Week, tasks/deadlines, workload planning, readiness checklist, support discovery, and privacy-thresholded aggregate reporting for one 50–200 student cohort and one milestone.

## What must not be sold or claimed

System replacement; official-record writes; live SSO/SIS/LMS/LTI without acceptance; certified compliance; formal accessibility conformance; Vanderbilt approval/deployment; 24/7 or uptime/DR guarantees; proven causal student outcomes; high-impact automated decisions; broad enterprise readiness.

The exact missing evidence is listed in [MARKET-READINESS-BASELINE.md](MARKET-READINESS-BASELINE.md) and [LAUNCH-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md](LAUNCH-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md). The current release remains withheld as described in [the GA release decision](../launch-readiness/GA_RELEASE_DECISION.md).
