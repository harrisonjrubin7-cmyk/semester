# Objection handling

Use the concise responses in `MESSAGING-HOUSE.md`. Additional rule: acknowledge the actual limitation, provide evidence, offer the lowest-risk path, and never convert an objection into an unsupported claim.

- **Need SOC 2/ISO/VPAT:** explain current gap; offer scoped evidence and remediation plan; accept that this may disqualify Semester today.
- **Need an SIS write:** propose manual/read-only validation first; block writeback until customer approval, reconciliation and rollback evidence.
- **Want individual risk alerts:** refuse profiling; offer aggregate cohort readiness and official human-led support workflow.
- **Want a free pilot:** tie price to implementation/evidence cost; a design partnership still requires written scope, safety gates and decision date.
- **Need faster launch:** reduce scope/integration, never security/privacy/accessibility evidence.
