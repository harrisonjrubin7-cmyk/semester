# Customer success playbook

Customer success owns outcome alignment and operating clarity, not unsupported outcome claims.

## Cadence

- **Kickoff:** confirm decision, scope, users, roles, data, communications, support, measurement, risks, and stop conditions.
- **Implementation:** track readiness evidence and adoption blockers; prevent scope creep.
- **Weekly review:** activation/first win, meaningful engagement, reliability, support, trust, decisions, changes, and next owners.
- **Midpoint:** continue/correct/stop; validate privacy/accessibility/support burden and outcome trajectory.
- **Final:** report results with caveats, incidents/data rights, sponsor decision, annual feasibility, reference permission, and offboarding.
- **Renewal/expansion:** expand only to an adjacent cohort/workflow supported by evidence and capacity.

Health combines outcome progress, implementation completion, product reliability, support burden, sponsor/champion engagement, trust/compliance gates, and decision readiness. Low usage alone is not a student-deficit label. See [CUSTOMER-HEALTH-SCORE.md](CUSTOMER-HEALTH-SCORE.md) and [RENEWAL-PLAYBOOK.md](RENEWAL-PLAYBOOK.md).
