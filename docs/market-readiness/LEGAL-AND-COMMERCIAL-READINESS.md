## Required legal package

Counsel-reviewed Terms, Privacy Notice, Cookie/Storage Notice, AUP, AI policy, accessibility statement, security addendum, DPA, pilot agreement/order form, SaaS terms, SLA/support schedule, subprocessor notice/change process, incident terms, retention/offboarding schedule and marketing-communications consent. Record version, approver, effective date, audience and superseded version.

## Deal desk rule

No salesperson or demo may promise a control, certification, integration, SLA, data use, outcome or roadmap item outside the approved registry and agreement. Novel terms route to CEO + responsible control owner + counsel (amounts and discounts follow the deal-desk ladder in `app/src/lib/governance/deal-desk.ts`); security/privacy/accessibility work is never traded away for price or speed.
