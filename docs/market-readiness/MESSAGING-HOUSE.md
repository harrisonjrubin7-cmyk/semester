# Messaging house

## Core problem

Students must translate fragmented academic information into a plan while institutions lack a privacy-respectful way to know whether the cohort is ready. The cost is avoidable confusion, repeated questions, missed actions and low-confidence registration—not a claim of causally reduced retention unless a pilot proves it.

## Value pillars

1. **Clarity:** schedule, workload, deadlines, sources and next actions in one student-owned view.
2. **Action:** Today/This Week and readiness checklists convert information into achievable steps.
3. **Trust:** minimum data, manual/read-only first, explicit permissions, aggregate evidence and clear limits.
4. **Controlled delivery:** one cohort, named owners, measurable scorecard, rollback/offboarding and a decision date.

## Safe claims now

- Semester has implemented and automated-tested planning, tenant, authorization, audit, data-rights, degraded-mode and governance controls in the repository.
- Semester can demonstrate a manual/synthetic registration-readiness workflow.
- Procurement, privacy, accessibility, AI and security materials are prepared as drafts/evidence indexes.
- The product is an experience/planning layer; official systems remain authoritative.

## Claims prohibited until proven

“Production-ready for any university,” “Vanderbilt-approved/partnered,” “FERPA compliant/certified,” “WCAG compliant,” “SOC 2/ISO certified,” “penetration tested,” “24/7 supported,” “integrated with your SIS/LMS,” “improves retention,” or “enterprise ready.”

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| We already have an LMS/SIS | Semester’s first offer does not replace either; it tests the student planning and readiness layer around one milestone. |
| We cannot approve a deep integration | Start with manual or approved read-only data; integration is optional and separately gated. |
| Is this FERPA compliant? | FERPA has no product certification. We provide a scoped control/evidence package and contract language for your counsel; approval remains institutional. |
| Does AI advise students? | AI can assist with approved sources but is not authoritative and is disabled for high-impact decisions. |
| How do we know it works? | Agree a baseline, activation/usefulness/outcome measures and guardrails before launch; decide convert/expand/pause/stop on that evidence. |
| Is it accessible? | WCAG 2.2 AA is the target; automated/self-assessed evidence exists, while a qualified pilot-flow evaluation remains a launch gate. |
