# Privacy program

## Principles

Purpose limitation, minimum necessary collection, student agency, institution-specific authority, transparent source/use/freshness, tenant separation, controlled sharing, short retention where feasible, verified rights requests, and no hidden secondary use.

## Data classes and purpose

| Class | Purpose | Pilot rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| identity/account | authenticate, recover, support access | minimum fields; privileged MFA target |
| student planning content | schedule, tasks, workload and next actions | student-controlled; not an institutional record unless contracted |
| approved institutional/source data | display authorized dates/context | read-only first; provenance/freshness required |
| preferences/notifications | deliver requested experience | granular controls; easy opt-out |
| support/audit/security metadata | diagnose, secure and evidence actions | content-minimized; access logged and time-limited |
| aggregate analytics | operate and measure the pilot | no cell under 10; no individual risk profiles |
| AI request/output data | provide disclosed assistance | approved sources/provider; training prohibited absent explicit approval |
| demo/test data | demonstrations and testing | synthetic; structurally separated from education records |

## Rights and lifecycle

Requests are identity-verified, scoped, logged, and fulfilled through export, correction/revocation, deletion or a documented exception. Institutional official-record responsibilities, legal holds and backup expiry are disclosed. Pilot offboarding revokes access/integrations, exports authorized data, deletes/de-identifies eligible primary/derived data, records exceptions, and confirms completion.

## Boundaries

- Individual use and institution-sponsored use have separate notices and authority.
- Institution-controlled records are never silently converted into consumer data.
- Marketing consent is separate from service operation.
- Family/guardian access is explicit, scoped and lawful; no relationship is inferred.
- Minor use requires a counsel-approved age/program design; the initial offer excludes unsupported minor contexts.
- Advertising may never use education records, private content, health/disability data, counseling/conduct data, location history, or institutional data.

## Operating cadence

Privacy reviews every new data class, integration, metric, AI/provider change and contract. Quarterly: rights requests, retention sweeps, subprocessors, access exceptions, incidents and unresolved data-map gaps. Annually and per customer: notices, DPA, retention schedule and offboarding terms.
