# Pilot offer

The primary offer is the **Semester Student Success and Registration Readiness Pilot** described in [PRIMARY-PILOT-OFFER.md](PRIMARY-PILOT-OFFER.md).

## Commercial scope

- One institution or program, one 50–200 student cohort, one registration or term-start milestone.
- 30–60 day implementation followed by an agreed measurement window and a fixed conversion/offboarding decision date.
- Manual/read-only inputs first; no official-record writes and no promise of live SSO/SIS/LMS/LTI.
- Included: student onboarding, Today/This Week, course/task/workload planning, registration checklist, support discovery, carefully controlled reminders, privacy-thresholded aggregate reporting, weekly review, and offboarding.
- Excluded: admissions, aid, discipline, disability, health, counseling, immigration, conduct, individual risk scoring, authoritative advising, automated registration, grade prediction, surveillance, and system-of-record replacement.

## Responsibilities

The customer supplies a sponsor, operational champion, approved cohort/data scope, accessibility/security/procurement reviewers, source materials, student communication authority, weekly participation, and an outcome decision. Semester supplies the configured scope, training, documented support hours/targets, evidence reporting, incident/rollback paths, export/deletion/offboarding, and transparent limitations.

## Launch condition

Discovery and proposal preparation can begin now. Activation remains **RED** until [the launch checklist](PILOT-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md) and [executive gate](EXECUTIVE-GO-NO-GO.md) are signed GO with no open P0/P1.

## Fallback

If institutional data or integration approval is unavailable, use the Student Productivity and Academic Workload Pilot with manual student setup, no institutional record access, synthetic admin demonstrations, and student-reported outcomes.
