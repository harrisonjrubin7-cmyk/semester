# Support operations

**Current status: RED for launch until the queue, hours, owners, backups, channels, and response targets are operationally accepted.**

## Model

- Publish distinct student and institutional/operator channels and supported hours.
- Triage security/privacy/data-rights and safety reports separately from product questions.
- Use severity based on safety, security/privacy, access, data integrity, cohort impact, workaround, and time sensitivity.
- Record issue, scope, consented diagnostics, classification, owner, communication, workaround, resolution, known-issue link, and follow-up without copying unnecessary student content.
- Escalate incidents to the incident plan; communicate known issues/releases; feed recurring product friction into prioritized review.

Targets in [PILOT-SUPPORT-RUNBOOK.md](PILOT-SUPPORT-RUNBOOK.md) are planning targets until staffing and contracts approve them. Semester must not promise 24/7 coverage, contractual SLA, dedicated success management, or an uptime percentage without evidence and authority.
