# Procurement questionnaire process

1. Intake questionnaire, due date, customer, scope, confidentiality and architecture.
2. Map every question to the evidence register/control owner; reuse only current approved answers.
3. Label `implemented`, `pilot-scoped`, `planned`, `not applicable`, `customer responsibility` or `gap`; never guess.
4. Security/privacy/accessibility/AI/legal owners review their sections; counsel reviews contractual assertions.
5. Resolve contradictions against code/config and the capability registry.
6. Approve, version and deliver through the required secure channel; record disclosed artifacts.
7. Log follow-ups, commitments, owners/dates and answer expiry; feed material commitments into contract and release gates.

Certifications, audits, penetration tests, insurance and customer relationships require the actual document—not a narrative substitute.
