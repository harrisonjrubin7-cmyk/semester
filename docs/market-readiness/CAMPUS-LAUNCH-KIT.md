# Campus launch kit

Contents: approved one-sentence description; synthetic screenshots/demo; QR link with campaign source; student quick-start; accessibility and privacy summary; official-record disclaimer; support and barrier-reporting contacts; advisor/faculty referral copy; event checklist; ambassador conduct; known limitations; withdrawal/export/delete paths.

Orientation activation: 10-minute “plan your first week” session using manual setup. Staff never ask students to display grades, schedules, private tasks or disability/health information. Materials identify whether use is individual or institution-sponsored and never imply a partnership beyond the signed scope.
