# Individual growth strategy

## Value proposition

“Semester helps you see what matters, organize your workload, turn academic chaos into a realistic plan, and follow through—without needing your university to buy software first.”

## Motion

1. Land on a use-case page: registration, first week, workload, deadlines or degree planning.
2. Start locally/manual-first with plain-language permissions.
3. Reach setup-only activation: one approved course/calendar source; then measure first win separately when the student reaches Today, understands one relevant recommended reversible action and its source/limitations, knows the relevant help route, and intentionally completes, schedules, snoozes or defers it.
4. Return through the ethical loop: capture → organize → plan → act → reflect → improve.
5. Invite or share only an intentionally selected, privacy-safe object—not a transcript, grades or private plan.

## Channels

Orientation QR kit; advisors/faculty and campus organizations; student ambassadors; search guides; short-form demonstrations; long-form proof webinars; lifecycle email. Optimize for activated and retained students, not impressions/downloads.

## Guardrails

No dark patterns, contact scraping, coerced disclosure, grade/health targeting, public-by-default plans, or notification spam. Core privacy/export/delete/opt-out controls remain free. Free individual use and institution-sponsored use are visibly distinct. Advertising, if ever enabled, is contextual only and disabled for institutional tenants by default.

## GA blockers

Counsel-approved consumer terms/privacy/age position; verified account lifecycle; staffed support; production reliability/telemetry; payment/tax/refund operations if monetized; critical accessibility review; current security review; and evidence that activation does not create disproportionate support or privacy harm.
