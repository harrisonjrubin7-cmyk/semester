# Email lifecycle

Transactional/service messages: verification/recovery; setup saved; requested reminder; security/privacy/support update; export/deletion/offboarding confirmation. Marketing messages require a separate lawful consent and one-click unsubscribe.

Suggested lifecycle: welcome and manual-first start; incomplete setup help after 24–48 hours; first-week planning prompt; weekly review only if opted in; milestone reminder; value check at week 2; feedback at midpoint; re-engagement with frequency cap; inactivity notice before any retention action.

Do not place grades, task titles, health/disability information, private conversations or sensitive support context in subject lines or marketing segmentation. Suppression and notification preference changes must take effect promptly.
