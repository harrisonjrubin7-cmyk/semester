# Pilot support runbook

Channels, hours and targets are contract-specific. Use the canonical on-call severity: P0 active security/privacy/cross-tenant/data-integrity/safety/rights threat; P1 core workflow unavailable, major accessibility barrier, or serious integrity/reliability risk—even when a temporary workaround exists; P2 other material impairment; P3 question/minor issue.

Verify identity and tenant; collect correlation/time/device/route/error—not unnecessary course content or screenshots of records. Use time-limited support access only when essential and consented/authorized; every read is audited. Give a safe workaround, owner and next-update time. Escalate privacy, security, accessibility and academic-deadline issues immediately to their named owners. Never provide authoritative academic/legal/health/financial advice.

Close with resolution, affected scope, communication, follow-up and linked incident/problem record. Weekly reporting is aggregate and suppresses cells under 10.
