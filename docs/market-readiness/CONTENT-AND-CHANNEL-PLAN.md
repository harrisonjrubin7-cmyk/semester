# Content and channel plan

| Channel | Job | Cadence | Success |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| search/use-case pages | answer registration/workload/first-week questions | 2 useful pages/month | activated starts, not traffic alone |
| Instagram/TikTok/Shorts | demonstrate one planning action | 2–3/week during campaigns | activation and safe saves/shares |
| YouTube/webinar | full student/pilot walkthrough and proof | monthly | qualified demos/activation |
| LinkedIn/email | trust, implementation and outcome evidence | weekly/biweekly | qualified conversations |
| campus groups/advisors | trusted referral and workshops | per term milestone | activation, week-4 retention |

Every asset has audience, claim source, date, owner, accessibility/captions/alt text, CTA, consent basis and review expiry. Remove claims when evidence expires. Never use identifiable student records as content.
