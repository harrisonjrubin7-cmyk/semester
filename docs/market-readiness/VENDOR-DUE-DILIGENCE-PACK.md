# Vendor due-diligence pack

## Pack contents

- system/architecture and data-flow overview;
- security, privacy, accessibility, AI-use, integration, support, and incident summaries;
- current subprocessor register and data-location/processing details once verified;
- evidence-indexed questionnaire responses and open-gap register;
- continuity, restore, rollback, retention, export, deletion, and offboarding procedures;
- pilot scope, roles, implementation plan, pricing/quote, success plan, and known limitations;
- counsel-reviewed agreement/DPA materials, insurance/tax/entity evidence, and approved public policies when available.

## Current limitations

This repository package is a draft evidence index. It is not legal advice, an executed agreement, an ACR/VPAT, a SOC report, a penetration-test report, insurance evidence, or proof of production operation. Missing items remain explicitly marked and may block a sale.
