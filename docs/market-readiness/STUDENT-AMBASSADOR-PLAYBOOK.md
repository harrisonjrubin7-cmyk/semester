# Student ambassador playbook

## Purpose

Help students discover and learn Semester without presenting ambassadors as university officials or pressuring anyone to disclose education records.

## Program

- recruit through transparent application and conflict disclosure;
- train on product limits, privacy, accessibility, referral safety and escalation;
- provide synthetic demos, QR cards, office-hour format and approved claims;
- compensate for time/work, not sensitive-data collection, sign-ups or positive testimonials;
- require separate written permission for quotes, images or case studies;
- review events weekly and remove misleading materials immediately.

## Approved activity

Orientation tables, student-organization workshops, “plan your first week” sessions, advisor-referred demos, privacy-safe invitation links and feedback sessions. Never request grades, schedules, disability/health status, immigration, conduct or private messages.

## Measures

Activated users, week-1/week-4 retention, workshop completion, support rate, opt-out/deletion rate, accessibility issues and reported pressure/confusion. Impressions and scans are diagnostic only.

## Escalation

Privacy/security or accessibility harm → stop activity and contact the named owner. Harassment/coercion/misrepresentation → suspend ambassador pending review. Academic or crisis questions → refer to official campus services; do not advise beyond training.
