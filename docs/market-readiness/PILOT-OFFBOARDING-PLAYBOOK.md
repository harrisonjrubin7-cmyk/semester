# Pilot offboarding playbook

Trigger: stop decision, expiry, nonpayment, customer request or risk. Confirm authority, effective date, data/record duties, legal holds and communications. Freeze new enrollment; disable integrations/features; revoke grants/tokens/support access; preserve only authorized audit/legal records.

Provide agreed export and verification window. Delete/de-identify eligible primary data and derived indexes/caches; queue provider deletion; allow encrypted backups to expire under the disclosed lifecycle; document exceptions, owner and deletion date. Verify tenant access fails, scheduled jobs stop and subprocessors are addressed. Send a completion record and retain only the contractually/lawfully required record.
