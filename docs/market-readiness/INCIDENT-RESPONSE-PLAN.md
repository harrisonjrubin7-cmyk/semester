# Incident response plan

The operational source of truth is [INCIDENT-RESPONSE-RUNBOOK.md](INCIDENT-RESPONSE-RUNBOOK.md). Its use is **RED-gated** until incident roles, contacts, communication authority, evidence storage, and escalation backups are named and a target-environment tabletop is completed.

## Minimum response

- Detect and validate without exposing secrets or unnecessary personal data.
- Classify severity and potential security/privacy/student impact.
- Assign incident commander and specialist/communication roles.
- Contain using feature disablement, credential revocation, provider isolation, read-only mode, or rollback.
- Preserve evidence and record decisions/timestamps.
- Assess notification duties with security, privacy, customer, and counsel owners.
- Recover through an approved, tested path; validate access, data integrity, audit, and core journeys.
- Conduct a blameless post-incident review with corrective actions, owners, deadlines, and verification.

Templates are in [incident communications](INCIDENT_COMMUNICATION_TEMPLATES.md). No notification deadline, 24/7 response, or recovery guarantee is promised unless contractually and operationally approved.
