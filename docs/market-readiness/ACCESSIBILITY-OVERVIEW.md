# Accessibility overview

Semester maintains automated accessibility checks, design guidance, issue tracking, and a critical-path conformance plan. Current evidence supports an accessibility program and self-assessment—not a claim of WCAG 2.2 AA conformance or a completed VPAT/ACR.

The paid-pilot gate requires a qualified manual evaluation of the exact student/admin workflows across keyboard, screen reader, zoom/reflow, mobile/touch, contrast, reduced motion, forms/errors, dialogs, calendars, and recovery states; remediation and retest of blockers; published known limitations; and a staffed accommodation/support path.

See [ACCESSIBILITY-CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md](ACCESSIBILITY-CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md), [ACCESSIBILITY-PROGRAM.md](ACCESSIBILITY-PROGRAM.md), and [current accessibility status](../launch-readiness/ACCESSIBILITY_STATUS.md).
