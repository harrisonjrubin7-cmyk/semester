# Ideal customer profile

## Hypothesis to validate

The best first design partner is a private or regional four-year institution, school, program, or student-success unit able to isolate a 50–200 student cohort around a registration or term-start milestone. This is a working hypothesis, not a proven market conclusion.

## Required characteristics

- A concrete planning, readiness, advising-preparation, or workload-visibility problem that Semester can address without replacing an SIS/LMS.
- One executive sponsor, one empowered operational champion, and named IT/security/privacy/accessibility contacts.
- Willingness to run a bounded, reversible pilot with manual/read-only setup first.
- Ability to approve a minimum-necessary data scope and avoid high-risk data or individual risk scoring.
- Agreement on 3–5 leading indicators, weekly reviews, a decision date, and clean offboarding.
- Realistic procurement and implementation timing; no requirement for unsupported certifications, writes, 24/7 support, or enterprise-wide rollout.

## Strong triggers

- Upcoming registration, orientation, transfer, first-term, or advising milestone.
- Students struggle to translate scattered deadlines and resources into a next action.
- Advising/student-success leaders want workflow evidence without surveillance.
- A program can sponsor a cohort and tolerate a product-partnership posture.

## Disqualifiers

- “Replace the SIS/LMS now,” mandatory writeback, authoritative degree audit, or institution-wide launch as entry scope.
- No operational champion, no support capacity, no baseline, or no conversion/offboarding decision.
- Required use of health, disability, conduct, counseling, immigration, financial-aid, or disciplinary data.
- Demand for unsupported certification, guaranteed outcomes, contractual uptime, or 24/7 response.
- Cohort below 10 for aggregate reporting or above 200 before repeatability is proven.
