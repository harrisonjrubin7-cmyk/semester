# Privacy overview

Semester’s intended pilot posture is minimum-necessary, purpose-limited, transparent, reversible, and privacy-thresholded. Students must receive clear notice and meaningful control; institutional access must be role-appropriate and auditable; support access must be scoped; AI features must disclose their boundaries; education records must not be used for behavioral advertising or model training without explicit approved authority.

The current package includes data inventory/flow, retention, export, deletion, rights, incident, subprocessor, AI-governance, and offboarding materials. Before launch, counsel and the institution must approve data roles, notices/consent, permitted purpose, retention/deletion, DPA position, subprocessors, support access, incident notification, and any AI vendor use. This is not legal advice and no blanket FERPA or privacy-compliance claim is made.
