# 90-day market-readiness roadmap

Time does not turn a failed gate green; each milestone closes only with evidence.

## Days 1–30 — establish authority and close P0 foundations

- Name CEO, product, engineering, security, privacy, accessibility, support, finance, counsel, incident, data-rights, and backup owners.
- Qualify one design partner; sign the scope-development process; freeze the 50–200 student manual/read-only wedge.
- Approve data flow, prohibited data/uses, AI/provider posture, subprocessor inventory, legal issue lists, public claim ceiling, and preliminary price logic.
- Commission independent security and qualified accessibility reviews.
- Configure a target-like environment and rehearse auth/roles/isolation, monitoring, incident, restore, rollback, degraded mode, export/deletion, support, and offboarding.
- Complete representative mobile, keyboard, slow/failure-network, and recovery UAT for the first win.

**Exit:** owners accepted; prospect/cohort/scope defined; no unknown P0; external reviews underway or complete; target evidence folder exists.

## Days 31–60 — make the pilot deliverable

- Close every P0/P1 and retest.
- Finish counsel-approved pilot agreement/DPA position, public policies, insurance/entity/tax/payment decisions, support expectations, and approved price book.
- Complete customer security/privacy/accessibility/procurement review and target integration decision.
- Train operators; run a synthetic full pilot simulation from invitation through weekly reporting, incident/support, outcome review, and offboarding.
- Approve baseline, metric dictionary, event validation, privacy threshold, midpoint/final criteria, conversion, and exit.

**Exit:** complete launch packet, passed UAT/rehearsals, approved commercial paper, staffed support, and no open P0/P1.

## Days 61–90 — controlled selling and operation

- Convene launch council; activate only on signed GO.
- Operate one or a very small number of scoped cohorts with weekly evidence reviews and strict change control.
- Hold midpoint continue/correct/stop decisions; document incidents, rights requests, reliability, support, accessibility, and scope changes.
- At final review, report outcomes with caveats and convert only to an adjacent supportable annual scope; otherwise offboard cleanly.
- Use permissioned evidence—not inflated claims—to refine pricing, qualification, implementation capacity, and the next roadmap.

**Exit:** one clean outcome decision and offboarding/conversion record. Broad enterprise sales remain RED until repeatability, independent assurance, references, integrations, scale, and staffing are proven.
