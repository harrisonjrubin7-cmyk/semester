# First 1,000 students adoption plan

Reach 1,000 eligible students through a portfolio of controlled channels: 3–5 institutional/program cohorts of 50–200, advisor/organization referrals, orientation workshops and privacy-safe individual acquisition. Avoid a single campus-wide blast before support and reliability are proven.

Stage gates per 100–250 students: activation and week-4 retention meet target; support backlog and response stay within capacity; no unresolved P0/P1; privacy/deletion/opt-out and accessibility guardrails remain acceptable; infrastructure/AI cost per activated student is understood. Pause the channel that fails even if total sign-ups grow.

Success is 1,000 students who reach activation with sustainable retention and trust—not 1,000 accounts.
