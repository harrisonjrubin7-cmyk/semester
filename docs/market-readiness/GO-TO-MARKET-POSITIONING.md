# Go-to-market positioning

> **Status of the offer described here:** a target, not a current one. Paid institutional pilots are **NO-GO / RED** ([`GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md`](../../GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md)); "paid pilot" must not appear in public copy until that changes. Public wording today is *design-partner discovery and scoping, non-activation*.

## Positioning

**One sentence:** Semester helps a defined student cohort become ready for registration or term start through one organized planning experience, while giving institutions privacy-conscious aggregate evidence and a controlled path to expand.

**Thirty seconds:** Students often face dates, tasks, support links and next steps scattered across portals and documents. Semester brings the student-owned planning layer together—schedule, workload, next actions and support—without asking an institution to replace its systems of record. A paid pilot starts with one cohort, manual or approved read-only data, clear privacy boundaries, weekly measurement and a conversion or clean-exit decision.

**Two minutes:** The first offer is not “replace the university stack.” It is a 30–60 day implementation for one 50–200 student cohort approaching registration or term start. Students build or import an understandable plan, see Today and This Week, act on a readiness checklist and find the right support. Operators see only approved, privacy-thresholded aggregate measures. The pilot begins read-only, excludes high-risk data and automated decisions, and has named owners, kill switches, support, offboarding and a signed outcome scorecard. Expansion follows evidence: another cohort, program, or approved source—not a platform-wide promise.

## Audience versions

- **Institutional buyer:** a scoped, measurable student-success intervention with a defined decision date and clean exit.
- **Student-success leader:** make the invisible work of getting ready visible and actionable without creating another surveillance dashboard.
- **CIO/CISO:** additive, least-privilege, manual/read-only first, tenant-scoped, auditable, reversible, and honest about assurance gaps.
- **Advisor:** students arrive with a clearer plan, questions and next actions; Semester does not replace professional judgment.
- **Student:** see what matters, turn academic chaos into a realistic plan, and know the next action—without waiting for your school to replace its systems.

## Ideal customer profile

A four-year institution, college, advising organization, transfer/first-year program, honors/learning community, or student-success unit with a near-term milestone, 50–200 students, an empowered champion, a paid-pilot path, and willingness to use manual/read-only setup. Avoid opportunities demanding SIS writeback, whole-campus replacement, individual risk scoring, unsupported 24/7 service, or a compliance certificate as a prerequisite.

## Proof sequence

Repository controls → qualified UAT → target-environment trust/operations evidence → cohort activation/retention/usefulness → outcome change → reference permission. Never reverse this sequence in a claim.
