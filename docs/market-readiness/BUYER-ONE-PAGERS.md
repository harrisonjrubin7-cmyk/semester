# Buyer one-pagers

| Audience | Urgent job | Semester value | Proof they need | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Student Success/Retention | cohort readiness and follow-through | actionable student plan + aggregate evidence | activation/usefulness/outcome baseline | sponsor one cohort |
| Provost/Academic Affairs | coherent student experience without platform replacement | bounded measurable intervention | academic limits, accessibility, outcome design | approve scope/champion |
| CIO/IT | avoid unsafe shadow integration | additive read-only/manual-first architecture | tenant/access/test/rollback evidence | technical reviewer |
| CISO/Privacy | minimize risk/data | scoped data, audit, rights and controls | HECVAT/evidence/finding plan | approve data scope |
| Registrar/Enrollment | readiness before milestone | checklist and next-action clarity | no authoritative write/claim; source/freshness | validate content/workflow |
| Advising | better prepared meetings | student-owned plan and agenda | limits and support escalation | operational champion |
| Dean/Program | improve one program’s execution | fast cohort configuration and weekly evidence | budget, implementation, decision date | fund pilot |
| Student government/champion | useful and trustworthy student experience | manual-first value, privacy/accessibility controls | transparent terms and opt-out | recruit feedback, not records |
