# Discovery call script

## Opening

“I’d like to understand the student workflow and the decision you need to make before we show Semester. We will separate what is available in the product, what could be configured for a bounded pilot, and what is not currently available.”

## Questions

- What academic moment or workflow fails today, for whom, and how is that visible?
- What do students and staff do now, in which systems, and what is the cost/friction?
- Which 50–200 student cohort could be isolated, and who owns it day to day?
- What would students be able to do after 7–14 days that they cannot do reliably now?
- Which 3–5 leading indicators and qualitative signals would support a continue/stop decision?
- What data is truly necessary? Which sensitive categories can be excluded?
- Are manual/read-only setup and no system-of-record writes acceptable?
- Which identity, SIS/LMS/calendar, security, privacy, accessibility, legal, and procurement requirements are mandatory?
- Who sponsors, operates, supports, approves, and signs? What are the budget and decision dates?
- What would disqualify the pilot? What must happen for annual conversion or clean offboarding?

## Close

Restate the problem, cohort, success criteria, scope, risks, stakeholders, evidence requests, and next decision. Do not promise a date or capability during the call; confirm it against the readiness matrix. See [DISCOVERY-CALL-PLAYBOOK.md](DISCOVERY-CALL-PLAYBOOK.md).
