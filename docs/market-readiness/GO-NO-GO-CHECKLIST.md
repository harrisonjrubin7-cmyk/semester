# Market-readiness go/no-go checklist

Launch is `GO` only when the executed scope, named owners, data/access approval, target-environment security/privacy/accessibility acceptance, UAT, monitoring/support, incident/restore/rollback/export/deletion/offboarding rehearsals, baseline/guardrails and every council sign-off are current and linked.

It is `NO-GO` for any P0/P1, missing seat/signature, uncertain tenant/data authority, unavailable support/monitoring, critical accessibility barrier, untested recovery, unsupported claim/contract commitment, or missing offboarding path. Only lower risks may be time-bound, owner-approved and disclosed; they produce `GO WITH CONDITIONS`, never a clean `GO`.

The executable launch-council rules and existing checklist remain authoritative: [`../LAUNCH-READINESS-COUNCIL.md`](../LAUNCH-READINESS-COUNCIL.md) and [`../GO-NO-GO-CHECKLIST.md`](../GO-NO-GO-CHECKLIST.md).
