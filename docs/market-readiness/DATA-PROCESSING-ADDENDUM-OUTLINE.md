# Data processing addendum outline

**Issue outline for counsel; not legal advice or an executed agreement.**

1. Parties, roles, scope, term, precedence, and documented instructions.
2. Processing subject, duration, purpose, nature, data categories, and data subjects.
3. Confidentiality, personnel/access controls, security measures, and audit evidence.
4. Student/education records and institution-specific policy allocation without blanket compliance claims.
5. Subprocessors, notice/objection, flow-down terms, locations, and transfers.
6. Data-subject/student requests, institutional assistance, correction, export, deletion, and legal holds.
7. Security incident definition, cooperation, evidence, and approved notification process.
8. Retention, return/deletion, backup/audit exceptions, certification of disposition, and offboarding.
9. AI/provider use, training restrictions, high-impact decision prohibition, and feature disablement.
10. Accessibility/support responsibilities, business continuity, termination, liability, and governing-law issues for counsel.

Counsel must reconcile this outline with [DPA-ISSUE-LIST.md](DPA-ISSUE-LIST.md), the actual data flow, subprocessor register, security measures, pilot agreement, public policies, and customer requirements.
