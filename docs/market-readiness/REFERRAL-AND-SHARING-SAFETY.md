# Referral and sharing safety

Share links disclose exactly what object is shared, with whom, for how long and whether resharing/download is possible. Default to the minimum object and private audience; require preview and confirmation; support expiry and immediate revoke; avoid embedding education-record content in URLs/previews; audit access where appropriate.

Classmate invitations never reveal course membership without the inviter intentionally selecting it and having authority. No contact scraping, mass address-book upload, coercive rewards or public-by-default plans. Aggregate referral analytics excludes invitee identity/content and respects opt-out/deletion.
