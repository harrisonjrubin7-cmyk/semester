# Discovery call playbook

Ask: What student milestone is failing? Which cohort and dates? What do students do today? Where is evidence lost? Who owns outcome, operations, privacy/security, accessibility, procurement and budget? What minimum data is truly required? Can manual/read-only work? What baseline and outcome would justify conversion? What would stop the pilot? What is the decision date and path to signature?

Disqualify or defer when there is no urgent workflow/champion/decision path; the request requires authoritative writeback, individual risk scoring or prohibited data; cohort is <10 or >200 without a separate capacity review; customer expects unsupported certification/24×7 service; or the schedule bypasses trust/UAT.

Next step records owner, artifact, date and decision. Never end with an unowned “send information.”
