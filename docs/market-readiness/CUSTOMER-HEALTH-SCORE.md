# Customer health score

Use a decision narrative before a composite number. If a score is used, publish the formula and preserve its components.

| Dimension | Suggested weight | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| agreed outcome trajectory | 25% | approved pilot measures and caveats |
| implementation/readiness completion | 20% | checklist evidence |
| meaningful adoption / first win | 15% | privacy-thresholded workflow data |
| reliability and recovery | 15% | accepted telemetry/incidents |
| support burden and resolution | 10% | support system |
| sponsor/champion engagement | 10% | meeting/decision record |
| trust/compliance gates | 5% | open risk register |

A P0/P1 security, privacy, accessibility, legal, safety, or data-rights issue overrides the score and can stop the pilot. Never use the score to label an individual student.
