# Accessibility conformance plan

**Current status: YELLOW self-assessment / RED formal conformance claim.** Automated and structural testing is meaningful evidence but does not establish WCAG 2.2 AA conformance or a completed VPAT/ACR.

## Critical workflows to evaluate

- sign-up/sign-in/recovery and notices;
- onboarding and first-win setup;
- Today/This Week, tasks, calendar/deadlines, courses, study/writing, and support;
- account, privacy, export, deletion, and destructive confirmations;
- pilot administration, reporting, configuration, incident/degraded states, and offboarding.

## Manual test protocol

For every critical workflow record keyboard-only completion, visible focus, screen-reader labels/announcements, landmarks/headings, form errors, dialogs/popovers, tabs/menus, date controls, non-drag alternatives, contrast/non-color cues, reduced motion, zoom/reflow at 200%+, touch targets, mobile orientation, plain language, slow/error states, and recovery. Name browser, OS, assistive technology, viewport, tester, date, result, issue, severity, owner, and retest evidence.

## Release rule

All critical blockers must be remediated and retested by a qualified reviewer before paid pilot launch. Publish limitations and an accommodation/support path. Do not issue a conformance or VPAT/ACR claim without the appropriate verified process.
