# Buyer personas

| Stakeholder | Main concern | Value proposition | Evidence required | Honest response / current limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | outcomes, risk, fit, budget | one bounded student-success workflow with a decision date | signed charter, scorecard, weekly review, cost and conversion logic | no proven causal retention/GPA outcome; measure leading indicators first |
| Student success/advising leader | student clarity, staff burden, adoption | organized next actions and support discovery for a defined cohort | journey UAT, activation/first-win metrics, support plan | not authoritative advising or an automated risk system |
| CIO / IT | architecture, identity, integration, operability | manual/read-only-first deployment with explicit controls and exit | architecture/data flow, role tests, monitoring, restore/rollback, integration matrix | no live institutional integration is assumed |
| Security/privacy officer | access, purpose, retention, incident response | minimum-necessary data, tenant/role controls, audit and rights workflows | current control matrix, subprocessor list, target test, incident and deletion evidence | no certification or blanket legal-compliance claim |
| Procurement/legal | contract, insurance, DPA, accessibility, support | tightly scoped paper with clear exclusions and offboarding | counsel-approved agreement/DPA, insurance decision, accessibility plan, support expectations | drafts are not executed agreements or legal advice |
| Faculty/staff champion | workflow fit, training, student burden | guided setup and weekly feedback with a reversible scope | demo, operator training, known-limitations sheet, escalation path | first pilot deliberately excludes broad faculty workflow replacement |
| Student champion | usefulness, privacy, friction | a coherent plan today without waiting for an integration | first-win demo, plain-language notice, controls, human help | participation and data use must be transparent; no hidden surveillance |

## Objection posture

- “Are you FERPA compliant?” — Describe implemented controls and contracting posture; the institution and counsel determine fit. Do not answer with a blanket yes.
- “Do you integrate with our systems?” — Name only approved, tested connections. Default the first pilot to manual/read-only setup.
- “Will this improve retention?” — State the measurement hypothesis and leading indicators; do not promise causality.
- “Is it accessible?” — Share automated/self-assessment evidence and the open qualified manual-review gate; do not claim conformance or a VPAT/ACR.
