# Market-readiness baseline

**Assessment date:** 2026-10-02
**Repository baseline:** `origin/main` at `92eeafd9`
**Scope:** repository evidence and the unactivated operating package in this directory. This is not proof of a live institutional deployment, customer approval, legal approval, accessibility conformance, or certification.

## Readiness scale

- **GREEN:** complete, tested, documented, supported, and safe to sell for the stated scope.
- **YELLOW:** usable only inside a clearly limited pilot or beta with named owners, explicit safeguards, and written limitations.
- **RED:** incomplete, unverified, insufficiently safe, or not supportable; do not sell or promote as available.

## What Semester is today

Semester is a broad student academic operating system with local/manual and authenticated workflows for orientation, daily planning, courses, workload, calendar, studying, writing, communication, progress, recovery, and institution-controlled experiences. The repository contains extensive product, tenant-control, privacy, security, data-rights, degraded-mode, and operating designs. Those controls establish a strong implementation foundation; they do not establish institutional authorization or production operation.

## Demonstrably ready in the repository

- Mature student planning and academic workflows with extensive automated coverage.
- Manual/local-first paths that can create value without an institutional write integration.
- Tenant, role, capability, feature-control, audit, privacy-threshold, export/deletion, degraded-mode, and rollback structures.
- Draft pilot, support, incident, privacy, procurement, accessibility, implementation, measurement, and offboarding materials.
- A fail-closed posture for institutional relationships and capabilities that have not been approved.

## Pilot-ready only after named-environment acceptance

- Student onboarding, manual course setup, Today/This Week planning, workload/deadline visibility, support discovery, and aggregate reporting.
- Institution/cohort configuration, role assignment, audit review, privacy-thresholded reporting, and offboarding.
- Optional read-only calendar or institution-approved data exchange after provider, consent, reconciliation, and recovery acceptance.

The current repository ceiling for these items is **YELLOW**, not GREEN.

## Not ready to sell or claim

- Production SSO, SCIM, SIS, LMS, or LTI integration for a named institution.
- SIS/LMS writes or official-record authority.
- FERPA compliance, SOC 2 certification, a completed VPAT/ACR, WCAG conformance, or “enterprise-grade for every institution.”
- Vanderbilt approval, partnership, deployment, or operation.
- 24/7 support, contractual uptime, disaster-recovery guarantees, or fixed implementation timelines.
- Proven retention, GPA, graduation, enrollment, advising-efficiency, or revenue outcomes.
- High-impact automated decisions in admissions, aid, discipline, grading, health, disability, conduct, or immigration.

## Strongest initial wedge

The recommended wedge is a **Student Success and Registration Readiness Pilot** for one 50–200 student cohort, one milestone, manual/read-only inputs, no system-of-record writes, privacy-thresholded reporting, a 30–60 day implementation window followed by an agreed measurement period, weekly operating review, and a pre-scheduled conversion/offboarding decision.

The fallback is a lower-integration **Student Productivity and Academic Workload Pilot** centered on manual course/task/calendar setup and the Today/This Week planning loop.

## Candidate personas

- **Institutional buyer:** VP/director of student success, advising, enrollment, or a dean/program leader with a defined cohort and near-term academic milestone.
- **Operational champion:** advising/program lead able to own onboarding, weekly reviews, support routing, and outcome interpretation.
- **Technical/risk reviewers:** CIO/IT, security, privacy, accessibility, procurement, and counsel.
- **Individual user:** a student seeking a coherent plan for today and the coming week without waiting for an institutional integration.

## Highest-value end-to-end use cases

1. Student enters or imports a limited academic plan and receives a prioritized Today/This Week view.
2. Student sees workload, deadlines, source/freshness, and recovery guidance without an official-record write.
3. Student discovers the right institutional or human support path.
4. Authorized pilot staff configure a bounded cohort and review aggregate, privacy-thresholded adoption/workflow evidence.
5. Pilot administrators export, delete, disable, roll back, or offboard according to the approved scope.

## Known assumptions and unknowns

- The suggested 50–200 cohort and buyer segment are hypotheses to validate, not proven market facts.
- Current operating capacity, legal entity details, insurance, budget, staffing, vendor contracts, production hosting, and payment readiness are unknown.
- Repository tests are not evidence of target-environment availability, institutional acceptance, or customer outcomes.

## Safe public claims now

- “Semester is building a unified academic operating system that helps students organize academic work and take clearer next actions.”
- “Semester supports manual and local-first planning workflows and is preparing a bounded institutional pilot model.”
- “Capabilities and integrations are assessed for each institution; unavailable or unapproved integrations are not represented as live.”

Use “designed to support” for security/privacy control descriptions and link every procurement claim to current evidence.

## Claims that remain prohibited

Do not use “compliant,” “certified,” “fully accessible,” “production deployed,” “Vanderbilt approved,” “proven outcomes,” “24/7,” “guaranteed,” “complete replacement,” or “enterprise ready” without the matching dated, external evidence and authority.

## 30 / 60 / 90 day direction

- **Days 1–30:** name owners and a qualified design partner; close legal scoping; freeze the pilot; complete data flow; obtain external security/accessibility reviews; prove target-environment monitoring, restore, incident, export/deletion, rollback, and support.
- **Days 31–60:** complete representative UAT; train operators; run a no-customer-data simulation; finish procurement responses; approve pricing and paper; convene launch council.
- **Days 61–90:** activate only on signed GO; operate weekly evidence reviews; hold midpoint and outcome decisions; convert, extend under written scope, or offboard cleanly.
