# Paid pilot proposal template

> Draft for business and counsel review; not legal advice or an executable agreement.

## 1. Problem and objective

Institution: `[name]`
Cohort and milestone: `[50–200 students; registration/term-start date]`
Current workflow/baseline: `[evidence]`
Objective: `[one measurable readiness outcome]`

## 2. Scope

Included workflows, data sources, roles, features and support: `[list]`
Explicit exclusions and disabled capabilities: `[list]`
Authoritative systems and safe fallback: `[list]`

## 3. Delivery and responsibilities

30–60 day implementation: discovery, trust review, configuration, UAT, training and launch. Name Semester/customer sponsor, champion, technical, privacy/security, accessibility and support owners. Attach the RACI and launch checklist.

## 4. Data, security and privacy

Attach data-flow/minimization map, permitted use, retention/deletion/export/offboarding, subprocessors, AI/provider use, incident notice, audit/access and integration mode. Manual/read-only is the default. No forbidden high-risk data or individual risk profiling.

## 5. Measures and decisions

Baseline: `[value/method/date]`
Three to five targets: `[activation, readiness completion, weekly planning, usefulness/confidence, operational outcome]`
Guardrails: `[privacy, accessibility, incidents, support, opt-out]`
Decision date and choices: `convert / expand / pause / stop`.

## 6. Commercials

Pilot fee: `[assumption subject to approval]`; implementation fee: `[assumption]`; taxes: `[as applicable]`; annual conversion method: `[assumption]`; pilot credit: `[rule]`. No price is final until an authorized quote and agreement are signed.

## 7. Support and service targets

Name hours, channels, severity definitions, response targets, maintenance, exclusions and status communications that staffing can actually support. Do not promise 24/7 absent an accepted rota.

## 8. Risks and exit

List known limitations, dependencies, stop conditions, rollback, data export, access revocation, deletion/retention exceptions, completion confirmation and transition assistance.
