# Security and privacy readiness

**Repository controls: YELLOW. Paid-pilot activation: RED. Certification status: none claimed.**

## Implemented or designed foundation

- Authentication/session, role/capability, tenant scope, row-level policy, audit, feature-control, and server-side secret boundaries.
- Data inventory/lineage, purpose and minimization guidance, retention/export/deletion machinery, incident and data-rights runbooks.
- AI governance, provider controls, feature disablement, user disclosure/correction patterns, and prohibition of unsupported high-impact decisions.
- CI/security configuration and a vulnerability-reporting posture.

## Required before paid pilot

- named security and privacy owners with backups;
- target-environment threat/configuration review and cross-tenant/authorization acceptance;
- current dependency, secret, static, dynamic, and scoped penetration evidence with no open P0/P1;
- production monitoring and audit export review;
- counsel-approved data roles, agreement/DPA position, retention/deletion schedule, subprocessor list, incident-notification process, and support-access policy;
- end-to-end export/deletion and backup-exception evidence;
- approved AI vendor/data-use/retention/evaluation boundaries for every enabled AI feature.

## Required language

Semester may describe verified controls and say it is “designed to support responsible handling” of student information. It must not claim FERPA compliance, SOC 2 certification, universal regulatory compliance, zero risk, or institutional approval. See [security matrix](SECURITY-CONTROL-MATRIX.md), [privacy program](PRIVACY-PROGRAM.md), [AI governance](AI-GOVERNANCE-PROGRAM.md), and [data flow](DATA-FLOW-DIAGRAM.md).
