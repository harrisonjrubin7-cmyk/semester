# Pricing and packaging

All figures require market discovery and finance approval. This document defines logic, not invented price points.

> **Status of the offer described here:** a target, not a current one. Paid institutional pilots are **NO-GO / RED** ([`GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md`](../../GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md)); "paid pilot" must not appear in public copy until that changes. Public wording today is *design-partner discovery and scoping, non-activation*.

## Price status

- Individual free plan: **[PRICE TO BE CONFIRMED]**; define the safe, useful core before publishing limits.
- Individual paid plan: **[PRICE TO BE CONFIRMED]**; do not accept payment until billing, tax, cancellation, refund, renewal, support, export, and deletion operations are approved.
- Institutional pilot: **[PRICE TO BE CONFIRMED]** fixed scope/cohort fee plus **[PRICE TO BE CONFIRMED]** implementation fee.
- Annual institutional license: **[PRICE TO BE CONFIRMED]** minimum platform commitment plus approved student/cohort/module bands.
- Optional integrations or professional services: **[PRICE TO BE CONFIRMED]** only after feasibility, security, delivery cost, and support scope are approved.

## Recommended structure

- **Paid pilot:** fixed cohort/workflow fee + implementation fee, sized by cohort, duration, data complexity, support and evidence burden.
- **Annual conversion:** minimum platform commitment plus active/sponsored student band; price approved modules/integrations separately.
- **Pilot credit:** credit a defined share of pilot software fees—not pass-through or bespoke implementation costs—only when the annual agreement is executed by the decision deadline.

## Tradeoffs

| Model | Strength | Risk | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| per student | easy to scale/explain | discourages broad access; roster disputes | annual sponsored cohorts |
| per cohort | clear pilot budget | weak beyond pilot | initial paid pilot |
| per module | aligns expansion | complexity and fragmented UX | later approved add-ons |
| platform fee | procurement simplicity | overprices small entry | mature annual deployments |

## Quote controls

Every quote names cohort, term, workflow, environment, included services, data/integrations, support, evidence deliverables, exclusions, taxes, payment, expiry, conversion credit and offboarding. Discounts require a reason and get something in return: multi-year term, reference rights, prepaid term, reduced scope or decision speed. Never discount around security/privacy/accessibility work.

## Minimum threshold

Do not accept a pilot whose fee cannot cover implementation, support, trust review, infrastructure/AI variable cost and outcome reporting with a documented contingency. Finance must calculate the threshold from observed delivery cost before a number is published.

## Commercial policy gates

- Billing cadence, taxes, payment terms, quote validity, auto-renewal, cancellation, refund, nonpayment, suspension, data export, deletion, and post-termination retention require finance/counsel approval before publication.
- Pilot pricing must include the cost of onboarding, training, weekly review, reporting, support, security/privacy/accessibility review, and clean offboarding.
- A student must never lose access to export or deletion rights because a subscription ends.
- No free trial, marketing consent, renewal, or cancellation flow may rely on dark patterns or ambiguous terms.
