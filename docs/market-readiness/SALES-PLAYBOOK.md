# Sales playbook

## Process

1. Qualify problem, cohort, sponsor/champion, data/integration risk, timeline, procurement, budget authority, and conversion potential.
2. Run discovery before demonstrating features.
3. Demonstrate only the approved workflow with a visible “available / pilot-dependent / planned” capability legend.
4. Complete technical, security, privacy, accessibility, legal, and operational validation.
5. Issue a scoped proposal with price placeholders or approved pricing, exclusions, evidence, implementation, support, measurement, decision date, and offboarding.
6. Contract only after counsel/finance/insurance and launch prerequisites are clear.
7. Kick off, configure, train, accept, launch on GO, review weekly, decide at midpoint/final, then convert or offboard.

## Sales claim ceiling

Sell the narrow result and the evidence process. Do not sell system replacement, certified compliance, live integrations, guaranteed outcomes, 24/7 support, universal accessibility, or a Vanderbilt relationship. Discovery and demos may begin now; accepting a paid launch obligation remains RED.
