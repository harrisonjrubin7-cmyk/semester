# Renewal playbook

Start the conversion decision before launch by setting the decision date, evidence requirements, approving stakeholders, annual-scope hypothesis, price authority, and offboarding alternative.

At midpoint, assess outcome trajectory, reliability, support cost, accessibility/privacy/security issues, champion strength, integration burden, and repeatability. At final review, present results with caveats, total delivery burden, unresolved risks, proposed annual scope, price, owner capacity, and data/offboarding disposition.

Convert only when the sponsor approves value, P0/P1 risks are closed, support/engineering capacity exists, and annual scope is adjacent and supportable. Otherwise extend only under written narrow scope or offboard cleanly. No auto-renewal or reference/case-study use without explicit approved terms and permission.
