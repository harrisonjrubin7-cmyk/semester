# Pilot success scorecard

Complete and sign before launch. Baselines and targets are customer-specific; bracketed values are fields, not defaults.

| Category | Measure | Baseline | Target | Method/frequency | Guardrail / decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| activation | cohort meeting the activation definition | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | event, weekly | no coercive reminder pattern |
| engagement | weekly prepared action rate | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | aggregate event, weekly | cell ≥10 |
| workflow | registration-readiness checklist completion | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | aggregate state, milestone | not proof of official registration |
| student value | clarity/confidence/usefulness | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | brief consented survey | report response rate |
| operations | avoidable question/contact proxy | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | approved category counts | no content surveillance |
| reliability | successful sessions, errors, recovery | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | telemetry/support | zero unresolved P0/P1 |
| trust | privacy, accessibility, deletion/support requests | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | case register | any severe harm pauses pilot |
| economics | delivery/support/AI cost per activated student | `[ ]` | `[ ]` | finance weekly | no hidden unpaid workload |

## Decision

- **Convert:** primary target met, guardrails green, supportable economics, signed annual scope.
- **Expand:** convert criteria plus adjacent cohort/module evidence and capacity.
- **Pause:** evidence inconclusive or remediable guardrail/operating gap.
- **Stop:** value absent, unacceptable harm/risk, unresolved high severity, or no sponsor decision.

Testimonials and referenceability require separate written permission and must state scope, cohort and dates.
