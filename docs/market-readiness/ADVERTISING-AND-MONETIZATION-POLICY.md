# Advertising and monetization policy

Semester does not sell student or institutional data. Behavioral targeting may not use education records, grades, schedules, tasks, private conversations, AI prompts, health/disability/counseling/conduct/immigration data, location history, support cases or institutional data. Core privacy, safety, accessibility, export, deletion and opt-out controls are never paywalled.

Institution-sponsored tenants have advertising/sponsorship disabled by default and may prohibit it contractually. Any future consumer advertising is contextual to public page content, visibly labelled, age-appropriate, separated from academic decisions, frequency limited, and reviewed by privacy/counsel. Sponsored content may not masquerade as an institutional recommendation.

Free individual and institution-sponsored use must be visually and contractually distinct. Monetization changes require a data/AI/privacy impact review and user notice before activation.
