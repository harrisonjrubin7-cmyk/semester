# Experimentation protocol

Pre-register owner, hypothesis, user benefit, eligible population, exclusions, randomization or comparison, primary metric, guardrails, data fields/retention, minimum practical effect, duration/sample rationale, stop/rollback rules and review authority. Obtain required institution/user consent.

Do not experiment on consent clarity, privacy/security defaults, accessibility access, adverse academic decisions, financial aid, discipline, disability/accommodation, crisis/support routing or manipulative notification intensity. Small cohorts use estimation, confidence intervals and qualitative triangulation; “not significant” is not “no effect.”

Stop on P0/P1, data integrity failure, credible guardrail harm, inaccessible variant, inability to honor withdrawal or unexpected sensitive collection. Archive result, limitations and decision, including null/negative outcomes.
