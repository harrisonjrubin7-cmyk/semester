Semester should also include complete systems for **student-athletes, varsity teams, club sports, intramurals, student organizations, and campus clubs**.

# Semester Athletics

Semester Athletics should replace separate applications used for team communication, scheduling, training, travel, academic monitoring, forms, and athlete services.

## Student-athlete dashboard

Each student-athlete should see:

- Practice schedule
- Competition schedule
- Strength and conditioning sessions
- Treatment appointments
- Team meetings
- Travel itinerary
- Required forms
- Academic deadlines
- Eligibility status
- Equipment reminders
- Coach announcements
- Meal and recovery information

Athletic events should automatically appear alongside classes, assignments, exams, and advising appointments.

## Schedule-conflict management

Semester should detect conflicts between:

- Classes and practices
- Exams and competitions
- Office hours and team meetings
- Assignments and travel
- Advising appointments and training
- Multiple athletic commitments

It should help athletes:

- Notify professors about official travel
- Generate absence letters
- Request alternative exam arrangements
- Schedule assignments before travel
- Create travel-specific study plans
- Find available tutoring times

## Team management

Coaches and authorized staff should be able to:

- Build rosters
- Publish schedules
- Record attendance
- Send announcements
- Organize practices
- Assign workouts
- Manage equipment
- Upload playbooks and videos
- Create team groups
- Manage travel
- Track required forms
- Schedule individual meetings

## Training and performance

Semester Athletics could include:

- Workout programs
- Strength sessions
- Conditioning plans
- Exercise demonstrations
- Personal records
- Training completion
- Wellness questionnaires
- Recovery tracking
- Coach feedback
- Performance reports

Medical and wellness information must only be visible to specifically authorized staff.

## Competition and travel

Athletes should be able to access:

- Competition dates
- Opponent information
- Departure times
- Flight or bus details
- Hotel information
- Room assignments
- Meal schedules
- Packing lists
- Required documents
- Emergency contacts
- Return times

Travel automatically updates the athlete’s calendar and academic planner.

## Athletic academics

Athletes should have access to:

- Academic-support appointments
- Tutoring
- Study-hall requirements
- Eligibility requirements
- Credit-progress information
- Academic calendars
- Missed-class documentation
- Advisor communication

Official eligibility decisions should remain controlled by authorized institutional personnel.

## Athletic forms

Semester should manage:

- Participation forms
- Emergency contacts
- Insurance documents
- Travel agreements
- Equipment agreements
- Compliance acknowledgments
- Physical examination confirmations
- Consent forms

Sensitive health records should be stored in a protected medical area with stricter permissions.

## Team communication

Include:

- Team announcements
- Direct messaging
- Position or unit groups
- Coach messages
- Emergency alerts
- File sharing
- Meeting links
- Read confirmations

## Video and performance review

Using Semester Video, teams should be able to:

- Upload competition footage
- Organize film by game or practice
- Add timestamps
- Create clips
- Draw annotations
- Add coach comments
- Build highlight reels
- Share position-specific film
- Create scouting presentations

# Semester Clubs

Semester Clubs should replace separate club-management, event, messaging, form, payment, and design applications.

## Club discovery

Students should be able to browse organizations by:

- Academic interests
- Sports
- Culture
- Community service
- Politics
- Professional development
- Religion
- Arts
- Media
- Greek life
- Recreation
- Social interests

Every club page should display:

- Club description
- Leadership
- Meeting schedule
- Upcoming events
- Membership requirements
- Contact information
- Photos and videos
- Open positions
- Required dues
- Application information

## Joining a club

Students should be able to:

- Request membership
- Complete an application
- Attend an introductory meeting
- Sign required forms
- Pay dues
- Join communication channels
- Add meetings to their calendars
- Select areas of interest
- Apply for leadership positions

## Club dashboard

Members should see:

- Upcoming meetings
- Events
- Announcements
- Tasks
- Shared documents
- Committee information
- Dues status
- Volunteer hours
- Elections
- Attendance
- Member directory

## Club leadership tools

Club leaders should be able to:

- Manage membership
- Assign officer roles
- Create committees
- Schedule meetings
- Send announcements
- Track attendance
- Assign tasks
- Store documents
- Create forms
- Collect dues
- Submit university requests
- Manage budgets
- Hold elections
- Create annual reports

## Events

Clubs should be able to:

- Create events
- Reserve campus spaces
- Request equipment
- Sell or distribute tickets
- Collect RSVPs
- Create waitlists
- Check in attendees
- Send reminders
- Display event QR codes
- Share maps and directions
- Collect feedback afterward

Events should automatically appear in the university calendar and on relevant club pages.

## Club finances

Authorized club officers should be able to manage:

- Annual budgets
- Funding requests
- Member dues
- Reimbursements
- Event expenses
- Purchase requests
- Receipts
- Account balances
- Fundraising
- Financial reports

All transactions should use university approval rules and audit records.

## Club documents and content

Semester’s productivity tools should connect directly to clubs:

- Semester Documents for meeting minutes and proposals
- Semester Sheets for budgets and membership lists
- Semester Forms for applications and surveys
- Semester Presentations for meetings
- Semester Design for flyers and promotional material
- Semester Video for event videos
- Semester Meetings for virtual meetings

## Elections and governance

Semester should support:

- Officer nominations
- Candidate statements
- Secure voting
- Anonymous ballots
- Eligibility rules
- Voting deadlines
- Automatic results
- Constitution storage
- Amendment voting
- Leadership transitions

## Volunteer and service tracking

Clubs should be able to record:

- Volunteer opportunities
- Registrations
- Attendance
- Service hours
- Supervisor verification
- Impact reports
- Certificates

Verified service hours could be included in a student’s extracurricular record.

# Student involvement record

Semester should create an optional verified record showing:

- Clubs joined
- Leadership positions
- Athletic participation
- Volunteer hours
- Awards
- Certifications
- Campus employment
- Projects
- Competitions
- Conferences

Students could use approved information to generate:

- Résumés
- Portfolios
- Scholarship applications
- Internship applications
- Leadership summaries

Students should control what information is shared outside the institution.

The student navigation could include:

1. Home  
2. Courses  
3. Calendar  
4. Assignments  
5. Messages  
6. Campus  
7. Create  
8. More  

The **Campus** section would contain:

- Athletics
- Intramurals
- Clubs
- Events
- Recreation
- Dining
- Housing
- Transportation
- Student services

Semester would therefore support the student’s full experience: **academics, productivity, communication, athletics, organizations, campus involvement, finances, and daily university life—all within one application.**
