# I want to flag something unsafe

> **Type:** help · **Audience:** students, support · **Owner:** `trust` · **Truth:** held · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04 · **Held by:** `app/src/lib/docs/support.test.ts`

Use this page to find where a safety concern goes and what Semester does and does not do with it; stop reading and contact emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED_NOT_RELEASED for Community reporting. The report form exists and is tested, and Community is off unless it is switched on for a school or a preview ([truth table](../../FEATURE-TRUTH-TABLE.md)). The Trust & Safety seat has no holder in the seat register, so no staffed review of reports is evidenced ([how support is staffed](../README.md#staffing-today)).

## Symptom

You say one of these:

- "Someone posted something that worries me."
- "I think someone could be hurt."
- "I'm worried about myself."
- "Where do I report this?"

## Check

The app shows this notice on every report form and crisis-adjacent screen. It is quoted exactly and is not paraphrased here:

> If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or your campus emergency/safety service now. Semester Community is not monitored as an emergency-response service.

1. Is someone in immediate danger? Follow the notice above before anything else.
2. Is it about a post in Semester Community? Use **Report this post** on that post. If Community is not switched on for your school, you will not see posts or this form.
3. Is it about your own wellbeing, or about care, basic needs or safety on campus? Open **Support**. It is a map of care, basic-needs, access, safety and campus offices, and says which door to use.

## Fix

1. Open the post, choose **Report this post**, and answer "What is wrong with this post?"
2. Tick "Someone could be hurt soon" if that is true. The most urgent reports are routed to professional Trust & Safety reviewers only; volunteer moderators never see them ([crisis response runbook](../../CRISIS-RESPONSE-RUNBOOK.md)).
3. Add "Anything a reviewer should know (optional)" if it helps, then press **Send report**. Do not include more personal detail than a reviewer needs.
4. A report cannot be taken back by deleting your account. A report you filed is a record about somebody else, and it stays, no longer naming you.
5. For a concern outside Community, use your campus emergency or safety service. The documents behind this page describe no other Semester channel for safety reports.

## Not your fault

- **Community is off.** There is then no report form to open. That is not a fault.
- **Semester Community is not an emergency service.** The notice says so.
- **No staffed review is evidenced yet.** The seat register shows the Trust & Safety seat vacant. No response time is committed.

## Contact

Support staff who receive a safety concern follow the [escalation map](../internal/escalation-map.md). They do not diagnose, and they do not treat a keyword as a diagnosis.

### What to send

- What the post or situation is, in a few sentences, and where in the app you saw it (the screen).
- When you saw it.
- Whether you believe anyone is at risk now.

### What not to send

- Passwords, or screenshots of private messages that are not yours to share.
- Another student's ID number, or health or conduct details beyond what a reviewer needs.
- Do not use support to reach emergency help. Use emergency services or your campus safety service.

### Status and known limits

- [Status page source](../../../app/public/status.html) and [known limits](../../pilot/KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md).
- No response time is committed. See [how support is staffed](../README.md#staffing-today).
