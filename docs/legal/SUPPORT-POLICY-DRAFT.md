## 1. How to get help

- **In the app:** *Settings › About › Say something* sends a message straight
  to the team.
- **By email:** harrisonjrubin7@gmail.com [DECIDE: dedicated support address].
- **Campus questions** (registration, aid, housing, advising): *Get help* in
  the app routes you to the right office at your school; that office answers,
  not Semester.
- **Is it down?** The status page checks the service from your browser.

A person reads every message. Semester is run by a very small team; **we do
not yet promise a response time**, and there is no phone or chat support.
[DECIDE: business hours and a first-response target, once support is staffed.]

## 2. Support tickets

In-app tickets are built but not switched on yet. When they are, you can open
a ticket about your account, sync, a bug, accessibility, privacy, how to do
something, or anything else — up to five a day — and follow it to resolution.
The system is set to aim for a first response within 24 hours for
accessibility and privacy, and 72 hours for everything else. [COUNSEL REQUIRED:
whether those become commitments when tickets are switched on; no response
time is decided, and this is not the privacy-request clock (P-03).]

## 3. We do not look at your data to help you — unless you let us

Support staff cannot see your semester. If seeing it would help, you can grant
one verified person at your school a view of your learning progress — a
summary, not your notes — for one to seven days. You can revoke it at any
time, and every look is recorded where you can see it.

## 4. What we will not do through support

Change official records at your school, register you for classes, or reset
another person's account. Security reports go to the contact in
[`SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md); accessibility barriers are covered by the
[Accessibility Statement](ACCESSIBILITY-STATEMENT-DRAFT.md).

## 5. Through your school

If your school provides Semester, contact its help desk first where it asks
you to; its agreement with us sets the support it receives.
