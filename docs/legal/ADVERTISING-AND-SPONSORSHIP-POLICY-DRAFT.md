## 1. No advertising

Semester shows no advertising. It does not sell your information, build an
advertising profile of you, or use your education records, grades, plans or
behaviour to target anything at you.

## 2. Things that are not advertising

- **Your school's own messages**, such as a registration reminder.
- **Opportunities** a campus office, employer or partner lists — a job, an
  event, a student deal. A moderator approves each one before it appears, and
  it is labelled with who listed it. Listing one is not paid placement.
  [DECIDE: whether a fee is ever charged; if so, it is sponsorship and section 3
  applies.]
- **Referral links**: Semester counts sign-ups from a link you share; nobody is
  paid for them today.

## 3. Sponsorship, if a school ever turns it on

Sponsorship is off everywhere. If a school enables it:

- Every sponsored item is **labelled as sponsored**, with the sponsor's name.
- It is **contextual**: it is never chosen using your personal data.
- Allowed only from: education and careers, student services, verified
  scholarships, non-clinical wellness, verified housing and transport, and
  campus community.
- **Never** from: predatory lending, gambling, alcohol, tobacco or cannabis,
  political campaigns, adult content, unverified employment, academic
  cheating services, discriminatory offers, data brokers, surveillance, or
  unapproved financial products.
- **Never shown** in AI answers, advising, course recommendations or
  rankings, your degree plan, registration, financial-aid deadlines, add/drop,
  crisis support, accommodations, or grades.
