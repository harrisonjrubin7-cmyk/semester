## 1. The rule

**Your work is kept until you delete it.** Semester does not remove what you
created on a schedule. What *is* removed on a schedule is information about
how you use Semester, and information whose job is done.

## 2. How long each kind of record is kept

| Record | Kept |
| --- | --- |
| Your courses, deadlines, notes, plans, files and settings | Until you delete them or your account |
| Files you attach to notes | Never leave your device |
| Assistant conversations (the transcript) | On your device only, until you delete them |
| Assistant "memories" (a short goal, preference, decision, focus area or context note, in the `ai_memories` table) | Until you delete them or your account. The table exists and syncs if used; no part of the app writes to it today. [DECIDE: keep it, or remove it before publication] |
| Three daily usage facts (opened, added a course, answered a study card) | 400 days |
| Log of who read your rows (e.g. a calendar feed was fetched) | 90 days |
| Queued reminders | Until sent; all of them when you switch reminders off |
| Support tickets | Until you delete your account [DECIDE: a period for closed tickets] |
| Community reports closed with no action | 90 days |
| Community reports that led to action or an appeal | 1 year |
| Audit record of moderation decisions | 3 years |
| Reports you made, after you delete your account | Kept, with your name removed |
| Unfinished sign-ups | 30 days |
| Messages sent through the company site's forms, with the campaign link, landing page and referring site they carry | [DECIDE: no period is set yet] |
| Payment records (individual plans) | Seven years after the end of the year each was made; never while the subscription is live. School billing follows the school's contract |
| Database backups | 7 days after each daily backup [VERIFY on the provider dashboard] |

For school deployments, your school's agreement sets retention for records it
provides, and AI usage counts follow its AI policy.

## 3. Deleting your data

- **Everything you synced:** *Delete my account* in Settings removes it in one
  server step. A short record that a deletion happened — the date and how many
  rows, nothing else — is kept so we can prove it.
- **Your sign-in (your email address):** email harrisonjrubin7@gmail.com
  [DECIDE: dedicated address]; an app in your browser cannot remove it.
- **This device:** clear Semester's site data in your browser, after
  exporting with *Take it with you* if you want a copy.

A marker that an account was deleted is kept 90 days so other devices stop
syncing it, then removed.

## 4. Backups

Something you delete can remain in a backup for up to 7 days [VERIFY]. Nobody
reads backups. If the service is ever restored from one, we re-apply the
deletions we can identify before reopening it, and tell everyone who used
Semester in that period that a deletion made then may need to be made again.
