## 1. What Semester holds that someone else could see

Most of what you put in Semester is never visible to anyone else. **Files you
attach stay on your device**; Semester does not upload them. What other people
can see:

- posts, messages and images in community spaces and class rooms (Community
  is switched off in production today; images cannot be published at all
  until a safety-scanning provider is configured);
- a form you publish, which anyone with its link can open;
- course study packs a faculty member publishes, which are links and
  citations, never files;
- listings a campus office or partner publishes, after a moderator approves
  them.

## 2. Respect other people's rights

Upload and share only what you have the right to. Course materials usually
belong to your instructor or school; follow their rules about sharing them.

## 3. Sending a takedown notice

Email [DECIDE: agent address; until then harrisonjrubin7@gmail.com with the
subject "Copyright"] with:

1. your signature (typed is fine) and contact details;
2. the work you say is infringed;
3. the exact place in Semester (a link, or a description precise enough to
   find it);
4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by
   you, your agent or the law;
5. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that
   you own or may act for the owner of the right.

## 4. What we do

We remove or disable access to the material promptly once a complete notice is
received, tell the person who posted it, and give them a copy of the notice.

## 5. Counter-notice

If you believe your material was removed by mistake or misidentified, send us
your signature and contact details, the material and where it was, a
statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake
or misidentification, and your consent to the jurisdiction of [DECIDE with
counsel]. We will forward it to the notifier and may restore the material
10–14 business days later unless they tell us they have filed a court action.
