## 1. The short version

Use Semester to plan, study and get help. Do not use it to hurt people, to
cheat, to break things, or to get at information that is not yours.

## 2. Do not use Semester to

**Harm people.**
- Harass, bully, threaten, stalk, or target anyone, including in class rooms,
  groups, community spaces and messages.
- Share someone's private information without their permission (doxxing), or
  intimate or sexual images of anyone without their consent.
- Impersonate a person, an instructor, an office or your school.
- Post hateful or discriminatory content, or content that sexualises minors.

**Cheat or help others cheat.**
- Break your school's academic integrity policy, or a course's rules about AI.
  An instructor can set a course's AI use to *allowed*, *allowed with
  disclosure*, *required* or *not allowed*; Semester shows that rule and you
  must follow it.
- Post or request answer keys, or ask someone to take an exam or do graded
  work for you.

**Break or abuse the service.**
- Upload malware, or links meant to phish or scam.
- Probe, scan, overload or get around Semester's security, rate limits or
  access controls; test for vulnerabilities outside the rules in
  [`SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md).
- Scrape Semester, automate accounts, or resell access without our written
  agreement. Semester has no public API.
- Send bulk or repetitive content. Semester limits how often any account can
  post, message, react, report and create things; the limits are set in the
  database and are not an allowance to use up.

**Reach what is not yours.**
- Use another person's account, or read or change their data without their
  permission.
- Claim a course, a role or a school you do not belong to. A course code you
  enter is taken on trust; using one to join a class you are not in is a
  breach of this policy.
- Use a support or advisor grant beyond what the student shared, or after it
  expires.

**Break the law**, or help anyone else do so.

## 3. What happens if you do

We may remove content, limit what an account can do in a community, pause or
suspend an account, or preserve evidence, following the moderation process in
the Community Guidelines. Where it is safe and lawful, we tell you what we did
and why, and you can ask a different reviewer to look again. Reporting
someone in good faith is never a breach, even if the report is not upheld.

If your school provides your access, a serious breach may also be reported to
your school where our agreement with it allows. [DECIDE with counsel: when,
and under which agreement.]
