Keep your sign-in secure, and tell us promptly if you think someone else has

## 4. Your content

What you create or enter in Semester is yours. You give us only the
permission we need to store, sync, display and process it to provide Semester
to you, including sending text to an AI provider when you press an AI button.
That permission ends when you delete the content or your account, except for
backups kept for the period our Privacy Policy states and anything you shared
with others. We do not sell your content or use it to train AI models.

You are responsible for having the right to upload what you upload, such as
course materials. Respect your instructors' and your school's rules about
sharing them.

## 5. Acceptable use

Do not use Semester to:

- break the law, or your school's academic integrity policy;
- harass, threaten, impersonate or harm anyone, including in class threads
  and community spaces;
- upload malware, or probe, overload or get around Semester's security,
  rate limits or access controls;
- access another person's account or data without their permission;
- scrape Semester, or resell it without our written agreement.

We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules, following
the moderation process described in the app. Where we can, we will tell you
why and let you respond.

## 6. AI features

AI answers can be wrong. Check anything that matters — a deadline, a grade
calculation, a requirement — against your syllabus, your instructor or your
school's official systems. Your school or instructor may limit how AI can be
used in a course. Semester shows those limits, and you are responsible for
following them. If you use your own API key, your agreement with that
provider also applies.

estimate, not an official one. Semester does not register you for classes,
submit official forms, or change your records at your school unless a feature
clearly says it does and asks you to confirm first.
