## 1. Cookies

Semester does not use cookies — not for sign-in, analytics, advertising or
anything else.

## 2. What Semester keeps in your browser instead

Semester works on your device first, so it keeps your work in your browser's
own storage, where only Semester's pages can read it:

| Where | What | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local storage and IndexedDB | Your semester: courses, deadlines, notes, plans, settings, assistant conversations, and your own AI key if you entered one | Until you delete it, sign out and clear it, or clear your browser's site data |
| Local storage | Your sign-in session, if you signed in | Until you sign out |
| Local storage | A daily backup copy of your semester, on this device only | One week |
| Local storage | Unsent drafts | Two weeks |
| Local storage | A count of which screens you open, on this device | Until you clear it |
| Session storage | Where you were on a screen, and short notes during sign-in | Until you close the tab |
| Offline cache | The app's files and media, so it works offline | Until the app updates or you clear site data |

On the company site, your choices on its preferences panel and your progress
on its checklists are kept in local storage on your device. For each visit it
also keeps, in session storage, the campaign tags in the page address (if
any), the page you landed on, and the address of the site that linked you
there. They are sent only with a form you choose to send, and are then kept
with your message (see the retention draft); your browser's copy is gone
when the tab closes.

## 3. Analytics

Semester has no third-party analytics, tracking pixels, advertising
identifiers or error-reporting scripts. If you are signed in, it records three
facts for each day you open it — that you opened it, whether you had added a
course, and whether you had answered a study card — and which screens you
have ever opened and when you last opened each. That is all, it is kept
400 days, and it is used only to learn whether Semester is useful.

## 4. Your choices

Clearing your browser's data for Semester removes everything above from that
device; if you are not signed in, that is the only copy, so export first with
*Take it with you*. Signing out stops syncing.
