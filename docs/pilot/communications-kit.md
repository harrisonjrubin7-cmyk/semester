# Registration Readiness pilot: communications kit

Status: drafted, not approved. Bracketed fields are placeholders. Do not send until the pilot owner,
support path and approved cohort are confirmed.

Rules: say what is true, what it means, and what the person can do. Never say Semester replaces the
official gradebook, registrar, or payment system, and never imply that a readiness result enrolls anyone.

## Student invite

```text
Subject: Check what may need attention before registration

Semester is available to help you review the information used for registration
planning. It can show what appears ready, what may need attention, and where
to get help.

Your institution's registration system remains the official record. A
readiness result does not enroll you in a course.

Open your readiness check: [link]

If something looks incomplete or out of date, Semester will show the source
and the next available step.
```

## Registrar launch note

```text
Subject: Registration Readiness pilot is available

The Registration Readiness pilot is now available to the approved cohort.
Semester provides a source-aware planning view, referral routing, receipts,
and reconciliation support.

It does not create official enrollment decisions or replace institutional
registrar records. Use the registrar workspace for routed cases, source
status, and reconciliation work.

Support route: [institution support path]
Pilot owner: [name and contact]
```

## Stale-source notice

```text
A source used for registration readiness is not current enough to support a
final readiness result. Your official registration record has not changed.

You can review the listed source details, try again later if a refresh is
available, or contact the Registrar's Office for an official decision.
```
