# First-day checklist — for pilot users

**As of 2026-10-03.** One page to tick through on the first day of a pilot,
for a student and for the staff member running it. Each line names the screen
it happens on. [`QUICK-START.md`](QUICK-START.md) is the longer walk;
[`KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md`](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md) is what to read before relying
on anything below.

## Student — before the first class

- [ ] Opened Semester in a browser and finished the five-screen tour
      (**Show me around again** on Help (`#/help`) replays it).
- [ ] Set the **term** on the third screen, and the **school** if you want the
      campus screens (meals, housing, map). Both can change later under Me.
- [ ] Added every course from its syllabus on Add a course (`#/import`) —
      PDF, Word or pasted text — and checked each on Edit the course
      (`#/edit`): dates, weightings, rooms, AI policy.
- [ ] Added the registrar's dates on Term deadlines (`#/registrar`):
      add/drop, withdrawal, registration.
- [ ] Looked at Today (`#/home`) and found the first deadline of the term on it.
- [ ] Decided about an account on Me → Account (`#/account`): signed in on
      every device you use, or chose to stay on one device.
- [ ] Downloaded a backup — Take it with you (`#/export`) → *Everything, as
      data* — and know where it is saved.
- [ ] Set alerts the way you want them under Me → Alerts (`#/notifs`), or off.
- [ ] Opened Support (`#/support`) once, so you know which door is which
      before you need one.
- [ ] Read [`KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md`](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md): Semester is not
      connected to your school's systems, rooms are not separated by school,
      and attachments do not sync between devices.

## Staff — before students arrive

- [ ] Walked the student list above yourself, on a phone and on a laptop.
- [ ] Have the support address to hand — **harrisonjrubin7@gmail.com** — and
      know that a reply comes from a person with no promised response time.
- [ ] Know the accessibility route: a report with **Accessibility** in the
      subject, escalated with **Accessibility escalation**, which reaches the
      accessibility seat. It is the route the public site's Accessibility page
      describes under *Report a barrier* (`app/src/site/pages.tsx`). No
      review by a person with a screen reader has been recorded yet, so
      expect to use it.
- [ ] Know the status page: from Help, *status page* — it checks the service
      from the browser that opens it, shows the last 90 days as recorded
      hourly since recording began, and sends no notifications.
- [ ] Told students what Semester will not do: register, pay, submit, or read
      the school's systems. The official system stays authoritative.
- [ ] Told students that the terms and privacy policy are drafts and not in
      force, and that everything can be exported or deleted at any time from
      Privacy and your rights (`#/privacy`).
- [ ] Agreed with the student who owns each course's syllabus that a corrected
      date goes in Semester *and* is confirmed against the official source.

## What a first day cannot include yet

Sign-in through the school, a connection to the registrar or the learning
system, multi-factor sign-in, a qualified accessibility audit, and a support
desk with a response time. Each is listed with its status in
[`KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md`](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md), and each is one of the
decisions in [`../LAUNCH-DECISIONS.md`](../LAUNCH-DECISIONS.md).
