# Registration Readiness pilot: support operating model

Status: specified, not staffed. Accountable owners are placeholders until the pilot is confirmed.

| Tier | Scope | Accountable owner | Required response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 0 | In-product explanation, source labels, safe next step | Product | Clear status and self-service path |
| Tier 1 | Navigation, account access, standard readiness questions | Support lead | Confirm context; do not interpret official records |
| Tier 2 | Source freshness, routing, reconciliation, functional defect | Registrar or integration owner | Create or update tracked case |
| Tier 3 | Access violation, privacy concern, production failure | Security or platform on-call | Incident process and preserved evidence |
| Institution office | Official academic decision, hold, enrollment, record correction | Named institutional owner | Institution-owned decision and outcome |

Semester organizes and explains a source-aware process. The institution makes official academic decisions.
