# RB-NN · Title as the responder would say it

## Symptom

What the responder sees: an alert, a student message, a failed probe. One or two sentences, in the words of the person who reports it.

## Impact

What a student experiences, and what still works. Be explicit about what is safe (their own work on the device) so the responder does not over-react or under-react.

## Diagnose

1. The cheapest check that splits the problem in two.
2. The next one. Name the dashboard, query or file exactly.

## Mitigate

1. The safest reversible step first (a kill switch, read-only mode, a redeploy of the last good version).
2. Never a step that cannot be undone before the cause is understood.

## Escalate

Who, after how long, and what to say. Say plainly when there is nobody to escalate to.

## Verify

- A check a stranger could repeat to confirm the fix.

## After

What is recorded, and when a postmortem is required.
