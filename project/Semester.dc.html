1A

Tab bar

Today / Courses / Calendar / Me. Today is a fixed sequence: next class, the checklist, the day's rail, the week. You always know where a thing lives — at the cost of four taps to compare two courses.

1B

One feed, filtered

No tabs. Classes and deadlines interleave in one chronological scroll; the chip row slices it — Due, Classes, or a single course. Fastest for “what's actually next”, weaker for browsing a course as a whole.

1S

Study

The study guide as a tool, not a document. Exam radar up top, one guide per course, units that go red under 40% mastery, and a tap-to-flip drill. The ECON guide carries your audio version. Study tab → ECON 1020.

1C

First run

Shared by both. Three screens: the promise, the four PDFs it just read, and the alerts you'll actually get. It ends on Today with the semester already loaded.

1D

Syllabus import

The one screen the app is built around. Real text from your ECON syllabus is already pasted — hit extract to watch it resolve “roughly every Friday” into nine dated rows you confirm.

1E

Alerts

Morning batch at 8am rather than a trickle. Clear all to see the empty state. Which of these fire is set in Settings.

1F

Calendar & search

September at a glance — one mark per deadline, tap a day for the list. Search runs across titles, professors, kinds and dates; clear the field for the no-results state.
