## A. Business

### What Semester can offer now

Semester can offer a controlled pre-pilot evaluation process: discovery, fit assessment, synthetic demonstration, evidence exchange, limitation review, implementation scoping and a conditional pilot proposal. This is not permission to invoice, accept live institutional data or promise activation.

The first institutional wedge is a proposed single-cohort, single-milestone student operating layer centered on manual or separately approved read-only context, Today/This Week, tasks and deadlines, workload planning, support discovery and privacy-thresholded reporting. A 50–200 student cohort is a planning range, not an approved commitment. Official-record writes, autonomous high-impact decisions and replacement of the SIS, LMS, registrar, financial-aid, student-accounts or housing systems are excluded.

The first prospect profile is a design-partner student-success, advising or first-year-program team with an engaged executive sponsor, cooperative IT/privacy/security/accessibility reviewers, a narrow problem, willingness to use synthetic or approved minimal data, and no requirement for immediate system replacement. The primary student motion is invitation-based validation through an approved program or direct controlled cohort—not paid mass acquisition, coercive referrals or outcome advertising.

Pricing is illustrative and unapproved. There is no validated willingness-to-pay, approved rate card, executed customer contract, payment/tax/refund operation or revenue evidence. Commercially safe language describes the product experience, repository-tested controls, proposed pilot scope and explicit limitations. Prohibited language includes certified compliance or accessibility, named-customer approval, production SSO/SIS/LMS/LTI operation, proven outcomes, system replacement, uptime/RTO/RPO promises, 24/7 support, or enterprise/GA readiness.
