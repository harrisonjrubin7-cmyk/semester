## Operating rules

- A phase may extend; dates never waive evidence.

- Keep individual validation, design-partner activation, paid pilot and broad enterprise decisions separate.
- Do not backfill metrics, imply customer status, or convert synthetic/repository evidence into operated outcomes.
- Reissue [`GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md`](GO-NO-GO-DECISION.md) whenever material scope, evidence, ownership or risk changes.
